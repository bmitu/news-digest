G7 emergency oil reserve release
4 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · 2 independent origins: BBC, G7

In a joint statement the leaders said the release would be coordinated through the IEA, begin immediately and run over four months, including a frontloaded substantial diesel release within the first 20 days by G7 members and partners. The Guardian reports, citing proposals reportedly drawn up by Emmanuel Macron, that the split would be 50m barrels of diesel and 50m of crude, while the BBC says only that the total is a mix of the two and that it is not yet clear which partner countries will release stocks.
