SpaceX quarterly results

From reports by News search: earnings, The Guardian, CNBC Markets and 5 more · Oct 20 – Oct 21

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.6 billion, up 161% from a year earlier and above the LSEG consensus estimate of $12.72 billion, with net income of $4.4 billion.123 Its Super Intelligence (AI) segment, now its largest unit, grew ninefold to $7.56 billion, while Starlink revenue rose to $5.2 billion from $3 billion.45 SpaceX also forecast first-quarter 2027 revenue of $33 billion to $37 billion, against an average analyst estimate of $22.2 billion according to LSEG.6 BBC says SpaceX is not profitable overall, having lost $1.1bn over the first nine months despite the $4.4bn profit for the latest quarter, and Investing.com notes capital expenditures climbed to $19.6 billion.78

On a Tuesday earnings call, Elon Musk said he supports combining SpaceX with Tesla and that special committees have been formed at both companies to explore it, but no terms have been agreed.192021 Musk cautioned that a deal is not certain and would need approval from at least 50% of Tesla's shareholders, which he called a "quite a high hurdle to achieve".2223 Musk said he is a "conflicted party" and is not on either committee, and he compared even discussing the idea to "tap dancing through a minefield".242526 Business Insider noted that a merger would be complicated, since SpaceX's US military contracts could put Tesla's China business in a difficult position.27
