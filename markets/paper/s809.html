Hot September PPI report

From reports by finance.yahoo.com, CNBC Markets · Oct 19 – Oct 21

According to a Motley Fool article, the September Producer Price Index report showed headline PPI up 6.3% and core PPI up 5%.1 CNBC reports that wholesale final demand goods prices rose 2.6%, the fastest pace in a Bureau of Labor Statistics series going back to December 2009.2 CNBC says Fed officials have said they are tracking the inflationary effects of AI investment, which is driving up the cost of chips and electronics.3

On Sept. 16, Fed Chair Kevin Warsh and the other FOMC members voted 12-0 to raise rates by 25 basis points to 3.75%-4.00%.4 Markets expect the Fed to hold rates at its meeting next week, but CME FedWatch futures pricing implies a strong chance of quarter-point hikes in December and March 2027, according to CNBC.56 The Motley Fool's Sean Williams argues that Warsh's repeated phrase "dose of accommodation" suggests the hiking cycle will ramp up in December and through 2027, which he says would be bad for the AI-driven bull market.78

CNBC reports that the inflation backdrop complicates the Fed's response to a slumping housing market. If the Fed raises short-term rates as expected, mortgage rates already at 7.43% could push even higher. Pending home sales slid 7.3% in September to an all-time low in records going back to 2001.91011 However, Lawrence Yun, the NAR's chief economist, thinks mortgage rates could be topping out, since they have already risen much more than the Fed's short-term rate hikes.12
