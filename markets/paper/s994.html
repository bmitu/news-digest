UK regular pay growth outpaces inflation

From reports by Bank of England, BBC Business · Oct 20 – Oct 21

The latest official figures show UK regular pay growing ahead of inflation in the three months to August, according to the BBC.1 Average annual growth in pay excluding bonuses was 3.7% over that period, and after taking inflation into account regular pay grew by 0.8% between June and August.23

Meanwhile, UK consumer price inflation rose to 3.5% in the year to September, the highest in nearly a year and above the Bank of England's 2% target, which the BBC says was slightly higher than economists had predicted and driven by higher petrol and diesel prices.45 Inflation is widely expected to rise again in the coming months, and the BBC links this to expected petrol price rises and a 4% increase in household energy bills from 1 October.67 On interest rates, the BBC reports that Bank governor Andrew Bailey said at the September meeting that energy price volatility made rate rises more likely, with the base rate currently 3.75%.89
