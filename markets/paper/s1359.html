Fed hold and December hike odds

From reports by CNBC Markets, kitco.com · Oct 19 – Oct 21

CNBC notes the Fed raised rates by a quarter point in September, its first hike this year.1 The inflation backdrop includes September consumer prices up 0.6% on the month and 3.7% annually, and producer prices up 1.3% and 6.3%, according to Kitco.2 Kitco notes the Fed is in its premeeting communications blackout, leaving incoming economic data and energy-market developments as the main drivers of rate expectations.3

Kitco says traders assign about an 83% probability that the Fed holds rates at its Oct. 27-28 meeting, with futures indicating roughly a 94% probability that rates will be higher by the Dec. 9 meeting.45 CNBC, citing the CME's FedWatch Tool, put the chance of an unchanged rate this month at 82.8% and the December hike expectation at 90%, so the two sources differ somewhat on December odds. inference67

CNBC reported that gold fell on Monday as a firm dollar and elevated Treasury yields pressured prices while investors awaited next week's Fed decision, when officials are expected to leave rates unchanged.8 Kitco reports that spot gold was slightly higher and silver lower in early U.S. trading Wednesday, as Strait of Hormuz energy concerns and expectations of further Federal Reserve tightening weighed on precious metals.9

Fawad Razaqzada of Forex.com says concerns that central banks must tighten further are boosting yields, and that the tone of the Fed's guidance will matter, with a hawkish message possibly bringing further short-term weakness for gold.1011 Kitco argues that softer jobs and business data could reduce December tightening expectations and support gold, while persistently elevated inflation expectations or higher energy prices would strengthen the case for more tightening.1213
