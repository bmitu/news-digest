Musk weighs Tesla-SpaceX merger

From reports by The Guardian, CNBC Markets, BBC Business and 5 more · Oct 21 – Oct 22

The BBC reports Tesla is valued at roughly $1.5 trillion and SpaceX at $2 trillion, which it says would make a combination one of the largest mergers in history.1 The talk came alongside SpaceX's results: revenue of $14.6 billion, up 161% from a year earlier and above the LSEG consensus of $12.72 billion, with net income of $4.4 billion.234 Sources give different moves for the stocks: the BBC said SpaceX rose 8% after hours and Tesla 2%, Investing.com said SpaceX settled 7.8% higher and Tesla 3.4%, and CNBC said SpaceX jumped 10% on Wednesday. inference5678

On Tesla's own earnings call on Wednesday, Musk said, "With regard to a potential merger with SpaceX, I think I said everything that should be said yesterday."9 Musk cautioned against treating a deal as certain, saying that doing so would be "overstating it" and that Tesla would need approval from at least 50% of its shareholders, which he called "quite a high hurdle to achieve".1011 Musk said the committees consist of independent directors, that he is excluded from them as a "conflicted party", and that there is "no guarantee" they will reach agreement.1213

Futurum Group's David Nicholson said the merger is "100%. It's just a question of when," and Telos Wealth Advisors' Dan Sheehan said Musk will likely get his way within a few years.1415 Sheehan also warned that Tesla shareholders would likely hold a "smaller slice of a much larger and more complex business" and that there is "no real precedent" for how Tesla would be priced in a deal.1617
