Netflix weak fourth-quarter profit forecast

From reports by CNBC Markets, reuters.com, hollywoodreporter.com and 2 more · Oct 20 – Oct 21

Netflix shares fell after the company issued a fourth-quarter forecast that disappointed investors, with Netflix guiding to Q4 revenue of about $13.44 billion, which missed consensus, according to Investing.com. inference12 Reports differ on the size of the stock move: CNBC said shares fell about 6% in extended trading, Reuters said they sank 4.5% on Wednesday afternoon, and Investing.com said Netflix ended 5.3% lower. inference345 Investing.com said the stock has lost more than 26% year to date amid struggles with user engagement, while Netflix's shareholder letter said "engagement... remains healthy." inference67

Netflix reported third-quarter earnings per share of 87 cents versus 82 cents expected by analysts polled by LSEG, with revenue of $12.87 billion in line with estimates.8 The Hollywood Reporter called the 11.8% third-quarter revenue growth "a bad look" for the company.9

Netflix expects fourth-quarter revenue to rise more than 11% to $13.44 billion, and The Hollywood Reporter says it forecasts $2.787 billion in net income and EPS of 67 cents. inference1011 Netflix said it expects the fourth-quarter results to be hit by last week's layoffs, with a negative impact on operating margin of about 120 basis points.12

The narrowed full-year revenue forecast of $51.1 billion is at the low end of Netflix's earlier range and below the $51.2 billion Wall Street expected, according to LSEG.13 Netflix also cut its 2026 advertising revenue forecast to around $2.7 billion from roughly $3 billion, citing a slower-than-expected rollout of some ad product capabilities.141516
