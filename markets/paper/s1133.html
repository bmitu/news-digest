IBM Q3 results and outlook

From reports by Investing.com, finance.yahoo.com, barrons.com · Oct 21 – Oct 22

IBM reported third-quarter 2026 results on October 21, beating Wall Street forecasts, with revenue of $17.1 billion and adjusted earnings per share of $2.95.12 Revenue rose 5% year over year, or 6% excluding currency effects, according to Investing.com.3 Software revenue of $7.80 billion grew 8%, and IBM raised its annual software growth outlook to 7%–8% from 6%–8%.45 IBM also raised its full-year free cash flow target to an increase of more than $1 billion year over year but kept its constant-currency revenue growth forecast at 4%–5%.67

Weaker spots included consulting revenue, which was flat at $5.34 billion against a $5.37 billion consensus, and an adjusted gross margin of 58.5% versus a 59.1% consensus, according to Investing.com; 24/7 Wall St. adds that mainframe revenue from IBM Z fell 20%.89 Investing.com reads the results as the software recovery and raised guidance investors had wanted, restoring confidence, though it flags consulting softness and margin compression as areas to watch.1011

The sources disagree on the market reaction: Investing.com reports IBM stock rose 3.3% in after-hours trading, while a 24/7 Wall St. article on Yahoo Finance reports it down 7% to $233.50 in morning trading. inference1213 24/7 Wall St. attributes the selloff to Krishna's warning and the unchanged revenue forecast, and notes that Accenture fell 0.5% and Infosys 2%, which it takes to suggest the damage is mostly contained to IBM. inference1415

On the call, CEO Arvind Krishna said enterprise customers are becoming "a lot more cognizant and a lot more aware of what is the true ROI of all of their AI spend," and that some clients fear AI spending "completely out of control with no real return."1617 Krishna framed this as an opening for IBM, saying "That is the tailwind for us."18
