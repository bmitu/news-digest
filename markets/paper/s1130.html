Tesla Q3 earnings

From reports by finance.yahoo.com, investors.com, Investing.com · Oct 21 – Oct 22

Tesla beat Wall Street estimates for third-quarter revenue, according to Reuters, reporting $28.05 billion for the quarter ended September 30 against an LSEG analyst average of $27.88 billion.1 Yahoo Finance and Investor's Business Daily frame the revenue differently: Yahoo cites a Bloomberg consensus of $28.13 billion, and Investor's Business Daily says revenue fell just short. inference23 Reuters says a rebound in deliveries and growth in energy storage lifted sales, while heavy spending on AI and robotaxis weighed on profit.4

Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles, down about 2% from a year earlier but above expectations of about 461,100, according to Reuters.56

Yahoo Finance reports adjusted EPS of $0.48 against a $0.44 estimate, and adjusted EBITDA of $3.90 billion against $3.66 billion.7 Free cash flow was $1.53 billion against an expected cash burn of $4.92 billion, and capital expenditure was $4.73 billion against an expectation of about $7.5 billion, according to Reuters.89 Yahoo Finance reports that on the earnings call Tesla cut its full-year capex guidance to $20 billion from $25 billion, and that Chief Financial Officer Vaibhav Taneja said the savings would come from managing capital deployment efficiently.1011

Tesla said its Optimus robot has started production at its Fremont factory, and Musk said reaching an annualized rate of 1 million units would probably take at least a year, maybe longer.1213

Musk said on SpaceX's earnings call that both companies have formed special committees of independent directors to discuss a possible merger, but said a merger should not be assumed to be certain and that he thinks it would be a good idea.1415 On Tesla's call, Musk added nothing new on the merger.16

Stock reaction was mixed: Reuters says shares rose more than 1% in extended trading, while Investor's Business Daily says they fell over 2% in late trade. inference1718 Regulators and analysts raised doubts: NHTSA issued Tesla a special order with 21 requests about how it self-certified the Cybercab, and Morningstar expects the Roadster to sell fewer than 2,000 units a year.1920
