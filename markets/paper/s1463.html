Brent crude surges above $116-117

From reports by News search: commodities, The Guardian, finance.yahoo.com and 2 more · Oct 23

Brent crude rose about 3.5% on Friday to pass $117 a barrel at one point, its highest level since April, according to The Guardian.1 Yahoo Finance reports that December Brent futures settled at $116.72 a barrel, the third consecutive weekly gain, while West Texas Intermediate closed just above $103.234 The Guardian says the jump followed Tehran's statement that it would attack oil facilities, undersea cables, datacentres and AI facilities in the Gulf if a new US-Israeli offensive were launched against Iran.5

Yahoo Finance says the New York Times reported this week that the US and Israel have reportedly drawn up plans for renewed attacks on Iran after the Nov. 3 elections.6 Yahoo Finance also reports that President Trump has said he will not attack Iran before the midterms and has suggested fuel prices will fall afterward.7 Richard Hunter of Interactive Investor said reports that the US is considering renewed strikes after the midterms were enough for the price to pop, in a conflict now in its eighth month.89

Susannah Streeter of Wealth Club said fears were rising of strikes on Iran's energy infrastructure after the 3 November elections, and that Ukraine's bombardment of Russian diesel refineries was also adding to supply concerns.1011 Attacks in the region added to supply worries: Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed to have struck four commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, and attacks hit Saudi airports and an Aramco oil field, with the US ordering some diplomats and nonessential personnel out of Saudi Arabia. inference121314 Yahoo Finance adds that Saudi Aramco on Thursday ordered the evacuation of its Dhahran headquarters, its first such evacuation since the Iran war began.15

Gulf Oil's Tom Kloza told Yahoo Finance that people should expect a lot of $5 gas prices in places like Michigan, Ohio, Minnesota, Illinois, and Indiana; Yahoo Finance reports US gasoline at $4.29 a gallon and diesel around $6.31.161718
