Meta Q3 earnings

From reports by Investing.com, finance.yahoo.com · Oct 21 – Oct 22

Meta beat Wall Street's third-quarter revenue expectations, reporting revenue of $64.60 billion, up 26% from a year earlier, on strong advertising growth.123 Meta forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $73 billion to $76 billion, above analyst expectations, and said its board approved a new class of nonvoting Class C shares.45

Advertising revenue was $63.06 billion, and ad impressions rose 10% while the average price per ad rose 14%.678 Operating income was $12.99 billion, below analyst expectations of $20.85 billion, weighed down by a $9.89 billion legal charge tied to an August settlement with 52 state attorneys general over teen mental health litigation.91011 Capital expenditures were $34.91 billion including finance lease principal payments, leaving negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion, and Meta narrowed its 2026 capex forecast to $135 billion to $145 billion while raising its expense forecast to $175 billion to $179 billion.12131415

CEO Mark Zuckerberg said, "Our investments are paying off, the core business is strong, and we're seeing velocity across the board."2122 Zuckerberg said more than 10 million people in the U.S. and Canada had set up Muse in its first six weeks, but described it as "very compute intensive to run" and said a more-than-10x efficiency gain would let it turn profitable at scale.232425 Reuters noted that some enterprises are restricting the Muse agent's access over security concerns, and that a judge in New Mexico ordered a $25 billion penalty against Meta on the same day.2627
