The official series behind this week's markets news, built 2026-10-22 01:18 UTC from the central banks' own data and FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis). Each card shows the latest figure, where it stood a year earlier and a chart of the last two years (five for monthly series, four for quarterly). Where this week's news reported a figure that matches the official one, the card quotes it.
Each card is tinted by its latest figure: normal or good worth watching a warning sign, against a line its publisher or a named benchmark draws (the Fed's 2% target, the Sahm rule, an inverted yield curve); the card says which. Interest rates, the mortgage rate and the dollar are left plain: not rated, which is not the same as normal, as there is no published line and a higher figure is good for some and bad for others (which industries move with or against them). Oil is read against the prices US shale producers need (Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas and Kansas City energy surveys) and Hamilton's oil-shock measure; gasoline by the share of income Americans spend on it.
