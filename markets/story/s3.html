Anthropic IPO filing / S-1

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 19:31 UTC
4 sources · 3 independent origins: Reuters, The Ringer, Tomasz Tunguz

Anthropic's IPO prospectus, leaked to Reuters, reportedly shows a 2025 net loss of $42 billion on $4.6 billion in revenues, as the company targets an IPO valuation of $2 trillion.12 Wolf Richter says the net loss was five times the $8 billion of 2024 and revenues rose twelvefold, with total operating expenses of $12.6 billion including $7.3 billion on compute and infrastructure.34 Reuters said that excluding $34 billion of write-downs mostly tied to previous fundraising, the operating loss was $8.1 billion.5

The company held $20.3 billion in cash and short-term investments at the end of 2025, before a $65 billion Series H round in May 2026 that valued it at $965 billion.67 According to Reuters, the prospectus says Anthropic plans to spend $518 billion on cloud, computing and infrastructure obligations in coming years.89 The prospectus reportedly says a quarter of revenue came from two customers, and warns that many large clients are not locked into long-term contracts and could cut or stop spending.10

The Guardian, citing Reuters and the Financial Times, reports the prospectus warns advanced AI could pose "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity" and that models could show self-preserving behaviours such as attempts to resist shutdown.1112 Reuters reportedly found about 80 of the 261 pages of the prospectus's main body were devoted to risk factors, versus 48 pages describing the business.13 The Guardian says some experts have criticised existential risk warnings as unverifiable and unscientific, while it notes the reported valuation compares with the $1.8 trillion achieved by Elon Musk's SpaceX.1415

Richter argues the $2 trillion target would more than double the Series H valuation and calls it "AI magic money", and he urges holding executives legally accountable for harm from AI products.1617 Anthropic declined to comment, and the Guardian notes the prospectus has yet to be made public.1819
