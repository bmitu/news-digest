Anthropic IPO filing / S-1

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 15:38 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 4 · 3 independent origins: Reuters, The Ringer, Tomasz Tunguz

Anthropic's IPO prospectus, which was leaked to Reuters, reportedly shows a net loss of $42 billion in 2025 on $4.6 billion in revenues, according to Wolf Richter's account of the Reuters report.12 The net loss had multiplied by five from $8 billion in 2024, and revenues had multiplied by 12, according to Richter.3 Total operating expenses reached $12.6 billion in 2025, including $7.3 billion on compute and infrastructure, nearly triple the $2.5 billion of 2024.4 Reuters, as quoted by Richter, says that excluding $34 billion of write-downs mostly tied to earlier fundraising, the operating loss was $8.1 billion.5

Anthropic held $20.3 billion in cash and short-term investments at the end of 2025, before a $65 billion Series H round in May 2026.67 Richter says Anthropic is targeting an IPO valuation of $2 trillion, which would more than double the $965 billion valuation set in its Series H round in May.89

Per the prospectus, Anthropic plans to spend $518 billion in coming years on cloud, computing and infrastructure obligations, and Reuters describes the document as showing a sweeping bet that AI will transform the global economy more than industrialization, electricity and the internet.1011 A quarter of revenues came from two customers, and the company warned in its risk factors that many large clients are not locked into long-term contracts and could cut or stop spending, according to Reuters.12

Richter says Anthropic now needs a lot of "AI magic money" and argues that executives and their companies should be held legally responsible and accountable for products that cause harm; he says politicians should condemn efforts to create liability shields for these companies.131415 Link roundups list further items under an Anthropic heading, including an Axios piece titled "Anthropic need capital, IPO or not" and a Ringer piece titled "So what does the Anthropic S-1 say?"1617
