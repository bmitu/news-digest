Fed watchdog finds renovation cost mismanagement, no criminal violations

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 15:38 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 4 · 1 new article today · single origin, unclear which

The Federal Reserve's internal watchdog said the Fed mismanaged costs of a $2.4bn renovation of its Washington DC headquarters but found no criminal violations, according to a report released on Wednesday.1 The report said the Fed's board of governors "has not effectively managed" the project and "repeatedly deviated from its cost-management provisions", but it found no administrative misconduct.23 Construction costs rose from an initially budgeted $921m in February 2020 to $2.018bn by December 2024, and completion, originally due in mid-2024, is not slated until December 2027.45

Attorney General Todd Blanche said the Justice Department would not reopen a criminal investigation of Powell based on the findings, but could investigate if a review by an independent auditor the Fed has said it will engage finds criminal misconduct.678

Powell has stayed on the board since leaving the chair in May, saying threats of criminal investigations left him no option, a stance Trump and other administration members condemned.9 After the report, Trump posted that Powell "should be forced to resign" from the board, and Kevin Hassett said on Fox News it was "time for him to move on".1011
