Anthropic IPO prospectus filed

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 16:14 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · single origin (Reuters)

Anthropic's IPO prospectus, which has not been made public, was reportedly seen by Reuters, which reported that Anthropic had a net loss of $42 billion in 2025.1234 Anthropic is reportedly seeking a valuation of more than $2 trillion, compared with the $1.8 trillion achieved by Elon Musk's SpaceX.5

Wolf Richter, as quoted by a commenter, says Anthropic generated a net loss of $42 billion in 2025 on $4.6 billion in revenues, according to its IPO prospectus that was leaked to Reuters.4 According to the prospectus as reported by Reuters, Anthropic plans to spend $518 billion on cloud, computing and infrastructure obligations in coming years.6 Reuters reported that the prospectus presents a bet that AI will transform the global economy more profoundly than industrialization, electricity and the internet.7

The prospectus reportedly warns that advanced AI could pose "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity", including models that "resist shutdown" or show behaviour "resembling blackmail".89 Reuters reported that about 80 of the 261 pages of the main body cover risk factors, versus 48 pages describing the business.10

Anthropic declined to comment, and the Guardian says some experts have criticised existential risk warnings as unverifiable and unscientific.1112 A commenter on Wolf Richter's post says the numbers should be enough to send investors running for the exit, while another invites bets on whether Anthropic ever IPOs or instead replaces WeWork as the biggest failed IPO in history.1314 A link-roundup headline from Axios, as listed by Tadas Viskanta, says Anthropic needs capital, IPO or not.15
