JetBlue shares and creditors

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
2 sources · single origin, unclear which

JetBlue Airways stock slid 7.7% in afternoon trading to $2.71 and touched a fresh 52-week low of $2.63 intraday, according to Investing.com.1 Investing.com attributes the drop to a sympathy sell-off across U.S. airline stocks after American Airlines reported Q3 2026 results that included a full-year loss forecast, driven by jet fuel near $4.70 per gallon, roughly double a year earlier.2 Delta Air Lines and United Airlines also declined sharply, and American's fuel expenses rose 67.5% year-over-year in Q3.34 The wider market was weak, with the NASDAQ Composite down 2.6% and the S&P 500 down 1.3%, and airline stocks underperformed even that backdrop.56

Earlier in the week, UBS upgraded JBLU to Neutral from Sell after a 33% decline since September 1, while cutting its price target to $3.50 and arguing risk-reward had become more balanced.78 JetBlue also announced an expansion of its TrueBlue program, adding complimentary BlueFirst domestic first-class upgrades for Mosaic members from February 2027, which the market viewed as longer-term retention rather than a near-term earnings catalyst.9

Investing.com concludes sentiment remains fragile with the stock near its 52-week low, and further deterioration in the fuel cost outlook could extend the pressure.10
