Oura Health IPO postponed

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 16:33 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · 2 independent origins: Axios, CNBC

Oura Health postponed its initial public offering on Tuesday, citing "uncertainty" in market conditions while also claiming there was "strong demand" for its stock.1 A link roundup separately noted, citing Axios, that Oura Health had pulled its IPO.2

Four companies in different sectors, all aiming to raise at least $50 million, announced postponements or withdrawals in the past week, according to Renaissance Capital data, bringing the third-quarter total to seven, up from four in the second quarter and three in the first.34 The other recent cases were Holtec Nuclear, which withdrew its IPO last Friday, Amaero, which postponed last Wednesday, and Bamboo Insurance, which postponed on Sept. 22.5 Despite the delays, about $146.9 billion has been raised in 110 IPOs this year, excluding SPACs, per Renaissance Capital, though that is down 30% from this time last year.67

Analysts say surging bond yields are largely behind the weaker market for new offerings, and Renaissance Capital's Matthew Kennedy argues the string of postponements says something about the market rather than about individual companies.89 Renaissance analysts wrote that third-quarter IPO activity fell below expectations because of AI spending concerns, a 19-year high in bond yields and resumed rate hikes.10

Jay Ritter of the University of Florida's Warrington College of Business says he has a little sympathy for market conditions as a rationale, while Gil Luria of DA Davidson says investors have been burned by narrow consumer products, drawing a line around Peloton, GoPro, FitBit and Oura, and that this is the reaction Oura is getting.1112
