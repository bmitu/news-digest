Meta Q3 earnings

Last scan Thu Oct 22, 01:18 UTC
3 sources · single origin (Meta)

Meta beat Wall Street's third-quarter revenue expectations, reporting $64.60 billion for the quarter ended Sept. 30, up 26% from a year earlier, according to Investing.com.1 Earnings per share were $3.83 against a consensus of $3.57, and advertising revenue was $63.06 billion, up 26% and ahead of expectations for $61.85 billion.23 Sources give slightly different revenue consensus figures: Investing.com cites $63.33 billion, while Reuters cites $63.37 billion from LSEG. inference45 Meta also forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $73 billion to $76 billion, a midpoint of $74.5 billion that is above analyst expectations of $73.57 billion.6

However, profit was weighed down by a $9.89 billion legal charge tied to an August settlement with 52 state attorneys general, and operating income of $12.99 billion fell below analyst expectations of $20.85 billion.78 Reuters adds that legal challenges are mounting, including a $25 billion penalty ordered by a New Mexico judge on the day of the report.9 Meta raised its 2026 expense forecast to $175 billion to $179 billion from $165 billion to $169 billion, and narrowed its capital expenditure forecast to $135 billion to $145 billion from $130 billion to $145 billion.1011

Separately, Meta's board approved a new class of publicly listed, nonvoting Class C common stock, effective Jan. 19, 2027, to be distributed as a one-time stock dividend of one Class C share per Class A and Class B share.1213 Investing.com says the shares give Meta another tool to fund growth, pay employees and make acquisitions, and Meta expects the dividend to act like a two-for-one split without changing voting power.1415 Reuters reports the new shares may let Meta finance acquisitions and attract talent without diluting Mark Zuckerberg's voting control.16

Zuckerberg said, "Our investments are paying off, the core business is strong, and we're seeing velocity across the board," and Investing.com reported shares rose 4% after the results.1718 In a Yahoo Finance discussion, an unnamed analyst (Speaker B) said Meta is being careful not to guide to capex or expenses that are too high, and that investors should be happy with this quarter's revenue trend and expense discipline.1920 The same speaker argued that Muse does nothing ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini cannot do, but that its head start should work in Meta's favor.2122
