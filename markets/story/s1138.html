RTX Q3 earnings

Last scan Thu Oct 22, 01:18 UTC
2 sources · single origin (RTX)

RTX reported third-quarter results that beat analyst expectations on both earnings and revenue and raised its full-year outlook, and shares rose 2.4% premarket, according to Investing.com.1 Adjusted earnings per share were $2.26 against an analyst consensus of $1.78, and revenue was $25.0 billion against estimates of $24.09 billion, an 11% rise from $22.5 billion a year earlier.23 The company attributed the performance to double-digit organic sales growth in commercial aerospace and defense, plus $0.25 per share from IEEPA cost recovery.4

By segment, Collins Aerospace sales rose 10% to $8.4 billion, Pratt & Whitney revenue rose 10% to $9.2 billion, and Raytheon sales climbed 14% to $8.0 billion.56 RTX reported a record backlog of $340 billion, made up of $176 billion in commercial orders and $164 billion in defense contracts.7

RTX raised its full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $7.65 to $7.75, whose $7.70 midpoint is above the analyst consensus of $7.25.8 It also lifted its revenue outlook to $97.0 billion to $97.5 billion, with a midpoint of $97.25 billion that Investing.com says is above analyst expectations of $96.2 billion.910 Chairman and CEO Chris Calio said strong execution enabled double-digit organic sales growth and that RTX is again raising its full-year outlook for adjusted sales, adjusted EPS, and free cash flow.1112
