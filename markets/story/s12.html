Gold falls to multi-month low

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 15:38 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 4 · 2 independent origins: Bespoke Investment Group, CNBC

Bespoke Investment Group put the decline at 3.5% for front-month gold as of its writing, and said the worst of the selling across metals was coming from gold.1 The two sources give slightly different sizes for the drop, roughly 3.5% in Bespoke's account and 4% in CNBC's. inference23 Bespoke says the drop ranks in the first percentile of all days over the past half century, and is the seventh decline of 3.5% or more this year.45

Bespoke adds that gold is trading in oversold territory for the first time since July 29th, following last week's break below its 50-day moving average, while silver has also fallen below its 50-day average for the first time since August 8th but is not quite oversold yet.67

CNBC reports that options flows suggest traders expect gold to rebound, with roughly twice as many calls as puts traded in the SPDR Gold Shares ETF (GLD).89 The biggest single GLD trade was a seller of 2,000 375-strike puts expiring January 2028, worth $5.9 million, which CNBC says may have been closing a bearish position or a bet that gold finds a floor near $375.1011

In contrast, options trading in high-yield bonds (HYG) was more lopsidedly bearish, and Nigam Arora, founder of The Arora Report, said the risk of defaults is probably much higher than the market anticipates and that he wouldn't touch them.1213

Why are gold and high-yield bonds both falling, and what is the link to the surge in Treasury yields?

Gold and high-yield bonds are being hit by the same surge in rates: gold fell 4% to its lowest since early August as the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.3% and the 30-year touched 5.4%.1 High-yield corporate bonds extended a five-day rout to their lowest level since April 2025, as measured by the HYG ETF.2 Both gold and high-yield bonds are trading with strong negative correlations to the 10-year yield, with a 10-day correlation of negative 0.8 for GLD and -0.99 for HYG, according to CNBC.3 CNBC describes the relentless rise in rates as breaking the back of two key macro trades that had been holding firm.4 Through Monday afternoon in September, GLD was down more than 7% for the month and long-term Treasuries, as proxied by TLT, were down 4.7%, according to Bespoke.5 Nigam Arora of The Arora Report says high-yield sentiment has been complacent and default risk is probably higher than the market expects, since many of these bonds carry variable rates and debt is coming due next year.678 The reports do not explain why Treasury yields are surging in the first place.

How high have the 10-year and 30-year Treasury yields climbed, and how closely does gold move against them?

Since August 2020, when the bond bear market began, the 10-year yield has risen from 0.5% to 5.28%.1 The 30-year yield closed one week at 5.63% and briefly hit 5.69% on Thursday, the highest since 2002, according to Wolf Richter.2 Gold has moved closely against the 10-year yield, with a 10-day correlation of negative 0.8, according to CNBC's reporting.3 Gold fell 4% to its lowest since early August as yields rose.4 The correlation of gold with the 30-year yield was not reported.

How big was the options activity in high-yield bond ETF HYG compared with its normal volume, and which contracts did traders favor?

Trading volume in HYG options on Monday was more than 2.5 times the 30-day average.1 Puts dominated: more than 2.5 times as many puts traded as calls, with 52,000 puts likely bought versus just over 15,000 calls, according to Cboe LiveVol data.23 Eleven of the top 12 contracts by dollar amount bought were puts, and puts made up 8 of the 10 most popular contracts by volume.4 The single most common contract bought was the 78-strike put expiring Nov. 20.5 Overall, about $35 million of options premium changed hands, with $30 million of it tied to calls, according to SpotGamma data.6
