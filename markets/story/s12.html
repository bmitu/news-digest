Gold falls to multi-month low

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 19:31 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Bespoke Investment Group, CNBC

Gold fell sharply on Monday, September 28, 2026, with sources putting the drop at roughly 3% to 4% as bond yields climbed. inference123 CNBC reported that the 10-year yield climbed to 5.3% and the 30-year yield touched 5.4%.4

Bespoke Investment Group said gold was the worst performer among metals, and that the drop extended last week's break below its 50-day moving average.56 Bespoke said gold is oversold for the first time since July 29th, and that a drop of 3.5% ranks in the first percentile of days over the past half century.78 Bespoke counted this as the seventh decline of 3.5% or more this year, the most since 2008, when there were 8 in the full year.910

CNBC reported that options flows suggested traders expect gold to rebound, with about twice as many calls as puts traded in the SPDR Gold Shares ETF (GLD).1112 The largest GLD trade was a seller of 2,000 375-strike puts worth $5.9 million, which CNBC said may have been closing a bearish position or betting gold finds a floor near $375.1314

Options activity in high-yield bond ETF HYG was more lopsidedly bearish, and Nigam Arora of The Arora Report said of high-yield bonds, "I wouldn't touch them."1516
