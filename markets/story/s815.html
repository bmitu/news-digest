UK September inflation rises to 3.5%

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 02:15 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · single origin (Office for National Statistics)

UK annual inflation rose to 3.5% in the 12 months to September, up from 3.1% in August, driven mainly by sharply higher petrol and diesel prices, according to the Office for National Statistics.12 CNBC reports that economists polled by Reuters had forecast 3.4%, and that the reading was the highest in almost a year.34

Motor fuel prices rose 29.8% over the year to September, and petrol rose 8.2p per litre to 169.5p between August and September.567 ONS chief economist Grant Fitzner said airfares, computer games and live events also contributed, and the BBC notes core inflation rose to 2.8% from 2.6%.89

Markets are pricing a 98% chance, according to LSEG data, that the Bank of England raises rates by 25 basis points to 4% next month.10

Healey says the Budget will keep to fiscal rules while giving businesses and families some breathing space, whereas Conservative Shadow Chancellor Andrew Griffith says the figures show Labour had "lost control of the economy".1112
