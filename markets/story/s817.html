UK Autumn Budget ahead of 28 October

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 02:15 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 2 independent origins: BBC, Office for National Statistics

The ONS said the government borrowed £24.5bn in September, £1.8bn more than a year earlier, leaving borrowing this financial year £6bn above the OBR's forecast, while national debt topped £3tn for the first time.123 The BBC reports that debt as a share of economic output, the measure used for the fiscal rules, fell, and that the OBR's March estimate of £23.6bn headroom is expected to have shrunk, with KPMG believing it could have fallen to £12bn and the Institute for Fiscal Studies estimating it could be £20bn.45

CNBC says the 3.5% figure was hotter than the 3.4% forecast by economists polled by Reuters, and the ONS said motor fuel prices rose 29.8% in the year, with petrol at its highest since August 2022.678 The BBC links the fuel price rises to the effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz by Iran after US and Israeli attacks, and ONS chief economist Grant Fitzner said airfares, computer games and live events also contributed.910

Healey says the Budget will stick to the fiscal rules while driving growth and giving businesses and families "breathing space", and Labour has committed not to raise income tax, National Insurance or VAT.111213 The BBC notes he has refused to rule out other tax increases, and bank bosses told the BBC they expect a tax rise on the sector.1415

Suren Thiru of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales called the Budget "a perilous high-wire act for the Chancellor", and Ruth Gregory of Capital Economics expects a cautious Budget that leaves the toughest fiscal choices for next year.1617

The Conservatives said the figures showed Labour had "lost control of the economy", and Shadow Chancellor Andrew Griffith called for Healey to use his Budget to tackle the deficit; markets price a 98% chance of a Bank of England rate rise to 4% next month, according to LSEG data cited by CNBC.1819
