September pending home sales hit record low

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · single origin (CNBC)

CNBC says the decline was far worse than Wall Street's estimate of a 0.4% drop.1

Wolf Richter writes that September volume was nearly 40% below September 2019 and 43% below September 2021, in data going back to 2010.23 Regionally, Richter reports September pending sales fell 9.2% in the West, 8.2% in the South, 6.5% in the Midwest and 4.2% in the Northeast, with the West, South and Midwest at record lows.456

The sources give different start dates for the record: Richter says the data go back to 2010 in one article and 2011 in another, while CNBC says records stretch back to 2001. inference789

NAR chief economist Lawrence Yun attributed the weakness to "fast-rising mortgage rates," and the NAR report said buyers and sellers may be holding back until rates move favorably.101112 Yun also suggested mortgage rates could be topping out, since they have risen much more than the Fed's short-term rate hikes.13

Chris Rupkey of Fwdbonds said the housing market is heading into a "deep freeze," and Goldman Sachs economist Ronnie Walker expects affordability headwinds to increasingly dominate in 2027.1415 Richter says weekly Mortgage Bankers Association data show purchase applications falling for a seventh straight week, which he reads as meaning October pending sales will be even worse.1617
