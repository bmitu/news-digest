September pending home sales hit record low

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 02:15 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · single origin (CNBC)

Wolf Richter of WOLF STREET reports that the National Association of Realtors (NAR) found pending sales of existing homes in September fell 7.3% from August, seasonally adjusted, to record-low volume in data going back to 2010.12 Richter says that is 11.3% lower than a year earlier and nearly 40% below September 2019.3 Richter writes that pending sales are down 43% from September 2021 and 48% from 2020, and that the NAR metric tracks signed contracts that have not closed and could still be canceled, with cancellations running high.45

By region, Richter reports month-to-month drops of 8.2% in the South, 9.2% in the West, 6.5% in the Midwest and 4.2% in the Northeast, with the South, West and Midwest at their lowest volumes on record (or in the data).6789

The NAR report said, "Buyers and sellers may simply be holding back until mortgage rates move favorably."10 Richter argues that this waiting is the problem, since mortgage rates have been in a normal 6% to 8% range since September 2022, and that high inflation and home prices are what is out of line.11121314

Richter also cites Mortgage Bankers Association data showing purchase mortgage applications fell for a seventh straight week in the week ending October 16, down 18% from a year earlier.1516 He says this indicates October pending sales will be even worse than September's.17 The two Richter articles give slightly different weekly 30-year mortgage rates: 7.41% from the MBA and 7.40% from Freddie Mac. inference1819
