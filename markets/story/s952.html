Fed expected to hold, with hikes priced for late 2026 and early 2027

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
5 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 5 new articles this week · single origin (CNBC) · Official data: CPI inflation (year over year)

The Federal Open Market Committee is widely expected to leave rates unchanged at its 27–28 October meeting, with analysts and futures pricing pointing to hikes later, in December and March 2027. inference1234 PIMCO says markets are pricing roughly an 80% chance of a hold in October.5 The Daily Economy article says the Atlanta Fed's tracker gives roughly a 94 percent chance of at least one more hike by year-end, and CME FedWatch shows an 80 percent probability of a December quarter-point increase.67

PIMCO expects the FOMC to hold at 3.75%–4.0% while keeping a clear tightening bias, with a 25-basis-point hike in December the most likely next move.3 Employ America's base case is a hold next week followed by hikes in December and March, and TD Securities likewise expects a hold, then a resumption of tightening in December and March.84 TD Securities argues the December hike is "all but locked in" barring a shock, but warns that one notably bad inflation report or strong labor data could lead markets to price a 50bp increase.910

The Daily Economy reports September CPI rose 3.7% over the previous year, up from 3.4% in August, and that August core PCE was 3.0%, with the September CPI data pushing the inflation side of the Taylor rule toward higher rates.1112

Officials differ on timing: New York Fed President John Williams has said the Fed has time to gather more information, while the Daily Economy says more hawkish officials, like Hammack and Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan, have argued for tighter policy immediately.1314 PIMCO says a few participants may dissent from a hold, but argues the Fed has room for a measured approach, pausing to see how tightening already priced in is affecting the economy.1516

TD Securities strategists say FX markets remain meaningfully long the dollar going into the FOMC, and that data suggests the Fed has the highest rate hike pressures in the G10.17
