French 10-year yield hits 5%

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 19:55 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: Investing.com, The Guardian

The Guardian reports that the benchmark 10-year Oat yield rose more than 8 basis points to 5% in morning trading before easing to 4.99%.1 The premium of French yields over German Bunds has also been edging back toward early October's 15-year high of nearly 160 basis points, according to Reuters.2 France's budget deficit was 5.1% of GDP in 2025 and its debt stood at 115.6% of GDP, according to the Guardian, which says both are forecast to rise without remedial action.34

Reuters reports that Carrefour fell 13% after cutting its guidance because of the protests, and that French President Emmanuel Macron pledged to prevent a repeat of last weekend's riots.56 The Guardian says no single party has had overall control of parliament since Macron called a snap election in 2024, and that the parliamentary gridlock has blocked his plan to raise the pension age from 62 to 64 and a series of measures to reduce or redirect state spending.78

Matthew Ryan of Ebury says the political risk premium in France looks unlikely to fade any time soon and the euro continues to lag most major currencies. He says it is unclear whether the 2027 budget will clear parliament, and even if it does, it seems set to be so riddled with concessions that the deficit problem is left insufficiently addressed.910 John Hardy of Saxo says the French debt situation "isn't tractable" and that France needs to make proper fiscal adjustments.11 Antonio Fatas of INSEAD says the ECB would not step in just to bail out France, though it would intervene in some form in a European crisis.12
