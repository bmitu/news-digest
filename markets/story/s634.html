S&P 500 and Nasdaq record highs

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 17:01 UTC
5 sources · 4 independent origins: Bloomberg, Financial Times, Investing.com, The Wall Street Journal

The S&P 500 hit an intraday all-time high on Tuesday, October 6, 2026, with the Nasdaq also trading at record levels, as optimism about AI and hopes of strong corporate earnings lifted tech stocks, according to Reuters.12 Investing.com described it as the S&P 500's first record high since mid-August.3 Reuters reported that the index rose as much as 0.6% to 7817.13 points, passing the prior intraday record of 7,816.7 set on August 13.4 By 11:36 ET, Investing.com had the S&P 500 up 0.9% at 7,840.01, the Nasdaq Composite up 0.8% at 27,708.47 and the Dow up 0.7% at 51,623.83.56

The rally extended gains that began after Friday's jobs report showed softer-than-expected September U.S. job growth, which led investors to scale back bets on a Federal Reserve rate increase in October.7 Reuters noted the gains came despite higher energy prices and a volatile summer for bond markets that raised fears of tighter monetary policy, while bond yields eased from multi-year highs on Tuesday.89 Deutsche Bank strategists said there were "initial signs that the pressure on France was stabilizing", while Vital Knowledge analysts said Marine Le Pen's "aggressive" fiscal consolidation plan is contributing to a "relief rally" in Eurozone sovereign bonds.1011
