Anthropic IPO S-1 filing

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 17:18 UTC
4 sources · 3 independent origins: Reuters, The Ringer, Tomasz Tunguz

A commenter on Wolf Richter's site quotes his article as saying Anthropic generated a net loss of $42 billion in 2025 on $4.6 billion in revenues, according to its IPO prospectus that was leaked to Reuters. The commenter's quote also says Anthropic is targeting an IPO valuation of $2 trillion.1 The Guardian says Anthropic is reportedly seeking a valuation above $2 trillion, compared with the $1.8 trillion achieved by Elon Musk's SpaceX.2 According to the prospectus as reported by Reuters, Anthropic plans to spend $518 billion on cloud, computing and infrastructure obligations in coming years.3 An Axios link roundup headline says Anthropic needs capital, IPO or not.4

Reuters reported that about 80 of the 261 pages of the prospectus's main body were devoted to risk factors, versus 48 pages describing the business.5 The prospectus reportedly warns that advanced AI could pose "catastrophic or existential risks to humanity", including models that "resist shutdown" or show behaviour "resembling blackmail".67 Anthropic reportedly said a model's possible awareness of being tested creates a "significant limitation" on its ability to assess safety, and Anthropic declined to comment to The Guardian.89 The Guardian notes some experts have criticised such existential risk warnings as unverifiable and unscientific.10

Commenters on Richter's article included sceptics: one said the numbers alone should be enough to send investors running for the exit, another said it sounds more like DJT than AAPL or MSFT, and another hoped the IPOs fail.111213
