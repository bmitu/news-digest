August PCE inflation release and revisions

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 4 · 2 independent origins: Employ America, Wolf Street

The Commerce Department reported on Wednesday that the PCE price index rose a seasonally adjusted 0.3% in August, putting the 12-month gain at 3.4%, while core PCE (excluding food and energy) rose 0.2% for a 3% annual level.12 Economists surveyed by Dow Jones had expected 0.3% and 3.7% for headline PCE, and 0.3% and 3.3% for core, so the annual figures came in lighter than forecast.345

The release came with annual revisions, and CNBC reports the BEA adjusted its methodology for how it measures prices for legal services, software and computer accessories, and portfolio management; CNBC says the revisions lowered the core July PCE level by 0.36 percentage point.678 Wolf Richter reports that July's year-over-year core PCE was cut by 37 basis points to 2.98% from 3.35%, and that the all-items index for July was cut 34 basis points from 3.70%, with August all-items at 3.42%.910 Richter stresses that despite the methodology changes, inflation remained substantially above the Fed's 2% target, with the revised core PCE price index at 3.01% and the all-items PCE price index at 3.4%.11

Employ America said its core PCE nowcast was 28bps month-over-month and 3.14% year-over-year, versus the realized 25bps and 3.01%, and noted the month-over-month figure was only 1 basis point from rounding up to the 0.3% consensus.1213 Employ America says Williams's dovish speech and the small downside surprise in August core PCE have moved October pricing to a coinflip, and that it sees upside risk in the September data from airfares, food services, wireless communication services, tech equipment and other core goods categories.1415

Sonu Varghese of Carson Group said PCE inflation is "still running hot however you cut it," and Heather Long of Navy Federal Credit Union said the data show no progress in August and that September will inevitably be higher.1617

How much could the 3-month annualized core rate change if September's reading comes in hot, and what risks do analysts see in September data?

Employ America calculates that, assuming no revisions to July and August, a 25 basis point September reading would put the 3-month annualized rate at 2.55% and a 5 basis point reading would drop it to 1.73%. It separately describes the measure as a noisy gauge of underlying inflation, since a single month can swing it in either direction.123 On the hot side, Employ America calculates that a September core PCE reading of 35 basis points would lift the 3-month annualized rate to 2.96% and 40 basis points would lift it to 3.16%, up from about 2% now. inference456 The measure is sensitive in part because June drops out of the window with the September data, and Employ America says Governor Waller and others heavily weigh this gauge in their policy thinking.78 Employ America sees upside risk to September data from airfares, food services, wireless communication services, tech equipment and other core goods categories.910 Navy Federal Credit Union's Heather Long says it is inevitable that September will be higher, and Goldman Sachs expects the next couple of months of inflation data to be "somewhat less favorable before a more benign trend reasserts itself."111213
