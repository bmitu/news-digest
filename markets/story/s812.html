CoreWeave bond and stock selloff

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 02:27 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 3 independent origins: Axios, CNBC, Financial Times

CoreWeave's stock and bonds sold off sharply in the week of Oct. 19–22, 2026, and its credit default swaps reached levels generally read as distress, as investors worried about AI overspending and debt. inference123 A CNBC Pro contributor wrote that the stock fell 27.5% from last Friday's close to Thursday's 52-week lows before a 17% rebound, and that bonds issued at par in June at 8.5% fell nearly 18%.45

CNBC reported that the yield to maturity on CoreWeave's 9.75% bond due 2031 reached 15.78%, and that the five-year CDS spread at one point topped 1,140 basis points before standing near 1,038 bps, 164% higher than a year earlier.678 Janus Henderson's John Lloyd put the same bond's yield at 15.97%, a more than 70% jump since April issuance, and CNBC said the company has roughly $51.6 billion in total debt.910

CEO Mike Intrator dismissed the market moves in a memo obtained by CNBC, saying the market sometimes "acts irrationally" and that the backlog "more than supports all of our debt obligations."1112 Views diverge: Lloyd calls the selloff "a little bit of overreacting" and expects the bonds to recover "just not in that straight line," while HSBC rates the stock Sell with a $39 target and other banks see it more than doubling. inference13141516

Evercore ISI's trading desk said the market is "basically saying 'no mas' to AI debt without operating cash flow support," and Deutsche Bank's Jim Reid warned the concern could prove "a self-fulfilling prophecy" that risks triggering broader issues in other AI-related names; CNBC noted Oracle's five-year CDS spread also spiked.1718
