CoreWeave bond and stock selloff

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
5 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 3 new articles this week · 5 independent origins: Axios, CNBC, Financial Times, Junk Bond Investor, The Wall Street Journal

CNBC describes junk-rated CoreWeave as the poster child of AI debt worries, and BlackRock's Rick Rieder says his firm has been more cautious around data center supply that is not hyperscaler guaranteed or backed.12 CNBC reports CoreWeave has roughly $51.6 billion in total debt, and its five-year credit default swap spread is nearly 1,081 basis points, a level generally read as a sign of credit distress.34 Its debt-to-equity ratio is 702%, up from 290% a year ago, and CoreWeave will spend an estimated $36.4 billion on capex this year against Ebitda of $7.5 billion, according to consensus estimates.56

A Junk Bond Investor piece says the bonds traded above par in June, then slid into the low 90s over the summer as five-year CDS nearly doubled to about 850 basis points.7 Janus Henderson's John Lloyd says the yield to maturity on CoreWeave's 9.75% bonds due 2031 is now 15.97%, a more than 70% jump since April issuance.8

Lloyd calls the bond selloff "a little bit of overreacting" and says he sees no huge fundamental change that would warrant it, other than higher financing costs.910 Marc Rubinstein is more critical, arguing CoreWeave functions as a conduit to capital markets whose cost of capital is rising, and calling it in effect a non-bank financial.1112

Luke Kawa noted record five-day net purchases of CoreWeave by Robinhood retail investors after the drop.13
