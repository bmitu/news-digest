September 2026 US jobs report (29,000 jobs added)

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 17:18 UTC
9 sources · 5 independent origins: Bureau of Labor Statistics, CNBC, Employ America, New Deal democrat, Wolf Street

US nonfarm payrolls rose by just 29,000 in September, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on Friday, and the unemployment rate rose to 4.2%.12 Economists surveyed by Dow Jones had expected 84,000 jobs and a 4.1% unemployment rate, while the Guardian put expectations at just under 70,000. inference34

Private employers added 46,000 jobs and governments shed 17,000, according to Wolf Richter.56 Healthcare added 17,000 jobs, the largest gain, while information lost 10,000 and financial activities lost 7,000.789 July and August were revised down, with July now showing a loss of 10,000 jobs and August a gain of 133,000, which CNBC says is 60,000 fewer jobs in total than previously reported.101112

Average hourly earnings rose 0.1% in the month and 3% over 12 months, which CNBC says is the lowest since May 2021 and below the 0.3% and 3.1% Wall Street expected.1314 The household survey was stronger than the payroll survey: CNBC reports household employment up 406,000 and the labor force up 485,000.1516

Bradley Saunders of Capital Economics called the figure "not disastrous", and George Brown of Schroders said a single soft report is unlikely to signal a lasting collapse.1718

Markets and analysts read the report as reducing the odds of a Fed rate hike in October: Thomas Simons of Jefferies said it should be "the nail in the coffin for an October hike", and CME FedWatch odds of a hold rose to 82.8%.192021 Heather Long of Navy Federal Credit Union said she doesn't think the Fed will be dissuaded from hiking in December, and the Guardian says another hike is now more likely then. inference2223

The BBC and the Guardian describe the report as the last before the midterm elections, and the BBC says it contrasts with President Donald Trump's claim that the economy is booming.2425

Why did the unemployment rate rise even though the household survey showed strong employment and labor force gains?

The household survey showed the labor force growing by 485,000, which was more than the 406,000 rise in household employment. inference12 Because the labor force grew faster than employment, the number of unemployed people rose slightly. The unemployment rate is the unemployed divided by the labor force. inference13 Employ America says the slight rise came from new entrants and re-entrants to the labor force, not from layoffs: unemployed job losers fell by 45,000.45 Participation rose 0.2 points to 61.8%, its highest since May, which fits with more people entering the job market. inference6

Which groups were hit hardest in this report, such as Black Americans or men compared with women?

Black Americans were hit hard in September: their unemployment rate rose a full percentage point to 7%, which is double the rate for white Americans, according to The Guardian.1 Men have lost ground to women over the past year. The seasonally adjusted number of employed men was about 1.2 million lower in September than a year earlier, while women's employment rose by more than 650,000.23 Women outnumbered men in the workforce for eight straight months as of September, the longest stretch since 2010, according to Indeed. Indeed economist Cory Stahle says that in 2010 men were losing jobs, while this time women are gaining them.456 Stahle attributes the gender gap partly to which industries are growing. Healthcare accounted for more than half of September's payroll expansion, and women make up nearly four out of every five workers in the sector. Payroll contraction in industries with a higher share of women workers, like government, also diluted overall gains for women that month, Stahle said.78
