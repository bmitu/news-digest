September 2026 US jobs report

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 15:38 UTC
8 sources · tracked since Oct 4 · 2 new articles today · 3 independent origins: Bureau of Labor Statistics, CNBC, New Deal democrat

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on Friday that US nonfarm payrolls rose by just 29,000 in September, well below the 84,000 that economists surveyed by Dow Jones had expected, while the unemployment rate rose to 4.2%.12 Revisions cut July and August by a combined 60,000 jobs: August is now a gain of 133,000 and July a loss of 10,000.345 Sources differ on the size of the miss and on the August figure: The Guardian says expectations were just under 70,000, and it cites an earlier August figure of 162,000. inference67

Private employers added 46,000 jobs while governments shed 17,000, according to Wolf Richter.8 CNBC reported that healthcare added 17,000 jobs, construction 11,000 and manufacturing 9,000, while temporary help fell 11,000, information 10,000 and financial activities 7,000.9101112 Sources differ on healthcare's contribution: Wolf Richter lists healthcare at +23,000, while CNBC reports 17,000, and The Guardian also reports 17,000. inference131415

The household survey was stronger: CNBC reported household employment rose by 406,000, the labor force grew by 485,000 and the participation rate rose to 61.8%.161718 Average hourly earnings rose only 0.1% in September, for a 3% annual gain that CNBC described as the lowest since May 2021.19

Markets treated the weak data as good news, as CNBC reported traders saw it cementing a Fed hold at its October meeting; CME FedWatch odds of a hold rose to 82.8%.2021 Thomas Simons of Jefferies said the number "should be the nail in the coffin for an October hike," while Heather Long of Navy Federal Credit Union called the labor market "stable" and doesn't think the Fed will be dissuaded from hiking in December.2223

Employ America called the report "soft, but not dire" and Capital Economics' Bradley Saunders called it "not disastrous," while Schroders' George Brown said a single soft report is unlikely to point to a lasting collapse.242526 Reuters noted the report was the last before the November 3 midterms, and that polls give Trump and Republicans poor marks on the economy despite the 4.2% unemployment rate.2728
