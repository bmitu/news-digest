10-year Treasury yield at 5.28%

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 17:18 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Bespoke Investment Group, Wolf Street

The 10-year Treasury yield closed the week at 5.28%, after a volatile stretch in which, according to Wolf Richter of Wolf Street, it hit 5.36% intraday on Thursday and fell to 5.15% on Friday morning before climbing back.12

Richter says the yield rose 11 basis points over the week and spanned a 21-basis-point trading range on Thursday and Friday.34 Bespoke Investment Group reports the yield ended September at its highest level since June 12, 2007, up 53.5 basis points over the month.56 Bespoke says that monthly gain ranks in the 95th percentile of all months since 1962 and was the largest monthly increase since September 2023, though far from a record.78

Bespoke highlights that yields rose on 16 of September's 21 trading days, the highest share of up-days in any month since April 1981.910

He argues that the deficit, heavy bond supply, inflation and a hot economy still weigh on the market.1112

Richter notes the 30-year yield closed at 5.63% after briefly reaching 5.69% on Thursday, the highest since 2002.1314 Richter does not consider current yields to be at "blood in the streets" levels, but he expects yields to rise further and says he will likely nibble at the coming 10-year auction, which he expects will be too early.151617
