Procter & Gamble fiscal Q1 earnings

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: CNBC, Procter & Gamble

Procter & Gamble reported fiscal first-quarter results that beat Wall Street estimates, with core EPS of $2.00 against a $1.89 consensus and revenue of $23.3 billion against $22.82 billion expected.123 Shares rose about 6% on the day, which Yahoo Finance reported was to $166.19 in morning trading, while the SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) was down 1%.45

Growth was driven mostly by volume: TIKR reports organic volume up 2.4% with price adding just 0.4%, and TIKR says Fabric & Home Care volume rose 4.3%. CNBC also reports that fabric and home care volume rose 4.3%.678 However, not every division grew: CNBC reports grooming volume fell 0.4% and healthcare volume fell 2.9%.910

P&G kept its fiscal 2027 core EPS guidance of $6.89 to $7.11, and CNBC reports it raised its all-in sales growth outlook to 2% to 4% from 1% to 3%, a change it attributes to the Thorne acquisition.111213 CEO Shailesh Jejurikar said the "strong start keeps us on track to deliver within our guidance ranges."1415

Costs are the main concern: core gross margin fell 150 basis points, and P&G now expects about $1.4 billion in after-tax commodity costs this fiscal year, up from about $1 billion.161718 CFO Andre Schulten described a split consumer, saying on CNBC that high-income consumers trade up while low-income consumers stretch brands like Tide and Pampers to fit tight budgets.19 TIKR's author says volume-led growth is what one wants from a defensive stock but wants to see margins steady before expecting more upside, while 24/7 Wall St. on Yahoo Finance sees pricing power facing a test with lower-income shoppers. inference2021
