Barclays to cut about 5,000 jobs

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
4 sources · 3 independent origins: BBC, Barclays, The Guardian

Barclays is cutting about 5,000 jobs, roughly 5% of its global workforce, as part of cost-saving plans that its bosses link to advances in AI.12 Barclays says 1,000 posts have already been closed and it expects a further 4,000 reductions to be complete by the end of this year.34 Venkatakrishnan also said AI "may have a more important role to play in the future".56

Chief financial officer Anna Cross said about 60% of the group actions relate to support functions, including fewer developers, and the Guardian reports the cuts are part of plans to save more than £1.1bn in 2027.789 The BBC reports that the cost reductions are focused on the UK retail bank, but that a significant proportion of the cuts are expected to fall in India, where some UK back office functions are done.10

Barclays reported £2.3bn in pre-tax profit for the July to September quarter, which the Guardian says is up 12% from £2.1bn a year earlier, while the BBC says it was slightly less than forecast.1112

Unite general secretary Sharon Graham said the company is using AI as an excuse to discard its workforce and called its approach short-term and kneejerk.1314 Analyst Russ Mould of AJ Bell said Barclays was "reaching for the AI lever" but had failed to match expectations with its financial results, while Will Howlett of Quilter Cheviot said the cuts show how AI and automation could reshape banking.1516
