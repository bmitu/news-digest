ETF fees barely falling

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 2 independent origins: The Wall Street Journal, etf.com

A Wall Street Journal piece, as headlined in Barry Ritholtz's morning reads, argues that "cheap" ETFs cost more than investors think, saying the costs of many popular funds have barely budged even though assets have soared.1 Tadas Viskanta's Abnormal Returns, meanwhile, links to an etf.com item stating that ETF industry average fees stopped falling in 2023.2 The two sources point the same way, with fee declines stalling or barely moving, though the passages give no figures on fee levels or the size of any change, and they differ in framing: one says fees stopped falling in 2023, the other that costs have barely budged. inference34
