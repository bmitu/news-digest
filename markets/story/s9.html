Q2 GDP final revision

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 03:32 UTC
3 sources · single origin, unclear which

The Commerce Department reported on Wednesday that second-quarter US GDP grew at a 2.2% annualized rate in its third and final estimate, up sharply from the prior estimate of 1.5%.12 CNBC says the upgrade reflected greater contributions from consumer spending, government spending and investment, while the Guardian, citing Reuters, attributes the growth to robust consumer spending and AI-infrastructure-related business investment. inference34 Real final sales to private domestic purchasers, a measure Fed officials watch closely for underlying demand, rose 4.6%, a 0.4 percentage point upward revision.5 Commenters under Wolf Richter's article cite differing figures: one says GDP was revised to 8.5%, while another cites 8.4% nominal GDP growth in Q2, and the passages do not explain the difference. inference67

Second-quarter inflation measures were revised down, with headline PCE prices rising 5% and core 3.3%, each 0.3 percentage point below the prior estimate.8 Sonu Varghese of Carson Group says PCE inflation is "still running hot however you cut it", and he describes the economy as running hot with easy policy.910

New York Fed President John Williams said in a speech on Tuesday that after the September action there is "no need for urgency", though he still thinks another hike "may be appropriate late this year".1112

On the day, the S&P 500 ended down 0.25% and the Dow fell 0.86%, while the Nasdaq ended up 0.24%, after stocks had risen in early trading.13

How did the August PCE inflation readings compare with what economists expected?

The annual headline PCE inflation rate for August came in at 3.4%, below the 3.7% economists had forecast.12 Core PCE, which excludes food and energy, rose 3.0% from a year earlier, against a forecast of 3.3%.3 On a monthly basis, headline PCE rose 0.3%, matching expectations, while core rose 0.2%, less than the 0.3% expected. inference456 The softer annual readings came as the Bureau of Economic Analysis changed how it computes several components of the index, adjusting its methodology for how it measures prices for legal services, software and computer accessories and portfolio management; the revisions lowered the core July PCE level by 0.36 percentage point.789 Bespoke described the PCE report as in line with forecasts, but said July was revised lower.10
