ADP September private payrolls 90,000

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 19:31 UTC
2 sources · single origin (ADP)

ADP reported on Wednesday that private-sector employers added 90,000 jobs in September, up from 36,000 in August.12 The figure beat the Dow Jones consensus estimate of 68,000, and CNBC says August was revised down.3

Service providers added 59,000 positions and goods producers added 31,000.4 Education and health services contributed the most, with 55,000 new hires, while financial activities (-16,000) and professional and business services (-11,000) lost jobs.56

Base pay rose 3.2% from a year ago, and gross pay rose 4.7%.7 ADP chief economist Nela Richardson called it "a strong report," saying job creation rebounded after a three-month slowdown and pay growth remained solid.8

The Bureau of Labor Statistics is due to release nonfarm payrolls on Friday, with consensus at a gain of 84,000 jobs, down from 162,000 the prior month, and unemployment expected to hold at 4.1%.910
