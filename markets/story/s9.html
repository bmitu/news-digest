ADP September private payrolls 90,000

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 17:18 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: ADP, CNBC

ADP reported on Wednesday, September 30, that private-sector employers added 90,000 jobs in September.1 The figure was up from a downwardly revised 36,000 in August and above the Dow Jones consensus estimate of 68,000, as CNBC reports.2 Sources cite different forecasts, however: Bespoke says the consensus was 74,000, while CNBC cites 68,000. inference34 ADP chief economist Nela Richardson called it "a strong report," saying job creation rebounded after a three-month slowdown and pay growth remained solid.5

Service providers added 59,000 positions and goods producers 31,000, while base pay rose 3.2% from a year earlier and gross pay 4.7%.6 Education and health services contributed the most with 55,000 hires, while financial activities (-16,000), professional and business services (-11,000), and natural resources and mining (-1,000) lost jobs.78 The Northeast added 56,000 jobs, and companies with 50 to 499 workers gained 54,000.9

CNBC says the report helped confirm sentiment expressed by multiple Federal Reserve officials that the labor market is mostly sound following a growth scare in 2025, with policymakers now seeing persistent inflation as the greater policy risk, after central bankers raised benchmark rates by a quarter percentage point earlier in September.1011 The Bureau of Labor Statistics is due to release nonfarm payrolls on Friday, with consensus at a gain of 84,000, down from 162,000 the prior month, and the unemployment rate expected to hold at 4.1%.1213
