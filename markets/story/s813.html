SpaceX quarterly results

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
6 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 3 independent origins: CNBC, Investing.com, SpaceX

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.6 billion, up 161% from a year earlier and above the LSEG consensus estimate of $12.72 billion, and Elon Musk said on the earnings call that he is considering merging SpaceX with Tesla. inference123 Musk said special committees at both companies have been formed to examine the idea, but that they have not agreed terms and there is no guarantee they will.456 Musk said he is a "conflicted party" and so is not part of the committees, and that at least 50% of Tesla's shareholders would need to approve a deal, which he called "quite a high hurdle to achieve".78 Musk cautioned that assuming a merger is certain would be overstating it, while saying he thinks there is "a lot of merit to combining the companies".91011

Net income was $4.4 billion and operating income $2.8 billion, compared with a net loss of $1.05 billion a year earlier, according to CNBC.1213 The AI business, which SpaceX calls Super Intelligence, was the largest unit, with revenue of $7.56 billion versus $813 million a year earlier, while Starlink revenue rose to $5.2 billion from $3 billion. inference141516 BBC reported that over the first nine months SpaceX as a whole was not profitable, losing $1.1 billion, and Investing.com said capital expenditures climbed to $19.6 billion.1718 SpaceX forecast first-quarter 2027 revenue of $33 billion to $37 billion, against an average analyst estimate of $22.2 billion, according to LSEG.19

Sources differ on the after-hours reaction: the Guardian said SpaceX shares rose 2%, while the BBC said 8%. SpaceX stock settled 7.8% higher on Wednesday according to Investing.com, and CNBC reported a 10% jump. inference2021222324 Marc Dizard of Huntington Bank said SpaceX had "crushed" expectations and that the market reaction reaffirmed that the AI trade is still intact.2526 Truist Securities analysts said in a Tuesday note that commentary on computing during the earnings call supported "continued pricing power and shorter payback periods" for AI cloud providers, and that SpaceX's quarter was the first data point of earnings season to support the bull case for AI cloud providers, explaining that demand appears "insatiable".2728

Questions about Starshield and Ukraine overshadowed the results: the Wall Street Journal reported that Musk personally ordered the throttling, while Musk denied responsibility and said the US government controlled Starshield. inference293031
