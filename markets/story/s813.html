SpaceX quarterly results

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 19:55 UTC
4 sources · 2 independent origins: Investing.com, SpaceX

Elon Musk said on SpaceX's earnings call on Tuesday that he is backing a possible merger of SpaceX and Tesla, and that special committees have been formed at both companies to examine it.12 Musk cautioned that the committees have not agreed terms and that a deal is not certain, and he said it would need at least 50% of Tesla's shareholders to approve.34 Musk said he is a "conflicted party" and is not part of the committees' discussions.56

The Guardian reported SpaceX posted revenue of $14.58bn, against analysts' projected $12.72bn, up 161% year over year, driven by its Starlink and AI segments.78 The Guardian reported net income of $4.4bn and earnings per share of 35 cents, and that the AI segment brought in $7.6bn, now outperforming Connectivity.910 The BBC said SpaceX as a whole is not profitable over nine months, with a $1.1bn loss on $27bn revenue, despite the $4.4bn profit in the latest quarter.1112

Sources differ on the after-hours move: the Guardian said SpaceX shares rose 2%, while the BBC said they rose 8%. inference1314 The sources also give slightly different figures for the AI/compute business: $7.6bn (Guardian) versus $7.5bn (BBC). inference1516

Marc Dizard of Huntington Bank said SpaceX had "crushed" expectations and that the reaction reaffirmed the AI trade, though he warned sentiment can shift quickly.1718 Bespoke said Musk noted compute demand remains extremely strong, which set off buying in AI infrastructure stocks.19
