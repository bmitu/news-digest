L'Oreal Q3 sales

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
2 sources · single origin (L'Oréal)

Chief executive Nicolas Hieronimus said, "We delivered an exceptional third quarter," and that L'Oréal significantly outperformed a beauty market estimated to have grown about 5 percent.12 For the first nine months, sales were 35.34 billion euros, up 7.7 percent reported and 6.9 percent like-for-like.34 Vara Consensus expected growth of 5.4 percent and Barclays forecast 5.7 percent.5 The BBC cites 7.7% growth for the quarter while WWD gives 7.2% like-for-like (7.7% adjusted) and 11.9% reported, so the sources use different measures. inference67

WWD says growth was spurred by the Professional Products and Dermatological Beauty divisions and a strong recovery in Luxe, with Professional Products reported sales up 19.1 percent.89

Hieronimus said China's market slowed down a tiny bit in Q3 but L'Oréal maintained its growth rate. WWD reports that travel retail returned to positive growth in the third quarter. Hieronimus said the Chinese airport stock issues were probably something that wouldn't be seen in Q4.101112 For the rest of the year, Hieronimus said he expects the beauty market to grow around 5 percent and L'Oréal to keep outperforming at a slightly more moderate rate.13

The BBC reports Hieronimus told investors that GLP-1 weight loss drug use is "having an impact on beauty consumption and skin care consumption in particular".14 Hieronimus said SkinCeuticals and Kiehl's have products designed to complement GLP-1 and its skin-sagging side-effects, and L'Oréal raised its stake in Galderma to 20% last December.1516
