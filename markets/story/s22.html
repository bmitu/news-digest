August JOLTS job openings

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 16:33 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · single origin (Bureau of Labor Statistics)

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that US job openings in August edged lower by 256,000 to 7.08 million, according to CNBC's account of the report, and Wolf Richter's article says the decline was from the upwardly revised July figure.12 CNBC reported the drop came amid sharp declines in professional and business services and healthcare-related jobs, and that the Wall Street consensus had been for 7.2 million openings.34 Wolf Richter noted the three-month average of openings declined to 7.20 million, while openings were up 160,000 from a year earlier.56

Hires rose by 46,000 to 5.19 million, voluntary quits declined by 23,000 to 3.07 million, and layoffs and discharges fell to 1.64 million, the lowest since March 2025, according to Richter.789 Richter argues that the low turnover means the labor market has normalized after the churn of 2021 and 2022, and says it is not a sign of a weak labor market but of a settled labor force.10

Bespoke Investment Group said job openings continue to improve despite dour consumer sentiment toward employment, and that the latest Conference Board Consumer Confidence data showed a stark disconnect between hard employment data and consumer evaluations of the labor market.1112

What definition does the JOLTS survey use for an open job, and does it count online job postings?

According to Wolf Richter of Wolf Street, the JOLTS job openings data is based on a survey of HR departments at 21,000 business locations, not online job postings.1 The JOLTS survey counts a job as open only if a specific position exists and there is work available for it, the job could start within 30 days, and the employer is actively recruiting from outside the establishment.23 Positions open only to internal transfers, promotions, demotions or recall from layoffs are excluded, as are positions where employees have been hired but have not yet started, and positions to be filled by temporary agency staff, contractors or consultants.45
