Tesla Q3 earnings

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 4 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: Tesla, Yahoo

Tesla beat Wall Street's third-quarter revenue estimate, reporting $28.05 billion for the quarter ended September 30, according to Reuters, though heavy spending on AI and robotaxis weighed on profit.12 Sources differ on the revenue comparison: Reuters, using LSEG data, reports a beat, while Yahoo Finance cites a Bloomberg consensus of $28.13 billion and Investor's Business Daily says revenue fell just short. inference345 Yahoo Finance reports adjusted EPS of $0.48 versus a $0.44 estimate, while TradingView cites 45 cents expected and says reported net income fell 43% to $787 million. inference678

Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles, down about 2% from a year earlier but above expectations, and energy storage deployments reached 13.7 GWh.91011 Tesla cut its full-year capex guidance to $20 billion from $25 billion on the earnings call, and CFO Vaibhav Taneja said the savings would come from managing capital deployment efficiently and funding capex mainly from operating cash flow.121314

On the call, Musk said he expects it to take at least a year, perhaps longer, to reach Optimus production of 20,000 units a week, and Tesla said Optimus production has started at its Fremont factory.151617 Musk said Tesla and SpaceX have formed special committees of independent directors to discuss a possible merger, but said it would be overstating things to assume a merger is certain.181920

Shares rose 3.4% to $386.50 in regular trading but fell about 3% after hours, according to TradingView, whereas Reuters reported a rise of more than 1% in extended trading. inference2122 Morningstar analyst Seth Goldstein claimed the market was reacting to capacity constraints in auto and battery growth, while Roundhill CEO Dave Mazza posits the capex cut means Tesla isn't shifting fast enough to an AI-focused company.2324
