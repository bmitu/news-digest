What risks do the Federal Reserve and Governor Waller see in AI agents that make purchases and payments for consumers?

Governor Waller says the main risk in the agent-delegated model, where a buyer gives an AI agent authority to shop and pay autonomously, is a higher chance of unintended purchases.1 He says that model therefore needs a more extensive buildout of trust mechanisms and guardrails, even though buyers can set constraints, because the agent still operates autonomously.23 Waller also warns that in business-to-business purchases, higher transaction values amplify the financial exposure from agent errors or unauthorized actions, so robust controls and monitoring will be needed.4
