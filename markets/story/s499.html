Euro falls to multi-month low

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 17:01 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 5 · 2 new articles today · 2 independent origins: Investing.com, The Guardian

The Guardian reports the euro fell as much as 0.8% to below $1.12, its lowest since May 2025, and is down about 1.2% this month after a peak of $1.20 in January; Reuters says it hit as low as $1.1161 and was last down 0.47%.123 Reuters reported that the euro posted its fourth straight weekly fall against the dollar on Friday, and that the dollar index rose to 102.23 after reaching 102.53, its highest since April 10, 2025.45

Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez's call for a snap election on Monday added to eurozone uncertainty, according to The Guardian and Reuters.67 French Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu's minority government announced a €54bn savings plan, and he said it would limit the deficit from 5.5% of GDP this year to 5% next year, warning it could reach 6.5% without action.89

The Reuters report says the gap between French and German bond yields widened to about 150 basis points on Friday, the highest since the euro zone's sovereign debt crisis in 2011, before pulling back to 140 bps, while The Guardian says French 10-year yields hit their highest since 2002 last week.1011 The sources differ on the spread's reference point: The Guardian says the gap was the widest since 2012, Reuters (Rebaudo) says the highest since 2011, and another Reuters piece says the highest since the 2010-2012 crisis. inference121314

Hauke Siemssen of Commerzbank said bond dynamics are "increasingly concerning and somewhat reminiscent of a sovereign debt crisis", and that the French spread selloff "seems to increasingly feed on itself".1516 BofA strategists estimate that each further 10 bps widening in the French-German spread is associated with a 0.4% fall in euro/dollar, while Goldman Sachs analysts said the typical response is closer to zero most of the time but can rise significantly in acute stress.1718

Roberto Mialich of UniCredit said a retest of $1.10 is possible in the near term, and Societe Generale's Kit Juckes said of the factors that held euro/dollar above key levels through the summer, "I think that's gone".1920 Stephen Jen of Eurizon SLJ Asset Management said euro/dollar could trade lower if fiscal contagion risk is not contained, even though the euro was already slightly undervalued.21
