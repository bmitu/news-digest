September US housing starts decline

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 02:15 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · single origin (CNBC) · Official data: 10-year Treasury yield

Housing Starts for September came in at 1.221 million annualized, below forecasts of 1.305 million, according to Bespoke Investment Group's Morning Lineup of October 20, 2026.1 Building Permits, reported at the same time, were in line with expectations.2 Bespoke says expectations were low after a weaker-than-expected homebuilder sentiment report the day before, but that they "weren't low enough."3

Bespoke also says the housing industry continues to struggle under high interest rates, citing Housing Starts and Pending Home Sales data from the week.4 Bespoke notes that the S&P Homebuilders ETF (XHB) recently hit new 52-week lows.5

The housing data landed in a market that Bespoke describes as already under pressure from AI investment concerns, rising interest rates and inflation, with oil surging more than 5% to $109 per barrel and the 10-year yield at 5.11%.678 Bespoke says oil prices were likely to be the market's primary focus that morning.9
