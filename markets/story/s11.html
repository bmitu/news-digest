September FOMC 25bp rate hike

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 16:14 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · single origin (Federal Reserve)

The FOMC voted in September to raise the target range for the federal funds rate by 1/4 percentage point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent, according to Governor Philip Jefferson.12 Governor Michael Barr said the committee agreed unanimously, and Lisa Cook said she voted with the rest of the FOMC for the increase. inference32

Employ America says the hike was not a surprise to most Fed watchers, since markets were pricing it in with near-certainty, though most had expected a hold as late as Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech.45 Employ America claims it was among the few to catch the Fed's pivot early, and says the upside it anticipated in airfares, food services, communication services and tech equipment inflation ahead of the August data materialized, all but locking in a September hike.67

Officials cite inflation well above target: Cook said total inflation rose an estimated 3.8 percent in the 12 months to August and core 3.4 percent, while Jefferson put 12-month PCE inflation at 3.4 percent in August. inference89 Jefferson said energy prices are the predominant factor behind the recent pickup in headline inflation, and Barr pointed to the Middle East conflict and the AI buildout as pushing prices up. inference1011

Officials describe a solid labor market: Barr cited a 4.1 percent unemployment rate and job creation averaging around 80,000 a month this year, and Cook said it appears well positioned to handle higher rates. inference1213

Fedspeak after the decision was hawkish, according to Employ America's Preston Mui, and Barr said further policy adjustments are likely to be needed in his base case. inference1415 Jefferson was more cautious on the path, saying future adjustments should depend on data and the balance of risks, and that his colleagues will need to reach their own judgment, which may take more time.1617
