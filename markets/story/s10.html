ADP September employment report

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 16:14 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · 2 independent origins: ADP, CNBC

ADP reported on Wednesday, September 30, that private employers added 90,000 jobs in September, up from a downwardly revised 36,000 in August and above the consensus estimate of 68,000.123 Sources differ on the forecast: CNBC cites a Dow Jones consensus of 68,000, while Bespoke says the 90,000 print compared with forecasts of 74,000. inference45 ADP chief economist Nela Richardson called it "a strong report," saying job creation rebounded after a three-month slowdown and pay growth remained solid.6

Service providers added 59,000 positions and goods producers 31,000, while base pay rose 3.2% from a year earlier and gross pay 4.7%.78 Education and health services contributed the most with 55,000 hires, while financial activities (-16,000), professional and business services (-11,000), and natural resources and mining (-1,000) lost jobs.910 The Northeast added 56,000 jobs, and companies with 50 to 499 workers gained 54,000.11

The Bureau of Labor Statistics will release nonfarm payrolls on Friday, with the Wall Street consensus at a gain of 84,000 jobs, down from a 162,000 increase the prior month, and the unemployment rate expected to hold steady at 4.1%.1213 The Guardian reported that traders see a roughly 35% chance of an October rate hike, down from about 45%, according to LSEG data, after a softer-than-anticipated inflation reading.14
