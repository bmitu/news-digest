Oura Health IPO postponed

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 4 · 1 new article today · 2 independent origins: Axios, CNBC

Biometric ring maker Oura postponed its initial public offering on Tuesday, citing "uncertainty" in market conditions while also claiming there was "strong demand" for its stock.1 A newsletter roundup also noted that Oura Health had pulled its IPO, citing Axios.23

Renaissance Capital data show four companies in different sectors, each aiming to raise at least $50 million, announced postponements or withdrawals in the past week, making seven in the third quarter, up from four in the second quarter and three in the first.45 Before Oura, Holtec Nuclear withdrew its IPO last Friday, Amaero postponed last Wednesday, and Bamboo Insurance postponed on Sept. 22.6

Despite the delays, about $146.9 billion has been raised in 110 IPOs this year, excluding SPACs, which is down 30% from this time last year according to Renaissance Capital data.78 Analysts attribute the weaker market largely to surging bond yields, and Renaissance Capital analysts wrote that concerns about AI spending, a 19-year high in bond yields and resumed rate hikes weighed on the fall pickup.910
