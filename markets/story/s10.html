August JOLTS job openings

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 17:18 UTC
3 sources · single origin (Bureau of Labor Statistics)

US job openings fell by 256,000 in August to 7.08 million, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics.123 CNBC reports the Wall Street consensus had been for 7.2 million openings, and that the decline came amid sharp drops in professional and business services and in healthcare-related jobs.45 Wolf Richter notes that the three-month average of openings declined to 7.20 million, while openings were up 160,000 from a year earlier.6

Hires rose by 46,000 to 5.19 million, and quits fell by 23,000 to 3.07 million, according to Richter.78 Layoffs and discharges declined to 1.64 million, which Richter says is the lowest since March 2025.9 Richter argues that labor market turnover has normalized after the chaotic churn of 2021 and 2022, which he says was driven by a massive wave of quits.10

Separately, the Conference Board's Consumer Confidence Index fell 6.7 points to 81.9 in September, well below the Dow Jones consensus forecast of 89, and the differential between those saying jobs are plentiful and those saying they are hard to get fell 2.5 percentage points to 1.7%.1112
