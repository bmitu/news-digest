Oura Health IPO postponed

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 17:18 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Axios, CNBC

Biometric ring maker Oura postponed its initial public offering on Tuesday, citing "uncertainty" in market conditions while also claiming there was "strong demand" for stock, which raised eyebrows on Wall Street.1 A newsletter roundup from Tadas Viskanta also linked to an Axios report that Oura Health had pulled its IPO.2

According to Renaissance Capital data, four companies in different sectors, each aiming to raise at least $50 million, announced postponements or pulled IPOs in the past week, making seven in the third quarter, up from four in the second quarter and three in the first.34 The other recent cases were Holtec Nuclear, which withdrew its IPO last Friday, Amaero, which postponed last Wednesday, and Bamboo Insurance, which postponed on Sept. 22.5

Renaissance Capital analysts wrote that third-quarter 2026 IPO activity came in below expectations as more concerns about AI spending, a 19-year high in bond yields and resumed rate hikes weighed on the fall pickup.6 Renaissance Capital's Matthew Kennedy says a string of postponements tells you something about the market, and that you can't point to all four of them and say it's company-specific issues.7

Jay Ritter of the University of Florida says he has some sympathy for market conditions as a rationale.8 Ian Schuman of Latham & Watkins says the depth of private capital and alternatives is enormous now, and that you don't necessarily need to tap the public markets if you're not getting the value you want.9
