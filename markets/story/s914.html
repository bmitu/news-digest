Netflix announces layoffs

Last scan Thu Oct 22, 01:18 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: Netflix, Variety

Netflix said it was laying off about 6% of its employees, roughly 1,000 workers, and that Chief Content Officer Bela Bajaria was departing that role.1 The Hollywood Reporter describes it as Netflix's largest layoff in years, with film head Dan Lin also among the high-profile casualties.23 Former Disney executive Peter Rice has been tapped as the new chief content officer, though CNBC says it is not yet clear how he will address viewership concerns.4

Netflix said its fourth-quarter results will be affected by the head count reductions, with a negative impact on operating margin of about 120 basis points.5 The Hollywood Reporter says the associated costs will be recognized in the current fourth quarter.6

The cuts came after co-CEO Ted Sarandos said at the Bloomberg Screentime summit on Sept. 30 that "overall, we’re not growing as fast as I want us to."7 In its third-quarter report, Netflix posted earnings of 87 cents per share against 82 cents estimated, and revenue of $12.87 billion in line with estimates, according to CNBC.89 Netflix lowered its 2026 advertising revenue forecast to around $2.7 billion from roughly $3 billion, which it attributed to a slower-than-expected rollout of some ad product capabilities.1011

Shares fell about 6% in extended trading, and The Hollywood Reporter reports the stock closed Tuesday at $68.69 and hit a new 52-week low after hours.1213
