US eases tariffs on Canadian goods

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 03:40 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: BBC, White House

The BBC says the US imposed a 50% levy on $20bn of Canadian goods after trade talks collapsed in August, and Canada responded with countermeasures.12 The BBC reports that the changes start on 30 October, and that the White House will also introduce tariffs on other items such as carpets and "waste products".34 The White House says the action reflects Trump's "continued efforts to defend American workers and ensure fair trade" and offsets Canada's "discriminatory measures".56

Collins announced that the exemptions cover nine agricultural products, including blueberries, potatoes, apples and seaweed, while the White House also named processing equipment such as plywood sheets, packing boxes, paper products and certain machinery.78 Collins said she had repeatedly asked senior administration officials for relief and will keep seeking additional relief and a broader trade agreement with Canada.910

Canada's internal trade minister Dominic LeBlanc said Canada is assessing the steps and "stands ready to work constructively and in good faith", while Prime Minister Mark Carney said Canada could sign a deal immediately if the US were ready but is willing to wait months or longer.1112
