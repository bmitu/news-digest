Musk weighs Tesla-SpaceX merger

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 19:55 UTC
2 sources · single origin (SpaceX)

The Guardian quotes Elon Musk saying that assuming a merger is a certainty would be overstating it, though he sees "a lot of merit" in combining the companies.12 Musk cautioned that a deal is not certain, saying the committees have not agreed terms and that approval from 50% of Tesla shareholders would be a high hurdle.345 Musk said he is not in the committee discussions because he is a "conflicted party", and that the committees consist of independent directors.67

By contrast, analyst David Nicholson of The Futurum Group says the merger is "100%" likely and "just a question of when".8 Dan Sheehan of Telos Wealth Advisors expects shareholders to back the deal, but says Tesla holders would likely have a "smaller slice of a much larger and more complex business".910

The Guardian reports SpaceX posted revenue of $14.58bn against analysts' projected $12.72bn, with net income of $4.4bn.111213 Reports differ on the share reaction: the BBC says SpaceX shares rose 8% in after-hours trading, while the Guardian says its stock rose 2% after hours. inference1415
