French bond spreads widen

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 5 · 3 new articles today · single origin (Investing.com)

The premium investors demand to hold French 10-year bonds over German bunds widened to about 150 basis points on Friday, which Reuters describes as the highest since the euro zone's 2011 sovereign debt crisis, before easing back.1 The euro fell to a 17-month low of about $1.116 against the dollar on Monday as concerns about France's ability to rein in its budget deficit and last week's sharp bond market selloff stirred fears of a return of sovereign debt crisis dynamics in the euro zone.23 Paris shares fell about 1.1% to six-month lows on Monday.4

The French government is trying to enact an unpopular 2027 budget to cut its deficit and contain record-high debt in a deeply divided parliament, with the presidential election approaching next year.56 Saxo strategist Neil Wilson notes that French government plans to reduce the budget deficit still face parliamentary scrutiny and could ultimately be watered down.7

Reuters reports that the French/German yield gap posted its biggest weekly jump in decades last week, and the Italian-German gap hit almost 130 basis points, its biggest weekly rise since the COVID-19 crisis.8910 BofA FX strategists estimate that each further 10 basis point widening in the French spread would be associated with a 0.4% fall in euro/dollar.11 Goldman Sachs analysts say the typical currency response is closer to zero most of the time but can rise significantly in acute stress.1213

Commerzbank strategist Hauke Siemssen calls the bond dynamics increasingly concerning and somewhat reminiscent of a sovereign debt crisis, saying the French spread selloff seems increasingly to feed on itself.1415 Societe Generale's Kit Juckes says the bond selloff has produced an outbreak of euro selling that has gathered momentum, and analysts said the euro could test $1.10.161718 Reuters notes that further euro weakness could leave the European Central Bank caught between fighting inflation and calming bond markets, and that its Transmission Protection Instrument allows unlimited purchases of bonds from a country facing an "unwarranted, disorderly" tightening of financing conditions.192021
