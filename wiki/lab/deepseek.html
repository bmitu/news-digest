Overview

Wikipedia describes DeepSeek as a Chinese artificial intelligence company that develops open weights large language models.1 Wikipedia says it is based in Hangzhou and is owned and funded by High-Flyer, a Chinese hedge fund.1 News reports cover its model and tool releases, its fundraising, a reported US ban and China export filings.23456

Models and tools

The launch of DeepSeek-V2.5 was reported on September 5, 2024.7 DeepSeek-R1-Lite-Preview was reported as live on November 20, 2024.8 DeepSeek-V3 was first reported on December 26, 2024.9

DeepSeek-R1-0528 carried a stated release date of May 28, 2025.10 DeepSeek-V3.1 was reported on August 21, 2025, and DeepSeek called it a first step toward the agent era.11 The launch of DeepSeek-V3.2 and DeepSeek-V3.2-Speciale was reported on December 1, 2025.12
