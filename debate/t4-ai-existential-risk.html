The factual questions the sides answer differently, what would settle each one, and the checked facts in the record that point one way or the other. Which way a fact points is our reading. The four questions follow the usual breakdown of the argument (how soon, whether such systems can be controlled, whether one out of control could do great harm, whether people respond in time), as in Joseph Carlsmith, "Is Power-Seeking AI an Existential Risk?" (2022).

1. Will AI match or pass people at most thinking work, AI research included, within a few years?

Most of the risk arguments assume it will. If it comes later, there is more time to learn to control such systems; if AI starts improving AI, events could move faster than people can react.
