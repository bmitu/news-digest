Claude Opus 5.5 launch

From reports by minimaxir.com, Latent Space, Nate B Jones · Oct 25 – Oct 28

Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5.5 on July 24, 2026, the same day OpenAI announced GPT-6 Sol, and Anthropic explicitly stated that Opus 5.5 requires less compute to serve than Opus 5 and that its pricing reflects that. inference1 Blogger minimaxir reports Opus 5.5 is priced at $4 / $20 / $0.20, with a 20% input/output cost cut and a larger cache read cut, for a total cost reduction of 40-60% versus Opus 5.2 According to Nate B Jones's account, Anthropic says Opus 5.5 reduced customers' cost per task by approximately 40% compared with Opus 5, through a combination of pricing and task efficiency.3 In a Latent Space episode, Anthropic also says Opus 5.5 costs less to run than its predecessor, though the exact saving depends on the workload and its use of cached context.4

Minimaxir suggests a less-cynical factor that could explain the price cuts is that LLM infrastructure has improved over the past few months, and notes that open-weight competition matters: Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro has comparable performance to Opus 5.5 (55 vs. 58) at half the input/output price.56

Latent Space describes Opus 5.5 as having lasted a month as the generally accepted #1 LLM in the world, and the launch discussion covers feedback that earlier Opus generations were too verbose.78 On the Latent Space episode, an Anthropic guest says Opus 5.5 is really cost efficient and less verbose, that the vibes are really, really high, and that one of their old bosses messaged that Opus 5.5 saved their butts in an incident.910

The launch also involved API changes: preserving thinking blocks across turns helps protect model reasoning against extraction used for distillation, but a new requirement can break a customer's existing harness.11
