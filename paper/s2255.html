Alphabet Q3 earnings and stock drop

From reports by Latent Space, The Verge · Oct 31 – Nov 4

Alphabet shares have fallen about 14 percent since its earnings report last week, as investors question the costs and returns of the company's AI ambitions, according to The Verge.1

Google Search revenue growth slowed to 12 percent year over year in the third quarter, from roughly 17 percent in the previous quarter.2 Paid click growth slowed from 13 percent to 8 percent, even as search queries grew at their fastest rate in five years.34 The slowdown comes as Google plans to spend about $200 billion this year on infrastructure, largely to support AI.5

Chief Business Officer Philipp Schindler said currency effects were the biggest driver of the slowdown, alongside changes in spending by advertisers in particular industries.6 Schindler also acknowledged that strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization Google sees in AI Mode.7 Google argues that most AI Mode queries are incremental, and Schindler says its ad experiments have shown promising results.89 The Verge notes it remains unclear whether the weaker monetization (AI Mode queries growing strongly but monetizing at a lower rate) reflects a deliberate rollout of ads, as Google says, or a more fundamental problem with advertising in AI search.10

Nate Elliott, an eMarketer principal analyst, calls this the first major disruption to Google's core business in decades and says that if people click through less often, Google must figure out that challenge.1112 Andy Taylor of Tinuiti says its advertisers raised Google paid search spending 14 percent in the third quarter while keeping return targets, though advertisers cannot isolate AI ad performance.1314
