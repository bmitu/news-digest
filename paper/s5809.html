Neon developer tooling and Workspace MCP GA

From reports by Latent Space, techstackups.com · Nov 25 – Nov 26

Google has declared its eight Workspace MCP servers generally available, along with the gwscli command-line tool, in a move techstackups.com dates to 24 November and says came with a new clause in Google's API terms. inference12 According to techstackups.com, tools that are AI agents or driven by them must use these servers to access Workspace services such as Gmail and Drive from 1 February 2027, and violators risk restricted access or account suspension. inference34 The site considers the rule's definitions broad enough to plausibly cover even a personal script feeding email into a language model, and says Google has not directly addressed hobbyists.56

“Create an OAuth client marked as agentic and move to the MCP servers.”

Al Smith, via techstackups.com

Developers initially self-declare agents through an OAuth client checkbox, which the MCP servers have required since 11 November, and older clients not marked before the cut-off must be replaced with new ones to count as agents. inference78 In techstackups.com's assessment, the design also lets Google control what particular agents may do, what data they may retain and whether products built on that data can rival its own.9

As for what agents can do, the public Gmail server can search and read mail, create drafts and apply labels, but techstackups.com notes that it cannot send, reply, forward or revise drafts, functions Google's quota page reserves for partners. inference1011 Although Google has not said who can become a partner, the site argues that large vendors already benefit, citing the email-sending features advertised by Anthropic's Claude connector, while independent developers' agents are limited to drafting. inference121314

On usage limits, the free tier allows 5,000 MCP requests daily for each product within a project, and because some Gmail calls such as thread searches count as 10, a search-only agent would get 500 searches a day shared among all its users. inference1516 Billing for a paid Scaled tier covering Gmail, Calendar and Drive begins on 1 April 2027, with per-request prices still unpublished and a structure that techstackups.com says favors the REST API, which gets large free daily allowances while MCP use is charged from the first request. inference171819

Separately, Latent Space reports that Google released an experimental "anti-slop" skill for frontend design with its Neon model.20
