DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 release

From reports by r/LocalLLaMA, Hugging Face, openrouter.ai and 1 more · Oct 17 – Oct 19

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, which it describes as a minor update to DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with substantial improvements across reasoning, coding, agentic tasks and visual benchmarks.1 OpenRouter and Artificial Analysis both list the release date as October 17, 2026.23 DeepSeek says the model reduces task execution time and output length, letting users finish tasks faster and at lower cost.4

In DeepSeek's own benchmark table, the new model scores 42.6 on HLE without tools versus 36.8 for the prior Flash, and 42.3 versus 31.2 on Terminal-Bench 4.0.56 The same table shows it at 77.0 on DeepSWE v1.1 versus 74.2 for the prior Flash, and 29.1 versus 20.3 on ProgramBench.78 The table also shows the final column, Opus 5, ahead on several entries, such as 56.3 on HLE and 51.8 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, which suggests the model does not lead every comparison. inference9

Artificial Analysis gives the model (Max) a score of 45 on its Intelligence Index, well above the median of 19 for open-weight models of similar size.10 Artificial Analysis measured output at 227.1 tokens per second on DeepSeek's API, but found that it generated 210M output tokens on the Index, at the higher end versus a 140M median.1112 Reported prices differ by source: OpenRouter lists $0.10/M input and $0.50/M output tokens, while Artificial Analysis cites $0.30 and $1.20 based on DeepSeek's API.1314
