Model releases

Meta releases Muse Spark Open

Meta announced Muse Spark Open, an open-weight multimodal model that it calls the "most capable US open-weight model to date", with 816 billion total parameters, about 42 billion active per token, and a 1 million token context window. Meta says the model is built for coding, long-horizon agents and multi-step reasoning, and that it can run on a desktop using two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios with the MLX framework, a release developed with Apple.

Artificial Analysis scores the model 39 on its Intelligence Index, the highest for a US open-weights model, though behind GLM-5.3 (45) and Kimi K3 (44). It leads open models on AA-Omniscience but ranks last among the nine highest-scoring open models on the index's four agentic evaluations.

The model ships under a custom Muse Model License that requires "Built with Muse Spark Open" attribution and a separate agreement for Model-as-a-Service businesses above $20M in revenue. Sources differ on the revenue threshold: Interconnects describes it as $20M per month across a company's whole business, while the others say annual revenue.

Critics are less impressed: Teortaxes calls it "grim at this param count" and notes Spark 1.3 is substantially stronger, while some commenters objected to its safety post-training, with one saying heavy safety tuning could make it unsuitable for cybersecurity work.
