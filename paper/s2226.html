Gemini 4 Carbon launch

From reports by The Verge, Google DeepMind, Google AI and 4 more · Nov 10 – Nov 12

Google has made Gemini 4 Carbon generally available, saying it is its strongest model, after releasing it two weeks earlier to a set of cybersecurity defenders through its Fairwind Program.123 Google says Carbon is meant for tasks that test the limits of a model's intelligence, while its earlier Argon model remains the better choice for most software engineering and knowledge work.45 Carbon is available to all Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search and Antigravity; The Verge notes it is not clear when, or if, lower-tier subscribers or free users will get it.6789

Google is also previewing a /swarm feature in its Antigravity platform that turns a single prompt into a coordinated team of agents.1011

According to Google, its Carbon model takes the top spot on three benchmarks: CursorBench 4.0, WANDR from Perplexity, and AutomationBench from Zapier. Google adds that each task costs over two times less with Carbon than with the strongest outside competitor.1213 Google also reports that its safeguards blocked 99% of attack trajectories in simulated cyberattack testing, with a per-attempt breach rate of 0.8% versus 3.1% for Argon.141516

Outside measurements, however, put Carbon behind Anthropic's Fable 5.2: Zvi Mowshowitz reports Artificial Analysis scores of 60 for Carbon against 65 for Fable 5.2, and The Verge notes companies can cherry-pick benchmarks. inference1718 Testers disagree about quality: Theo calls it slow, awkward and prone to hallucination, while Latest Space summarizes early users reporting hallucinations, overconfidence and verbosity, and Martin Casado reportedly calls it really good at code. inference192021

On pricing, Carbon launches at an introductory $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, and Simon Willison reports the price is scheduled to double to $4/$20 on December 1st.222324 On cost per task, Latent Space cites a median of $1.5 for Carbon (High), compared with $7.3 for Fable 5.2 and $5.5 for Opus 5.5.25
