OpenAI and Anthropic financing reports

From reports by Latent Space, TechCrunch · Oct 21 – Oct 23

OpenAI is reportedly working on a raise of up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, in a round led by Coatue and UAE investors, according to sources who spoke to CNBC.1 OpenAI declined to comment, and both CNBC and Bloomberg report that terms are still being negotiated and could change.23 CNBC also reports that the UAE funding could be paired with a deal to secure more computing capacity for OpenAI from the UAE.4

The reported valuation differs between accounts: Bloomberg reportedly said a month earlier that OpenAI was seeking $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation, and another summary also cites a $1.4T+ valuation. inference567 Latent Space stresses that these are attributed reports, not completed transactions or independently verified financial disclosures.89

TechCrunch says OpenAI filed confidential IPO paperwork in June, after a March raise that valued it at $852 billion post-money.10

On the Anthropic side, a reported WSJ review of its draft prospectus cites $16.3B first-half revenue, nearly $9B in compute and infrastructure spending, and more than $600B in future compute commitments.1112 TechCrunch notes that Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation in May, and that CNBC reports it is now seeking a $1.5 to $2 trillion valuation for its public debut.1314 TechCrunch says a valuation above $1 trillion would make OpenAI the most valued private company ever and would raise the bar for its IPO, and that it could also affect Anthropic's IPO, which many believe is closer to materializing.1516
