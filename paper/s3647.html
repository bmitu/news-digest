Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen

From reports by The Verge, Google AI, Google DeepMind and 5 more · Nov 12 – Nov 13

Ars Technica reports that Hydrogen has replaced Gemini 3.8 Flash across Google's products, and Google Search VP Robby Stein says it is now the default model in AI Mode and handles the hardest questions in AI Overviews.12 Sources differ on Gemini app access: Ars Technica says AI Plus and free users remain on an older Flash-Lite model, while Latent Space's roundup says free Gemini app users get Hydrogen through auto-selection. inference34 Ars Technica points out that the API rates of $1.50 for every million input tokens and $7.50 for every million output tokens match what 3.8 Flash charged.56

“Gemini 4 Hydrogen at $1.50/$7.50 per million input/output tokens is viewed as expensive for a”

Reddit commenters, via Latent Space

Google claims Hydrogen finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at its default high setting, scores 57% versus 18.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, and cuts fabricated claims by more than 5x.7 According to Google, on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index Hydrogen earns 51 to Argon's 50 when set to its default high level of thinking, and reaches 54 when run at the newly added xhigh setting.8 Coverage of the Argon comparison is inconsistent: Google and TechCrunch tie the win over Argon to the default high level, while Ars Technica cites the xhigh score of 54 against Argon's 50. inference910 Google's two posts also name different benchmark versions for the Antigravity Orchestrator result, which it says reached near-Carbon success with 24% less quota and 19% faster completion: Terminal-Bench 4.0 in the DeepMind post and 4.1 in the Antigravity post. inference1112

Latent Space, summarizing independent results from Artificial Analysis, reports that Hydrogen reaches 54 at xhigh, a single point below both Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol. On AA-Omniscience its hallucination rate is 8%, compared with 43% for Sonnet and 52% for Sol.1314

Simon Willison finds Gemini 4's pricing confusing, noting that Hydrogen is currently only a 25% discount on its more capable peers and costs 15 times as much as Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna.1516 Per Latent Space's summary of online discussion, commenters view the price as high for a Flash-tier model, while one early tester reports it is very fast even in high thinking mode, though without any measured figures.1718
