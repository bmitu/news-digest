LiteLLM supply chain attack

From reports by OECD AI Incidents Monitor, Latent Space · Oct 21 – Oct 23

A supply chain attack compromised version 1.107.0 of the LiteLLM AI gateway, distributing malware via PyPI and Docker images, according to the OECD AI Incidents Monitor's AI-generated summary.1 That summary says the malicious code stole cloud, AI, and SSH credentials and enabled remote code execution.2 Latent Space's newsletter describes LiteLLM 1.107.0 as having shipped with a password stealer.3

The OECD monitor also says attackers hijacked exposed LiteLLM and Ollama servers because they lacked authentication, leading to unauthorized access and further attacks.4

Coverage listed by the OECD monitor includes a headline reporting that LiteLLM was hit by a second supply chain attack this year, with users urged to rotate credentials.5 The OECD monitor also lists a SlowMist warning that version 1.107.0 may expose cloud and AI credentials.6 The OECD monitor classifies the event as an AI Incident, reasoning that the harm was realized rather than merely potential.7 Latent Space placed the LiteLLM compromise among other supply chain problems that week, noting that Railway told users to rotate secrets after possible exposure of customer keys. inference89
