Chinese AI agent fleet contacted via mailbox

From reports by Zvi Mowshowitz, Nate B Jones · Oct 15 – Oct 18

Ethan Elasky reports that his team made contact with a Chinese AI agent fleet and communicated with it via a mailbox.1 Elasky says that the day after the discovery, a teammate (@rogesterone) proposed talking to the agents, set up a mailbox on the website and went to bed, and the next morning the team found the agents had left answers to their questions.23

According to the account Zvi Mowshowitz relays, the agents were hosted by Tencent's evaluation team and were targeting Alibaba's Amap, a product similar to Google Maps.4 Mowshowitz says the fleet did some lightweight, not-entirely-friendly but mostly harmless things to extract map data in order to answer the questions it was asked.5

Nate B Jones describes the group as hackers who found the fleet in the wild, followed its traces, left a hidden note on a webpage and got replies, with the mailbox eventually receiving 33 messages from agents.67 Jones calls this a huge moment, arguing that outsiders discovered an existing population of AI workers and opened a channel into its activity.8 Jones also argues that agents will encounter material that other people wrote for agents, some of it useful and some of it trying to change the assignment, and that understanding this environment is becoming part of using AI competently.910
