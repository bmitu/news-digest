Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB release

From reports by r/LocalLLaMA, Latent Space · Oct 14 – Oct 17

NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB, a commercial-use vision-language and agent model, appeared on Hugging Face with 74B total and 9B active parameters.1 It uses a Mamba-2–Transformer hybrid LatentMoE backbone, a C-RADIO v4-H vision encoder, native IQ2 2-bit QAT quantization and up to 256K context.2 It targets local 24GB deployment with vLLM on NVIDIA Ampere, Hopper and Blackwell, with optional speculative decoding using 7 draft tokens for better throughput.34

Commenters viewed the native 2-bit QAT footprint as unusually strong, quoting “74GB QAT coming in at 24GB on disk is pretty incredible”, and suggested it could replace Qwen3.6-35B for some users.56 Debate centered on whether Bolt is competitive with Qwen 3.8 27B: some argued Qwen remains stronger, while Bolt may be preferable on sub-24GB or constrained VRAM systems because it avoids heavier post-hoc quantization damage and can use offload/speculative decoding effectively.78 Another commenter said benchmarking mainly against Qwen 3.6 is a tough comparison point, implying Qwen remains a strong baseline, and some users wanted to test whether the model avoids being overly optimized for coding benchmarks.910
