Storm-0940 ARTEX ConnectWise breach

From reports by OECD AI Incidents Monitor, TechCrunch, Latent Space and 1 more · Oct 21 – Oct 23

Microsoft Threat Intelligence says the China state-aligned group Storm-0940 used the agentic AI penetration testing framework ARTEX to automate attacks on hundreds of organizations worldwide, and that it assesses Storm-0940 was responsible for the recently disclosed ConnectWise supply chain compromise.12 TechCrunch reports that Microsoft's researchers say the hackers broke into ConnectWise's cloud backup service and stole application credentials, which let them log in to customers' Microsoft-hosted environments as if they were authorized users and access emails and other files.34 ConnectWise said it paused access to its cloud backup system and is investigating, and as of Thursday it was working on restoring the service.5

Microsoft says the ARTEX use sped up abuse of already-authorized applications, letting the actor authenticate without an interactive user sign-in, with follow-on activity that included reconnaissance, application credential changes and attempts to modify privileges.67 Per a Latent Space summary of Microsoft's findings (via MSTIC posts on social media), the group used ARTEX against 2,250+ Microsoft 365 tenants at about 2,900 sign-in attempts per minute, and in some cases reached privileged access in under 3 hours.8910 Microsoft said this speed gives defenders "little time to detect and respond."11 Sources differ in how they describe the scale: Microsoft's own post speaks of "hundreds of organizations," while TechCrunch and others cite around 2,250 organizations or tenants. inference1213810

TechCrunch and the OECD's incident monitor say ARTEX was also used in a recent compromise of South Korea's financial systems, with the OECD entry citing over 16 Korean financial institutions; TechCrunch says many of those had weak security and lacked multi-factor authentication.1415 The OECD's AI Incidents Monitor, in an AI-generated assessment, classifies the event as an AI incident, saying the AI system's involvement was direct and pivotal in enabling the attack at scale and speed.16
