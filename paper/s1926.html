Walmart AI agent gateway

From reports by The Verge, TechCrunch · Oct 21

Walmart announced on Wednesday that it is launching an "Agent Gateway" and other features aimed at customers who use AI agents to shop.1 The Agent Gateway is currently in beta and connects AI agents to Walmart through the Model Context Protocol, according to The Verge.2 TechCrunch reports that the gateway lets an AI agent search Walmart's online inventory and make purchases for its user, and that the technology will support agentic shopping at both Walmart and Sam's Club.34

The Verge reports that purchases will by default need the shopper's approval, though users can let an agent spend within a budget such as up to $100, and can turn the agent off at any time.56 Walmart says Agent Profiles, rolling out next month, will give each agent a dedicated profile so customers can see its purchases separately from their own.78 Walmart says the gateway can help an agent find a shopper's usual items without sharing the customer's broader shopping history with the agent.910

Walmart is also introducing terms for agents: they must not act beyond what the shopper intends, cannot access payment information, and may access only the information needed for the task.1112 Walmart says agents that do not follow the terms will be blocked, and TechCrunch notes that its returns process applies even when an agent does not act as intended.131415 TechCrunch argues the returns condition is customer-friendly and could sway consumers worried about being early testers of agentic commerce.16

Both outlets contrast Walmart's approach with Amazon, which The Verge says has blocked Perplexity's shopping agent and Meta's Muse assistant. inference1718
