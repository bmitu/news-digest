Opencode breached, rotate keys to be safe.

From reports by r/LocalLLaMA, TechCrunch, Latent Space and 1 more · Oct 17 – Oct 23

OpenCode, an open source AI coding agent, said on Saturday that attackers broke into an older system overnight, affecting 1.4% of users of its online platform, OpenCode Console, where some users store API keys.1 The company wrote on X that the breach was related to a large-scale distillation attack, and said it had notified affected users by email.23 Some skeptics called the distillation framing an overstretch, arguing the incident was a straightforward breach whose use for distillation remains unproven.4 Co-founder Dax Raad said the open source app and command-line tool were not affected.5

Developers posted spending alerts and unauthorized charges, with some reporting hundreds of dollars and a few above $1,000.6 Anthropic and Google blocked the keys they believed were exposed, OpenAI said it worked with OpenCode to restore access and refund its API customers, and OpenCode said refunds are underway.78 OpenCode has told affected users to revoke and replace their keys directly with each provider, and says the incident remains ongoing.910

Raad said attackers had tried to break in nonstop for 48 hours, requiring AI defensive agents to run around the clock.1112 AI models the team used for analysis started refusing when the malicious code triggered safety filters, until OpenCode got access to OpenAI's Daybreak Blue program, which Raad said improved its investigation.1314 David Sacks wrote on X that the guardrails backfired, while the article notes Hugging Face hit the same wall in July.1516
