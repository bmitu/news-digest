Steam Next Fest flooded with AI games

From reports by OECD AI Incidents Monitor, Ars Technica, ign.com · Oct 19 – Oct 20

Steam Next Fest, Valve's festival for indie games and demos, began on October 19, 2026, and IGN reports it appears to contain more low-effort and AI-generated games than usual. inference12 IGN's Jacqueline Thomas says that, now that Claude Opus 5.5 is out and has been at the center of the decompilation and mashup mod explosion of the last few weeks, it's not surprising there's a deluge of games either promoted with AI-generated images or flat-out generated with AI.34 IGN adds that low-effort games at Next Fest are nothing new, since Valve has long relied on the algorithm to bury them, and suggests most people should ignore the event for a couple of days until popular demos rise.56

Steam users complained on Reddit: ScalesGhost asked for a filter to exclude games with AI-generated content, noting Steam already requires disclosure, while EmmaBooney called the event "all AI slop" and aesoprowwy hoped Valve would adopt stricter submission guidelines.789 IGN notes that such a filter would probably require Valve to add an 'AI' tag, and that Valve cut user-defined tags in May.1011

Separately, developer Alliance Arts delayed the public demo of Drunken Goddess Reflux, which had been set to launch with Next Fest, saying it feared the demo could be quickly imitated with AI before launch.1213 Views differ on the cloning: Charlie Greenman, who posted a clone, defended it, while Ivan Lago of Howltide Games argues that a game AI can copy overnight "wasn't a real game."1415 The OECD's AI Incidents Monitor, whose summary is AI-generated, classifies both the demo cancellations and the flood of low-quality AI demos as an AI incident, though it says its content does not represent official OECD views. inference1617
