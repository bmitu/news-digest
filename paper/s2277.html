Qwen4 preview

From reports by Latent Space, r/LocalLLaMA · Oct 31 – Nov 4

A conference photo shared on Reddit from Alibaba Cloud's Apsara Conference indicates that the Qwen4 series is "coming soon", with a quoted CTO timeline of "next week".12 The slide reportedly lists Qwen4-Max, Qwen4-Flash, Qwen4-Plus and Qwen4-27B, which suggests a multi-tier release spanning flagship, faster/cheaper, plus and mid-size dense variants.3

A Qwen Code pull request (QwenLM/qwen-code#15762) added pattern-based context-window handling for apparent qwen4* model IDs such as qwen4-max, qwen4-coder-plus and qwen4-omni-120b-think, changing their fallback limit from 262,144 to 1,000,000 tokens.45 The names appeared in parser and test logic rather than in a public release announcement, and a later PR (#15987) reportedly removed or corrected "wrong model names", which weakens the claim that they are confirmed upcoming products.67

Top commenters were skeptical, arguing the names are likely synthetic examples for testing model-ID parsing rather than evidence of imminent Qwen4 releases.8 Commenters on the Apsara post mostly anticipated whether Qwen4 would keep strong coding and cybersecurity capabilities, with some hoping it could reduce reliance on Claude and others interested in a possible 27B dense model.910 One commenter also questioned how durable Alibaba's "forever open-weight" commitment for Qwen is, arguing that corporate open-source promises depend on current management and business incentives.1112
