Google Gemini in schools

Google disables Search/AI for schools

From reports by The Verge, Hacker News, Fairplay and 8 more · Nov 3 – Nov 6

Google announced on Tuesday (November 3, 2026) that Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search will be off by default for K-12 student accounts unless administrators explicitly turn them on, and told The New York Times that schools rejecting AI would lose Search. inference12 Chris Phillips, Google's vice president of education, said "Search and AI are deeply integrated," and that schools disabling AI "will not have access to Search, and they'll see a message from their school that indicates it's not available."3 Schools that enable AI can choose standard Gemini-based AI or a new "Guided Learning" version, which Google says is meant to tutor without giving away all the answers.456

Lawmakers and officials reacted sharply: Senator Bernie Sanders called the move "extortion," New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani said "no corporation should have the power to dictate how we teach our children," and California official Rohit Chopra said Big Tech shouldn't be able to "strong-arm schools."78

Following the backlash, Google said the change will "give schools more choice, not less," and that schools can turn on one, two, or all of the services.910 Ars Technica reported that Google's clarification lets schools disable Gemini apps while keeping search active, but a Google spokesperson said the policy itself was unchanged and Google still offers no AI-free Search.111213

Critics remain unsatisfied: Fairplay's Josh Golin said the 'clarification' doesn't appear to change the fact that students won't be able to use Google Search without being subjected to Google's AI, and warned that schools shouldn't be fooled by this 'bait and switch.'1415 Some online commenters argued the framing is misleading, saying it is a product-integration consequence rather than retaliation, while Zvi Mowshowitz called Google "actively user hostile" and noted a teacher's report that search still worked without AI Overviews. inference161718

Separately, a lawsuit filed in California Superior Court claims a 13-year-old girl chatted with Gemini over several months; Google said it is reviewing the claims and takes them "very seriously."1920
