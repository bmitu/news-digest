SpaceX reports third-quarter results

From reports by TechCrunch, The Verge · Oct 20 – Oct 22

SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.6 billion in a regulatory filing on Tuesday, with TechCrunch saying growth came in all segments, especially the renamed "Super Intelligence" business.12

SpaceX posted net income of $4.4 billion, a turnaround from a $1 billion loss a year earlier, though it still had a $413 million net loss over the first nine months of the year.34 Capital expenditures totaled $19.6 billion, and CFO Bret Johnsen said the company would spend $35 billion to $40 billion on new capital investments in the fourth quarter.56

On the investor call, Elon Musk predicted annual recurring revenue by the end of 2027 of "somewhere between $500 billion and $1 trillion," while Johnsen forecast first-quarter 2027 revenue of $33 billion to $37 billion.78 Musk argued that the constraint on deploying super intelligence is power, cooling and infrastructure more than chips, and said the next Starship test flight, expected in November, would attempt to recover both stages.910

The Verge is more skeptical of the AI business, saying it looks more like CoreWeave than Anthropic and that Grok's contribution was not broken out from bare-metal revenue.1112 Analyst Gil Luria of DA Davidson says most of the rest of the roughly $7 billion in solutions and infrastructure revenue was likely compute rented to Anthropic and Google, with Grok probably contributing less.13

Fortune's Shawn Tully writes that SpaceX and Tesla combined will likely spend close to $50 billion on capex in Q4, and that their businesses won't come close to covering that.14
