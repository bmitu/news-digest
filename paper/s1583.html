Mistral Timestral

From reports by Mistral AI, Latent Space · Oct 19 – Oct 20

Mistral AI says it is releasing Timestral, its first family of time-series foundation models, and claims its largest model, at 209M parameters, ranks first on the three main public benchmarks GIFT-Eval, TIME and fev-bench.12 Mistral says the 209M model outperforms the previous state of the art with only two-thirds of the parameters, and that results have been submitted to the GIFT-Eval and TIME leaderboards.34

The family has four models of 5M, 28M, 123M and 209M parameters, which Mistral says share one architecture and training recipe and each sit on the Pareto front of accuracy versus size.56 Mistral claims its 28M model beats Chronos-2, about four times larger, on GIFT-Eval and TIME, and that the 209M model beats TimesFM-3 and Falcon-2.0 with fewer parameters.78

According to Mistral, Timestral needs no GPU at inference: on a server CPU with 8 threads a one-day forecast takes 10 ms with the 5M model and 140 ms with the 209M model.910 Mistral says the models give probabilistic, multivariate forecasts, predicting 21 quantiles, and support past-only and known-future covariates.1112

On training, Mistral says most data was synthetic, with pre-training at context size 8192 followed by post-training on real and synthetic data with a 4x longer context.1314

Mistral says Timestral is available through Mistral Forge for adaptation, that it has published a GitHub repository, and that a technical report will follow.1516 Passage [1], from the Latent Space AINews roundup, briefly mentions Timestral as 'Open time-series foundation models' and attributes it to qtnx_.1718
