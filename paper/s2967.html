Anthropic AI R&D acceleration

METR report on Anthropic AI R&D acceleration

From reports by Cognitive Revolution, Zvi Mowshowitz, MIT Technology Review and 5 more · Nov 7 – Nov 13

METR, an AI evaluation nonprofit, published a report estimating that Anthropic is improving its models' capabilities on well-specified tasks at about twice the rate it did between March 2024 and October 2025.12 METR says the speedup is mostly attributable to Anthropic's use of AI for R&D, and that it received evidence the acceleration did not come from faster growth in training compute.34 According to METR, Anthropic's models have improved on benchmarks at roughly 1.9 times the speed seen before October 2025, as gauged by AECI, the company's own take on the Epoch Capabilities Index. The estimate comes with a 95% confidence interval spanning 1.5x to 2.8x, and METR lists it among the main empirical results of its report.5

Zvi Mowshowitz notes that the point estimate of 1.88x for Mythos 5.2 is close to a 2x threshold, so Anthropic cannot confidently rule out acceleration above 2x, and he says Anthropic's S-1 amendment acknowledged that it met its Responsible Scaling Policy AI R&D threshold. inference67

Sam McCandlish, an Anthropic cofounder, said the company concluded it shouldn't disclose precisely what is behind the acceleration, since the information is commercially sensitive and could effectively give competitors a guide to speeding up. Daniel Kokotajlo responded that it is "pretty rich" to invoke the danger of "others could go faster" to justify redacting so much, given that Anthropic doesn't seem to be decelerating much at all.89

Some analysts dispute the interpretation: Toby Ord says that allowing a level shift from the larger Mythos pretrain drops the slope change to about 1.4x, while Buck Shlegeris replies that METR's conclusion rests mainly on redacted evidence rather than that graph.1011
