Meta non-voting share class

From reports by The Verge, TechCrunch · Oct 21

Meta says it is creating a new class of non-voting shares to help fund its AI initiatives, announced with its third-quarter earnings report on Wednesday.12 TechCrunch reports that Semafor first said Meta might issue non-voting stock, and that Meta confirmed this on its earnings call.34 The Verge reports that Meta says the stock will also go toward potential acquisitions and employee compensation, with trading beginning in 2027.5

Chief financial officer Susan Li said the new non-voting stock would be distributed to all existing shareholders through a stock dividend.6 Li said the class was created to give Meta a publicly traded non-voting stock for capital raising, employee compensation, acquisitions and other general corporate purposes while preserving the voting power of existing Class A and B shareholders.7 Li added that the shares will let Meta keep its "founder-led structure" and expand its funding tools.8910

In its press release, Meta said the scale of investment needed to compete amid the AI transformation is significant.11 The Verge reports Meta now expects capital expenditures of $135 billion to $145 billion this year, up from a previous estimate of $130 billion to $145 billion.12 TechCrunch reports the quarter beat Wall Street expectations, with $64 billion in revenue, up 26% from a year earlier, while costs and expenses rose 68%.131415

CEO Mark Zuckerberg said more than 10 million people in the U.S. and Canada had set up Muse personal agents in a month and a half, and said Meta is entering a phase focused on scaling the product.1617 The Verge notes Muse has had snags, including a tech YouTuber's report that the agent gave out his address to a stranger on Facebook Marketplace, and a paused conversation-sharing feature after researchers discovered chats publicly available on the web.1819

Li also warned that legal and regulatory matters, including youth-related scrutiny and several trials scheduled in the coming months in the US, could significantly affect Meta's business and results.2021
