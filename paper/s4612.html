Megaspeed investigation

From reports by The Hill technology, TechCrunch, Semafor and 4 more · Nov 16 – Nov 20

Following reports that a probe may have been shut down last year, the Justice and Commerce Departments announced that they are looking into Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center firm tied to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation.12 According to the departments, Commerce opened a probe of Megaspeed in 2024 over possible export control violations, then in May 2025 asked Nvidia for its records of sales to the company; after an enforcement action, the DOJ took over as the lead agency on the criminal investigation.345 Both departments vowed to pursue Megaspeed to the fullest extent of the law over any criminal wrongdoing, but they did not explain where the case currently stands.6

The New York Times had reported that the Commerce probe came to a sudden halt late last year after investigators got an email from Nvidia's general counsel saying Commerce official Jeffrey Kessler had permitted Nvidia to reach out; The Hill notes neither agency confirmed this.78 Zvi Mowshowitz cites New York Times trade data showing Megaspeed's Malaysian subsidiary received more than $800 million of shipments with controlled Nvidia chips between July 2025 and February 2026, even though Nvidia and Commerce say Megaspeed was put on a "no sell" list in June 2025.910

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated that for months Treasury, War, USTR and State had supported adding Megaspeed to the entity list, and Axios reported that he confronted Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick over the lack of action.11121314 Lutnick has agreed to appear before the Senate Banking Committee on Dec. 10, and Democratic Sen. Chris Coons said Lutnick "let the American people down".1516

Singapore's digital minister Josephine Teo said investigations into Megaspeed are still continuing and that Singapore's own probe predated its knowledge of the US Bureau of Industry and Security investigation.1718 Singapore's Ministry of Home Affairs says authorities seized Alice Huang's passport in June 2025, that police found insufficient evidence she had committed offenses related to the suspected false declarations, and that its investigation into Megaspeed and six other individuals remains ongoing.192021

Separately, the SEC is reportedly opening a civil probe into whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors, according to The Wall Street Journal.22
