Oregon sues OpenAI over child safety

From reports by OECD AI Incidents Monitor, koin.com, yahoo.com and 14 more · Nov 17 – Nov 21

Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield sued OpenAI on November 17, 2026, in Multnomah County Circuit Court, seeking at least $2.035 billion and alleging that ChatGPT endangers children and that the company misled parents about its safety.1234 The complaint alleges violations of Oregon's Unlawful Trade Practices Act, plus negligence, unjust enrichment and public nuisance, and asks the court to order stronger safeguards and an end to misleading safety claims.5678 The suit launched Rayfield's "Keeping Kids Safe" initiative and follows lawsuits by eight other states in October, with Rayfield promising more actions against companies in coming weeks and months. inference9101112

Rayfield said OpenAI knows ChatGPT is harming children and has failed to fix the platform, and that the company put profits ahead of children's safety.131415 One outlet reports that the complaint also refers to the February 10 Tumbler Ridge, British Columbia school shooting, with Oregon's attorneys alleging OpenAI flagged the shooter's conversations but company leaders rejected employees' recommendations to alert Canadian police; these claims are allegations, not established findings.161718 Crypto Briefing reports that the suit centers on claims that ChatGPT lacked adequate age verification and parental controls, which it says allegedly contributed to a school shooting in British Columbia.19

The $2.035 billion is a requested amount, not an award; Oregon says it reflects estimated costs of addressing the harm, and the final amount is to be set at trial.202122 Reports differ on how the figure was reached: Rayfield said his office estimated it from the number of children in Oregon using ChatGPT and related penalties, while one outlet says the complaint gives no detailed calculation or count of harmed children. inference232425

OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri responded that the company has built teen protections many AI products lack, will keep working with experts, and wants AI policies that apply to the whole industry rather than one company.262728 OregonLive, as relayed by Legal Reader, noted that OpenAI's response did not directly address the allegation that it knowingly endangered children.29 Crypto Briefing also says market pricing reflects a lower likelihood of an OpenAI IPO by the end of 2026, along with a slight uptick in bankruptcy prediction odds.3031
