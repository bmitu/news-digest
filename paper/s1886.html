Kimi K3.1 Pro launch

From reports by Simon Willison, Latent Space · Oct 23 – Oct 24

Moonshot launched Kimi K3.1 Pro, which it describes as a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 2.8 trillion total parameters, 104 billion active parameters and a 1-million-token context window.1 Moonshot says K3.1 Pro is a post-trained version of Kimi K3 with substantially larger post-training compute.2 The model is not yet open-weight, but Moonshot promises that full weights will be released by November 4, 2026.3

Artificial Analysis placed K3.1 Pro fourth on its Intelligence Index with a score of 55, behind Opus 5.5 at 58 and Sonnet 5.5 and Muse Wonder Full at 56.4 Simon Willison notes that this is ahead of Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Argon (all at 53), and calls it the first time a soon-to-be open-weight model has scored this highly against the frontier.56 Arena placed it #8 in Code Arena: WebDev, and it scored 64% on Artificial Analysis' Cyber Index, second overall behind a trusted-access GPT-6 model at 69%.78

Standard pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, down from K3's $3 and $15, while the Highspeed version generates output 2–3× faster at twice the price.910 Latent Space notes that K3.1 Pro generated 55K output tokens per task on the Intelligence Index versus 48K for K3, offsetting some of the token-price reduction.11

Reaction was mixed: enthusiasm about a prospective open-weight frontier contender was followed by concerns about inference economics, weaker results on some interactive tasks, benchmark presentation, and allegations of undisclosed paid promotion.1213 Teortaxes argued the model might be 30×+ more expensive to serve than DeepSeek Flash, though the summary calls these unverified estimates.1415
