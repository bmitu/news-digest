Kimi K3.1 Pro launch

From reports by Latent Space, Interconnects, Zvi Mowshowitz · Oct 24 – Oct 29

Moonshot launched Kimi K3.1 Pro, which it describes as a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 2.8 trillion total and 104 billion active parameters and a 1-million-token context window, and Nathan Lambert says the company promised open weights by November 4th.12 Standard pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, down from K3's $3/$15, while the Highspeed tier costs $4/$20 and Moonshot claims it generates output 2–3× faster.345

Artificial Analysis placed K3.1 Pro fourth on its Intelligence Index with a score of 55, behind Opus 5.5 at 58 and Sonnet 5.5 and Muse Wonder Full at 56.6 Vals reportedly ranked it the top open-weight model on its Science Index at 44.4%, versus 31.7% for the next open model.7 After Artificial Analysis moved to a v5 index, Zvi Mowshowitz's summary of its results has K3.1 Pro as the top open-weights model at only 46, with Claude Opus 5.5 leading at 58, so the headline standing depends on the index version. inference8910

Lambert says Moonshot's own blog shows some more jagged results, such as Terminal-Bench Science about 20 points behind GPT-6 Astra, and that community impressions are a mixed bag.1112 Cheaper token prices may not mean cheaper work, since Artificial Analysis found K3.1 Pro generated 55K output tokens per task versus 48K for K3.13 The poster teortaxesTex argued that the model might be 30×+ more expensive to serve than DeepSeek Flash, though this is an unverified estimate rather than a measured cost.1415

Florian Brand says the launch drew criticism for a bigger social media campaign that allegedly involved paying people to post about the model without disclosure, and another summary likewise reports criticism over undisclosed paid-influencer briefs.1617 Moonshot says K3.1 Pro's offensive cyber capability is by design below what its pipeline can produce, and Brand calls removing such material from the training mix a reasonable decision.181920
