PHP RCE vulnerabilities and leak

3 stories · Oct 21, 2026 to Oct 30, 2026
The stories of one ongoing situation, in the order they were first reported. Which stories belong together is a model's grouping; each story's sources are the reports that carried it.

What happened

Simon Willison reported on October 21, 2026, that patched PHP 8.x releases fixed two remote code execution bugs.1 Willison said the releases were brought forward after a leak and early disclosure.1 Wiz Research reported flaws in core PHP, found with AI models, involving unauthenticated remote code execution and memory disclosure.2 OpenAI said Calif found a critical flaw with GPT‑5.6 Sol, Wiz independently reported both vulnerabilities, and maintainers led the fixes.3

How the details leaked

Willison reported that CVE IDs leaked with a wrong release time and CVSS score.4 According to Willison, a firm then accidentally posted a proof-of-concept video, speeding up exploit development.4 Zvi Mowshowitz reported on October 23 that two flaws rated CVSS 9.2 leaked before patches after an embargo broke.5 Mowshowitz also reported exploitation seen from October 17.5

Affected sites and unverified claims

Latent Space reported on October 22 that two PHP-engine memory-corruption flaws were disclosed, with demonstrated pre-auth attacks against WordPress, Drupal and Joomla.6 Latent Space also said viral compilations claimed about 1.5 million unpatched servers and shutdowns at several sites, none independently verified.7 Another Latent Space report said the cause of the outages in such a compilation was not established.8

Transformer reported on October 23 that leaked details forced government sites and the Internet Archive offline to patch.9 Mowshowitz reported that 4chan and the Internet Archive went offline briefly.5

Who found the flaws and whether attacks followed

Latent Space reported on October 23 that the bug was reportedly found by OpenAI models in August and kept under embargo until the patch.10 Exponential View reported the same day that a researcher using AI found the flaw in a week.11

Interconnects said on October 25 that no mass exploitation had been reported so far.12 Latent Space reported on October 27 that the roughly 15-year-old flaw was confirmed as exploited in attacks.13 The Center for AI Safety reported the same day that exploit attempts reached millions.14

Latest reported

TechCrunch reported on October 28 that the two critical bugs were fixed the previous week by Wiz, OpenAI, Calif and the PHP team.15 TechCrunch also said the bugs left organizations scrambling, and some took sites offline.16
