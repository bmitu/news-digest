What happened

Large technology companies reported third-quarter earnings in October 2026, and the first reports, on October 21, covered Meta.12 TechCrunch reported that Meta's revenue rose 26% to $64 billion, while its costs rose 68%.2 The Verge reported that Meta expected capital expenditures of $135 billion to $145 billion this year, up from an estimate of $130 billion to $145 billion.3

More companies report results

TechCrunch reported on October 28 that Microsoft posted $92.3 billion in revenue, up 19%, its 10th straight record quarter.4 According to TechCrunch, a Microsoft regulatory filing reported $10 billion from OpenAI arrangements in the September quarter.5

TechCrunch reported the same day that Alphabet's Search revenue grew 12% in the third quarter, down from 17% in the second.6 Alphabet said Google Cloud brought in $30.7 billion in the quarter, TechCrunch reported.7 TechCrunch also reported that Google was bringing ads in AI Mode and AI Overviews to international markets.8

TechCrunch reported on October 29 that Amazon's capital spending for 2026 was $220 billion.9 The same report said Amazon's shares fell about 6%.9

Alphabet's share fall

Latest reported
