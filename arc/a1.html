Meta Muse agent and Amazon shopping block

11 stories · Sep 27, 2026 to Nov 6, 2026
The stories of one ongoing situation, in the order they were first reported. Which stories belong together is a model's grouping; each story's sources are the reports that carried it.

What happened

Reports on September 24, 2026 said Meta had released Muse, a personal AI agent.1 Ars Technica reported that Muse became the most-downloaded US app on iOS and Android within two weeks of its early September launch.2 Latent Space said Muse gained voice, real-time video, an email address, Mac computer use and over 1,500 app integrations.3 Zvi Mowshowitz reported on September 24 that Amazon had blocked Muse from its platform over concerns.4

Amazon's stated reasons

Nate B Jones wrote on September 29 that Amazon locked Muse out because of a conflict with its $68.6 billion advertising business.5 The Verge reported on September 30 that Amazon cited privacy and security concerns over an unauthorized AI agent identifying itself.6 Ars Technica said on October 2 that Amazon cited concerns that AI apps must operate openly and respect service providers' participation decisions.7

On October 17, The Verge reported Amazon saying that Muse's continued access violates its site policies.8 The Verge also reported Meta saying it prefers working with businesses and has partnered with Walmart, Gap, Sephora and Best Buy.8

Privacy claims and Meta's replies

appleinsider.com reported on September 29 that Jason Aten found Muse had synced 187,000 lines of Apple Messages without permission, despite Full Disk Access being disabled.9 TechCrunch reported on September 30 that a Meta vice president and an engineer denied Muse read a user's private messages without explicit permission.10 Ars Technica said on October 2 that Aten's case contradicted a claim by Meta's CTO that the Messages connector must be explicitly enabled.11

The Verge reported on September 30 that tech YouTuber Matt Robb's home address was disclosed to a stranger through Muse.12 TechCrunch reported Robb's claim that a buyer showed up while Robb was out, and said the user later admitted fault.13 Ars Technica reported on October 2 that Patrick Wardle disclosed a Muse vulnerability allowing attackers to hijack the assistant.14

The Verge reported on October 20 that Claroc found over 6,800 Muse share links through DNS records.15 The Verge and Ars Technica both reported that Meta paused the sharing feature during a review.1516

New models and download figures

TechCrunch reported on October 12 that Meta's Superintelligence Lab released its first frontier models, Wonder and Wonder Full.17 Latent Space reported on October 13 that Wonder scored 52 and Full 56 on Artificial Analysis' index, behind Opus 5.5 at 58.18

MIT Technology Review reported on October 19 that Muse was downloaded 5 million times in its first month.19 TechCrunch cited Sensor Tower data on October 20 showing 10.2 million U.S. downloads in Muse's first 40 days.20

Latest reported

TechCrunch reported on October 21 that Meta said on its earnings call that Muse has over 10 million users, with 25% using it daily.21 On October 22, The Verge reported that Meta restored sharing after researchers found public Muse links exposing private documents.22 Mowshowitz reported that day that Meta's open-weight Muse Spark scored 39 on Artificial Analysis, against 46 for top Chinese open models.23
