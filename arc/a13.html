Reflection Beam and Nvidia acquisition talks

3 stories · Oct 6, 2026 to Oct 21, 2026
The stories of one ongoing situation, in the order they were first reported. Which stories belong together is a model's grouping; each story's sources are the reports that carried it.

What happened

TechCrunch reported on October 5, 2026 that Reflection had unveiled Beam, an open-weight AI model.1 According to TechCrunch, the claim was performance parity with Chinese models like GLM-5.2 while requiring 3-4x less compute.1

Early details and comparisons

Latent Space reported on October 6 that Beam was a 501B/23B MoE model for coding and scientific work.2 The same report said it was US-trained from scratch, with Apache 2.0 weights and an 80.9 SWE-bench score.2 On October 7, Latent Space cited a Zhihu analysis describing training on 23.8T tokens, with RL on about 10,500 GB300s over four weeks.3 Import AI reported on October 12 that Beam trailed GLM-5.3 on several benchmarks and that weights were promised later that month.4

Talks with Nvidia reported

Zvi Mowshowitz reported on October 15 that Nvidia was in talks to buy Reflection AI, last valued at $25 billion.5 The post by Mowshowitz saw this as commoditizing open-source models.5

Beam 1.0 release details

Hugging Face reported the release of Beam-1.0-501B-A23B on October 16, describing it as Reflection's first model.6 Hugging Face said it was for coding and agentic work, with day-0 support in transformers, SGLang and vLLM.6 Latent Space reported on October 17 that Beam 1.0 had 1M context and came with BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 weights.7 Latent Space also listed a drafter, the Mirror CLI and a safety report.7

Latest reported

Hacker News carried a report on October 17 that Nvidia was in talks to acquire US 'open' model startup Reflection AI.8 Interconnects reported on October 20 that Beam was called the strongest open American model.9 Interconnects also described it as token efficient but not the smartest open model.9
