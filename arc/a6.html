Claude 5.5 model releases

3 stories · Sep 27, 2026 to Oct 30, 2026
The stories of one ongoing situation, in the order they were first reported. Which stories belong together is a model's grouping; each story's sources are the reports that carried it.

What happened

Simon Willison reported on September 22 that Anthropic had released Claude Opus 5.5.1 Zvi Mowshowitz reported that it was the first model in a new family.2 Willison reported a 20% price reduction, to $4/$20 per million tokens, and said cache reads were cut by 60%.13

Early reports on Opus 5.5

Willison also reported on September 22 that Opus 5.5 failed an SVG generation test at its maximum thinking level.4 The model hit a 128,000 token output limit, according to that report.4 On September 23, Mowshowitz reported Anthropic's claim that it matched or exceeded Fable 5.1 performance while costing less than Opus 5.5 Latent Space reported that day that it was 40% cheaper than Opus 5, faster, and the default in Claude Code and the app.6

Latent Space reported on September 24 that Opus 5.5 ranked first on the Coding Agent Index.3 On September 25, Latent Space reported that it led SimpleBench at 88.4%.7

Sonnet 5.5 arrives and Haiku 5.5 is announced

Willison reported on September 28 that Anthropic had released Claude Sonnet 5.5.8 Willison said it ran more than 30% faster and cost up to 30% less, while having the same price as Sonnet 5.8 Latent Space reported on September 29 that it cost 30% less than Sonnet 5 and powered the free tier and third-party platforms.9 Mowshowitz reported on October 1 that it used 30% fewer tokens, at half the price of Opus 5.5.10

Willison reported on September 28 that Haiku 5.5 had been announced for the coming weeks.11 According to Willison, it was intended to be price-competitive with GPT-6 Luna.11

Magnet candidates

A report on vals.ai on October 5 said an author's AI agents had designed YBaMnFeO₅ and identified KV[Cr(CN)₆] as magnet candidates.12 That report said both were calculated predictions, not measured.12 A Hacker News report on October 6 described them as two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates discovered by Opus 5.5 agents.1312

Latest reported

Mowshowitz reported on October 8 that Anthropic had released Claude Haiku 5.5.14 The report put its cost at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens, a tenth of Haiku 4.5.14 It said Anthropic recommended the model for high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks.14
