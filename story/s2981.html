Gemma 85% pruning

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 6 · single origin (Reddit author (unnamed))

A Reddit post titled "I removed 85% of gemma's weights (and it is mostly fine)" describes a pruned Gemma 4 26B-A4B mixture-of-experts model that keeps only 8 experts per layer, turning it into a dense 3.81B-parameter model of roughly 2.75 GB at Q4_K_M.1 On the author's narrow classification workload, the pruned model reportedly reaches 91.7% agreement with the original 26B model, slightly above Gemma 4 12B's 90.0%.2 It reportedly runs at 79 tokens/s with 3.8 GB peak memory, versus 8.3 GB for the 12B model and 14.5 GB for the full 26B-A4B.3

However, the author acknowledges that tasks heavy on general knowledge degrade because the pruned weights remove capabilities.4

Commenters were mostly humorous or skeptical, calling it a "neural network lobotomy" and noting that the removed weights likely encode capabilities that only show up on broader recall-heavy tasks.5 One commenter asked for a comparison against Gemma 4 E4B as a more size-aligned baseline, rather than evaluating the pruned model in isolation.67 Several commenters also questioned the approach for a narrow task, suggesting a purpose-built lightweight classifier might be simpler and more efficient than pruning a generalist 26B-A4B model.8
