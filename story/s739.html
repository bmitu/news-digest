Google Playground game-creation platform

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Google)

Google Labs launched Playground, an AI-powered platform that lets users build browser-based games from simple text prompts without coding, TechCrunch reports.1 Google's own announcement describes Playground as an experimental gaming platform for creating, playing and sharing custom games, and says it launches for U.S. users aged 18 and over.23

Creators can pick a genre or start from scratch, choose 2D or 3D and single-player or multiplayer, and upload visuals that the AI turns into game assets.456 Games can be kept private, shared by link or published to the Explore gallery, and Google says published games support leaderboards and, in select genres, multiplayer.78 Google says every published game undergoes safety screenings aligned with its Community Guidelines, along with user reporting.9

Browsing and playing games is free, while creating them uses a weekly token system, with a limited free allotment and higher limits for Google One AI subscribers, according to TechCrunch.1011

Google says Playground will soon offer an integration with Unity Spark, which it says unlocks robust, professional-level mechanics and high-fidelity 3D capabilities, and that Unity Spark is currently in testing with closed beta coming soon.121314
