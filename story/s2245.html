Anthropic Slack breach by Cathedral

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Elizabeth Dwoskin, Will Depue

Cathedral, a small new organization, probed Anthropic under a new open program for outside researchers and, according to Zvi Mowshowitz, got access to Slack messages via third-party admin software.12 Anthropic says it also says Cathedral broke the rules of the outside-researcher program and was outside its safe harbor.3

Anthropic confirmed in a statement, quoted by the Washington Post, that Cathedral had accessed "a limited set" of its sales, human resources and IT systems; it said it shut off the exposed pathways and briefed federal officials.45 Anthropic says its monitoring flagged some of Cathedral's activity before Cathedral reported it, that it cut off their access the same day, and that it has no evidence anyone else exploited the vulnerabilities.67

Will Depue says he is hearing that Cathedral achieved full administrator access to Anthropic's Slack workspace (able to see every message and log in as arbitrary users), and says Anthropic is massively underplaying the breach.89 Latent Space's newsletter stresses that Depue's claims are attributed and unconfirmed, and that the scope of the breach is disputed.1011 Reports also differ on the speed and attackers: the Washington Post's Elizabeth Dwoskin posted that ex-DOGE cyber researchers got in within 12 hours using AI agents, while Anthropic's spokesperson said Cathedral spent multiple days trying to break in directly before turning to third-party tools.121314

Nathan Calvin acknowledges Cathedral did not access Anthropic's weights or customer data but says there is no question Anthropic has a ton of immensely sensitive information in its Slack; Sarah Guo called the timing "savage".151617
