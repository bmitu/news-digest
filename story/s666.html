Reflection Beam model launch

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 6 · 1 new article this week · single origin (Reflection AI)

Reflection AI unveiled Beam, its first frontier open-weight model, in a blog post on Monday, and the company claims it matches leading Chinese open models on advanced reasoning benchmarks at much lower cost.123 Reflection says it will release Beam's weights and full technical details this month, and Latent Space reports the weights will be under Apache 2.0.45

Beam is a text-only mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total and 23 billion active parameters, pretrained on 23.8 trillion tokens, with a 1 million token context window.6789

Reflection says Beam scores on par with Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning benchmarks while using "3-4x less inference compute"; TechCrunch notes these claims haven't been independently verified.101112 Reflection's own benchmarks show Beam outscoring Thinking Machines Lab's Inkling on four coding tests, but Inkling is multimodal while Beam is text-only.13 Latent Space says Reflection compares itself to Inkling, Nemotron and GLM 5.2, but that newer models such as GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, Qwen 3.8 Max and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash are generally ahead.14

Latent Space adds that because Beam is US-trained from scratch, part of the market has been waiting for such options, and that Reflection has announced its arrival as a functional "neolab".1516 Reflection is aiming Beam at enterprises and sovereign nations with "AI factories" for custom local AI systems, and has begun testing the concept with Shinsegae Group in South Korea.1718
