Google Personal Intelligence expansion in Search

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 09:22 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 24 · single origin (Google)

Google announced it is expanding Personal Intelligence in Search's AI Mode to connect with Google Wallet, Google Drive and the Gemini app.1 Google says the Wallet connection began rolling out on the announcement day, while Drive and Gemini app support are to follow in the coming weeks.2 The new apps join Gmail, Calendar and Photos, which already feed Personal Intelligence.34

Google says Wallet access can help users map financial goals, trim everyday spending and make better use of credit card rewards.567 Google says AI Mode can only read account information and cannot move, manage or invest money.8

Google says Drive files can inform answers such as a personalized preparation plan ahead of tax season.910 Sharing context between the Gemini app and AI Mode means an idea explored in one can carry over to the other.1112

Connecting apps is opt-in, and users can switch connections on or off at any time.1314 Google says users will soon be able to see the context AI Mode used through a "Personalization" icon below tailored responses.1516 Google also says AI Overviews and AI Mode will show more personally relevant responses based on recent search context.1718

TechCrunch notes that not everyone wants AI looking at their Wallet and Drive, and argues Google's ecosystem of user data lets it offer personalization rivals cannot match.1920
