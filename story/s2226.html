Gemini 4 Carbon launch

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 3 new articles this week · single origin (Google)

Google says it announced Gemini 4 Carbon, which it calls its strongest model yet for long-horizon engineering and cybersecurity defense, about two weeks after rolling out Gemini 4 Argon.12 Carbon is not yet broadly available: Google says it is in pre-release testing with the U.S. government and a subset of trusted cyber defenders via the Fairwind Program while it refines safety guardrails ahead of a broader launch to developers, businesses, and consumers.3

Latent Space's roundup reports that Arena ranks Gemini 4 Carbon #1 in Text Arena, #2 in Agent Arena and #4 in WebDev, and says it shifted the Pareto frontier in all three.4 Perplexity reports a new state of the art on its WANDR long-form research benchmark, and Aravind Srinivas's post says Carbon is coming to Pro and Max users inside Computer "once we have sufficient token capacity".56

A Reddit-sourced item describes Demis Hassabis highlighting an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index chart that shows Carbon ranked #1, with Elon Musk replying "Well done! When can it be accessed?"7 The post gives no benchmark scores, model card, release date, API availability or methodology, so it shows only a claimed leaderboard placement rather than verifiable performance data.8 Commenters mostly treated Musk's reply as trolling or strategic interest, joking that he wants access to distill it into Grok, and others framed the ranking/access question as part of the recurring frontier-model cycle in which another lab may surpass Carbon before public availability.910

AI Explained counted Carbon's arrival among the week's headline-worthy events, and Dylan Patel apologized to Google over Gemini Carbon, though the reason for the apology was not reported. inference1112
