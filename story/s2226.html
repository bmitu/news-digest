Gemini 4 Carbon launch

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
8 sources · 1011 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Google

Google announced Gemini 4 Carbon, which it calls its strongest model, on Wednesday, but it is available only to a subset of cyber defenders for now.12345 Google says access will phase in to more trusted partners through its Fairwind Program, and it gives no firm date for developers, businesses and consumers, saying only "as soon as possible".67 Pricing is stated differently across sources: Google lists a $2/$10 per million token introductory price, while Artificial Analysis says standard pricing is $4/$20 with a 50% launch discount that has no confirmed end date.8910

Google says Carbon scores 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro for finding vulnerabilities, 54.8% on ExploitGym for exploitation and 71.0% on CWE-bench for patching.11 Google also claims safety gains over Argon, including successful cheating on Terminal-Bench 4.0 falling to 0.0% and unsupported claims cut by about 2x.1213

Artificial Analysis independently ranks Carbon #1 on its Intelligence Index v5 with a score of 59, leading 4 of the 9 index evaluations, though it says Carbon is not leading in coding and knowledge work.141516 Artificial Analysis says Carbon scores 59 on its Coding Agent Index versus 62 for Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5, and costs $7.76 per index task versus $10.04 for Opus 5.5 and $6.25 for GPT-6 Astra.1718

A Reddit summary notes that the vendor benchmark table came without methodology or independent validation, and that the lack of public access limits independent checking.19202122 Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam is more skeptical, saying Carbon could be a post-training upgrade of Argon and that the gap between Carbon and the competition is just a few points.2324

Zvi Mowshowitz says Carbon looks to be a large leap over Argon but complains that nobody can use it yet, saying Google "fails marketing forever".2526 The Transformer reports that Alphabet shares fell 13%, and that an Epoch AI researcher speculated Google shipped Carbon before OpenAI and Anthropic release their next-generation models and is still about two months behind.2728
