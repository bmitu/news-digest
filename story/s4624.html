Gemini Tasks rollout

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Google)

The Verge reports Google is rebranding its Spark AI agent as "Tasks" and making it free to use, and Google says the free rollout uses its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.123 Spark was first introduced in May and was limited to paying subscribers, so the launch extends its capabilities to all users in six countries.45 Latent Space's newsletter briefly noted that Hydrogen now powers free Tasks in six countries, including tasks needing a phone call or text.6

Google says Tasks can call businesses and book appointments on users' behalf and complete purchases for them via Google Pay, and users enable it by flipping a toggle in Gemini from Chat to Tasks.789 Google's announcement says the advanced features start in the U.S., and that it is working with American Express (coming soon), Mastercard and Visa to keep purchases secure with digital tokens on supported cards.1011 In Google's account, Gemini runs tasks in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to each user, and is designed to pause and ask confirmation before steps like clicking "Buy"; users can also set a spending cap.121314

Josh Woodword, who is VP of Gemini, says he had Tasks phone nearby car dealerships and book a service slot for the family minivan. Google's post also notes that the heat pump switched on at about 4 p.m., right when peak rates were in effect.1516 The Verge places Tasks among many agentic AI features from rivals, and TechCrunch later called Google a little late to agentic features on desktop compared with OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta. inference1718
