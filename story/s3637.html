Reports Nvidia board moving to oust Huang

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
8 sources · 1448 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Semafor, The Wall Street Journal

Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to oust CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang, with the Wall Street Journal reporting that it could meet as soon as Wednesday to decide his future.123 The Verge reports that Nvidia's board had already commissioned an investigation, headed by Wachtell Lipton, to determine whether Huang might have leaked company secrets or put national security at risk. The publication adds that his apparent removal signals the directors may not hold off until that inquiry concludes. inference45

Huang acknowledged having had an affair outside his marriage with a woman whom U.S. intelligence officials have described as a likely Chinese spy. Separately, Kimberly Powell serves as Nvidia's vice president of healthcare.678 The New York Times says five employees told it of the Powell affair, and that Huang and Powell were allegedly filmed at a San Jose hotel, though the Times has not seen the video.910

Bloomberg reported that Nvidia is working with an outside firm to identify possible successors, and Semafor's sources named Apple hardware chief Johny Srouji as one person under consideration.1112 Nvidia's stock fell roughly 7 to 8 percent in after-hours trading on Wednesday, with Semafor saying it hit a 52-week low.131415

Politically, Senators Chris Coons and Elizabeth Warren asked Cabinet members to preserve documents on Nvidia's China chip exports and on Huang, while two House Republicans said Congress may subpoena Huang if he does not voluntarily brief them.1617 Commentators reacted strongly: Gary Marcus warned that an ouster could unsettle AI financing, while Elon Musk posted that hiring Huang "would be an honor."1819
