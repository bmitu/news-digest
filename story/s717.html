OpenAI withdraws three math papers

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
4 sources · 718 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: OpenAI, The Verge

OpenAI released a very large batch of AI-generated mathematics to GitHub and then withdrew three of the papers, which it said were invalidated by a sign error. inference12 Zvi Mowshowitz says the release covered 722 manuscripts in 372 families, including 90 of the top 500 open problems in math according to Proof of Atlas.34 The Verge reports that OpenAI disclosed its model attempted more than 4,000 problems and that a typical result used around three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking compute.5

Mowshowitz says the three withdrawn papers all trace to one sign error and sat in the roughly 58% of manuscripts without Lean formal verification, and he regards the retraction as a point in OpenAI's favor. inference678 Mathematicians quoted by The Verge were more critical of the quality, saying some papers were hard to follow, and Brown professor Brendan Hassett said a write-up he read "made little sense after a quick read."910

Reactions were mixed: Constantin Kogler called the release "the most important single moment in the history of mathematics," while Terence Tao said his feelings were "very mixed and complex," and that he was excited by the possibility of using these tools for ambitious projects but horrified by the many person-years of patient, deliberately slow research efforts being casually disrupted or destroyed by such a release.111213

OpenAI says it will fund workshops and conferences on its mathematics work and that it wants to keep evaluating its internal frontier models on mathematics.1415
