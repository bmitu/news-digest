Ultrafast tier

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 14:51 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · 2 independent origins: Latent Space, OpenAI

OpenAI describes Ultrafast as a premium speed tier that offers up to 8× faster token generation (300 tokens per second) in Codex and up to 6x in the API.12 OpenAI says GPT‑6 Astra Ultrafast is available today in the API and in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans, while GPT‑6.1 Sol Ultrafast is coming soon.3 OpenAI's new Pro 500 plan offers its highest usage allowance at 25 times the ChatGPT Plus allowance and includes Ultrafast access.4

Latent Space reports pricing is 6x, i.e. $60/$300 per M tokens for Astra.5 Zvi Mowshowitz describes the tier as costing $500 per month and consuming subscription budget eight times faster, or six times standard API costs, and says it cannot be used for regular chat.6

Mowshowitz says that for chat enjoyers the main difference is being down to 100 Pro messages per week, and that heavy coders are down by half, from 20x the basic plan to 10x. He says everyone involved gets a one-time $2,500 API usage credit as an apology, which expires at the end of the year.78 According to Mowshowitz, OpenAI's Tibo defends the change by arguing users still get more work done than on the $200 subscription a month earlier, because of price reductions and quality improvements in Luna and Sol.9

On the hardware, Latent Space notes OpenAI's silicon was unspecified, and relays a claim that SemiAnalysis reports it runs on NVIDIA GPUs at low batch sizes, not on Cerebras.1011 A hands-on report relayed by Latent Space found generation about 8x faster but end-to-end agent tasks only 2–4x faster because tool latency dominates, with the largest gains in computer use.1213

Mowshowitz says that if he used ChatGPT as his baseline he would want Ultrafast "rather badly", but because his primary model is Opus he is not that tempted.14
