Ultrafast tier

2 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · 1 new article today · 2 independent origins: Latent Space, OpenAI

OpenAI introduced Ultrafast, a premium speed tier offering up to 8× faster token generation (300 tokens per second) in Codex and up to 6x in the API.12 OpenAI says GPT‑6 Astra Ultrafast is available today in the API and in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans, while GPT‑6.1 Sol Ultrafast is coming soon.3 The new Pro 500 plan includes access to Ultrafast and, per OpenAI, offers 25 times the ChatGPT Plus allowance.4

Latent Space's newsletter reports Ultrafast pricing is 6x, i.e. $60/$300 per M tokens for Astra.5 Latent Space describes the mode as running on unspecified silicon, but it later cites SemiAnalysis, via a post by @kimmonismus, as reporting that it runs on NVIDIA GPUs at low batch sizes, not on Cerebras. inference67

A hands-on report from @sayashk found generation is about 8x faster, but end-to-end agent tasks speed up only 2–4x because tool latency dominates.8

Can I get Ultrafast on the cheaper Pro 100 or Pro 200 plans, or by buying extra credits?

No. OpenAI says Ultrafast is available only on the Pro 500 plan among Pro plans, and Pro 100 and Pro 200 do not include it.12 Buying extra credits does not change that: OpenAI says that at launch, buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast, which it says uses the plan's included usage first, then draws from the user's credit balance after that allowance is used. inference3452 Staying on Pro 200 with a kept usage allowance does not help either, since OpenAI says that does not upgrade the plan or add Ultrafast.6 Pro 500 costs $500 a month and offers 25 times the ChatGPT Plus allowance.789

What will GPT-6.1 Sol cost at Ultrafast speed, and when will it launch?

The standard API price for GPT-6.1 Sol is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.12 Latent Space reports that Ultrafast pricing is 6x, which works out to $60/$300 per million tokens for Astra.3 Applying that 6x multiplier to Sol's standard rates would imply about $12 per million input tokens and $60 per million output tokens, though this is an extrapolation and not a published price. inference34 On timing, OpenAI's DevDay recap says GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is "coming soon", and the launch post says it will be offered "in the coming days".56 An exact launch date was not reported.

Which chips does Ultrafast actually run on, and is it Cerebras hardware?

Launch coverage (Latent Space) describes Ultrafast as running on unspecified silicon.1 SemiAnalysis reports that Ultrafast runs on NVIDIA GPUs at low batch sizes and not on Cerebras, as relayed by @kimmonismus.2 On a podcast, the host noted that OpenAI is neither confirming nor denying a Cerebras link to Ultrafast, even though people are wondering about it.3 A Cerebras connection would not be implausible, since Cerebras has a multiyear deal to deploy 750 megawatts of its systems for OpenAI from 2026 through 2028. inference4

According to OpenAI, GPT-6.1 Sol costs one-fifth of Astra's standard input and output token prices.12 Sol's standard API prices are $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.34 Astra's own standard prices were not stated, but a fifth of Sol's rates implies about $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens. inference1567 Astra's exact standard per-token price was not reported.
