Google releases Gemini 4 Argon

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · single origin (Epoch AI)

Google DeepMind publicly released Gemini 4 Argon on October 13, after a limited release to partners on September 30, according to Epoch AI.1

Epoch AI reports that Gemini 4 Argon scores 166 on its Epoch Capabilities Index, tying Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 and just behind Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra (both 169) and GPT-6.1 Sol (168).2 Epoch describes this as Google's closest approach to the frontier since Gemini 3.1 Pro in February, and a big jump from Gemini 3.8 Flash's 157 in early September.3

By Epoch's account, Gemini 4 Argon performed best on benchmarks in knowledge, agentic tasks and image understanding, and worse in math and software engineering, where it currently ranks 8th on Epoch's math index and 6th on its software engineering index.4

The Two Minute Papers video on the release is titled "Gemini 4 Argon Is Not What I Expected" and links to a model card and safety report, but the passage gives no further view of the model. inference5
