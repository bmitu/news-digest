SEC probe of Nvidia

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
3 sources · 303 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · 2 independent origins: MarketScreener, TechCrunch

According to TechCrunch, the Wall Street Journal has reported that the SEC has begun a civil investigation of Nvidia to determine whether statements the company made publicly gave investors a false impression.1 According to TechCrunch, the report appeared on the same day Nvidia released its earnings.2 Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, while its stock has fallen more than 10% since Jensen Huang departed, TechCrunch says.34

On the earnings call, interim CEO Colette Kress said Nvidia would not immediately file its quarterly 10-Q with the SEC and gave no reason, although TechCrunch notes the company has typically filed it the same day it reports results.567 UBS analyst Timothy Arcuri wrote that management told him on a callback that the delay was related to the ongoing investigation and to whether more risk factors would need to be added to the filing.8 Arcuri cut his price target to $220 from $240.9 Investment analyst Edward Dowd called the delay a "red flag" on X.10

The SEC probe is one of several inquiries, and the sources differ on who is looking at Megaspeed: one names the Commerce Department, which it says has investigated since late 2024, while another names the Department of Justice. inference1112

Techdirt's author argues that the Wall Street Journal's coverage fails to tell readers that the SEC has been largely hollowed out under Trump.13 Techdirt says Nvidia is claiming to be investigating itself, and TechCrunch says Nvidia had not responded to its request for comment.1415
