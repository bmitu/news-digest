Mirendil releases

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Mirendil)

AI R&D startup Mirendil announced Mirendil-Base, its first major output, a pretrained LLM that it says was built by a single researcher in 3.5 months with less than $10m in compute.12 Mirendil says the figure includes all the research and small-scale experiments as well as the final training run, that the code was written from scratch on top of libraries like PyTorch and JAX, and that the model learned only from publicly available data.34

Mirendil-Base is in the 100B–500B parameter range, with larger Mirendil-Base-Large (500B–2T) about 80% through training and Mirendil-Base-XL (2T–10T) about 19% through training.567 On evals, Mirendil-Base outperforms Nemotron-550B-Base, GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base but underperforms Kimi-K3, which both the Large and XL versions exceed.89

Separately, a Latent Space newsletter notes Fern 0.1 from Mirendil, pretrained from scratch with up to 10M tokens of context, with loss still improving at 10M, and described as the smallest of three planned models.1011

Import AI calls the release less a technical breakthrough and more a proof-of-concept for how AI development could work in the future, in which a single human working in partnership with AI can build systems that would have taken teams of ten or more people to do a year ago.1213 Import AI also argues it shows a new organization can get close to the open-weight frontier from a cold start, at least for this size class, with little human effort and many AI tokens.14

Mirendil says its next model will be built partly by Mirendil models, and that while it does not think it has reached fully autonomous recursive self-improvement, it is closer than ever.1516
