The 50 questions were compiled by physicists around the world, according to Import AI's account of the paper.1 Quoting the physicist Suvrat Raju, Import AI says the initial evaluation put Astra's score in the late eighties, followed closely by Opus 5.5.2

Raju says the models, especially Astra, insisted the expert-supplied answers were wrong, and that experts found subtle errors in their calculations in multiple instances.34 As the experts revised their answers, Raju says, Astra's score rose and finally reached 98% (49/50).5 Raju also said he knew of no individual who could solve a significant fraction of the final problems without AI assistance.6

Import AI's author reads the result as the teacher becoming the student, noting that experts built a test out of problems an AI could never have read the answers to, and that the AI got a higher score by finding errors in the humans' answers.78

Elsewhere in the same newsletter roundup, Epoch's Capabilities Index put GPT-6 Astra at 166, just behind Opus 5.5 at 167, in a passage about Gemini 4 Argon.9
