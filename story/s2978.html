Qwen open-source commitment

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · single origin, unclear which

A post summarized by Latent Space's AINews says Qwen leadership publicly reaffirmed at the APSARA Conference that future Qwen 4.0 and Qwen 5.0 models will remain open source/open-weight.123 The statement was presented as contradicting rumors that Qwen4 would be the last open-weight release, or that future releases would become API-only.45 The slide, shown by Qwen Head Liu Dayi Heng under the theme "Intelligence Goes Beyond," cited 3B+ cumulative downloads, 460+ open-source models and 300,000+ derivative models.678

Commenters were mostly supportive, with one endorsing the "AI for all" philosophy attributed to Liu Dayi Heng.910 One commenter argued the open-weight releases are a deliberate market strategy, with Chinese labs competing on lower cost and openness to win global developer adoption, especially in developing markets.11 Another commenter questioned how durable the commitment is, arguing that corporate open-source promises depend on current management and business incentives rather than immutable principles.1213

Separately, a leak of apparent qwen4 model names in the Qwen Code repo was met with skepticism: a later PR reportedly removed or corrected the names, and commenters thought they were likely synthetic test examples rather than confirmed upcoming releases.1415
