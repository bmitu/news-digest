Gemini computer use on Mac

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Google)

According to TechCrunch, Google said Thursday that its Gemini app for Mac desktops is gaining the ability to operate the computer on a user's behalf.1 Google says the feature lets the assistant carry out multi-step tasks across local files, desktop apps and the web.2 Google suggests uses such as filling in forms, making presentations and listing items for sale, and also mentions organizing files into folders.34

Google says users can choose which apps Gemini may access and can watch it work through a live preview in the chat, shown in its own background window.5 As with other computer-use agents, users can pause, stop and approve actions before they happen, and they can keep using their screen and cursor meanwhile.67

The feature is rolling out in English in the US and requires the Gemini Mac app plus a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra subscription; Google did not say when it will reach other markets or platforms.89

TechCrunch characterizes Google as a little late, saying OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta have offered agentic features in their desktop apps for some time.10 The Verge notes the launch comes ahead of Apple's new restrictions on apps seeking full disk access, which are motivated by risks from AI agents with computer use, with a preview in this week's macOS 27.3 beta.1112
