Meta releases Muse Spark Open

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 new articles this week · single origin (Nathan Lambert)

Meta released Muse Spark Open, which Interconnects calls the strongest American-made open model "by quite some margin."1 Zvi Mowshowitz says Meta calls it "the most capable US open-weight model to date," which he thinks seems likely to be true.2 According to its Hugging Face model card, it is an 816B total/42B active parameter open-weights model with a 1M-token context window, built for coding and agentic work and natively multimodal.3

It scores 39 on Artificial Analysis, below the open-weight frontier of 46 held by MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Qwen-3.8, according to Mowshowitz.4 Latent Space notes that the 180B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next scores 40, slightly above Muse Spark Open despite being much smaller.5 Interconnects says the release roughly halves the gap between the best American and Chinese open models, and that Muse Spark Open should be "good enough" for the open ecosystem.67

Mowshowitz says a US open model is useful because it can be used where Chinese models would be uncomfortable to run, though he does not say he trusts Meta.8

Interconnects says some observers saw the release as a model "broken" in safety training, but it argues it was more likely a safety tune of Muse Spark 1.2.910 On Reddit-style commentary summarized by Latent Space, one commenter was skeptical, saying it looks like "just llama scaled up and trained with new data" and doubting that it matches Muse Spark 1.2 weights.1112
