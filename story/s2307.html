Jack Clark says singularity has begun

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark said in a speech at the IMF that the singularity has begun, according to Exponential View.1 MIT Technology Review says Jack Clark, Anthropic's cofounder and head of policy, gave a speech earlier this month at the IMF meeting in Bangkok, where he told the assembled economists, bankers, and lawmakers that the singularity is already here.2 Clark said: "I believe that this year we have gained convincing evidence that it has now begun."34

Exponential View points to Anthropic's claim that in August, 26% of its AI R&D was led by Claude with a human supervising, and to OpenAI posting 719 math manuscripts made by its internal model in October.56 It also cites examples of self-improving systems: Sakana AI and University of British Columbia agents gained 30 pp on SWE-bench, AlphaEvolve sped up a kernel used for training Gemini by 23% and cut Gemini's training time by 1%, and MiniMax reported a 30% gain after 100 rounds of rewriting on its M2.7 model's agent harness, skills, sampling and memory, per its internal evaluations.789

However, Exponential View is cautious about these results: in each case what was being rewritten was not the model itself but everything around it, and a September result in which an AI-built agent proved as good as a human-built one was inconclusive because the differences between runs were greater than the difference between the AI- and human-built agent results.1011 It adds that where Claude leads its own R&D, a human still supervises, and that only types 1 and 2 of five types of AI self-improvement have so far been observed.1213

The piece appeared as Anthropic filed its IPO prospectus, and its author says that Anthropic being many things to many people does not make it more trustworthy. inference1415
