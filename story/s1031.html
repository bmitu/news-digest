Anthropic Singapore office and MOU

Last scan Mon Oct 19, 23:45 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic says it is opening a new office in Singapore, its fifth in the Asia-Pacific region, and has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information.12 Kai Fong Chng, Permanent Secretary of the ministry, and Anthropic co-founder Ben Mann signed it on the sidelines of Singapore International Cyber Week, and Minister Josephine Teo and Michael Sellitto, Anthropic's Head of APAC Policy, witnessed the signing.34 A Latent Space newsletter briefly noted the office opening and the AI safety MOU, and the item was among the day's most-discussed topics there; the passages show no disputes over the announcement. inference25

The MOU sets out cooperation on AI safety, security and model evaluation, research on AI adoption and its impact, and responsible adoption across the public and private sectors.6 Anthropic says it plans to hold technical exchanges with Singapore's AI safety, security and assurance institutions on model evaluation, testing standards and incident response.7 It also says it plans to share research and data, including from the Anthropic Economic Index, and to offer technical and policy expertise as Singapore develops its AI rules.8

Anthropic reports that Singapore ranks second of 121 countries in Claude.ai usage relative to population, and that more than 1,500 GovTech Singapore engineers and technical staff have adopted Claude Code.910 The company also says Grab's BriX sales assistant, built on Claude, saves Grab's sales teams approximately 40,000 hours every quarter.11

Ben Mann says Singapore has been a leader in responsible AI and that Anthropic will partner with it to help the transition to powerful AI go well.1213 Chng says the partnership reflects the government's commitment to scaling up responsible AI adoption.14
