Muse downloads

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 1 new article this week · single origin (TechCrunch)

On Meta's third-quarter earnings call, CEO Mark Zuckerberg confirmed that more than 10 million users have adopted Muse, and said about 25% of people who set it up now use it daily.12 Zuckerberg said Muse has reached "product-market fit", that Meta will now focus on scaling it, and that Meta expects to improve its compute cost efficiency by more than 10x in the next year so it can be profitable once revenue is at scale.34

In the U.S., Sensor Tower's data shows Muse retaining 30% of users after 7 days and 23% after 14 days, versus rival averages of 19% and 16%, and daily active users hit 2.5 million on October 16.567 Sensor Tower analyst Kara Lee said only 11 other apps have hit this post-launch milestone, the most recent being Meta's Threads in July 2023, and that most of the others were mobile games.89 Meta is also spending heavily to promote the app: Sensor Tower says Muse took 39% of Meta's advertising spend in October, up from 23% in September.10

Sources differ on scale and comparisons: MIT Technology Review says Muse was downloaded 5 million times in its first month, while TechCrunch reports 10.9 million downloads in 40 days. inference1112 TechCrunch notes that ChatGPT reached about 15 million global downloads in its first 40 days versus Muse's roughly 10.9 million, but says the comparison is unfair because Muse is available only in the U.S. and Canada. inference1314
