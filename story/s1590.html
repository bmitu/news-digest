Muse downloads

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
2 sources · single origin (TechCrunch)

Meta's AI app Muse has reached 10 million U.S. downloads in its first 40 days, according to Sensor Tower data reported by TechCrunch.12 Sensor Tower analyst Kara Lee said only 11 other apps have hit such a post-launch milestone, the latest being Meta's Threads in July 2023, and that most were mobile games.345

Per Sensor Tower, Muse had a 30% U.S. retention rate after 7 days and 23% after 14 days, versus averages of 19% and 16% for its AI rivals such as ChatGPT, Claude and Grok.67 Its daily active users reached 2.5 million for the first time on October 16, a 15% week-over-week increase.8

TechCrunch reports that Muse's U.S. downloads exceed those of ChatGPT (3.7 million), Grok (500,000) and Claude (200,000) in their first 40 days, while ChatGPT led globally with around 15 million.910 TechCrunch says the global comparison isn't fair, since ChatGPT benefited from a wider reach: Muse is only available in the U.S. and Canada for now, while ChatGPT's app attracted attention from markets like Japan, Brazil and Germany.11

MIT Technology Review gives a different figure, saying Muse was downloaded 5 million times in its first month.12
