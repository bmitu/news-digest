Grokipedia v0.3 update released

Last scan Fri Oct 2, 18:55 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 1 · 1 new article on Oct 2 · 2 independent origins: SpaceXAI, The Verge

Grokipedia, SpaceXAI's AI-powered competitor to Wikipedia, got design tweaks on the day of The Verge's report as part of a v0.3 update, including a new logo and refreshed homepage and live edits page.1 SpaceXAI head of design Benji Taylor calls it a "newly refreshed Grokipedia."2

The old homepage was essentially a logo and a search bar, while the new one adds featured articles, a most-read list and a tracker of "latest edits", with book-style visuals for the featured and most-read sections.34 The live edits page makes it easier to see more recent changes at once, and article pages get a nicer format for key-facts tables and smaller line spacing, though The Verge's Jay Peters says articles otherwise look largely the same.567

The site launched as v0.1 in late October 2025 with AI-cloned Wikipedia pages, and v0.2 followed a month later.8 The Verge says the recent updates suggest renewed interest inside SpaceXAI in Grokipedia, but the company did not immediately respond to a request for comment.9
