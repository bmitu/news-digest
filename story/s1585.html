PHP RCE vulnerabilities

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
11 sources · 453 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 21 · 11 new articles this week · 4 independent origins: 404 Media, Financial Times, National Security Agency, Wiz Research

Two critical remote-code-execution flaws in core PHP, named PHP2Shell, were patched on October 21, 2026, and the US National Security Agency warned organizations to update immediately or take affected systems offline. inference123 Wiz says PHP powers 70% of all websites and that every PHP version of the last decade is affected by at least one of the flaws, going back to version 5.4.45 Wiz says the flaws stem from how PHP handles fatal errors, particularly out-of-memory errors, which can leave corrupted state that a later request reuses, leading to memory disclosure or corruption.67

OpenAI says Calif found a flaw using GPT-5.6 Sol under its Patch the Planet initiative, while Wiz independently found and reported both vulnerabilities; Wiz says it used Wiz Atlas, Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol.8910 OpenAI calls it one of the most impactful security issues in recent memory and says AI found the flaws before attackers did, though it hedges with "to our knowledge"; it also warns the same capabilities can help attackers.11121314

Reports differ on exploitation: TechCrunch, citing OpenAI, says there were no reports of exploits in the wild, but Amazon said it saw an attempt four days before disclosure and that the flaws were being broadly exploited. inference1516 Sites including 4chan, several Wikimedia services and Archive.org were temporarily taken offline to be updated, and the White House said agencies were assessing the impact.1718

Chainguard was criticized, including by the PHP Foundation, for posting fixes before the official disclosure; its CEO Dan Lorenc said the company independently found the same bugs.1920
