Perplexity $1B run rate

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Semafor)

Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas announced onstage at a Semafor event on Monday that the company had just crossed a $1 billion annual run rate.12 The newsletter Latent Space points to a report from The Information saying annual revenue was below $250M in January and had climbed past $750M by August, partly thanks to the Computer product; the newsletter's title presents this as a quadrupling during 2026. inference3 Srinivas also said Perplexity's burn rate is five times lower than that of hyperscalers, which he attributed to the startup's capital-efficient model.4

Latent Space estimates that, at usual agent-lab multiples, Perplexity would be worth roughly $30-50B, but only if the unit economics hold up.5 It notes this would be a bump on the $20B round in September 2025.6

Semafor describes Perplexity as primarily a model aggregator that routes users to models such as OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude rather than running a flagship foundational model of its own.7 Srinivas says the company is built on the idea that users do not care which model they use, only whether the work gets done.8
