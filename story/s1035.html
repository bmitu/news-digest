YouTube Music AI labels

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 08:37 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (YouTube)

YouTube is adding a "Made with AI" label to tracks that were "altered or fully generated" using AI, rolling out starting today in the YouTube Music app and expanding to the main YouTube app later.123 Tapping the label opens a menu with more information about AI's role in the track.456

YouTube says it is working with music partners and using its own detection tools to identify AI-generated tracks.78 According to TechCrunch, partners can declare AI use as Fully Gen AI, Partly Gen AI or No Gen AI, though Ars Technica says disclosure is optional. inference910 The Verge reports that without disclosure YouTube may use "other signals" to decide, and Ars Technica adds that SynthID watermarking and C2PA metadata may also be used. inference1112

TechCrunch says YouTube is focusing on disclosure rather than blanket restrictions, and AI involvement alone does not make a track ineligible for monetization.1314 Other platforms vary: Spotify launched a voluntary AI label, Deezer added a switch for filtering AI tracks, and Bandcamp banned AI music outright, according to The Verge.1516

Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam cautions that the absence of a label should not be taken as proof that something is human-made, since many AI uses leave no identifying mark.1718
