Character.AI files for bankruptcy

Last scan Mon Oct 19, 23:45 UTC
8 sources · 480 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 new articles this week · 5 independent origins: Ars Technica, Character.AI, TechCrunch, The Rundown AI, The Verge

Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection to facilitate a court-supervised sale of the company, and said it is selling some assets to Disney.123 The company says it took meaningful steps over the past year to sustain its business amid significant legal, regulatory and operational headwinds, and that its board concluded a court-supervised sale offered the best chance to maximize value for stakeholders.456 Character.AI listed estimated liabilities of $50 million to $100 million, and Ars Technica says it is possible Disney will be outbid. inference789

Disney's involvement follows its hiring of Character.AI's former CEO Karandeep Anand as chief technology officer, and Disney previously complained that Character.AI was damaging its brand and exploiting its intellectual property by allowing unauthorized chatbots of its characters.101112

The company expects the sale to finish by late November or early December and says services, including paid subscriptions, continue meanwhile, but CEO Colin Adams wrote that the platform will be wound down if no suitable bid emerges.131415

New York Attorney General Letitia James is among 40 bipartisan attorneys general who filed a notice of interest in the case, warning of regulatory issues in any sale, citing the sensitive conversations and large number of minors using the product.1617 Ars Technica notes that Character.AI's privacy policy says information including chat logs may be transferred in asset sales, and that the company declined to comment on whether sensitive data might be sold.1819

TechCrunch argues the bankruptcy shows consumer-facing AI products are hard to scale and monetize, noting that high engagement raises AI costs.2021
