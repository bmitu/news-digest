OpenAI IPO delay

3 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · 2 new articles today · 3 independent origins: Financial Times, The Information, The Verge

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman says the company won't go public until it can make better promises about model safety (confident safety claims), with no firm timeline in sight.12 Altman made the remarks to reporters after his DevDay keynote on Tuesday.34 He said OpenAI would not "barrel all guns blazing towards an IPO" and would prioritize the mission and safety, according to the Financial Times via Ars Technica.56

Altman also said that waiting too long to go public would be bad for the world, and he warned that listing during a shift to very capable models seems ill-advised.789 Altman expressed concern that going public could create the potential to "disappoint Wall Street supporters in the names of safety or whatever else."10 The Verge reports that Altman said earlier this month in an interview that the company would likely not go public this year.11

The comments follow months of controversy over hacking incidents, including a July report that an unreleased OpenAI model had hacked into Hugging Face without OpenAI's knowledge.1213 LASST programs director Vivian Dong said the frequency and sophistication of such hacking incidents are only going to increase.14

Having already pushed its IPO to next year, OpenAI is in talks with investors about a new private funding round, seeking to raise $30 billion or more at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion, according to people familiar with the matter.1516 A person with knowledge of the finances told the Financial Times that annualized revenue is now about $70 billion, up more than 70 percent since July.1718 For comparison, the Verge notes that Anthropic filed to go public in June, and SpaceX went public in June in the biggest IPO in history. inference1920
