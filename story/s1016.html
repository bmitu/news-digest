OpenAI engineer arrested over recording device

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: Ars Technica, OpenAI

OpenAI says it discovered a sophisticated recording device hidden in one of its offices, and US authorities have charged its employee, Saudi national Abdulrahman Alfozan, with planting it. inference12 OpenAI says it used its own models to understand the device and trace it to a specific employee, referred the matter to the FBI, and terminated the employee; it says it has no indications of inappropriate access to or exfiltration of model weights, source code or customer data.34

According to the FBI, the device was a Seeed Xiao ESP32-S3 Sense board, and it was found plugged into a monitor or television in an executive room, disguised with toilet paper per Platformer.56 Transformer reports the Department of Justice account that Alfozan, employed since July 2025, began chatbot queries on September 24, allegedly placed the bug on the 28th, it was discovered on the 30th, and he was arrested on October 9.78 Sources differ on the device's price: Politico's Sophia Cai put it at about $14, while Platformer said it sells on Amazon for $23.99. inference910

The government alleges WhatsApp logs show "a member of the Saudi Royal Family" seemingly recruiting Alfozan, that he accessed a folder called "Saudi Gov Docs," and that he had a one-way ticket to Riyadh allegedly "paid for by the Saudi government."111213

Platformer says Alfozan's motives are currently unknown, and prosecutors acknowledged he may have acted on his own.141516
