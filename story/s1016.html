OpenAI engineer arrested over recording device

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
4 sources · 653 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: The San Francisco Standard, U.S. Department of Justice

OpenAI engineer Abdulrahman Alfozan, a 35-year-old Saudi national, was arrested on a Friday and charged with using or attempting to use a device to intercept oral communications, after OpenAI said it found a "sophisticated recording device" hidden in one of its offices. inference123 Federal prosecutors say the device was plugged into a USB port on the back of a television in an executive conference room, wrapped in toilet paper, installed around Sept. 28 and found Sept. 30, with encrypted contents.456 FBI agent Lydia Liang's affidavit describes the device as a Chinese-made Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense that had been customized to obfuscate stored data.78

Prosecutors say Alfozan was not authorized to enter the room, but surveillance footage shows him near it on multiple dates before the discovery.910 The complaint alleges that on Oct. 5 he appeared to look behind the television and left a bag unattended in another room, where security found his wallet, ID and a second similar recording device.111213

Prosecutors allege a chatbot account tied to his spouse, which shared identifiers with his own, submitted prompts about building, installing, obscuring and remotely accessing a recording device, including how to "build a continuous audio recorder" and "encrypt the SD card," and on Sept. 27 and 28 asked about the specific device later found.141516

OpenAI says it gradually restricted his access and timed his firing with the FBI's arrest.1718 If convicted, Alfozan faces up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine.
