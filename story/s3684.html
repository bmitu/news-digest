Oura acquires Counsel Health

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Oura)

Oura announced on Tuesday that it is acquiring AI-native primary care company Counsel Health for $625 million.12 The deal comes weeks after Oura indefinitely postponed an IPO of up to $2.2 billion, citing uncertainty in the IPO market.3 Oura also reported revenue up 153% year over year to $533 million for the quarter ended September 30, and about $1.75 billion for fiscal 2026, up 92%.45

Counsel's platform pairs a chat-based medical AI with access to licensed physicians, and it was integrated into the Oura app in June.67 The companies plan to expand their existing partnership across preventative care, acute care, and specialty and chronic care.8 Oura CEO Tom Hale says Oura helps members understand their physiology and that with Counsel they can act on their health, from quick questions and prescriptions to chronic care and referrals.9

Counsel CEO Muthu Alagappan will become Oura's chief clinical AI officer, joining with the company's 70 employees, according to Bloomberg as cited by TechCrunch.10 Oura says Counsel will keep serving enterprise partners, aiming to extend its care model beyond Oura's membership, and the deal is expected to close before the end of 2027.1112 Counsel had raised a $25 million Series A a year earlier, and PitchBook estimates its total funding at $36 million; STAT likewise says it had disclosed $36 million.1314

TechCrunch notes that rivals such as RingConn and Ultrahuman are emerging, pressuring Oura to keep innovating, while STAT frames the deal as reflecting a push to connect wearables to clinical care and a belief that AI chatbots will matter in health care.1516
