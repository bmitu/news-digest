QGIS bans AI contributions

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
2 sources · 647 points on Hacker News · single origin (QGIS)

QGIS merged QEP428, "No AI contributions permitted", a proposal that replaces the project's earlier AI tool policy with a total ban on AI-assisted code in the qgis/qgis repository.12 The proposal passed its discussion period on Oct 5, moved to voting, and was merged into the QGIS-Enhancement-Proposals master branch on Oct 7.3 The ban replaces QEP-408, Even Rouault's human-in-the-loop policy adapted from LLVM's AI Tool Use Policy, which required reviewing all LLM output, transparency via Assisted-by trailers, and banned unreviewed agent comments.4 Under the new text, contributors found to have used an AI, LLM or agent-based workflow will be subject to code reversion and contributor rights sanctioning.5

In the PR discussion, Nyall Dawson said the intent is to start with a closed door and selectively open it case by case later, for example for AI as an autocomplete replacement or for translations.6 Dawson also said the earlier policy's process clearly did not happen in reality, and that the project had already lost at least one extremely high value contributor as a result.7
