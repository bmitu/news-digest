Tencent Advanced Chip Lease

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 6 · 1 new article this week · single origin, unclear which

The Financial Times reportedly says Tencent leased about 100,000 advanced chips in Oracle's Southeast Asian data centers for roughly $7 billion over five years.1 Zvi Mowshowitz writes that the White House allowed Tencent to lease the 100,000 chips from Oracle.2

Mowshowitz puts the lease at about 125k H100 equivalents, or roughly 12 times the compute used for Kimi K3.3

Mowshowitz questions the decision, asking why the lease is being allowed.4 He cites Joe Weisenthal's argument that if having the best AI were a matter of national security, the government would act that way more broadly.5 Mowshowitz adds that letting massive chip rentals happen, while not brain-draining AI talent or trying hard to supercharge the electrical grid, suggests the US does not actually prioritize this.6

Separately, the Latent Space newsletter reports that US prosecutors charged a California reseller with smuggling more than $300 million of GPU servers to China.7
