OpenAI models on Google Cloud

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
5 sources · 325 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 21 · 3 new articles this week · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI and Google Cloud announced a partnership to bring OpenAI's frontier models and Codex agentic workflows into Google Cloud's Model Garden.12 Ars Technica reports that the full GPT 6.1 suite (Astra, Sol and Luna) will launch on Google Cloud, and that Google says all three are rolling out now in private preview for select enterprise customers.34 OpenAI says general availability is planned in the coming weeks, and that Microsoft remains its primary cloud partner.567

Customers will buy the models through the Google Cloud Marketplace, and enterprises with Google Cloud spend commitments will be able to draw those commitments down for the purchases, according to OpenAI.89 OpenAI models are also due to come to Google Antigravity and the Gemini Agent, though Ars Technica notes Google has not specified whether all three GPT models will appear in Antigravity.1011

Google says it is now the only large cloud provider offering access to all three leading closed LLMs, and Ars Technica notes Claude was already available in Google Cloud.1213

Greg Brockman, OpenAI's president and co-founder, says the partnership makes its most capable models and Codex available where customers are already building.14 Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud's chief executive, says the focus is on a broad selection of models so customers can choose the right one for each job, helping deliver optimal quality and lower costs.15

Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam says bringing GPT models to Google Cloud could be a big deal for enterprise, and that the OpenAI deal offers another alternative to Gemini for those not feeling the Argon love; he also says Gemini 4 Argon burns through tokens at an astonishing rate.1617
