X Subscription bundle

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 08:37 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (xAI)

SpaceX Super Intelligence (SpaceXSI) is bundling all its subscriptions, including X Premium, Grok, and Cursor, into a single plan called "The X Subscription," TechCrunch reported on October 13, 2026.1 TechCrunch says the bundling is a bid to attract more subscribers, with the idea that the combined offering could potentially attract more subscribers than any single subscription, given the competitive paid AI subscription market where SpaceXSI faces rivals like OpenAI, Anthropic, and others.2

The bundle gives access to Grok for AI-powered chat (with support for voice, images, video, and the API), Grok's agentic AI bot, Cursor for AI coding, and X Premium paid features like the blue verified checkmark, creator tools, and fewer ads.34

There are five tiers: free, Plus ($8/month), Premium ($30/month), Super ($100/month), and Ultra ($200/month), with AI usage caps rising at higher tiers.5 The free plan offers only limited chat and agent usage, and Premium or higher is required for the latest Grok models.6

The company noted that the comparable plan costs the same or less for existing X Premium+ subscribers, who can upgrade from their X subscription settings or keep their current plan.78 However, the upgrade option will roll out gradually, so customers need to watch their billing settings.9

Separately, a Latent Space newsletter roundup noted that Cursor joined the X Subscription, with paid plans sharing one usage pool with Grok Build and third-party models in Cursor moving to API pricing, citing a post from @cursor_ai.10
