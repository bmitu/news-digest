X Subscription bundle

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
3 sources · single origin (xAI)

SpaceX Super Intelligence (SpaceXSI), which TechCrunch says is the company now known by that name, is bundling its subscriptions, including X Premium, Grok and Cursor, into a single plan called "The X Subscription."1 TechCrunch says the aim is to attract more subscribers in a competitive paid AI market where SpaceXSI faces rivals such as OpenAI and Anthropic.23 xAI says it will make the plan available to everyone globally over the next few days, and that new customers can sign up starting today.45

There are five tiers: Free, Plus at $8 a month, Premium at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200, with AI usage caps rising at higher tiers.6 xAI says the Premium tier carries the benefits of SuperGrok, Cursor Pro and X Premium+, which previously cost $90 a month combined, and that the Super and Ultra tiers include 15x and 50x the Plus plan's usage.78 xAI also says paid plans share one pool of Grok model usage across Grok and Cursor, and that each plan includes at least twice as much coding usage with its models as the comparable Cursor plan.910 Third-party models remain available in Cursor on Premium and above, but xAI says they are billed separately at API list prices through top-ups.11

A Latent Space newsletter roundup also noted that Cursor joined the X Subscription, citing a post from Cursor's account that drew about 3.9K engagements.1213

Existing subscribers can keep their current plans or switch, and xAI says a switch brings a prorated refund for the old plan.14 xAI says it will eventually move remaining customers to the new plans, but there is no timeline yet and it will give advance notice.15
