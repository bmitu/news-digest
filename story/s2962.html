OpenAI funding round shortfall

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 190 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 6 · 4 new articles this week · single origin (24/7 Wall St.)

OpenAI has raised about $11 billion in new funding at a $1.25 trillion valuation, which is below the $1.4 trillion it reportedly first sought.1 Sources with knowledge of the transaction say roughly $11 billion of fresh capital had landed in OpenAI's accounts as of Friday, November 6, 2026. According to 24/7 Wall St., the remainder might push the overall haul up to $20 billion.23 Chief financial officer Sarah Friar confirmed the $1.25 trillion pre-money figure in a TV interview, while OpenAI declined to comment on the funding itself.45

Press reports said OpenAI first aimed for $30 billion or more at a $1.4 trillion valuation, and two investors put the initial ask at $1.5 trillion.6 Friar disputed that account, saying the higher figure began with a media report and did not come from OpenAI.7 Friar further characterized the valuation as nearly 50% higher than in March, when OpenAI brought in $122 billion at an $852 billion valuation. 24/7 Wall St. describes the approximately 46.7% increase as a rough estimate, since her number is pre-money and it is unclear what basis March's valuation used.89

Following a selloff in AI shares, 24/7 Wall St. reports that the round came in both smaller and at a lower price, although OpenAI maintains that its business is speeding up. The outlet also points out that the second tranche has yet to close, and that neither the lead investors nor the terms have been made public.1011 24/7 Wall St. also argues the shortfall matters to Microsoft and NVIDIA because both depend on OpenAI's ability to fund its plans, with NVIDIA seen as more exposed than Microsoft.1213

A Semafor commentary reads the episode as the start of the end of the AI trade, saying OpenAI appears likely to fall far short of its $30 billion target.1415 Zvi Mowshowitz's newsletter noted the raise as near the low end of Anthropic's valuation range, while 24/7 Wall St. reports some investors now value Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion, down from roughly $2 trillion. inference1617
