DeepMind Genie 4 launch

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 3 new articles this week · single origin (Google DeepMind)

Google DeepMind says it is rolling out a new Project Genie experience, including first-time access to its Genie 4 world model, to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers starting the day of its announcement.123 Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist.4

Google describes Genie 4 as its most realistic and interactive world model yet, letting users edit a world in real time by typing a new prompt mid-session.5 Google says it is the first time the visuals are paired with real-time native audio, from ambient soundscapes to character dialogue.6

Also included in the update are an agent for Project Genie that assists people in shaping their ideas, plus a shared gallery where users can find, browse and remix creations made by others.7 Genie 3 remains available and was upgraded, with sessions of up to 3 minutes per generation, up from 60 seconds, plus 360-degree panorama grounding.8910

Google says all generated video and audio carries SynthID watermarking and that inputs and outputs are safety-screened, while acknowledging open challenges such as camera latency, complex physical interactions, real-time audio precision and long-term scene permanence.1112

Secondary coverage was brief: Latent Space listed the Genie 4 release among media news alongside Google's Gemini 4 Neon launch, and Zvi Mowshowitz's newsletter only noted that Genie 4 exists.1314
