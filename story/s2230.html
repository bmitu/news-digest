DeepMind Genie 4 launch

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
6 sources · 2 independent origins: Google DeepMind, TechCrunch

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, its new world model for generating interactive simulated worlds, releasing it as a research preview for trusted testers.123 Google says Genie 4 is its first real-time world model to generate audio natively, including ambient sound and interactive dialogue that adapts to user inputs.456 Google says Gemini 3.8 Flash acts as a guiding agent that breaks goals into steps and checks incoming frames, and that Genie 4 simulates physics such as aerodynamics and collisions.789

TechCrunch reports Genie 4 can maintain a consistent scene for up to two minutes, double its predecessor's limit, while The Verge likewise says experiences run about two minutes, up from one minute for Genie 3. inference1011 The Verge reports Google is pitching Genie 4 for pre-visualization for filmmakers, prototyping game ideas, and making training scenarios for robots, and TechCrunch reports a research experiment in which a Gemini 4 Argon model broke down tasks into steps that Genie 4 simulated in real time.121314 However, Google acknowledges limits: details and object permanence can drift as simulations run longer, and complex physical interactions, audio accuracy and legible text remain active research areas.151617

Access is expected to expand to Google AI Ultra subscribers through Project Genie and to early access partners via the Gemini API and AI Studio, with a waitlist open, though Ars Technica says there is no specific timeline and API pricing is not yet available.18192021 Ars Technica notes that Ultra plans start at $100 per month and that Google may eventually expand access to AI Pro and AI Plus plans.2223

Early-access users described Genie 4 as "not flawless" but a large leap over Genie 3, per a Latent Space roundup, and TechCrunch places the launch amid a crowded, consolidating world-model field including Runway's GWM Worlds 2. inference2425
