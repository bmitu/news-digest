DeepMind Genie 4 launch

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 3 new articles this week · single origin (Google DeepMind)

Google DeepMind has published details on Genie 4, a new iteration of its Genie world model, which Google describes as bringing real-time interactive world simulation with native audio.12 Google says Genie 4 is its first real-time world model to generate audio natively, and that it embeds Gemini 3.8 Flash as a guiding agent in the loop that tracks goals step by step and adapts its plan.345 Google also says Genie 4 advances its ability to create safe training environments for robots and AI agents as a crucial step toward AGI.6

Access is currently a research preview for some trusted testers; it will eventually come to Google AI Ultra subscribers, and is coming soon to Google AI Ultra in Project Genie and to Gemini API and AI Studio early-access partners.78

Import AI's commentary, however, calls the technology currently quite janky, citing robotic voices and dreamlike inconsistency in the environments, but says it points toward generating endless entertainment and potentially data by prompting custom worlds.910
