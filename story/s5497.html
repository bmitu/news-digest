Data processing incidents on inference providers

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
2 sources · 289 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 22 · 1 new article today · single origin (OpenRouter)

OpenRouter says security researchers reported earlier this week that certain inference providers had data compromised or showed evidence that their data policies were not being upheld.1 OpenRouter states that the findings showed no breach or unauthorized access of OpenRouter itself.2

In response, OpenRouter says it off-boarded three providers, removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention (ZDR) eligibility, and notified all potentially affected customers.3456 OpenRouter says the removals covered providers flagged by the researchers plus further endpoints taken out as a precaution.7 OpenRouter says it is adapting the researchers' testing for ongoing use, verifying network paths for possible forwarding of requests to third parties, and requiring assurances before restoring ZDR eligibility.8910

OpenRouter lists provider announcements from Novita (about MiniMax requests), Phala, and Together (prompt leakage in June and July of 2026), and notes that not all affected providers have posted publicly.1112

The OECD's AI Incidents Monitor, in an AI-generated entry, describes the case as an AI incident in which user prompts and sensitive data were accessed by unauthorized third parties, a framing stronger than OpenRouter's own account of the findings. inference132 The OECD entry says the incident is a realized breach with concrete evidence of stolen data, and it notes that its monitor's content should not be taken as official OECD views.1415
