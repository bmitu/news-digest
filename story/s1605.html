Jack Clark's IMF Per Jacobsson lecture

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 1 new article this week · single origin (Jack Clark)

Zvi Mowshowitz's newsletter describes Jack Clark as spending over an hour telling the IMF the singularity has begun, in a talk called 'Inside the Event Horizon', and notes he brought a lot of slides.1 Clark says he thinks it is evident that we are in the early stages of the singularity, while acknowledging others may quibble over details.2 He says the speech was inspired by AI beginning to operate at the frontier of human science, sometimes autonomously, and he says that in 2026 there are first signs AI systems may be capable of doing science at the frontier.34

Clark says his focus was on how humanity can share in AI's benefits if it exercises foresight and restraint, and he calls for unusual and serious rules for the technology and the companies building it.56 He also says society will need to go slowly and deliberately on developing the technology, and he raises choices about how much humans should understand of what AI systems do.78

The roon account writes that Clark is right, that limited superintelligence is already here in broad domains, and that an intelligence explosion could happen within the next year, possibly a controlled one.910
