JPMorgan Chase account access outage

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 1 new article today · single origin (Semafor)

Customers of major US banks reported difficulty accessing their accounts on Monday, while United Airlines also reported a widespread system outage.12 According to a JPMorgan Chase spokesperson speaking to the Associated Press, the disruption stemmed from a short-lived software bug affecting a hardware setup housed on the company's own premises, and it had no connection to a cyberattack.34 United said it quickly resolved its own outage and clarified that it was not a cybersecurity-related issue.5

The Verge placed the JPMorgan banking outage among several recent failures, including a nationwide FAA ground stop and a multi-day Outlook outage.6 The Verge said the recent outages were not believed to be caused by cyberattacks, after correcting a typo that had originally stated the opposite.78
