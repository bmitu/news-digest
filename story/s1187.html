Agent access blocks on Muse and Doubao

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Semafor)

At present, Amazon blocks Meta's Muse and other consumer-facing agents from reaching its site. According to Semafor, though, the company continues to hold discussions with Meta and similar firms on ways to make the site available to shoppers who wish to use such agents.1 TechCrunch reports that the block prevents AI agents from browsing Amazon's catalog and making purchases on behalf of their users.2 Jassy said Amazon would open the site to agents if it could find a "good exchange of value" and a positive customer experience, which he said is not happening at the moment.3

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, speaking at The New York Times DealBook Summit, predicted that AI agents will reshape the internet's economics and affect businesses built on advertising and "human web" traffic.4 Nadella said that "if you don't accept the agent, somebody else will."5 Discussing Amazon's block, Nadella suggested that companies protecting existing business models could lose out to more agent-friendly rivals, giving the example that Shopify could gain an advantage if it allowed agents while Amazon kept blocking them.67 Nadella's view that agent-blocking is a competitive risk contrasts with Jassy's position that Amazon will open up only when the exchange of value and customer experience are right. inference58
