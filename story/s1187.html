Agent access blocks on Muse and Doubao

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 independent origins: SemiAnalysis, The Verge

The Verge reports that users of Meta's Muse, xAI's Grok, OpenAI's Dots and Instinct have reported blocks on top websites in recent weeks.1 Meta's Muse agent has reportedly been blocked by Amazon, which said its "continued access" violates its site policies, and users of several other AI agents have reported blocks on major websites.23 SemiAnalysis, as summarized by Latent Space, says Jefferies found about a third of tested US sites block Muse, and that in China 155 apps opted out of ByteDance's Doubao phone agent.45

Yelp told TechCrunch it only allows agents that pay for its data licensing program, Delta Air Lines is preventing them from booking tickets, and JPMorgan Chase and Charles Schwab confirmed they block agents from accessing accounts over security concerns.67

Meta responded in a post that "Turning away a personal agent means turning away the customer behind it," and The Verge reports the company has partnered with retailers like Walmart, Gap, Sephora, Best Buy, and others, allowing Muse to shop on their sites.89 The Verge notes that not all failures may be deliberate bans: Reddit reports suggest Google is blocking some Muse accounts over "suspicious activity," and some users had checkout issues at Walmart despite the partnership.10

Chris Canal, CEO of EquiStamp, says the blocks affect the perceived value of AI companies, and that anti-bot safeguards built to stop scams also catch agents.1112
