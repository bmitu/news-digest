Harness² test-time harness evolution

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · 2 independent origins: Akshay Pachaar, Google

Google Research has open-sourced Harness², a framework that lets an agent edit its own harness (system prompt, skills, hooks, memory) while the model weights stay frozen.12 As described, an editor agent proposes candidate edits, a composer diffs trajectories against a baseline, and a reflector saves lessons for future tasks, with no grader needed.34 Reported results are that Gemini 3.5 Flash gained 8.4 points on WorkBuddy-Bench and 7.5 on JobBench, and that skills and scripts make up about 50% of edits.56 The project is released under Apache 2.0, with a repo at google-research/harness2 and links to a paper, docs and benchmark guides.78

Reddit commenters were cautiously interested but raised two concerns: self-refinement may consume substantial context budget, especially for local models, and benchmark gains may reflect harness-level overfitting rather than robust capability improvement.9 One commenter said the extra context cost may be manageable for hosted models with 1M-token windows but limiting for local deployments with smaller context sizes.10 Another commenter framed the main uncertainty as whether Harness² is a meaningful agent-systems advance or mostly another evaluation-optimization layer.11
