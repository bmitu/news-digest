OpenAI data center community commitments

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
4 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced a National Community Compact and a Compute Campus Policy Blueprint, pledging that every future campus it owns and operates will have a local compact, including a promise not to use non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with public officials.123 The NDA pledge keeps a carveout for "narrow protections covering legitimate security information, trade secrets, or proprietary or competitively sensitive information."45 Platformer reports the no-NDA promise follows similar commitments this year from Microsoft, Amazon and Google, and The Verge notes Amazon announced in October it would stop using NDAs with government agencies.67

OpenAI says it will pay the full cost of the electricity it uses and the infrastructure needed to serve it, and provide financial assurances so other customers are protected if a project is delayed, reduced or canceled.89 The company also cites a $4.8 million grant for free school meals for all 14,000 students in Effingham County, Georgia, and a joint $6 million childcare commitment with SB Energy in Pike County, Ohio.1011

OpenAI's blueprint also asks federal, state and local policymakers to set rules, saying voluntary company commitments are only a starting point.1213 The Verge and The Hill tie the announcement to the midterm elections, in which voters showed opposition to data centers; The Hill says OpenAI appears to be rebranding data centers as "compute campuses".1415 Platformer notes that 40 of 41 data center-related ballot measures across 10 states went against data centers, and argues OpenAI's call for federal regulation could benefit tech companies given the scale of voter opposition.1617

Brad Carson, president of Americans for Responsible Innovation, criticized the compact on X, saying it applies only to campuses OpenAI owns and operates while OpenAI mostly leases from providers like Oracle and SB Energy, and that it does not say OpenAI will stop seeking tax abatements.181920
