Trump signs AI rebranding executive order

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 19:31 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · 1 new article today · single origin (White House)

Before the signing, President Donald Trump said he would sign a document that day renaming artificial intelligence, saying it is not artificial and claiming everyone agreed.1 He then signed an executive order officially rebranding AI as "super intelligence," according to TechCrunch.234 Zvi Mowshowitz writes that Trump claims the lab leaders signed off on the renaming.5 Trump later announced a Super Intelligence Force, which he said will be led by national intelligence director Jay Clayton.6

The order came in the same week the White House got nearly every major tech CEO, including Mark Zuckerberg, Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk and Anthropic's Dario Amodei, to sign an AI safety pledge that Trump called "morally binding."78 TechCrunch's weekend coverage describes that pledge as non-binding and says it contained a typo, and that the executives seemingly embraced the "super intelligence" term with some stumbles.9 Mowshowitz notes that Microsoft and Amazon were present but did not visibly sign, and that it is unclear whether they declined or were not considered worthy of inclusion.10

TechCrunch reported that Slovenia's .si domain saw a surge in registrations after the order.11 Adam Cochran says he believes the order was another criminal plot to enrich the Trump family, and that insiders seem to have profited millions from .si domain names before Trump's Truth Social posts.12
