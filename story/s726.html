Meta Muse agent launch and updates

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
3 sources · 3 independent origins: Meta, TechCrunch, The Verge

Meta announced on a Wednesday that its Muse assistant is now available in a dedicated iPad app, just a month after launching on iPhone, according to TechCrunch.12 Microsoft's Pavan Davuluri also announced that Muse will get a native Windows app, with no timeline given.3

Meta has also added retail partners including Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, Walmart and Wayfair, which let the agent complete transactions on users' behalf.4

Sensor Tower estimates Muse has topped 6.6 million installs since launching on iOS and Android on September 8, and The Verge says it spent several weeks as the top free app in Apple's App Store.56
