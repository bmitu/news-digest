Edge0 open models

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · 345 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Edge0, Samuel Zeng

Edge0 has announced that it is releasing Edge0-145B and Edge0-120B as open source, together with its unified inference framework and tooling for deployment. The company describes Edge0-145B as the first open-source AI model of 145 billion parameters able to run on a device.12 According to Edge0, the model delivers coding, reasoning and agentic abilities comparable to top cloud-hosted models, and it does so on a personal computer.3

Edge0 reports that the 145B model runs on a compatible laptop with peak memory of 29GB and decoding above 80 tokens per second, based on internal testing.45 Edge0 says the model scores a 72.8 mean across six benchmarks, against 71.7 for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, which it estimates needs about 70GB for standard 4-bit deployment.67 Edge0 attributes the efficiency to shared matrices across expert groups, with only about 10B of the 145B parameters active per step, plus tiered loading across GPU, system memory and SSD.8910

The figures differ somewhat between sources: Edge0's page gives 29GB and 80+ tokens/s in one place and 28GB in another, while Latent Space relays a developer claim of about 29GB and 75+ tok/s. inference1112 Edge0 also says the measurements on its page apply to different models and hardware.13 Latent Space relays a claim from the developer, credited to Samuel Zeng, that the model scored 86.5% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, while Claude Opus 4.6 scored 78.2%. The outlet emphasizes that these figures come from the vendor.1415

Edge0 concedes some limitations. Devices with very little compute can't presently run a 145B model locally, and both memory that persists across devices and searches that depend on up-to-date information still rely on cloud services.1617 No independent evaluation of Edge0-145B was reported, so the performance claims rest on Edge0's own testing and a developer's claims relayed by Latent Space. inference515
