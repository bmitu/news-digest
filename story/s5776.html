Unverified factoring rumor

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 25 · single origin (Scott Aaronson)

An unverified rumor holds that OpenAI or Anthropic can factor in polynomial time, and Latent Space reports that Scott Aaronson has reportedly heard "many, many rumors" to that effect.1

A November 26 CryptoPotato headline described Aaronson as "Virtually Certain" that one of the two companies holds cryptography "Bombshells", while The Next Web's headline the same day credited him with saying AI labs are sitting on "one or more bombshells". inference23 Coverage varies in tone: Next Big Future's headline speaks of "Credible Rumors" that factoring has been solved, whereas FinanceFeeds' headline says Aaronson holds that RSA is not currently at risk. inference45

The OECD.AI incident monitor, in text marked as AI-generated, says the rumored integer-factorization advances could endanger the RSA encryption protecting digital and financial systems.67 According to the OECD.AI monitor, no confirmed incident or direct harm has occurred, and no actual cryptographic breach has been reported.89 Even so, it categorizes the issue as an "AI Hazard" in its assessment, on the grounds that talk of AI labs possessing breakthroughs of this kind, along with credible cautions from experts, points to harm plausibly arising in the future.101112

Igor Babuschkin argues that RSA faces a nearer threat from a classical algorithm discovered by a large language model than from Shor.13
