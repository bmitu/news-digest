Wave of tech outages blamed on patching

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
7 sources · 6 independent origins: CNBC, Semafor, TechCrunch, The Atlantic, The Hill, The Washington Sun

Trump administration officials told lawmakers that a string of recent U.S. tech outages came from simultaneous patching and upgrading of legacy systems rather than cyberattacks, according to The Hill.1 The outages reportedly hit banks, Microsoft, Apple, government agencies, an FAA ground stop and the NSA's classified network.23

Several experts say AI is part of the story indirectly: it finds vulnerabilities quickly, which pushes organizations to roll out emergency patches that may not be fully tested.4 Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said patches now have to move instantly from availability to production because flaws are being exploited in the wild, which he said will create instability.5 Nick Carroll of Nightwing said companies once had a very lengthy window to test emergency patches, but that window is drying up.6

Not everyone accepts a clean AI-patching story: Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton blamed patching but not AI, and the FAA pointed to a router in Arkansas and a Texas radar facility.78 The Atlantic notes the FAA ground stop does not appear to have been caused by AI-related patching, while Techdirt's writer guesses that corporate layoffs removing expertise may be a deeper cause, and calls it only a guess. inference910 Gary Marcus argues that AI appears partly responsible, citing vibe coding and unrestricted AI agents, while acknowledging that how much is malice versus incompetence is unknown.1112

Politically, the episode led to the departure of National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross, with Semafor's sources disputing whether he was fired or resigned.13 Mullin's public comments drew sharp criticism, with Techdirt mocking his account of a tech contact blaming AI for the outages.14 The Atlantic argues Trump's message to "relax" understates the risk, and Semafor reports a bipartisan Senate push for AI cybersecurity legislation. inference1516
