ChatGPT Pro 500 plan launch

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 03:07 UTC
2 sources · 218 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced Pro 500, a new $500/month ChatGPT Pro plan that includes access to Astra Ultrafast.1 The launch was part of OpenAI's DevDay 2026 recap, where OpenAI says Pro 500 offers its highest usage allowance at 25 times the ChatGPT Plus allowance and is available now.2 OpenAI describes Ultrafast as a premium speed tier offering up to 8× faster token generation (300 tokens per second) in Codex and up to 6x in the API, with GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast available on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans in ChatGPT Work and Codex.34 Among Pro plans, Ultrafast is only on Pro 500, and it draws on the plan's included usage first and then on the user's credit balance; at launch, buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock it.56

Pro 200 is again open to new subscriptions at $200 a month, but OpenAI says new subscriptions that aren't eligible for grandfathering get a lower usage allowance than before, to reflect its "increasingly efficient models."7 Existing Pro 200 subscribers who were active at the eligibility cutoff or in the seven days before it can keep their previous allowance through Oct 29, 2026, after which they move to the lower allowance at the same $200 price.89

Latent Space's AINews digest reports the plans were re-tiered to Plus 1x, Pro 100 5x, Pro 200 10x and Pro 500 25x.10 The digest says this roughly halves the old Pro 200's value and drew heavy backlash, a characterization that comes from the digest rather than from OpenAI.11

Why did OpenAI cut the Pro 200 allowance, and is 'increasingly efficient models' the real reason?

OpenAI says new Pro 200 subscriptions carry a lower usage allowance than before "to reflect our increasingly efficient models," while the price stays at $200 a month.1 Existing Pro 200 subscribers whose subscription was active at the eligibility cutoff or during the seven days before it keep the previous allowance through Oct 29, 2026 while they have an active Pro 200 subscription, and then move to the lower one.2 The cut came with a re-tiering of plans to Plus 1x, Pro 100 5x, Pro 200 10x and a new Pro 500 at 25x, which Latent Space says roughly halves the old Pro 200's value and drew heavy backlash.3 There is some support for the efficiency claim: OpenAI pitches GPT-6.1 Sol as near-Astra intelligence at a fifth of the price, and Artificial Analysis puts it about 1 point below Astra on its Intelligence Index at $0.72 versus $3.26 per task. inference456 Still, the efficiency explanation is OpenAI's own, and it also launched a $500 Pro 500 tier with the highest allowance and exclusive Ultrafast access, so whether efficiency is the only reason is not established. inference78 OpenAI gave no other reason for the cut, such as cost pressure or demand, so no independent confirmation of the motive was reported.
