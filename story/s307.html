ChatGPT Pro 500 plan launch

3 sources · 210 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · 3 new today · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced a new $500/month ChatGPT Pro plan, Pro 500, which includes Astra Ultrafast, and reopened Pro 200 to new subscriptions.1 The Verge reported on Sep 29 that OpenAI is adding a $500/month ChatGPT Pro tier with the company's highest usage limits and access to its Ultrafast mode, and that it reopened its $200/month tier with new usage limits.23 OpenAI says Pro 500 offers its highest usage allowance, at 25 times the ChatGPT Plus allowance, and is available now.4

OpenAI says Ultrafast offers up to 8× faster token generation (300 tokens per second) in Codex and up to 6x in the API, and that GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast is available in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans.56 Among Pro plans, Ultrafast is only on Pro 500, and OpenAI says buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock it at launch.78

OpenAI says new Pro 200 subscriptions that aren't eligible for grandfathering get a lower usage allowance than before, to reflect its increasingly efficient models, while the price stays $200.910 Existing Pro 200 subscribers active at the eligibility cutoff or in the seven days before it can keep their previous allowance through Oct 29, 2026, after which they move to the lower allowance.11

Latent Space's AINews digest describes the plans as re-tiered to Plus 1x, Pro 100 5x, Pro 200 10x and Pro 500 25x, and says this roughly halves the old Pro 200's value, which drew heavy backlash.12 The digest also notes that Tibo clarified how Pro $200 usage is recalculated, and that a post on it drew 27.9K engagement. inference13

How much faster is Ultrafast than standard GPT-6 Astra, and what does it cost in the API?

OpenAI says Ultrafast, its premium speed tier, delivers up to 8× faster token generation in Codex (300 tokens per second) and up to 6× faster in the API.1 Per Latent Space's summary of the launch, Ultrafast pricing is 6x, i.e. $60/$300 per M for Astra.2 The API speedup is thus roughly matched by the price multiplier, so Ultrafast costs about six times as much for up to six times the speed. inference31 GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast is available now in the API, and in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans.4

Does the new Dots agent count against Pro plan usage limits, and does Pro 500 change that?

OpenAI says conversations with your dot do not count toward ChatGPT usage limits.1 However, tasks the dot hands off to Codex or ChatGPT Work do count toward usage limits as usual.2 OpenAI's Tibo made the same distinction, saying the primary dot's direct work does not draw on plan usage while Codex tasks it spawns do.3 The first dot is included in Pro (and Business Premium) at no extra cost, and the plan also includes an allowance for deeper work, with extended limits for the first month after launch.45 Pro 500 does not appear to change the dot rules, since it is described as the plan with the highest usage allowance, at 25 times Plus, plus Ultrafast access, and the dot rules are stated for Pro generally. inference61 Still, Pro 500's bigger allowance does mean more room for the Codex and ChatGPT Work tasks a dot starts. inference786

When will GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast be available, and will Ultrafast ever reach Pro 100 or Pro 200 users?

OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is "coming soon" and, in its model announcement, that it will be offered "in the coming days"; no exact date was given.12 Among Pro plans, OpenAI says Ultrafast is available only on Pro 500, and it is not included in Pro 100 or Pro 200.34 OpenAI also says that at launch, buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast, and that keeping a grandfathered Pro 200 allowance does not add it.56 Whether Ultrafast will ever come to Pro 100 or Pro 200 was not reported.
