Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6 release

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · single origin, unclear which

Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro was released under an MIT license as an omni-modal model with 1M context, and Xiaomi also released its RL code and training environments.123

Per a Latent Space recap, MiMo-V2.6-Pro scores 46 on the AA index, just behind GPT-5.6 Sol at 47, at $0.13 versus $1.99 per task.456 Zvi Mowshowitz says the 46 on the new Artificial Analysis scale is a new high for open models, one ahead of Qwen-3.8 at a much lower price, which puts it on the efficiency frontier.7 Xiaomi attributes an estimated 10x boost in new-materials R&D productivity to MiMo-v2.6-Pro, but Mowshowitz says to treat the estimate with skepticism, though he doesn't doubt major acceleration.89

The commenter @teortaxesTex notes that the model's RL gains don't generalize to harder math evals.1011 One user reports that MiMo 2.6 Pro "overthinks" and links a system-prompt mitigation post.1213

A screenshot of an announcement from Fuli Luo states that MiMo-V3 will adopt a new architecture centered on HySparse2, and the claimed significance is an efficiency-oriented sparse-attention design.141516

What does the HySparse2 architecture change for MiMo-V3, and how much could it cut prefill cost and KV-cache size?

Xiaomi's Fuli Luo announced that MiMo-V3 will adopt a new architecture centered on HySparse2, a sparse-attention design aimed at efficiency.123 It is described as working through KV Bridging, KV Reuse, token-level selection and a shared KV-cache design.45 A commenter estimates that by reading only half the model and doing roughly 1/5 of the math during prefill, prefill time could drop by about 60–70%.6789 The same commenter says that could cut total task latency by around half for long-context workloads, and make 1M context more practical on 48GB unified-memory systems running roughly 27B–35B models.101112 A specific figure for the KV-cache size reduction was not reported.
