Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6 release

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin, unclear which

Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.6-Pro was released under an MIT license as an omni-modal model with 1M context, and Xiaomi also released its RL code and training environments.123

It scores 46 on the Artificial Analysis index, just behind GPT-5.6 Sol at 47, at a reported cost of $0.13 versus $1.99 per task.45

Zvi says an estimated 10x boost in new-materials R&D productivity comes from Xiaomi, so it should be treated with skepticism, but he does not doubt major acceleration; Xiaomi attributes this to its MiMo-v2.6-Pro model.6

However, critical notes appear: @teortaxesTex says its RL gains don't generalize to harder math evals, and one Reddit user reports MiMo 2.6 Pro "overthinking".78910

Separately, Fuli Luo announced that the next model, MiMo-V3, will use a new architecture centered on HySparse2, aimed at lower prefill FLOPs and a smaller KV-cache footprint.11121314
