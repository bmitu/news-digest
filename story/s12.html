TypeSafe Jev decision model launch

4 sources · 483 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · 3 independent origins: Allan Bo, Simon Willison, TypeSafe AI

TypeSafe AI launched Jev, a decision model trained with reinforcement learning that returns typed decisions with probabilities rather than reasoning text, and TypeSafe is reportedly raising $1B+ at a $10B+ valuation a week after a $200M round.123

A Jev-as-a-Judge paper reports that Jev costs $0.044 per 1K judgments at 152ms median latency, about 277× cheaper than GPT-6.45

According to the same paper, Jev stays within 3 points (of GPT-6) on RewardBench and HaluEval but trails by 14.5 points on JudgeBench, and a cascade that escalates low-confidence calls to GPT-6 Astra keeps 99% of accuracy at 57% of the cost.6789

Adoption signals cited include turbopuffer's native reranking including Jev and Jev being the top model at 1K–10K context on OpenRouter, and Simon Willison released an LLM plugin, llm-typesafe, supporting Jev's yes/no "noul", choice and score question types.1011

A highly engaged Reddit post argued that Jev is not new tech, citing an external Banking77 baseline where BGE-small plus logistic regression reportedly scored 93.3% versus Jev's 83.2%.121314

The same post challenged Jev's "0% hallucination" framing, noting that TypeSafe's own explanation only guarantees schema-valid outputs, not that the chosen class is factually correct.1516

Commenters were split: some likened Jev to long-standing classifiers like spaCy and scikit-learn, while others said a scaled zero-shot classifier could still be commercially valuable and should be judged on classification workloads rather than as an LLM substitute.171819

An open-weights alternative, CLM, was pitched as a self-hostable Jev replacement built on Qwen3-8B with 4×–13× lower latency in agent-style benchmarks, but its own stated limitations include weaker zero-shot breadth (BFCL v4 95.2% vs Jev's 99.2%) and a shorter calibrated context (2K–8K vs 64K).20212223

Top commenters disputed the "Jev competitor" framing, arguing that Jev's core value is precisely zero-shot broad knowledge, so API parity alone is insufficient.242526
