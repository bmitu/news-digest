TypeSafe Jev decision model launch

2 sources · 155 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · 2 independent origins: Allan Bo, TypeSafe AI

TypeSafe, maker of the Jev decision model, is reportedly raising $1B+ at a $10B+ valuation, a week after a $200M round.12

Jev is trained with reinforcement learning for calibrated decisions and returns typed decisions with probabilities rather than reasoning text.3

A Jev-as-a-Judge paper reports a cost of $0.044 per 1K judgments at 152ms median latency, about 277× cheaper than GPT-6.4

According to the paper, Jev stays within 3 points on RewardBench and HaluEval but trails by 14.5 points on JudgeBench, and a cascade escalating low-confidence calls to GPT-6 Astra keeps 99% of accuracy at 57% of the cost.56

Ecosystem signals cited include turbopuffer's native reranking including Jev, and Jev being the top model at 1K–10K context on OpenRouter.7

A Reddit post argues Jev is not new technology but standard constrained-choice classification, and cites a Banking77 baseline where BGE-small plus logistic regression reportedly scored 93.3% versus Jev's 83.2%.8910

That post also challenges Jev's "0% hallucination" framing, saying Typesafe's own explanation only guarantees schema-conforming outputs, not factual correctness.11

Commenters were split: some likened Jev to long-standing classifiers like spaCy and scikit-learn, while one said scaling zero-shot classifiers could still be commercially valuable.121314

Commenters dispute the "Jev competitor" framing, arguing Jev's core value is zero-shot broad knowledge that CLM lacks.15
