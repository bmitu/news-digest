Google SynthID detector expansion

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Ars Technica, Google

Google announced on Oct 7, 2026 a new SynthID website, open to anyone, that checks images, video and audio for AI watermarks.12 Google says the expanded detector supports the distinct watermarks of its SynthID partners, including OpenAI, Nvidia, Kakao and, soon, Apple.3

Previously, only testers could use the tool outside Gemini, and TechCrunch reports it was offered to select journalists, media professionals and researchers at Google I/O last year.45 Google says Gemini alone has integrated SynthID with over 180 billion images and videos plus 240,000 years of audio, and that people currently make 1 million verification requests a day.67

Users must log in with a Google, OpenAI or Apple account and get a daily quota of approximately 10 image, video and audio checks, and Google engineers told Ars that limiting access is necessary to prevent people from using the checks to develop SynthID removal tools.8910 The site gives only a message on whether SynthID was used to produce or edit the media, without the highlighted regions the internal tool showed.11

Whitwam also says someone may find a way to remove SynthID, suggests cryptographic labeling of real media such as C2PA might be a better approach, and still calls SynthID the best bet for spotting AI fakes.121314 TechCrunch adds that Microsoft and Meta have their own watermarking standards, but such tools are not infallible and often fail to identify content from their makers' own models.15
