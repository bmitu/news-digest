DeepSeek OpenRouter token share

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · single origin, unclear which

A chart from dirac.run, shared on Reddit and summarized by Latent Space, showed DeepSeek accounting for about 53% of the roughly 27 trillion tokens routed through OpenRouter in one day.12 The posts also claim that open-weight labs together now exceed 75% of OpenRouter's token volume.34 A separate post said the next 10 labs combined totaled under 47%, and that DeepSeek's share exceeded the combined share of competitors such as Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, Meta, xAI and Mistral.56

One commenter cited opencode usage data putting DeepSeek at 84% of tokens, with 24T tokens tracked, suggesting its dominance may be even stronger in coding-focused traffic.78

Commenters cautioned that OpenRouter may not represent the broader enterprise AI market because users there are already more likely to route to open-weight models.910 As a scale comparison, commenters noted that Google reportedly processes around 100 trillion tokens a day, roughly three times OpenRouter's cited 27 trillion.1112

Commenters also claimed that OpenRouter only pins a prompt to the same provider for about 10 minutes, while KV-cache retention on DeepSeek's API was claimed to last "up to days" instead of minutes.13141516 Commenters attributed DeepSeek's adoption to a combination of model quality, efficiency and low pricing; one user anecdotally reported about $4 for a day-long DeepSeek coding session versus over $400 for a similar Claude session.171819
