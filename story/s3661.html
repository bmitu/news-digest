Fireworks AI breach

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 166 points on Hacker News · 3 independent origins: Fireworks AI, Notion, The Information

Fireworks AI has confirmed a cyberattack in which an attacker breached its cloud provider and reached a Fireworks development system, exposing some customers' data.12 According to its security advisory, the stolen material included billing information and some datasets, and TechCrunch's second article adds that model weights were also accessed, with Fireworks temporarily disabling some self-service dataset functions. inference345 Fireworks says it believes the attackers are financially motivated and aim to sell tokens from breached access to large language models.6

Notion is a confirmed affected customer; it says the incident touched around 1% of its customers (its own page says fewer than 1% of workspaces), and that hackers took chat transcripts from Notion AI users of the "Insights" feature. inference789 Notion says the transcripts include any input to or output from the model written to the chat, and, as TechCrunch relays it, the company added that much of the data was unattributable to a specific customer.101112 Notion says Fireworks had given it a contractual commitment to zero data retention, but its investigation found that data was retained from the Fireworks endpoints it used.1314

Notion reports that starting November 3 it severed its systems' link to Fireworks, erased everything stored in the Fireworks environment, and ceased routing any traffic or information there; while the company looks into the matter, the Insights feature remains turned off.1516 Notion advises affected users to treat the data as exposed and to rotate any passwords or API keys that were pasted into Notion AI.1718

TechCrunch reports this is Fireworks' second breach in as many months, following an October incident.19 Hugging Face's Irene Solaiman did not tie the attack to Fireworks' openness, but TechCrunch says it shows how much valuable information is concentrated in AI infrastructure providers. inference20
