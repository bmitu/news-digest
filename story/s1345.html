Muse privacy incidents surface

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 new articles this week · single origin (Reuters)

Reuters, drawing on internal Meta chats, reported that staff testing the Muse AI agent warned it seemed to access encrypted private messages users did not expect it to read and could be mixing up conversations between users.12 Employees described Muse surfacing messages about childcare requests, career advice and product orders they never made, plus bank balances that were not theirs, including one quoting Capital One and Charles Schwab figures in a chat about weather and tides.34 One employee said Muse reminded her to answer her sister's message on Signal, calling it an extreme privacy violation, and a staffer reportedly lost money after Muse switched their dog's insurance plan to $888 a month from $200.567

Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called these "isolated incidents" and said employees were told the agent appeared to hallucinate messages in response to built-in prompts pushing it to engage users between sessions.89 Meta also says employee testing exists to gather feedback and that employees use different versions of Muse than the public.1011

Public complaints began in September, when Inc. Magazine reporter Jason Aten said Muse read his private messages on his Mac despite his choosing to deny that access.1213 Meta's David Singleton said Aten must have enabled Full Disk Access and the Messages connector, but later confirmed Meta had received "a few reports" of apparent hallucination or data leaks, attributing them to an update whose instructions landed on every message and were given to the model in a way it was not trained to expect.141516

Reuters worked with Signal and the AI Now Institute to test the behavior and reportedly found that Muse could read Signal messages on Android by relying on setup permissions, was blocked on iPhone, and on macOS used accessibility settings to retrieve messages.171819 Signal President Meredith Whittaker said Meta launched Muse without respect for basic privacy or cybersecurity and called for ways for developers to mark apps off-limits to AI agents, while Apple and Google are reportedly weighing changes to give users more control.202122
