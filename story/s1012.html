Meta launches Muse Wonder

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 18:59 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · 3 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Meta, Simon Willison

Meta's Superintelligence Labs released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full on Monday, which TechCrunch describes as the company's first attempt at a frontier model.1 Meta says the models can rival GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1, citing internal testing on several industry benchmarks.23

Wonder is available through the Meta Model API at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, while Wonder Full is limited to Muse Code subscribers and not served through the API.45 Meta says both models will also power its Muse agent, and it emphasized an "active memory" ability, claiming (per TechCrunch) that Muse Wonder scores higher on MemoryBench than Fable, Opus or Astra.67

Artificial Analysis scored Wonder Full (max) at 56 and Wonder (max) at 52 on its Intelligence Index, up from Muse Spark 1.3 at 48, with Opus 5.5 leading at 58.89 Arena placed Wonder Max 15th in WebDev but second in Text Arena, and one tester, Pawel Huryn, scored Wonder 44 in a 105-bug test versus 45 for Astra and 41.7 for Opus.1011

Latent Space notes that some headline wins rely on internal evaluations or omit relevant competing models, and that the MemoryBench result does not establish durable cross-session memory.1213

The Verge says Meta's launch means it has arrived at the AI frontier, while Interconnects' Nathan Lambert-style analysis says the models are very strong on traditional benchmarks, behind only Opus 5.5. inference1415
