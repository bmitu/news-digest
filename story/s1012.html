Meta launches Muse Wonder

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 new articles this week · single origin (Meta)

Meta unveiled its closed frontier models Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full a few days before it released the open-weight Muse Spark Open, according to Interconnects.1 Meta says it trained Muse Wonder on a gigawatt-scale cluster with hundreds of thousands of GPUs, and reports 85%+ pretraining goodput and 90%+ RL goodput.23

Interconnects says that on traditional benchmarks the models are very strong, behind only Anthropic's Opus 5.5 and similar to OpenAI's GPT-6-Astra or Google's Gemini-4-Argon.4 Muse Wonder Full (Max) debuted at #6 in Code Arena WebDev with 1726 points, which moved Meta to the #3 lab spot, and it ranks #4 on Vending-Bench 2.567

Interconnects says the vibe checks have been solid, though expectations were that the models would not immediately challenge the refinement of the latest Anthropic and OpenAI models in real-world use.8 Kelsey Piper thinks it is more narrowly a coding model than the latest Claude and ChatGPT offerings, and markedly worse at research and writing.9

Zvi Mowshowitz says Meta certainly claims Muse Wonder is a good model, with a pitch that, although they don't say the line, is full 'introducing the world's most powerful model,' and notes that the term quoted in Bloomberg is 'Meta says new Muse model hits AI frontier.'1011 Zvi Mowshowitz also says Muse Wonder will not get a full capabilities post from him because it 'has proven to have been oversold.'12
