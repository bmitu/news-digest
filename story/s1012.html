Meta launches Muse Wonder

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · 2 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Meta

Meta unveiled a model on a Monday, and The Verge reports that, according to Meta's testing, Muse Wonder Full can compete with GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.1 on numerous benchmarks.123 The Verge says Meta's launch came days after Google released Gemini 4 Argon, and that the two releases put the AI race back on. inference45

Interconnects says the models are very strong on traditional benchmarks, behind only Anthropic's Opus 5.5 and similar to GPT-6 Astra or Google's Gemini 4 Argon.6 Zvi Mowshowitz says the Full version scores 56 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence, two points behind Opus 5.5.7 Arena placed Muse Wonder Full (Max) at #6 in Code Arena WebDev with 1726 points, and Muse Wonder (Max) at #12 in Agent Arena at $1.13 per task.89

However, Mowshowitz is skeptical of the benchmarks. He says Meta presented them in a way that looked suspect, and he notes that Muse Spark 1.3 scored nearly as high as models he doesn't think it can match, which he takes as signs of possible benchmaxxing. inference1011

Alexandr Wang acknowledged ongoing inference bugs in the deployment, and the model card lists 3D and spatial reasoning as a known limitation.1213 Interconnects says the vibe checks have been solid, but it expected the models not to immediately match the refinement of the latest Anthropic and OpenAI models in real-world use.14 One user reportedly counted 15 PRs in a day with Muse Wonder but also burned through a $200 weekly allowance.15
