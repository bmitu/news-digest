OpenAI teen features and College Planner

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced that it will soon add a College Planner to ChatGPT for Teens, which it says brings together application requirements, deadlines, tasks and financial-aid steps for schools on a student's list in one plan.123 OpenAI says the initial US experience is designed for high-school students in grades 10–12 planning to attend a four-year college, and that it plans to expand to more countries and to two-year colleges, technical colleges and trade schools over time.45 Ekaterina Struett, CEO of College Advising Corps, said the group sees promise in College Planner as a tool to help students prepare for conversations with advisers and stay on track.67

OpenAI is also supporting the nonprofit College Advising Corps, helping it expand into new states and create an AI fellowship program, and is backing Boston Children's Hospital's Digital Wellness Lab and its Student Advisory Council for three years.8910

OpenAI also reported early usage figures: it says teens spend under 15 minutes a day on ChatGPT on average, and less than 2% spend more than three consecutive hours.11 OpenAI also says nearly 1.2 million teens used Learning Visualizations and more than 180,000 used Study Mode in one week, and that as ChatGPT for Teens became available to more teens globally, those with access sent about 2.7 million more learning-related messages on average than those who didn't have it yet.1213

The Verge notes the announcements came shortly after Common Sense Media published a report calling ChatGPT for Teens an "unacceptable risk," saying it still does kids' homework and doesn't alert parents when it should.14
