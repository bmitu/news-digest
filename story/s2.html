OpenAI GPT-6 Sol and Luna release

3 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 3 new today · 4 independent origins: OpenAI, Piotr Skalski, Simon Willison, Vals AI

OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, cheaper models derived from its flagship GPT-6 Astra, about an hour after Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5. inference12

Pricing is Sol at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens, which sources describe as about 50% below their GPT-5.6 predecessors.34

Simon Willison calls the pricing very competitive and notes Luna is one of the cheapest models OpenAI has released, beaten only by GPT-4.1 Nano and GPT-5 Nano.56

Per Latent Space's summary of a @reach_vb post, OpenAI's published examples claim Sol at xhigh effort beats Claude Opus 5 max on AutomationBench at roughly 9% of the cost per task, and Luna max exceeds GPT-5.6 Sol medium on OSWorld 2.0 offline at one-tenth the cost.78

OpenAI also says it improved caching and inference efficiency, with up to 90% discounts on cached input-token reads.9

However, Latent Space says the day was always going to belong to Claude Opus 5.5, and Zvi says no one is talking about Sol and Luna because of Opus 5.5, though he thinks they should be an important upgrade under the hood.1011

Reactions were mixed: many praised the economics, while others felt Anthropic won on model quality and OpenAI on affordability. Simon Willison says he now uses GPT-6 Sol as his default in Codex.1213
