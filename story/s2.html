OpenAI GPT-6 Sol and Luna release

5 sources · 1777 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 27 · 3 new today · 4 independent origins: OpenAI, Piotr Skalski, Simon Willison, Vals AI

OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, cheaper models it says were trained with methods similar to GPT-6 Astra, and cut API prices for both by 50% compared with GPT-5.6 promotional pricing.12

Sol is priced at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens, and Simon Willison notes Luna is one of the cheapest models OpenAI has ever released.345

OpenAI also says Sol makes about half as many mistakes as its predecessor on an internal factuality evaluation, and that both models improve on their GPT-5.6 counterparts in alignment evaluations, which it says test challenging situations rather than typical use.678

Third-party measures put Sol at 48 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index for $1.06 per task and Luna at 37 for $0.068, while Vals reports Luna within 8 points of Astra on the Vals Index at about 100x lower per-token cost.910

The launch came about an hour after Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, which Latent Space says was always going to dominate the day, and Zvi says no one is talking about the OpenAI models because of Opus 5.5.111213
