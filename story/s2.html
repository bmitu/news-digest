OpenAI GPT-6 Sol and Luna release

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · single origin (Piotr Skalski)

OpenAI released a new cheaper and improved Sol and Luna, according to Zvi Mowshowitz.1 Mowshowitz reports Sol is priced at $2/$10 and Luna at $0.10/$0.50, which he describes as slashing prices on both lines by 50%.2 OpenAI says Sol and Luna build on Astra's alignment advances and improve on their GPT-5.6 counterparts.3

Mowshowitz says Astra remains OpenAI's strongest model, so Sol will not get full coverage, but he thinks Sol and Luna look very good at their price points, at least until Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 appear.45 Mowshowitz says no one is talking about the new Sol and Luna because of Opus 5.5, but he thinks they should be an important upgrade under the hood.6

Luna [Max] entered the Code Arena WebDev leaderboard at #24 (1593), 74 points above GPT-5.6 Luna, at about $0.40/Mtok blended, according to a Latent Space roundup of Twitter posts.7 In DOOM agent matches, Astra reportedly had an 82.5% win rate, Sol was fastest, and Luna had the best wins per dollar.8 On vision evals, @skalskip92 ranks Opus 5.5 above GPT-6 Sol but below GPT-6 Astra.910

What did OpenAI do next with Luna, such as its image-encoding bug fix and its use in the Decisions API?

OpenAI fixed an image-encoding bug in GPT-6 Luna, which added 1 point to its Intelligence Index score.1 At DevDay, OpenAI introduced a Decisions API that runs on Luna, giving near-instant multiple-choice classification and routing over text and images.2 Sam Altman described it as a way to give Luna a predefined set of options, such as image categories or agent behaviors, and he said focusing the model on that choice makes it extremely fast while keeping image understanding, broad language support and safety protections.34
