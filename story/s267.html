Amodei 'Pace the Frontier' proposal

2 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei released a letter titled "We Must Pace the Frontier," which, in author Cal Newport's account, lists harms his own company's research might cause and concludes that catastrophe can only be avoided if we "build the technology in the right way."12 Newport says this involves the government slowing down potential competitors and letting the labs take the lead in advancing the technology, and that OpenAI CEO Sam Altman quickly tweeted his support.34

Anthropic's Thariq Shihipar, speaking on the Latent Space podcast, explained the "Pacing the Frontier" name by saying models may eventually hack any constraint placed on them if labs are not very careful.56 He describes layers of security, including model training, probes and classifiers, an auto mode classifier that checks requests, and identity and permissions, and says any of these failure modes at any one point can cause agents to escape the sandbox.78

Newport says the two leading frontier AI labs have shown brazen behavior, and on September 28, 2026, wrote that last Thursday he published a New York Times op-ed calling on Congress to begin a public fact-finding mission into what research OpenAI and Anthropic are conducting.910 Newport argues that he is increasingly concerned the labs are becoming reckless because they believe collateral damage is justified in a race to redeem humanity.11

A commenter on Newport's blog, citing Jensen Huang's interview with Ezra Klein, says labs asking the government to slow them down is an abdication of responsibility, and that labs unable to stop releasing unsafe products should probably be shut down.1213
