Claude Duet launch

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 24 · 2 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, The Verge

Anthropic has released Claude Duet, a consumer-oriented AI agent. The product remains a "research preview" and can be used only by US subscribers on the Pro, Max and Ultra plans, while others can join a waitlist.12 A later Latent Space roundup reported that every waitlisted US user on the Pro, Max and Ultra plans has now received an invitation to Duet.3

According to The Verge, Duet is meant for "professionals and small business owners" and is more work-focused than recent agents from Meta (Muse) and OpenAI (Dots).45 Once connected to the productivity and chat services a user chooses, it looks for ways to help and suggests them as cards that users swipe left or right to hide or accept.6

Allison Johnson of The Verge found it truly helpful at times, for example linking a meeting summary in email to a Slack message and spotting that upcoming time off left her few working days before a draft was due.78 She also found it like a "slightly neurotic" assistant, since it nagged her about an unedited story and missed the nuance that the deadline was not firm.910

Johnson says she likes Duet as a backstop for her own organizational systems, not as a replacement for them, and she found it kind of weird knowing it can read every email, draft, and Slack she sends.1112 She notes Anthropic says it is still testing Duet's judgment about when to message users, so it may calibrate better, though she doubts AI will ever grasp nuance and tone well. inference1314
