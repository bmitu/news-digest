Claude Duet launch

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
5 sources · 565 points on Hacker News · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic announced Claude Duet, a proactive AI agent built on Claude Opus 5.5, launching as a "research preview" that is available through a waitlist.123 The Verge and TechCrunch both describe it as a rival to Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots.45 Zvi Mowshowitz likewise describes Duet as Claude's answer to other proactive agents like Muse and Dot.6

It works in its own cloud virtual machine with a browser, and TechCrunch reports it connects to over 6,000 connectors and plug-ins and can make purchases via Stripe's Link or Shopify's Shop Pay.789 Safeguards include approval requests before big steps like payments or deletions, plus a new classifier and safety system prompt meant to reject requests to share the strictest tier of information, such as credit card numbers.1011

Anthropic concedes Duet is an "early version" that may misread a thread or miscalibrate urgency, and is not "as strong" at personal errands like booking tickets.121314 Access is limited to Pro and Max subscribers in the US, on iOS, Android and the web; Team and Enterprise plans are not yet included, and Duet shares the plan's usage limits.15161718
