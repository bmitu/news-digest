Google AI publisher payment pilot and disputes

2 sources · single origin (The Information)

The pilot reportedly includes around 100 publishers and pays based on how much their content contributed to AI Overviews, AI Mode and the Gemini chatbot.12 Payouts vary widely: The Information reports one early-joining publisher earned over $1 million in a year, while another that joined a few months ago has earned around $50,000 to $60,000.34 Several larger publishers have reportedly declined to join, hoping that refusing will force Google to offer a more generous deal.56

Ars Technica reports, citing The Information, that several small and mid-sized publishers say the payments equal roughly one-tenth of one percent of their advertising revenue.7 Ars Technica adds that various smaller sites are seeing less than $1,000 over several months, which it says won't come close to offsetting declining traffic.8

Publishers also say that tracking monthly payments in the search console is often confusing, and it is unclear what counts as a meaningful contribution, which makes planning future earnings impossible.9 Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam argues that the small payments and unclear nature of AI contributions could make expanding the program difficult.10
