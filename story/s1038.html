Facebook Your Algorithm

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 18:59 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Meta)

Meta announced on Tuesday that it is launching "Your Algorithm" on Facebook, an AI-powered feature that lets users shape what appears in their Feed and Reels.12 TechCrunch reports that users can describe their preferences in their own words using natural language, and The Verge reports that they can also mark topics "interested" or "not interested". inference34 Meta says recommendations "respond right away" and update in real time after users make changes.567

Users can reach Your Algorithm, or choose "interested" or "not interested", from the three-dot menu on individual posts, and the buttons may also appear under posts.8910 Meta says it may ask follow-up questions for more detail on what users liked or disliked.1112

Meta says the feature is part of a set of AI-powered tools, and that it is using large language models (LLMs) more in its ranking systems.1314 Meta argues that LLMs can incorporate the interests, context and intent behind natural language, "unlocking an algorithm you can actually talk to."15

The feature had already launched elsewhere: TechCrunch says Instagram introduced Your Algorithm in June, after first launching it for Reels in December 2025, and that Threads has a related feature called "Dear Algo".1617 TechCrunch characterizes the move as a shift away from traditional recommendation systems, bringing Facebook closer to streaming services that allow personalized recommendations.18
