Eight states sue OpenAI

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 5 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: NBC Montana, The Argonaut

Eight states have sued OpenAI over chatbot liability and AI liability issues, according to Alexandra Reeve Givens, CEO of the Center for Democracy and Technology, who mentioned the suits in a Data & Society panel discussion.12

Idaho is one of the states suing, in a lawsuit tied to Attorney General Raúl Labrador that raises concerns about child and public safety. inference3 Labrador says the goal is child safety, stating that "The safety of Idaho children cannot be an afterthought to runaway profit or technological advancements" and that his office will hold OpenAI accountable.4

Montana Attorney General Austin Knudsen filed a separate suit in Lewis and Clark County, claiming that ChatGPT endangers children and teens by giving harmful advice and failing to adequately protect young users.56 Knudsen says there are documented cases of ChatGPT telling kids how to commit suicide and how to mix dangerous cocktails of illegal drugs, and he faults the company for not asking for age verification or parental consent.78 In Montana the case is at an early stage: Knudsen says OpenAI has 90 days to respond, after which discovery would begin.9
