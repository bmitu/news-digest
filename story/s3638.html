Second Huang affair reports

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO and board member after reports of extramarital affairs with accused spy Alice Huang and Nvidia healthcare VP Kimberly Powell, and CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO with no permanent successor named.123 The New York Times reported, citing five people who worked at the company, that Huang had a relationship with Powell, an executive who reports to him; two of those people said the pair were filmed being affectionate at a San Jose hotel during a March Nvidia conference, though the Times has not seen the video.456 Reuters later reported, per TechCrunch, a separate affair with another Nvidia employee that had not been reported before, and Reuters is withholding her name.78

Once the report of a second affair surfaced, the Wall Street Journal wrote that Nvidia's directors were getting ready to remove Huang from his post, while Data Center Dynamics reported that the board, already investigating the earlier affair, was about to convene an emergency session.910 Sources differ on how many direct reports Huang had: Zvi Mowshowitz's post says over 50, while Data Center Dynamics says 60, and both say there was no clear successor or succession planning. inference1112

Congressional pressure was building: two House Republicans said Congress may subpoena Huang over the spy allegations if he does not voluntarily attend a closed-door briefing, and TechCrunch says he has been called to testify in the coming weeks.1314

Fortune's CEO Daily argued Nvidia could carry on without Huang, citing Apple after Steve Jobs and Uber after Travis Kalanick, though it said his departure would hurt talent, sales, reputation and revenue, and one CEO customer said "With or without him, this is a mess."151617
