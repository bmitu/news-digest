Liquid AI DSpark speculative decoding for VLM

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin (Liquid AI)

Liquid AI released an experimental DSpark speculative-decoding draft model for its vision-language model LFM2.5-VL-3B, saying it speeds up inference without changing output quality.123

Liquid AI says the approach is exact because the target model verifies every proposed token, so greedy output equals the target alone.4

The drafter uses the same architecture as Liquid's text DSpark drafters: it conditions on the target model's hidden states to draft a block of candidate tokens, and image patches and text tokens are projected into a shared representation.56

The drafter adds about 280M parameters, an 8.9% increase over the 3B target, and Liquid AI says it ships with day-one support for llama.cpp, MLX-VLM and SGLang.78

Liquid AI reports decode speedups of up to 3.13x on device and 2.66x on an H100, with end-to-end gains of up to 2.62x and 2.27x.9

By hardware, Liquid AI reports MLX on an M5 Max decoding 2.30x to 3.13x faster and llama.cpp on an M3 Ultra improving decoding by 1.57x to 2.14x, with end-to-end gains of 1.56x to 2.62x and 1.30x to 1.77x respectively.1011

However, Liquid AI cautions that speculation only accelerates decoding, not vision encoding or prefill, so end-to-end gains are smaller than decode gains where those stages dominate, citing Amdahl's law.1213

Latent Space's AINews newsletter also covered the release, repeating the 3.13x, 2.14x and 2.66x figures; the Hugging Face post's H100 decode range is printed as "20.4x to 2.66x", which appears to be a typo. inference1415
