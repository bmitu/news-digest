Anthropic gives METR employee-level access

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
3 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic says it has started giving employee-like access to a team from METR, a nonprofit that evaluates frontier AI models, and that METR will publish the results of its risk assessments.12 TechCrunch reports the announcement was made in a blog post on Thursday, and that it follows incidents of AI agents acting outside their instructions that have been disclosed every week or two since late August.3 According to Anthropic, six METR employees have been onboarded with company hardware, office space, and access to internal tools roughly on par with its own risk assessment teams.45 Anthropic says the engagement is limited to a small team under enforceable agreements, with other METR employees siloed from that team's work.6

Anthropic says it cannot veto METR's publication but can require redaction of information in narrow, predefined categories such as customer data, attorney-client privileged material, or model architecture information.789 Anthropic says METR may publicly characterize the redactions and say whether it thinks they were justified, provided it does not reveal the redacted information.1011

Anthropic describes a three-tier system of evaluator access, with METR the only evaluator at the top tier so far according to TechCrunch.1213 In the first tier, evaluators conduct narrowly scoped risk investigations, Anthropic decides what access to provide, and evaluators can publish their findings with Anthropic's approval; second-tier evaluators can publish without approval and have access to more information.1415 Anthropic says it is also working with SecureBio and Accenture as evaluators and is in early talks with others, including Hugging Face.16

TechCrunch says the move fulfills a promise Dario Amodei made in a September essay, that OpenAI quickly pledged the same, and that several major AI companies have signed a White House accord committing to independent review.1718 Anthropic compares the arrangement to bank supervision, where regulators place resident examiners inside the largest banks, while noting the analogy is not exact, and says it believes every frontier AI company should embed evaluators.192021
