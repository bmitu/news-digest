Anthropic gives METR employee-level access

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 new articles this week · single origin (Anthropic)

In October it was announced that Anthropic would give six METR staff "employee-like" access to assess its models and safety protocols.1 Transformer presents the arrangement as part of an existing network of voluntary evaluators, alongside Redwood Research, FAR.AI, RAND and SecureBio, which may be too small for the scale of oversight now being proposed. inference23

METR president Chris Painter has said on X that the main limit on METR doing more of this work is technical capacity rather than access to frontier developers.4 Similarly, OpenAI spokesperson Shana Mansbach said the company is concerned about capacity regarding the Massachusetts auditing bill, though Transformer notes OpenAI's motives could be questioned while saying the company has a point.56

METR says its independence is protected by its disclosure policies, and by the fact that it is not paid for the evaluations it performs on frontier companies.7 According to another newsletter entry, Beth Barnes is credited with explaining that METR permits labs to sign off on which of their employees speak with its assessors, provided those sign-offs are made public.8
