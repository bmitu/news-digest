Grok Voice 3.0 release

Last scan Mon Oct 19, 23:45 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (xAI)

xAI announced Grok Voice 3.0, which it calls "the world's most intelligent speech-to-speech model", and made it available in its Voice Agent API at $0.08 per minute ($4.80 per hour), the same price as Grok Voice 2.0.12 Latent Space's roundup says SpaceXAI describes it as its most intelligent speech-to-speech model, and the roundup's top-tweets list gives the launch 8.9K engagement.34

Citing Artificial Analysis's independent testing, xAI says the model ranks #1 on the Speech-to-Speech Index with 86.9, ahead of GPT-Live-1 (Astra, Medium) at 83.2, Gemini 3.8 Live at 82.6 and Grok Voice 2.0 at 81.3.567 Latent Space reports that Artificial Analysis also gave it 71.6% on Tau Voice, 98.5% on Big Bench Audio and 0.81s time to first audio.8 xAI says it scores 97% on Full Duplex Bench and that its τ-Voice result is comparable with GPT-Live-1 (Astra) at 40% lower latency.910

xAI says it costs less per task than GPT-Live-1 (Astra, Medium) and GPT-Realtime-2.1 on Artificial Analysis's Big Bench Audio run, and that it reached the highest accuracy, 74%, on ServiceNow's EVA-Bench.1112 ServiceNow's Nitin Aggarwal is quoted saying the model sets a new Pareto frontier on EVA-Bench, pairing top accuracy with natural, responsive conversation.1314

xAI says the model handles millions of conversations a day in Tesla vehicles, and that Starlink call centers use it for 20,000 customer calls daily, with roughly 70% resolved without a human.1516

Zvi Mowshowitz's newsletter notes in passing that Grok Voice 3.0 exists.17
