Microsoft Reduces Anthropic Spend

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
3 sources · 375 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 6 · 3 new articles this week · single origin, unclear which

The Information reported on October 5 that Meta and Microsoft have begun sharply reducing employee use of Anthropic's Claude AI as they shift toward their own coding tools.1 The cut is to internal use, not customer access: the report says it does not mean Microsoft is withdrawing Claude from client-facing products.23

The Information reportedly found that Microsoft cut its projected internal Anthropic spend by more than a third.45 One account says Microsoft had expected internal spending on Anthropic to exceed $1 billion a year, and that the estimate fell after management told employees to use less Claude and more Microsoft tools such as GitHub Copilot and OpenAI frameworks.67 Within Microsoft's cloud and AI sector, monthly AI spending limits were reportedly cut from $100,000 per employee to about $10,000 in most cases, but the article stresses these are ceilings, not actual spending.89

At Meta, Claude Code users reportedly fell from about 60,000 to about 30,000, largely because of a push toward Meta's own tools.1011

Sources differ on the size of Microsoft's Anthropic business: Zvi Mowshowitz's newsletter says Microsoft previously provided over $2 billion in ARR, while the RS Web Solutions article says internal spending was expected to exceed $1 billion annually. Zvi Mowshowitz also describes the cut as "a third" for both companies. inference1213 Zvi Mowshowitz says he understands the temptation to use their own AI products and that he is not worried about Anthropic's revenue numbers, and the RS Web Solutions article calls this a tightening of budgets and rising competition, not an end to engagement with Claude.1415
