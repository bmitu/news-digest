Liquid AI LFM2.5-24B-A2B

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 18:59 UTC
3 sources · 288 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · single origin (Liquid AI)

Liquid AI released LFM2.5-24B-A2B, a 24B-parameter reasoning model with just 2B active parameters that it says is built for fast, private agents on-device.12 Liquid AI calls it the largest and final model in the LFM2.5 family, and says the base and post-trained models are open-weight on Hugging Face and its Playground.34

Liquid AI says the model is competitive with Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, which has about 50% more total and active parameters, on five of eight agentic and tool-use benchmarks, scoring 48.09 versus 38.19 on LiveClaw and 27.69 versus 8.72 on AutomationBench.5 Liquid AI says it also leads on all three instruction-following benchmarks.67

Liquid AI acknowledges that larger models keep an edge on knowledge benchmarks such as GPQA Diamond, HLE and AA-Omniscience, and on coding benchmarks such as SWE-Bench Verified (OpenCode) and SciCode.89 For knowledge-heavy workloads it recommends pairing the model with retrieval or web search.1011

On speed, Liquid AI reports 184 tokens/s on an Apple M5 Max and 114 tokens/s on an AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395, up to 1.7x faster than the next-best model with the least memory.1213 On a single NVIDIA B300 GPU, it reports over 21K output tokens per second at high concurrency, measured with SGLang 0.5.22 in BF16.141516

Compared with LFM2-24B-A2B, it is reasoning-only, extends context from 32,768 to 131,072 tokens, doubles the vocabulary from 65K to 128K, and scales pre-training from 17T to 33T tokens.171819
