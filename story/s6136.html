Huawei prepares memory chip technology disclosure

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Bloomberg, Lianhe Zaobao

Huawei is reportedly preparing to disclose a new high-bandwidth memory (HBM) technology that may be more advanced than what South Korean industry leaders Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix currently offer. inference12 According to Latent Space, which cites Zaobao, a paper's title claims the design doubles the data-pin density of Samsung and SK Hynix HBM now in mass production, potentially giving AI accelerators more bandwidth if it can be made at scale.3 However, Latent Space notes that it remains unclear whether the technology is ready for production.4

Latent Space argues that successfully commercializing Chinese HBM would directly address a major bottleneck created by U.S. curbs on HBM exports to China and controls on Nvidia AI accelerators.5 The report summarized by Latent Space also points to homegrown ways around lithography limits, including Huawei's “LogicFolding” and the “4F²” memory architecture from ChangXin Memory.6

Semafor places the news amid surging Chinese factory profits driven largely by the AI boom, with memory chip production a notable strength.78 Semafor also says that the memory makers ranked third and fourth by market share, both from China, are rapidly closing the gap in output and sophistication.9

Online comments summarized by Latent Space focused mostly on geopolitics, backing Huawei and other Chinese chip companies and arguing that sanctions will speed China's drive for semiconductor self-sufficiency.10 One commenter speculated that Huawei's broad engineering strength might eventually produce a domestic EUV lithography system, which would be strategically useful even without immediately matching ASML.11
