GLM-5.4: Toward Generalized Coding with Our First Loop Transformer

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · 1186 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 23 · 2 independent origins: Simon Willison, Z.ai

Chinese AI lab Z.ai has released GLM-5.4, its first model built as a looped transformer.12 Z.ai says the design repeats a set of layers that share weights, refining the prior pass's hidden states and adding depth without adding parameters.3 Latent Space reports that GLM-5.4 is a 750B-parameter model that runs 28 layers twice, adds vision, and was adapted from the GLM-5 base with further training instead of being pretrained anew.45

At launch, GLM Coding Plan users get the model and 50% more weekly quota, while API access awaits a safety evaluation and Z.ai plans to open the weights after "necessary safety hardening." inference678

On performance, Z.ai claims GLM-5.4 is on par with the "2T–3T" parameter class, and a summary cited by Latent Space says a 30B test version's AIME25 score rose from 19.2 to 30.4 using about 10% more compute.9 Despite citing that test result, Latent Space also said no benchmarks had been published yet, a gap that drew skepticism. inference1011

Some commenters questioned whether safety hardening means weakening the model's cybersecurity skills, arguing that open weights matter to defenders who lack access to closed frontier models.1213 Commenters also faulted the absence of checkpoints smaller than 30B despite Z.ai citing tests at such sizes, and others doubted that the coding-focused release would improve roleplay. inference1415

Simon Willison, who tested GLM-5.4 by having it draw pelicans, judged GLM-5.3's drawings to be of similar quality to those from GLM-5.4. inference1617 Willison also noted that GLM-5.4 was the second looped transformer mention he had seen in a week, following Thinking Machines Lab's Inkling 2 Mini on November 20th.18
