Google complexity and algorithms papers

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 09:22 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 25 · 1 new article today · 3 independent origins: Andrew Curran, Google, Scott Aaronson

According to Zvi Mowshowitz, Google dropped six additional papers, including ones on unconditional integer factorization in time L_N[1/3, O(1)] and breaking the square-root barrier for the log-rank conjecture.123 Latent Space reports that the six papers were posted to GitHub without an announcement and were produced by an agent harness running on unnamed Gemini 4 models.4 Latent Space also lists among the results a proven factoring bound of the NFS class that beats a record from 1992.5 According to Mowshowitz, a claim in one of the papers is that bimatrix games can have 1/5-approximate Nash equilibria found by an exact algorithm that runs in polynomial time.6

The Google release came amid other claimed proofs: Mowshowitz says OpenAI added nine results, including a 111-page claim that the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4.7 Mowshowitz calls the 9/4 exponent result actively disappointing, since many had hoped the exponent was 2.8 Mowshowitz also says both OpenAI and Anthropic independently proved VP ≠ VNP, with Anthropic posting first and OpenAI saying it had the result on November 6.9 Latent Space also says Anthropic posted its own VP ≠ VNP solution the same day as the other announcements.10
