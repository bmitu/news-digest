Anthropic wet lab established

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic announced a new life sciences research group and laboratory, and says Claude discovered a novel enzyme system with properties reminiscent of CRISPR, with only high-level direction from its scientists.12 According to Anthropic, about 950 Claude agents searched a DNA sequence database for 21 hours using 210 million tokens, and one agent spotted a repeating DNA pattern next to the gene for an odd-looking reverse transcriptase (RT).34 Anthropic calls the system array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART), found mainly in bacteriophages, and says early experiments show the array is expressed as distinct short RNAs.56

Feng Zhang, a CRISPR pioneer who reviewed the pre-print, called it an exciting example of AI agents contributing to discovery and said the RNA-repeat arrays associated with reverse transcriptases are genuinely intriguing and merit further investigation.78

Zvi, however, judges it not a big deal as biology, saying a PhD student finding it would be cool but not headline-making.910 Zvi says it is not obviously bad for Anthropic to have a wet lab if it is set up and supervised responsibly, while an included comment questions Anthropic's reliance on large compactions and its swapping of models under a persistent agent. inference1112
