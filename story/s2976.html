Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash GA

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
4 sources · 299 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 6 · single origin (Google)

Google says it is rolling out Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash and making it generally available across the Gemini API and Gemini for business.12 Google says Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is available in Google AI Studio and Google Ads, and is rolling out in the Gemini app to Google AI Ultra subscribers.345 Google also says that, for the first time, Gemini Omni is generally available, backed by Google Cloud's enterprise-grade infrastructure and security, with Provisioned Throughput available and outputs carrying an imperceptible SynthID watermark.67

Google says the model adds native 15-second video creation, multi-turn conversational editing and enhanced reference following.89 Clips can now be up to 15 seconds, up from 10 seconds with Omni 1.1, with duration settable in one-second increments from 3 to 15 seconds.101112 Google also claims better speech and lip-sync with distinct multi-speaker conditioning, and more controllable camera movement with significantly fewer unwanted jump cuts.131415 Google says users can draft at 360p or 720p, fix details with a follow-up edit rather than regenerating, and then upscale to 1080p or 4K.1617

Google says it cut the price 30% from Gemini Omni 1.1, to $0.07 per second for 720p video.1819 A Latent Space newsletter roundup noted that pricing is reduced 30% vs. Omni 1.1 to $0.07/s for 720p.2018

Google acknowledges that development is ongoing on limitations such as fast sports action, rapid script changes and complex physics.2122
