OpenClaw app launch

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · single origin, unclear which

OpenClaw, which appeared on OpenRouter's coding-app leaderboard at the end of 2025, is described by Anjney Midha as a new form factor that brought in a new type of user beyond developers.1

Midha also says its architecture was unusual, calling the user's chosen model for "heartbeats" to check whether it was still alive, in addition to using the model for real tasks.2

Simon Willison says that by the end of January the repository that started in November had renamed itself, first to CLAWDIS, then CLAWDBOT, then Moltbot, and finally to OpenClaw.3

Willison reports it had 8,300 commits less than two months in and over 100,000 when he checked later, and he calls it the most vibe-coded software in existence.45

Willison also says Bay Area Apple stores sold out of Mac Minis because people bought them to run OpenClaw, and cites Drew Breunig's view that a Mac mini is an "aquarium" for a digital-pet claw.67

In China, companies hosted OpenClaw install parties where non-technical people queued for help, which Willison takes as proof of real market demand for personal AI agents.89
