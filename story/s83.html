OpenClaw app launch

Last scan Fri Oct 2, 18:55 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · single origin, unclear which

Simon Willison writes that by the end of January the repository had been renamed several times, from CLAWDIS to CLAWDBOT to Moltbot and finally OpenClaw.1 Willison says OpenClaw had 8,300 commits less than two months after the project started, and that it now has over 100,000. He calls it the most vibe-coded software in existence.23

Willison also reports that Bay Area Apple stores sold out of Mac Minis because so many people were buying them to run OpenClaw.4 He cites Drew Breunig's view that an OpenClaw is a digital pet and the Mac mini is its aquarium.5 In China, companies hosted OpenClaw install parties where non-technical people queued for help installing it, and Willison thinks this proved real market demand for personal AI agents.67

Anjney Midha said that at the end of 2025 OpenClaw appeared on OpenRouter's leaderboard, where the other coding apps had all been IDEs or terminal agents.89 Midha said OpenClaw was a new form factor that brought in a new type of user, such as productivity users or internet creators, rather than only developers.10 Midha also pointed to its architecture, which called the user's chosen model for "heartbeats" to check it was still alive, in addition to using the model for real tasks.11
