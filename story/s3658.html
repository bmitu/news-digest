ChatGPT Wallet

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: OpenAI, The Rundown AI

OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet, a feature that lets its Dots agents buy items on a user's behalf after the user reviews and approves each purchase.12 Availability is limited at first to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older with eligible US-issued cards from American Express, Chase, Mastercard or Visa.3 TechCrunch reports the limited rollout will let OpenAI test and improve the feature before expanding it more broadly.4

OpenAI says each purchase requires a security check with biometric verification such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan.56 OpenAI says the wallet also substitutes a payment token for the real card details, so neither the agent nor the merchant sees the actual card information.78 OpenAI also says users will still earn card rewards and keep protections such as the ability to dispute transactions.9

OpenAI expects eventually to let users delegate purchases entirely by setting limits such as spending caps, purchase categories or time periods.1011

Retailer blocking of AI agents may limit where the wallet works; The Verge and TechCrunch both point to Amazon, which has blocked agents. inference1213 OpenAI says websites must ensure agents are not inadvertently blocked, and it published a Commerce Partner Playbook to help retailers adapt to agentic commerce.1415

TechCrunch frames the launch as part of a push to find product-market fit for consumer AI tools, noting DoorDash and Uber have reported low agent-placed order numbers while Meta's Muse has seen AI-assisted shopping become a top activity.16
