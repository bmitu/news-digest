OpenAI model math claims (Astra)

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new article this week · single origin (The Verge)

OpenAI says it found a solution to the Navier-Stokes problem, using an internal AI model more powerful than the newly released GPT-6 Astra together with 10,000 concurrent agents.123 Navier-Stokes is one of seven Millennium Prize Problems, each carrying a $1 million reward. OpenAI says it began training the internal model on August 28th, and says the model has "exhibited unprecedented performance in our benchmarks, including mathematics."45

The Verge's Robert Hart calls the result an "undeniable achievement," but says the breakthrough was complicated by the unusual circumstances that prompted OpenAI to pursue the problem. Hart writes that, after hearing other researchers were making progress, OpenAI "seems to have" thrown its considerable resources into a last-minute effort to beat them to the punch.67 The controversy has surfaced allegations of scooping, spying and violations of academic norms. Abhishek Saha, a mathematics professor at Queen Mary University of London, says OpenAI has done the "kind of things that mathematicians will generally not do."891011

OpenAI announced a new independent panel of mathematicians to advise it and other AI companies on how new results are presented and released. Researchers told The Verge it is a good first step, but many asked what it will do, how much influence it will have, and whether such a small group can represent the wider community.121314

Commentator Zvi Mowshowitz writes that his forecasting "Question #4 was settled yesterday, with at least one instant Fields Medal, and also by solving Navier-Stokes."15
