OpenAI said earlier this month that its own team's agents had cracked a million-dollar problem in mathematics, according to MIT Technology Review's newsletter.1

Latent Space host Swyx describes the effort as semi-public in cost, at 10,000 agents running for 88 hours. The episode's summary line adds 130B output tokens and a roughly $40M-equivalent cost.23

A widely shared article asked whether the problem was the one that really mattered. MIT Technology Review says the piece did not argue that OpenAI's solution was wrong, only that the result may not be the one mathematicians care most about.45 A mathematician has also accused the models of possibly using some of his work without credit. The newsletter says this leaves people thinking either that OpenAI cheated or that the solution wasn't important anyway.67
