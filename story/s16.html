OpenAI model math claims (Astra)

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 16:18 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new article today · single origin (The Verge)

OpenAI says it found a solution to the Navier-Stokes problem, which has been unsolved for around 90 years, using an internal AI model more powerful than the newly released GPT-6 Astra alongside 10,000 concurrent agents.12 The Verge reports that Navier-Stokes is one of seven Millennium Prize Problems, each carrying a $1 million reward, and that OpenAI says it began training the internal model on August 28th.34

On a podcast, Swyx said the spend is semi-public at 10,000 agents over 88 hours, while Alex Zhang suggested that throwing 40 million dollars at the problem solved an unsolved problem. inference56 Zhang argued that OpenAI clearly trained its system to work as a swarm rather than just relying on a smart base model, though he also said that much of a swarm's output can be useless search. inference78

Abhishek Saha, a mathematics professor at Queen Mary University of London, said OpenAI has done the "kind of things that mathematicians will generally not do."9 Mathematician Andreas Thom, described by The Verge as a second mathematician challenging OpenAI, accused it of unethical and "dishonest" behavior and a lack of transparency about the origins of its training data, and raised concerns that his and colleagues' earlier ChatGPT interactions may have contributed to its success.1011

OpenAI announced a new independent panel of mathematicians to advise it and other AI companies (announced on a Monday, per an article dated Sep 23), and The Verge reports that researchers called it a good first step but had questions about its influence and whether so small a group can represent the wider mathematical community.1213 The Verge's later reporting describes the panel's rollout as messy and confusing, and says its first task is helping coordinate the release of scores more results OpenAI says its unreleased model has produced, which is already stirring dread among researchers.1415
