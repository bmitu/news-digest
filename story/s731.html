Google universal Gemini work agent

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
3 sources · single origin (Google)

Google announced a "universal" Gemini agent at its Gemini at Work / Google Cloud event on Thursday, available within the Gemini Enterprise app and letting users assign tasks from a single interface.123 The Verge reports it is currently available only to enterprise customers in private preview.4 Thomas Kurian, CEO of Google Cloud, said the agent can be given "objectives, not just instructions," planning the work, using custom skills and tools, and connecting to internal systems.56

The Verge reports that the agent can work inside Workspace apps and be reached from mobile, desktop, the web and third-party apps such as Slack or Microsoft 365, and that it keeps the same context across devices because it runs in the cloud.789 Google also says it can work with job-specific sub-agents and act as a "coworker agent" with its own identity and email address.1011 By default the agent picks the best model for a task, but users can choose one, including Anthropic's Claude models, and Google said the picker will later add open source and private models.1213

Early testers reportedly included On, Shopify and PayPal, and Google said new spending options such as real-time spend caps will help businesses control costs.1415

TechCrunch reports Google will focus on businesses first and consumers later; Sundar Pichai said this lets Google solve "harder problems around security, scale, and performance."1617 Pichai said Gemini has over 1 billion monthly active users and that nearly 90% of Fortune 100 businesses use Gemini Enterprise.1819 The Verge says the launch may offer a glimpse of Google's broader vision for a single-window assistant, noting the consumer Gemini Spark agent launched in May runs through a separate interface.2021
