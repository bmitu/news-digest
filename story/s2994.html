Census Bureau AI survey question change

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 6 · 2 independent origins: Simon Willison, U.S. Census Bureau

The U.S. Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS) now asks businesses about "Super Intelligence" or "SI" instead of artificial intelligence, with the wording changed beginning with the October 19, 2026 collection due to Executive Order 14434, according to the survey's about page as quoted by Simon Willison.12 Willison says the current published numbers still reflect the old AI wording, and the first results using the Super Intelligence wording are due for publication on Thursday.3

The survey goes out every two weeks to 200,000 of 1.2 million businesses, and the panel cycles so that each business completes it only every twelve weeks.45 The question changed in a material way on November 17th, 2025, which Willison says makes answers before and after that date not directly comparable.67

In Willison's updated chart of the Bureau's data, the largest companies (250+ employees) answered "yes" around 12% of the time in September 2025, around 30% by February 2026 under the broader wording, and around 40% by October 2026.89 Exponential View separately reported that 24.6% of firms said they use it in early October, up from 17.3% in late November 2025.10

Willison calls the order "the asinine Super Intelligence one" and says he cannot help laughing at the absurdity of examples such as "SI-powered data analysis" and "virtual SI agents for customer service".1112 He says it is an obvious open question whether the wording change will affect the survey results, and hopes the new language won't confuse respondents.1314 Exponential View remarked that the survey's examples include voice recognition, which it says is "not quite what Bostrom had in mind".15
