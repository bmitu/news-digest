Google Ask Maps trip itineraries

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Google)

Google announced new features for its Gemini-powered Ask Maps chatbot that let it build custom trip itineraries, ranging from a one-day outing to a multi-day trip, with real-time details about each stop.12 As an illustration, Google described planning a three-day road trip that includes coastal hikes and meals by the ocean. Ask Maps responds with an itinerary organized by day, along with local tips and numbered pins on the map, which users can share or, on desktop, export into Google Docs.3456 During a trip, users reportedly see live traffic, whether a place is open and the "vibe" at the next stop, and can adjust the plan by asking to add a scenic viewpoint or lunch spot.789 Maps directions also show "Know before you go" tips such as where to park or charge, weather, potential delays and tolls.10

Users can additionally search their saved lists for places with live availability, such as spots that can seat five people in the next 30 minutes.1112

Ask Maps is expanding to 30 European countries, including Spain, Germany and the U.K., bringing it to more than 200 countries and territories.1314 Itineraries and list search are available wherever Ask Maps is offered, while "Know before you go" tips and appointment finding launch first in the U.S.151617

TechCrunch frames the release as part of a broader shift toward AI positioned as an assistant that completes tasks rather than only answers questions, noting that Meta's AI agent Muse recently gained the ability to call businesses.1819
