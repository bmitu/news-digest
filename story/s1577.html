OpenAI acquires Orchestra Bio

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
3 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced that it has acquired Orchestra Bio, a platform that helps biopharma teams coordinate the complex process of drug development.1 Terms were not disclosed, though TechCrunch says the startup had reportedly raised around $20 million as of 2025 from backers including Andreessen Horowitz, Atria and 8VC.2

OpenAI describes Orchestra's platform as one where scientists and program leaders delegate the monitoring and tracking of studies to agents, which help identify which activities and timelines are affected by a problem such as a failed manufacturing batch.34

OpenAI says it is bringing Orchestra's team and technology together with Rosalind, its AI models and tools for life sciences research.5 According to TechCrunch, OpenAI says it is adding Orchestra's team and technology to work on Rosalind, its AI model for life sciences research, and on the related Rosalind Workbench tool, a harness that aims to help researchers plan experiments and analyze results.6 OpenAI says Rosalind already has big-name customers, including Moderna and Novo Nordisk.7 A Latent Space newsletter likewise describes the deal as extending OpenAI's life-sciences work beyond research tools into operational execution.8
