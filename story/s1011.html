Google Fairwind program for cyber defenders

Last scan Mon Oct 19, 23:45 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Google)

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon and said it is beginning an expansion to hundreds of additional Fairwind Program partners, giving trusted cyber defenders access to the model.12 Cybersecurity defenders can apply to join the Fairwind Program to get access to Argon without cyber guardrails, on a rolling basis.34 Google says Argon ties for #1 on CWE-Bench (68%) for vulnerability remediation and is its most resilient model against indirect prompt injections.5

Google says Wiz, which had early access, is using Argon for its "Scan for Good" initiative, which protects critical infrastructure for free.67 In one case, Google says, Argon chained complex vulnerabilities to help Wiz find and fix a public file download bypass that exposed a master key and granted administrator access, safeguarding hundreds of thousands of private patient records.89 In another, working with a federal agency, Argon reportedly helped identify and remediate a gap that risked exposing sensitive data and government infrastructure.10

Google says that, because Argon is highly capable, it worked with third-party partners on measuring capabilities and safeguards, and that external bio red teaming could unlock less than 1% of the bio sub-tasks tested.1112 Google also says violations on an adversarial stress-test set fell nearly 2-fold, and that the same capabilities that help defenders require strong safeguards against malicious actors.1314
