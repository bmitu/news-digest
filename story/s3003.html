Siri AI App Intents in iOS 27.3

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Apple, Federico Viticci

Apple announced with the iOS 27.3 update, rolling out on Tuesday, that developers can integrate Siri AI's new features in "all apps," letting the assistant use a user's personal context, process natural language prompts and use on-screen awareness to complete requests, according to The Verge.12 The update is rolling out as part of a Developer Preview and adds an "app skills" feature that third-party developers can create to combine several tasks into a single action.3 The Verge notes, citing 9to5Mac, that Apple had previously made a smaller set of features available to third-party developers.4 A Latent Space newsletter roundup also noted, attributing it to Federico Viticci (viticci), that any app can expose content and actions to Siri AI via App Intents.5

Apple's developer documentation says users won't have to mention an app's name while it is in the foreground, during a Live Activity, or in a conversation with Siri AI about the app.6

Apple is also bringing Siri AI's expanded capabilities to its own apps, including Calendar, Mail, Maps, Messages, Photos, Safari and others.7 Apple says users can do things like find a coffee shop they visited last week or archive files not opened in over a year, and can ask Siri AI what it can do in each app.89
