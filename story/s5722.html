Alice Huang Chinese intelligence allegations

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 24 · single origin (The New York Times)

The New York Times reported that Alice Huang stayed in contact with an officer of China's Ministry of State Security from spring 2025 until this fall, citing multiple people familiar with U.S. intelligence reports.12 According to the Times, those contacts overlapped at least partly with her secret affair with Jensen Huang, then Nvidia's chief, who has since been reinstated as CEO. inference345

However, a Wall Street Journal report says a classified briefing found no evidence that China had directed Alice Huang to spy on Jensen Huang.6 The Journal also reported that some U.S. officials called the intelligence links circumstantial, since Chinese business leaders are often forced to stay in touch with security agencies, while others saw a national security risk in signs that she gathered and relayed information.78 Under Xi, China's spy agencies have become so sprawling and quick to seize opportunities that it is now hard to separate a recruited agent from someone being used unknowingly, a wider issue that the question of spy or not overlooks, according to what Journal interviews with China analysts, lawmakers and former and serving U.S. intelligence officials suggest.910

Warzel, speaking in The Atlantic, said lawmakers in both parties are upset about Alice Huang's alleged intelligence tie and its possible bearing on questions such as whether Megaspeed, a company she worked with, evaded U.S. chip export bans.1112 He said there is good evidence to suggest a lot of chip smuggling was going on and that Megaspeed certainly had some role in that ecosystem, though it is unknown whether Jensen Huang and Nvidia were directly related to it, and Megaspeed "has declined all allegations of wrongdoing."1314

President Donald Trump has played down Alice Huang's role, saying that attractive Chinese women are always labeled spies.15 Warzel argues that Trump and Silicon Valley executives are sidestepping the geopolitical concerns and treating the scandal as a question of infidelity.1617
