Grok Bot usage limits

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · single origin, unclear which

Over Halloween weekend, some Grok Bot users found their weekly usage limits unexpectedly maxed out, according to TechCrunch.1 Some users reported on X that their usage jumped from as low as 50% to 100%.2

Elon Musk confirmed in an X post on Sunday that this was not a glitch, writing that the cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue.34 Nate B Jones describes a customer who had run through their allowance with four days left before the reset and asked for it early, which prompted Musk's explanation.56 TechCrunch reports that Musk has not addressed the issue on his main feed, and has not yet shared how subscribers' usage limits will be affected going forward.78

TechCrunch presents Grok Bot as a success story for the newly public SpaceX, which said in a filing last month that AI makes up 52% of company income.9 Its renamed "Super Intelligence" division reportedly turned a profit of $431 million, which would have been higher without $1.2 billion of one-time costs tied to the Cursor acquisition.1011 Musk recently described Grok Bot as having "the fastest growth of a product that I’ve ever seen in my life."12

Jones notes that xAI's own September procurement write-up reported Grok Bot found 43 paid seats with no activity in 90 days, producing $14,220 in savings, and argues both things can be true: an agent can create substantial value for its user while costing its provider too much to supply at the current price.1314
