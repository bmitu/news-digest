Ring Rover and Drone Cam

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: Amazon Ring, The Verge

Amazon-owned Ring announced a new Ring Robotics line on November 10, 2026, introducing the Ring Drone Cam, an AI-powered indoor flying security camera, and the Ring Rover Cam, a security robot for businesses.123 Both devices run on a system called AI Pilot, which Ring says combines vision models, sensors and cloud processing to map a space and navigate around obstacles.456

The Drone Cam is due January 27, 2027, and The Verge and Ars Technica put its price at $249.99 and about $250, while TechCrunch says $270. inference789 It offers 2K video and a 148-degree field of view, and Ring claims five to eight minutes of flight per charge, with about eight minutes of charging for each minute flown.1011 The Drone Cam still needs FCC authorization; Ring says it is in the process of obtaining certification, and founder Jamie Siminoff said it complies with FCC rules and is assembled in the US.121314

The Rover Cam costs about $1,000, is open for preorder, and is expected to ship December 16, 2026, with 4K video and a mast for seeing over obstacles; Ars Technica notes it cannot climb stairs.1516171819

The Verge's reviewer saw a one-minute demo flight with smooth navigation but did not see obstacle avoidance, stairs or night use, and questions whether the drone can replace multiple cameras given its short flight time.202122 The Verge also notes the drone depends entirely on an internet connection and can only land without Wi-Fi, and Ars Technica reports Amazon warns of degraded performance on reflective, blank or transparent surfaces.2324

Ring says the devices have privacy built in, with videos protected by its Throw Away the Key Encryption, but the outlets expect new privacy controversy. inference25262728 Ars Technica reports that the Electronic Frontier Foundation calls the encryption an improvement but well short of end-to-end encryption, and criticizes Siminoff's "surveillance-first-privacy-last" approach.2930
