Claude Haiku 5.5 launch and pricing changes

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
4 sources · 1044 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, Simon Willison

Anthropic announced Claude Haiku 5.5, which it calls "the cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model we’ve ever released," and says it costs around 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5 on average.123 Anthropic positions the model for high-volume, cost-sensitive work such as summaries, compactions, database queries and classification, and as a subagent alongside Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5.456 Anthropic itself says Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 remain better choices for complex agentic coding tasks.7 The same day Anthropic halved Sonnet 5.5 cache-read prices from $0.20 to $0.10 per million tokens, which it says makes Sonnet 5.5 about 20% cheaper on most agentic work.8910

Haiku 5.5 is priced at $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens for prompts up to 100k tokens, rising 5x to $0.50/$2.50 beyond that, versus $1/$5 for Haiku 4.5.111213 Anthropic says prompts up to 100,000 tokens made up around 90% of requests to the previous Haiku model.14 Simon Willison notes the price matches OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna, which he says is 10x cheaper than Haiku 4.5.15 Willison says a new, less generous tokenizer uses around 1.25x as many tokens as Haiku 4.5 for the same long prompt, which he calls a hidden price increase; he concludes Haiku looks good within 100,000 tokens but Luna is the better deal above that.1617

Per the Latent Space summary, Artificial Analysis found heavy token use is the main caveat: about 162k output tokens per Index task at max effort, roughly 3x Luna's ~50k.18 Vals reportedly found that, with heavier reasoning, Haiku 5.5 costs more per test than Haiku 4.5 on every shared benchmark.1920 Latent Space suggests these effects likely explain why Cursor's "10x cheaper on short requests" differs from Anthropic's "75% cheaper on average." inference21

Latent Space says the launch is widely read as aimed at OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna, and reports that Artificial Analysis measured an AutomationBench score of 35% versus 53–60% for Luna, Gemini 3.8 Flash and GLM-5.3 Flash, with a pre-release safety bug causing the model to over-refuse.2223 Reactions were largely positive: Zvi Mowshowitz said it looks promising given the price, and Theo praised the tiered pricing for making clear what the model is for.242526
