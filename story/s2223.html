Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe release

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
4 sources · 133 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Latent Space, Nvidia

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, a free, open-weight model that transcribes audio in up to 40 languages, along with version 2 of its open source Granary speech dataset, TechCrunch reports.12 Nvidia's Hugging Face post says the model combines transcription, speaker attribution, translation and audio question answering in one checkpoint, translating 31 languages to or from English, attributing speakers in 13, and handling recordings up to an hour with up to eight speakers.34 TechCrunch notes a drawback: the model works with recorded audio, not livestreams, though Nvidia promises the 30-billion-parameter model can run on a single chip.56 Sources differ on the model's size: TechCrunch gives 30 billion parameters, while Latent Space describes a 30B mixture-of-experts model with about 4B active parameters. inference78

Nvidia says the model is first among open-weight models on the Hugging Face Open ASR Leaderboard for English, and has the lowest error rate, 18.26%, on David AI's 21-language diarization leaderboard, narrowly ahead of Nvidia's own Nemotron 3 Diarization at 18.38%.910 On VoiceArena's English benchmark, Nvidia reports the model leads 14 systems at 32.4% cpWER, ahead of NVIDIA Multitalker Parakeet with Nemotron 3 Diarization at 34.2%.11 Artificial Analysis, as relayed by Latent Space, measured a 2.8% AA-WER, tied with Voxtral Small for first among open models, and a 75x speed factor at $2 per 1,000 minutes on DeepInfra.1213

Granary v2's public manifest covers 463,062 hours across 48 languages and 16 source corpora, up from 25 languages in Granary v1, and Nvidia says fine-tuning cut word error rate by 45.8% relative to the best prompt in a medical case study.1415

TechCrunch frames the release as part of a busy period for AI voice, citing Mistral's Voxtral 3, ElevenLabs' Realtime and Salesforce's Launa-Omni, and notes Nvidia claims to beat rivals on benchmarks.1617
