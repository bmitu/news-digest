Gemini Decision-1 API launch

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
5 sources · 562 points on Hacker News · 3 independent origins: Google, Latent Space, Simon Willison

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new model called Gemini Decision 1, which scores a fixed set of options in one forward pass instead of generating text token by token.123 Sundar Pichai and Logan Kilpatrick announced it, and a Latent Space newsletter called it a surprise that Google beat Anthropic to launching a production decision model.4

Google says the model costs $0.10 per million input tokens with no output tokens billed, answers in 150-250 ms end to end, and accepts text, images, audio, video and PDFs of up to 256K tokens along with up to 1,000 questions.5678 It supports yes/no, multiple-choice (2 to 255 options) and rubric-score questions, and each answer carries a confidence score from 0 to 1.91011 Google claims the model leads its benchmark suite with the lowest calibration error and is nearly 5x cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite with the Interactions API on the shared suite.1213

Against TypeSafe's Jev, Google's own tests reportedly show Decision 1 winning 5 of 7 shared benchmarks, including 92.2% versus 90.0% on JevBench, though Jev is cheaper at $0.042 per million input tokens.14 Simon Willison notes the API is almost identical to Jev's, reusing its term for yes/no questions, and that unlike Jev (text only) it handles video; in his test it returned probabilities like 0.92 and 0.05 rather than Jev's and OpenAI's flat 1.0 or 0.0.1516

Artificial Analysis found more mixed independent results: a Decision Index score of 66 that ties Jev 1.14 and sits 7 points behind the top two, with 33% of documents fully correct on AA-Paperclip versus 63% for Drex Max.1718 Artificial Analysis also reported a cost of $345 per 1M tasks versus $156 for Jev, and 211 ms average time per task versus 92 ms for Jev.1920

Google says internal teams already use it, for example Gemini Notebook for routing queries with up to 40% lower latency, and LangChain shipped day-zero support.2122
