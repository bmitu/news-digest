Anthropic $150M Genesis Mission commitment

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic announced a commitment of $150 million over three years to the Genesis Mission, a federal initiative to accelerate scientific and technological discovery through AI.1 Anthropic said it announced the commitment at the Science: A New Golden Age Summit, hosted by the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy in Washington, DC.2 Anthropic says the funding will make Claude available to more than 15 agencies in the mission, including NASA, the National Institutes of Health and the National Science Foundation.3

Over the three years, Anthropic says it will provide Claude, Claude Code and API credits to several hundred Genesis Mission research projects.4 Anthropic also says it will partner with agencies and national laboratories on priorities including fusion energy and quantum computing, and provide training, onboarding and technical support to scientists.56

Anthropic says this builds on its partnership with the US Department of Energy and the Genesis Mission, first announced last December, through which it has brought Claude to scientists across the national laboratories.78 Anthropic describes the commitment as part of broader science efforts, including the Claude Science workbench and 10,000 free and discounted Claude seats for academic scientists.9 Anthropic says it shares the administration's view that AI can have a profound and positive impact on scientific discovery.10
