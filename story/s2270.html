OpenAI API credits for subscribers

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI has launched a benefit that gives eligible ChatGPT subscribers monthly API credits scaled to their plan, according to Simon Willison and a Latent Space roundup. inference12 Simon Willison says subscribers on $100, $200 or $500 plans get the same quantity of credits, which can be applied to one of their API organizations.3 Latent Space, citing @OpenAIDevs, adds that business plans pool up to $500 per workspace.4 The Latent Space roundup also lists an @OpenAIDevs post about up to $500/month in API credits with the figure 6.9K.5

Willison welcomes the change, saying he has subscriptions with both companies, calling it great news for him, and saying he may ship some public-facing demos because the credits are covered by his existing subscriptions.6
