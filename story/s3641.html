Employee petition to bring back Huang

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 14 · single origin, unclear which

A petition urging Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO after his resignation has drawn well over 100,000 signatures, with counts that grew across sources from about 115,000 to more than 200,000. inference1234 According to the sources, people who have put their names to the petition include Elon Musk, Amjad Masad, Tim Sweeney and Garry Tan. The site is also said to show 5,965 Nvidia staff members currently employed, 1,108 ex-employees, and 63,063 shareholders among its backers.567

The petition says Huang was removed without meaningful due process and asks the board to bring him back while an independent investigation establishes the facts.8910 Huang himself said in his first public statement since resigning that he would be honored to return as CEO if the board decides that is in the interest of shareholders, employees, the company and America.11 In the same statement he said he had made serious mistakes in his personal life and would cooperate with the board's independent investigation.1213

However, Nvidia's board appears unlikely to comply: CNBC's source said it is focused on an investigation and not discussing his return, and Nvidia executives said on an earnings call they have no plans to reappoint him.141516 On TechCrunch's Equity podcast, host Kirsten Korosec said interim CEO Colette Kress announced that Nvidia will stop selling H200 chips in China, a step she linked to the Trump administration's talk of closing off remote access to the chips. inference1718 Anthony Ha said critics of Nvidia's China stance estimate that 20% of its business comes from China.19 Korosec said she believes the board wants to look in control and not be pushed into a decision by Elon Musk, while Tim De Chant suggested that the board's quick rejection of the petitions may point to more happening behind the scenes than is publicly known. inference2021 Resistance also exists in Congress, where one senator told Politico that reinstatement was "one of the worst ideas I've heard today."22

Supporters question the spy allegation behind the scandal: David Sacks said the narrative is collapsing because US officials have not confirmed that Alice Huang was a spy.2324 Some backers go further and suggest a plot: Amjad Masad called the timing of Huang's resignation "suspicious," while SemiAnalysis posted a self-described "conspiracy theory take" speculating that Huang's removal is a way to "remove opposition to an extremely hawkish China export control bill."252627

Other reactions are mixed: Palmer Luckey says China is thrilled to have Huang out, while Sam Altman declined to say whether Huang could return, and Donald Trump said he "wouldn't mind" a reinstatement.282930 Not everyone backs a return, as one Huang ally told the Financial Times that having an affair with a subordinate is a fireable offense, and MIT Technology Review argues that new research on Chinese access to Blackwell chips makes reinstatement more of a long shot.3132
