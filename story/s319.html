ChatGPT Spaces, Pages and collaboration features

Last scan Sun Oct 4, 19:31 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · 1 new article today · 2 independent origins: Nate B. Jones, OpenAI

OpenAI announced ChatGPT Spaces and Pages at its DevDay 2026 as shared workspaces for teammates, ChatGPT and agents, alongside a larger launch of "dots" agents and the GPT-6.1 Sol model. inference123 OpenAI describes a space as a new home for a team to collaborate with AI, where ChatGPT can keep it organized based on the instructions you provide.45 Latent Space describes ChatGPT Spaces, together with Dots, as OpenAI's answer to Notion and the office productivity suite.6

Spaces and Pages are available to Pro, Business and Enterprise plans on desktop and web, with mobile finding, reading and sharing now and mobile creation and editing coming soon.7 Business and Enterprise plans also get team tasks for recurring work and the ability to mention @ChatGPT in a channel, thread or DM. The Meetings plugin, in beta in the ChatGPT desktop app on macOS for Pro and Business users with Enterprise coming soon, saves summaries with action items in ChatGPT Space.891011

Commentator Nate B Jones calls Space, Dots and GPT-6.1 Sol the three announcements he would watch, and argues that shared pages give one person's reasoning somewhere to stay for colleagues, though the value depends on people contributing the relevant knowledge.312 Jones notes that sharing a Page does not automatically share private chats or memories, though anything written on the Page, including details drawn from memory, is visible to collaborators.13 Jones sees this as putting OpenAI into the workplace territory Microsoft pursues with Copilot and Autopilot and Anthropic with Claude Tag, and advises starting where a team's decisions, documents and people already are.1415

What does GPT-6.1 Sol cost per million tokens, and how does it perform against Astra and Opus 5.5?

OpenAI prices GPT-6.1 Sol at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.10 per million.12 That compares with $10 and $50 for Astra, so Sol is about a fifth of Astra's price. inference3 Independent tester Artificial Analysis puts Sol 1 point below Astra on its Intelligence Index, at $0.72 per task versus $3.26.4 In one planted-bug test by @PawelHuryn, Sol found 44 of 105 bugs for $6.56, against 45 for Astra at $33 and 41.7 for Opus 5.5 at $58.53.5

Can teammates use @ChatGPT in Slack or Teams without needing their own ChatGPT license, and which tools can it use?

Yes. OpenAI says teammates can add context and refine results in the same Slack or Teams conversation without needing an individual ChatGPT license.1 OpenAI says people can mention @ChatGPT in a channel, thread or DM to get help with their team's work.2 According to OpenAI, it can use tools connected by the admin, or a user's own tools with that user's permissions.3 However, OpenAI has not published a specific list of which tools @ChatGPT can use in Slack or Teams.

When will collaborative slides arrive in Spaces, and can they be exported to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

OpenAI says it will soon let users create interactive slides with ChatGPT and their team, available to all Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans in the coming weeks.12 OpenAI says multiple teammates and agents can edit the deck at the same time and leave comments, as part of the slides feature that is coming soon.3 Nate B Jones likewise notes that collaborative slides and spreadsheets are still to come.4 OpenAI says slides can be presented in ChatGPT or exported to PowerPoint or Google Slides with formatting intact.5 No exact launch date for collaborative slides was reported.
