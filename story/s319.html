ChatGPT Spaces, Pages and collaboration features

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 16:18 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · 2 independent origins: Nate B. Jones, OpenAI

At its DevDay, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Space and Pages as shared workspaces for people and AI agents, alongside its new Dots agents and GPT-6.1 Sol model. inference12 Latent Space frames ChatGPT Spaces as OpenAI's answer to Notion and the office productivity suite.3

Nate B Jones says Space and Pages are available on Pro, Business, and Enterprise, with collaborative slides and spreadsheets still to come.4 Jones says Business and Enterprise also get team tasks for recurring work and ChatGPT participation in Slack and Teams.5

On privacy, Jones says sharing a Page does not automatically share private chats or memories, though anything written on the Page, including details drawn from memory, is visible to collaborators.6

Jones argues that shared pages give one person's reasoning somewhere to stay so colleagues need not re-explain the work, but says the value depends on people making relevant knowledge available, including why a decision was made.7 Jones says the launch puts OpenAI into the working day that Microsoft pursues with Copilot and Autopilot and Anthropic with Claude Tag, and he suggests teams start where their decisions, documents, and people already are.89

How much does GPT-6.1 Sol cost per million tokens, and how does it compare with GPT-6 Astra on price and benchmark results?

OpenAI prices GPT-6.1 Sol at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.10 per million tokens.12 GPT-6 Astra costs $10/$50 per million input/output tokens, so Sol is priced at one-fifth of Astra, which matches OpenAI's own pitch of "a fifth of the price". inference345 On benchmarks, OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol matches Astra on DeepSWE v1.1 at roughly one-fifth of the cost, and nearly matches Astra's intelligence on agentic coding, computer use and professional work.678

Will the Meetings plugin keep a recording of my meetings, or what happens to the audio after the notes are made?

OpenAI says the Meetings plugin deletes the audio once your notes are ready, and that it can't be accessed or replayed afterward.1 What stays is the notes: OpenAI says the plugin saves personalized summaries with action items in ChatGPT Space, which you can keep private or share with your team.2 The plugin is in beta in the ChatGPT desktop app on macOS for Pro and Business users, with Enterprise coming soon.3

Do teammates need their own ChatGPT license to take part when someone mentions @ChatGPT in a Slack or Teams conversation?

No. OpenAI says that when someone mentions @ChatGPT in a channel, thread or DM, teammates can add context and refine the results in the same conversation without needing an individual ChatGPT license.12 The feature is available to Business and Enterprise plans; a separate OpenAI help page also lists Edu accounts.34 An OpenAI help page says usage for requests handled through @ChatGPT's service account is attributed to that account, but if a member authorizes work using their personal connections, that work runs under their account and the usage is attributed to them.56

When will collaborative slides arrive, and can they be exported to PowerPoint or Google Slides?

OpenAI says it will soon let users create interactive slides with ChatGPT and their team, available to all Pro, Business and Enterprise plans "in the coming weeks", in its DevDay recap dated September 29, 2026.12 A separate OpenAI community post, published the same day, likewise says collaborative slides are coming in the next few weeks.3 OpenAI also says multiple teammates and agents can edit a deck at the same time and leave comments.4 Yes, the slides can be exported: OpenAI says users can present in ChatGPT or export to PowerPoint or Google Slides with formatting intact.5
