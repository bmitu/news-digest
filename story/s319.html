ChatGPT Spaces, Pages and collaboration features

2 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · single origin (OpenAI)

At its DevDay 2026, OpenAI announced ChatGPT Spaces and Pages, which it describes as shared workspaces and documents built for human and agent collaboration.12 OpenAI says a space is a dedicated place where teammates, ChatGPT, and a user's dot can build on shared knowledge, and that ChatGPT can keep it organized based on instructions the user provides.34 OpenAI says anything you can do in ChatGPT can be done on a page, including writing, research, charts and images, and users can create a page in a conversation and invite their team to contribute.56

Spaces and Pages are available to all Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans on the desktop app and web, according to OpenAI; finding, reading and sharing pages also work on mobile, while mobile creation and editing are coming soon.7

OpenAI also announced team features: Business and Enterprise users can share pages, slides, plugins and spreadsheets as a team and delegate recurring work, such as weekly project updates, to team tasks that run on a schedule or in response to events.89 Users can also mention @ChatGPT in a channel, thread or DM, and teammates can add context without needing an individual ChatGPT license, per OpenAI.1011 OpenAI says interactive slides will arrive in the coming weeks, with multiple teammates and agents able to edit at once and export to PowerPoint or Google Slides.1213

Latent Space's AINews framed the launch as OpenAI's answer to Notion and the office productivity suite.14

Does a Meetings plugin feed notes into ChatGPT Spaces, and what happens to the meeting audio?

Yes, OpenAI says its Meetings plugin takes notes and saves personalized summaries with action items in ChatGPT Space.1 Users can keep those summaries private or share them with their team, and then ask ChatGPT to update a project plan or draft a follow-up, according to OpenAI.2 At DevDay, OpenAI demoed the meeting app inside ChatGPT, where a user picks AI note-taking for an upcoming calendar meeting and gets a summary of follow-up items afterward.34 On the audio, OpenAI says it is deleted once the notes are ready and cannot be accessed or replayed.5 The plugin is available in beta in the ChatGPT desktop app on macOS for all Pro and Business users, with Enterprise coming soon.6
