ChatGPT Spaces, Pages and collaboration features

3 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

At its DevDay developer conference in San Francisco on Tuesday, OpenAI launched Space, a shared workspace inside ChatGPT where co-workers can collaborate with the chatbot and with their own Dots.12 CEO Sam Altman said, "Your pages and files all live together in Space, like they would in a drive," adding "But spaces feel alive."3 OpenAI says Space lets teammates, ChatGPT and a user's dot build on shared knowledge, and that ChatGPT can keep the space organized based on instructions provided.45

OpenAI also launched Pages, which it describes as a new type of document built for human and agent collaboration, where users can write, research, generate charts, create images or visualize information.67 TechCrunch says Pages appears to be OpenAI's answer to Google Docs and Microsoft Word.8 Collaborative slides were also announced but are not yet available; an OpenAI representative said they will roll out in the coming weeks, and OpenAI says they can be exported to PowerPoint or Google Slides.910

OpenAI also announced a Meetings plugin that saves personalized summaries with action items in ChatGPT Space; it is available in beta in the ChatGPT desktop app on macOS for Pro and Business users, with Enterprise coming soon.1112

TechCrunch frames the launch as OpenAI increasingly going after Microsoft's workplace software business, even as Microsoft and Salesforce try to become more like OpenAI.1314 Latent Space's newsletter likewise characterizes Spaces as OpenAI's answer to Notion and the office productivity suite.15
