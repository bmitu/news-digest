Mistral Large 4

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
6 sources · 2849 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 6 · 9 new articles this week · 4 independent origins: Mistral, Simon Willison, TechCrunch, Wired

Mistral AI launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4 (nicknamed "Le Chonk"), a 1 trillion-parameter natively multimodal model with 49 billion active parameters, available through its API, with weights promised by the end of the month.1234 Mistral says it trained the model from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own European datacenters, while TechCrunch quotes Mistral VP Science Pierre Stock as saying about 4,000 Nvidia GPUs were used.56

Mistral claims the model is competitive with the strongest open-source models globally and significantly outperforms any open-weight model from the US or Europe, and says it is state-of-the-art among open models in cybersecurity, finance and law.78 Mistral reports that on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index the model ranks among the top five globally, and that on a test asking a model to reproduce and patch a real vulnerability it scores 82%, the highest of any model. Mistral also says several leading closed models score near zero on the same test because they refuse to perform the task.91011 In a Surge AI blind human evaluation of coding quality, Mistral says ML4 Preview ranked second of five models at 3.74, behind only Claude Opus 5 at 4.22.1213

Independent views are more measured: Simon Willison says Artificial Analysis scores the model 38, just behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash, a big improvement over Mistral Large 3's 9, and describes it as maybe about six months behind the frontier.141516 One Reddit commenter, as summarized by Latent Space, tested the model and found it "quite dated", with chess performance near a Nov 2024 Mistral model.1718

Mistral says it is red-teaming the model with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners and state authorities before the weights are released, and TechCrunch notes the model is not yet open-weight.1920
