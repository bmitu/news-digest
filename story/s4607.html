Huang offers or seeks return as Nvidia CEO

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
6 sources · 314 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · 5 independent origins: Ars Technica, Financial Times, Jensen Huang, Reuters, The Wall Street Journal

Jensen Huang, who resigned as Nvidia CEO after reports of affairs, said in a statement that he would be honored to return as CEO if the board decides that is in the best interest of shareholders, employees, the company and America.123 In the same statement Huang said he had made serious mistakes in his personal life and was committed to cooperating with the board's independent investigation.4567

The Financial Times reported that Huang had discussed a comeback with confidants, and one ally said they were assembling a "war room" of Silicon Valley executives to lobby for his return and seek White House support.8910 An open letter urging the board to "bring Jensen back" has drawn Elon Musk and Tim Sweeney among its backers, though The Verge says its signature counts, over 150,000 at one point, are hard to verify and The Verge later cited 193,000.111213

The board appears unreceptive: it was reportedly "not considering" his return, CNN reported the same, and TechCrunch says Nvidia chairman Harvey C. Jones affirmed the company is not considering reinstating him.141516 Opposition also comes from Washington: Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for a "dramatic shake-up" at Nvidia, and one senator called reinstatement "one of the worst ideas I've heard today," while President Trump said a return "certainly isn't a bad idea."171819

Andrew Yoon of CivAI said he thinks it is "pretty unlikely" that Huang returns any time soon, and even an ally conceded that an affair with a subordinate is a fireable offense.202122
