OpenAI enterprise marketplace

2 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced a new enterprise app marketplace at its Dev Day on Tuesday, with some 30-plus launch partners including Adobe, Figma, Salesforce, ServiceNow, HubSpot, CrowdStrike and Baseten, according to TechCrunch.1 OpenAI says eligible customers can apply part of their OpenAI commitment toward approved partner software.2 Latent Space's newsletter describes the marketplace as a B2B feature in which enterprises can apply OpenAI commits to open models via Baseten.3 Commentator @apoorv03, cited by Latent Space, frames the marketplace as OpenAI competing to own the enterprise AI budget.4

The marketplace was one part of a wider push that TechCrunch says aims to disrupt the traditional app store model by making ChatGPT a place where software is discovered, launched and used by people and agents.56 OpenAI also introduced "Sign in with ChatGPT", which lets users bring their AI allowance to third-party apps, with 16 launch partners including Cognition's Devin, Notion and Vercel, according to TechCrunch.78 OpenAI says ChatGPT now has 1.2 billion weekly users and that its Dots agents can connect to an ecosystem of over 4,000 apps.910 However, TechCrunch notes that OpenAI did not announce a billing or revenue-sharing system comparable to the economic layer of traditional app stores, and has not said the features would remove the need for separate third-party subscriptions.1112
