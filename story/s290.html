Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows AI vision, surging costs

5 sources · 134 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · 4 new articles today · 2 independent origins: Ars Technica, Reuters

Anthropic has formally warned investors that its technology may pose "existential risks to humanity" in an IPO prospectus circulated to a small group of partners, according to the Financial Times.1 The FT says nearly a third of the S-1 filing is devoted to risk factors, including AI models that could manipulate, blackmail, or behave unpredictably.23 TechCrunch calls the disclosures grim for a company whose backers expect a huge listing, and says the "existential risks" wording appears to be a first in the SEC's database.45

Reuters, as cited by TechCrunch, reported that Anthropic recorded an operating loss of more than $8 billion in 2025, with revenue up twelvefold to nearly $4.6 billion and total operating expenses of almost $13 billion.678 Per Reuters, as cited by TechCrunch, the prospectus also reveals plans to spend $518 billion on cloud, computing, and infrastructure in the coming years.910 The FT reports that second-quarter 2026 revenue was $11.5 billion and that Anthropic is on track for a second straight quarter of operating profit on an adjusted basis.1112 The filing also flags customer concentration, with close to a quarter of last year's revenue coming from two clients, according to people familiar with it.1314

Anthropic's backers are confident it can list at more than $2 trillion, over double its May funding-round level, though the FT says safety concerns now threaten to dim that optimism.151617 Separately, Altman said OpenAI won't go public until it can make confident safety claims, though waiting too long would be "bad for the world".1819
