Anthropic's IPO prospectus shows AI vision, surging costs

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 16:18 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 29 · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic has filed for an IPO, with Latent Space reporting a potential valuation above $2 trillion, and Zvi Mowshowitz saying it would be the largest IPO of all time and that the prospectus leaked. inference123 The filing reportedly lists about $518 billion in compute obligations and roughly 80 pages of risk factors, and Mowshowitz says the prospectus warns that Anthropic's products pose an existential risk to humanity. inference45 Mowshowitz says the $518 billion in compute commitments runs over 7-10 years, which he says means the company still has work to do.6

Latent Space reports Q2 revenue of about $11.5 billion and ARR reportedly above $65 billion, while Mowshowitz says ARR is rising above $100 billion, so the sources differ on run-rate revenue. inference78 Latent Space adds that OpenAI's ARR is reportedly nearing $70 billion, while Mowshowitz says OpenAI is looking to raise another $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation. inference910 Mowshowitz says valuations keep going up because the models keep improving and the revenue graphs keep going up (and also the Efficient Market Hypothesis is false), and that Anthropic has gone from a private valuation of roughly $500 billion in secondary markets to roughly $2 trillion.1112

Mowshowitz says the Anthropic cofounders are seeking 50.1% voting control after the IPO, alongside the Long Term Benefit Trust's ability to appoint board members who could eventually overrule that.13 Mowshowitz says he understands anyone who would not invest under such a structure, but finds it entirely appropriate and thinks it makes the company more valuable.14
