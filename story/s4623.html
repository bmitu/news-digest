Meta Muse Charm $300 price

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (The Information)

Meta is reportedly planning to price its Muse Charm, a small AI gadget with a screen, at $300, according to The Information.12 Meta did not deny the report; spokesperson Tyler Yee said the company is "charmed by the interest in our AI devices" and will have more to share soon.34

Mark Zuckerberg revealed the device earlier this year, said it was expected to ship in time for the December holidays, and gave no price at that point.5 Zuckerberg said the device packs in the Muse experience, including real-time voice and avatar technology, and The Verge says a fingerprint sensor lets users tap to talk to a Muse agent without unlocking a phone.67 The Information also reports that the Charm will come with a free 12-month Verizon 5G data plan, and that a Meta smartwatch has been pushed to 2027 while the company focuses on getting the Charm out.89

TechCrunch calls $300 arguably steep for a keychain-sized product, though in line with rivals' pricing, citing Friend's wearable at $250.1011 By contrast, Amazon's Bee Frontier lists for $75 (or $52.49 on a Black Friday sale), and TechCrunch says it remains far cheaper than the Charm even with its $99/year subscription.1213 TechCrunch says it remains to be seen whether customers will buy it, noting that AI wearables have had a rough run so far, as with the Humane Ai pin.14
