Critical PHP bugs patched

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
4 sources · 3 independent origins: PHP Foundation, TechCrunch, The Hacker News

PHP maintainers announced on October 20 that they would release an emergency update for two vulnerabilities in PHP, which the Center for AI Safety estimates is used by about 70% of websites whose server-side language can be identified.12 The Center for AI Safety says several security researchers, all using AI, developed an exploit, and that OpenAI's Patch the Planet initiative led a team that developed fixes issued on October 21.34 TechCrunch reports that Wiz's CTO of Exposure Management & AppSec, Tomer Schwartz, took part in the scramble to fix the bugs alongside OpenAI, research group Calif and the PHP team, and says the researchers who found the bugs claim this is only the beginning as AI speeds up discovery and exploitation of vulnerabilities.5678 Nathan Lambert says the PHP disclosure was in the works and people were fixing it when it got leaked.9

The National Security Agency urged website owners to apply the fixes immediately or take sites offline until patched, and many sites, including government ones, were temporarily taken down.1011 According to Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince, the company detected millions of attempts to exploit the PHP flaws across its network after the announcement.12 Sources differ on exploitation: a Latent Space roundup says a roughly 15-year-old PHP remote code execution bug is confirmed exploited in the wild, while podcaster Florian Brand says there are numerous reports that mass exploitation has not happened yet. inference1314

Sam Altman said in a recent interview, "I no longer think we’re going to be able to just patch all the bugs. I think we’re going to have to completely switch the defense model."15
