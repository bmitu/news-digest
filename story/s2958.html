Claude tiers renamed

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic renamed its Claude subscription tiers so the lineup is now Free, Pro ($20/month), Max ($100/month) and Ultra ($200/month), according to Zvi Mowshowitz's roundup.12 Latent Space's recap says the former Max 5x is now "Max" and Max 20x is now "Ultra", and that existing subscribers do not need to take any action.3 Mowshowitz adds that the Max and Ultra plans include API credits equal to the total plan cost, on top of the monthly limits, and says Ultra usage limits are 10x Pro.45

Verified Ultra accounts no longer have the 5-hour session limit, a change that reportedly starts today for most existing subscribers.67 Newer accounts keep a temporary limit until they are verified, which Anthropic describes as an anti-fraud measure, and weekly limits stay the same.89

Commenters in the recap argue the removed 5-hour cap may matter little because the weekly cap is the real bottleneck for heavy users; one Max20 subscriber said the 5-hour cap was effectively irrelevant while the weekly limit was exhausted consistently.1011 Commenters further argued that dropping the explicit 5x/20x labels makes future quota changes harder to audit, since Anthropic could reduce effective usage ratios later while keeping the same plan names.1213
