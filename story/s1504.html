Anthropic public benefit fund

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Oct 19 · 2 new articles this week · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic announced it is committing a portion of its shares to a new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, with 5% of its shares committed initially, subject to stockholder approval.12 Anthropic says the board can add 2% at its discretion for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline.34 Anthropic says that, combined with co-founder pledges to donate 80% of their equity and an employee equity match program, roughly 20% of its shares will be dedicated to the public good.56

Anthropic says the fund is a wholly-owned LLC meant to give flexibility between forms of giving, and that its Anthropic equity will not be used for lobbying or political giving.789 Anthropic suggests it might explore uses such as large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations; it gives labor market impacts as one example, an issue to which it has already committed $200 million.101112 Anthropic has said it will share more about the fund's structure and governance in the coming months.1314

Kevin Roose, as quoted by Zvi Mowshowitz, said the announcement could be the largest commitment of philanthropic capital ever made, at $200 billion if Anthropic goes public at $1 trillion, which he called probably conservative.15 Zvi Mowshowitz worries the fund will not focus on making AI go well and will choose less impactful causes, noting that only lobbying and political giving are ruled out.1617 A Latent Space roundup reports that @willcb reads it as a creative capital structure that restricts float.1819
