Anthropic public benefit fund

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 18:59 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 19 · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic announced it is committing a portion of its shares to a new entity, the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, which it says will give it increased flexibility and scale to pursue its mission in new ways.123 Anthropic says that, subject to stockholder approval, 5% of its shares will be committed, with an additional 2% added at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline.4 Anthropic says the Fund is a wholly-owned LLC rather than a 501(c)(3) to allow adjustability between different forms of giving, and that none of its equity will be used for lobbying or political giving.56

Anthropic says that, combined with today's announcement (and the existing co-founder pledges and employee equity donation match program it builds on), roughly 20% of its shares will be dedicated to the public good, plus any additional percentage that could be added if the company reaches specific valuation milestones.7 Anthropic says its co-founders pledged to donate 80% of their equity, and that approximately half of its employees participate in the matching program.89

Anthropic offered broad examples of use, such as large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations, and cited labor market impacts, for which it has already committed $200 million to study and address.101112 Anthropic also says that given the pace of AI progress, bolder approaches may be needed and the Fund will have the versatility to pursue them.1314

TechCrunch frames the announcement as coming amid increasingly negative public sentiment toward the AI industry, which CEO Dario Amodei has described as "fundamentally a crisis of trust."15 Who will decide which public benefits are funded remains open: Anthropic says it will share more on structure, governance and the valuation milestones in the coming months, and will report progress in an upcoming public benefit report.1617
