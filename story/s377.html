Gemini 4 Argon launch

Last scan Sat Oct 3, 14:34 UTC
6 sources · 1799 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 1 · 3 new articles on Oct 3 · 2 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Google

Google announced Gemini 4 Argon, which it calls its new frontier model, rolling out first to trusted cyber defenders through its Fairwind Program rather than to the general public.1 Google says safely releasing frontier capabilities at this level requires a phased approach, and that it will iterate on guardrails before making Argon available to developers, enterprises and consumers "as soon as possible", starting with paid API customers and Google AI Ultra subscribers.23 Ars Technica notes that Google gave no specific timeline for enterprise and consumer availability.4

Google says Argon has an industry-leading 1M-token output limit, up from 64K, and an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input 95% off.56 However, sources differ on output limits and efficiency: Vals lists a 262K maximum output while Artificial Analysis reached 1M tokens through the Long Decode Continuation API feature, and Argon uses 62K output tokens per task versus 27K for Astra, so its cost savings come from price rather than efficiency. inference789

Google claims a new state of the art on DeepSWE v1.1 at 77.9%, says Argon is the leading model on the Vals Index, and says it ranks #1 on AutomationBench at 51.3%.101112 Google also says Argon agents analyzed telemetry to identify memory optimizations that will free over 300 TiB of memory in its data centers once rolled out, and are working on migrating C/C++ code to Rust, scaling up to 800K+ lines for the Fuchsia Zircon kernel.131415 Google says that Wiz, using Argon, found a critical healthcare-software vulnerability that previous frontier models had missed, though Ars Technica notes Google gave no specifics.161718

Latent Space also reports that a Bloomberg report attributed coding weaknesses to anonymous insiders while a senior DeepMind engineer reportedly rejected that account, and it calls the coding-quality dispute unresolved.1920 Zvi Mowshowitz says it is far too early to say what we have with Argon because access to the model is unavailable, so the promising initial benchmarks may not translate into it being actually good.2122 On Reddit, a user who canceled a Google One AI plan complained that Pro users remain on Gemini 3.8 Flash, though top comments mostly dismissed the complaint, and another thread's dominant view was skeptical of announcing a model the public can't use, with one commenter speculating it was aimed "purely for investors."2324

What safeguards is Google building in before broad release, and why is it running Argon without cyber guardrails for trusted defenders?

Google says that before rolling out Gemini 4 Argon broadly, it is continuing to strengthen critical frontier safeguards across four main areas.12 On misuse, Google says Argon is designed to refuse harmful requests, including cyber and CBRN attacks, while preserving legitimate dual-use research. It says it is improving techniques to monitor the model's internal activations, and that internal and external red teams tested the safeguards.3456 Google also calls Argon its most resilient model yet against indirect prompt injection. It says it is deploying mitigations that monitor the model's chain-of-thought and actions and stop execution when necessary. It is also isolating and sealing its sandboxed environments before high-risk training or evaluations begin.78910 Google says it will release Argon without cyber guardrails to trusted defenders and its own internal teams so they can use its full frontier-level cybersecurity defense capabilities. The stated reason is that Argon was trained to be highly capable at cyber defense and can autonomously find, validate and patch critical software vulnerabilities.111213 Google frames this as part of a phased release. It says it will gather feedback from early testers and iterate on guardrails before opening access to developers, enterprises and consumers.141516

Which rival models did Argon beat on the benchmarks, and where does it fall behind, for example on Terminal Bench 4 and Code Arena WebDev?
