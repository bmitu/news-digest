Mistral Large 4 open weights

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 4 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: Mistral, Politico

Mistral has released Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk", as an open-weight model of about 1T total and about 52B active parameters, a native multimodal mixture-of-experts design with a 1M-token context.1 The model takes text and image input, offers configurable reasoning effort and function calling, and is claimed to be fluent in 160+ languages.2 It is released under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License, which allows commercial and non-commercial use but requires a separate license for Model-as-a-Service operation.3

Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch said the model "puts us very much back in the race" and claimed it outperforms Chinese open-weight rivals in areas such as cybersecurity and geospatial imaging.45 Mensch said Mistral does not need to lead on every benchmark and that it deliberately focused on areas where it saw strong demand.67 One third-party coding benchmark reportedly ranks the model as the second-best open-source model.8

Reaction on a forum was skeptical about local use: commenters called the model technically interesting but impractical given its footprint, and one argued it is not competitive for local AI against DS4.1, which they described as smaller, faster and smarter.910 Another commenter asked what "secret sauce" Mistral is missing, saying its recent models are perceived as not topping benchmark leaderboards.1112
