Mistral Large 4 open weights

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
5 sources · 1687 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Mistral, TechCrunch

Mistral has released the weights for its trillion-parameter open source AI model Mistral Large 4 (nicknamed "Le Chonk"), making them available on Hugging Face and via its API.123 The model is released under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License, a modified MIT license that is free to use, modify, fine-tune and commercialize, with the condition that businesses operating a Model-as-a-Service offering must obtain a separate commercial license.4 Sources give slightly different active-parameter counts: Artificial Analysis, as cited by Latent Space, lists 1.05T total and 52B active parameters with a 1M context window, while Hugging Face's post says about 49B active. inference56

Mistral says the new version improves on its preview through continued reinforcement learning, and that on Terminal-Bench 4.0 it rose 13 percentage points to 41% while cutting output tokens by 30%.7 Mistral also says it is comparable to China's GLM-5.3 while using 47 percent fewer tokens per task, and that it outranks other open models on Artificial Analysis' Coding Agent Index.89 Independent figures are more mixed: Artificial Analysis scores it 26 on its Intelligence Index, narrowly the top open model developed outside China, but 6 points behind DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 and GLM-5.3, and it scores 15% on FrontierCode 2.0 versus 29% and 22% for those models.1011

Cyber results are reported inconsistently: Mistral's post says 51% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index, while Hugging Face's post says 82% on that index. inference1213 Mistral pitches the release for defensive cybersecurity and sovereignty; Pierre Stock, its Vice President of Science, said open weights give access to frontier intelligence that "can't be cut off by definition".1415 Reddit commenters, as summarized by Latent Space, welcomed a strong non-Chinese open-weight model but said the benchmark and security claims should be independently validated, and some doubted its value for local use compared with DeepSeek's DS4.1.1617
