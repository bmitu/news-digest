Qwen4-Max release

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Artificial Analysis)

Qwen4-Max, previewed the previous Friday, is now a shipping model from Alibaba, according to Latent Space's AINews roundup.1 Artificial Analysis data cited by Latent Space show that at its highest reasoning setting the model scored 43 on the Intelligence Index, up from 34, while average cost per task fell from $10.02 to $3.27.23 Alibaba lowered its price for output from $6 to $4.50 for every million tokens, while the model generated roughly 39% less output.4

Alibaba kept total parameters at 2.4T but cut active parameters per token by 25% to 71B; the model accepts images and text and has a 1M-token context window.56 Smaller open-weight Qwen4 models have not yet been released but are still to come.7

According to Latent Space, Kimi K3.1 Pro had a cost of $3.68 for each index task and reached 67% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, compared with 48% for Qwen.89 Zvi Mowshowitz likewise notes that Qwen4 Max remains behind Kimi K3.1 Pro on Artificial Analysis.9 Mowshowitz judges that at a price of $1.50/$4.50 the model looks well below the cost-capability frontier.10
