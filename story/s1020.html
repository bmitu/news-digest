Anthropic quarterly financials and IPO

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
4 sources · 243 points on Hacker News · 3 independent origins: CNBC, Financial Times, Lisan al Gaib

Anthropic reportedly told investors at an event on Wednesday that its third-quarter revenue was $16.8 billion, up nearly 50% from the prior quarter, ahead of an expected IPO, according to the Financial Times as summarized by Simon Willison. inference123 Willison's reading of the FT report gives Q1 revenue as about $4.8 billion and Q2 as $11.5 billion.4

Bloomberg, citing people familiar, reports that Anthropic had positive free cash flow, an adjusted gross margin of 85% and an adjusted operating margin of over 11% in the third quarter.56 The FT figures also reportedly show $1.70 of revenue for every $1 spent on computing power in Q3, with adjusted operating margins of 11% excluding stock-based compensation.7

Per the FT, Anthropic told investors that distillation by rival labs accounted for 5% to 7% of revenue and that it had cut this back "materially" in the third quarter.89 Willison's napkin maths suggests that if distillation was 5% of Q2 revenue, unnamed labs spent over half a billion dollars distilling Anthropic's models that quarter, and he notes Anthropic no longer appears to lean on "annualized run-rate revenue" with investors. inference1011

Bloomberg has reported that Anthropic could begin formally marketing its IPO as soon as the week of Nov. 9, putting it in line to trade before Thanksgiving.12 Zvi Mowshowitz called the $16.8 billion "highly disappointing" (about $67.2 billion annualized) and said Manifold's expected IPO value for Anthropic fell about 4%, which he thought fair because growth was expected to be faster.131415
