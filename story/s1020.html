Anthropic quarterly financials and IPO

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
5 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · 3 independent origins: Financial Times, Lisan al Gaib, TechCrunch

Anthropic told investors that its third-quarter revenue was $16.8 billion, up about 45% from the previous quarter, according to reports of its investor briefing.12 According to Simon Willison's reading of the Financial Times report, Q1 revenue was about $4.8bn and Q2 was $11.5bn, and Anthropic made $1.70 in revenue for every $1 spent on computing power in Q3.345 The Financial Times reported that Anthropic told investors distillation by rival labs accounted for 5% to 7% of revenue and that the company had cut it back "materially" in the third quarter.6

Exponential View calls the results a "miss" because expectations, anchored on a July leak suggesting a $65bn annualized figure, had run ahead of reality.7 It says Q2-to-Q3 growth of 45% compares with 147% from Q1 to Q2, a deceleration, though it is unclear which quarter is the outlier.89

TechCrunch, citing an S-1 filing leaked to the Wall Street Journal, says Anthropic is having trouble securing enough cloud capacity and had an $80.7 billion net loss in the first half of the year against $16.3 billion in revenue.1011 Bloomberg also reported that Anthropic could begin formally marketing its IPO as soon as the week of Nov. 9, which would put it in line to begin trading before Thanksgiving, and TechCrunch says it is reportedly targeting a November IPO.1213
