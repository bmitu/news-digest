Meta releases Ray-Ban Gen 3 glasses

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new article today · 2 independent origins: Ars Technica, Meta

At its Connect event, Meta presented its Muse personal agent as the centerpiece of a hardware-plus-agent strategy, and announced new glasses including Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3. inference12 According to the Latent Space recap, Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 has a longer battery, upgraded microphones and new styles including Aviators.2 The Financial Times, in Ars Technica's republication, reports that Mark Zuckerberg also announced a pair of Ray-Bans with no camera that users interact with by audio only, alongside several new frame styles from Ray-Ban and the cheaper Meta glasses range.3

Meta also launched VR glasses, priced at about $1,300 according to the FT and $1,299 according to @kimmonismus, which the FT says arrive next spring and are five times lighter than the Quest 3. inference45 Zuckerberg unveiled Muse Charm, a keychain-sized device activated by a fingerprint sensor, with a screen showing the Muse avatar, which Meta says will ship in December.67

The FT reports that Muse has become the most downloaded app on both the Apple and Android app stores in the US since its launch two weeks ago, and that Meta's stock has bounced back since the September 8 launch, adding $300 billion to its market capitalisation.89

The FT notes critics have dubbed the camera-equipped Ray-Bans "pervert glasses" and claim they are used to record others without their knowledge.1011 The FT also says Meta had to fix a vulnerability that could let a hacker take over another person's Muse agent, and that Amazon has blocked Meta's agents from its shopping platform.1213 Latent Space's recap says Meta's edge is distribution, with a differentiator of distribution plus owned hardware, and notes its frontier model remains unreleased.1415
