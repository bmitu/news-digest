Project Suncatcher TPU satellite

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin (Google)

Google has given an update on Project Suncatcher, its initiative announced last year to put computers in space and eventually train AI systems there, according to Import AI.1

Latent Space's AINews reports that Google is flying four TPUs in orbit on a Planet prototype satellite aboard SpaceX Transporter-18.23

Import AI describes the launch as still being prepared, with Google and partner Planet sending some of its chips to space on the SpaceX Transporter-18 rideshare mission.4

Google reports that it stress-tested its TPUs for the g-forces of going to space.5

Google also says its Trillium TPUs survived radiation testing, withstanding a total ionizing dose greater than what they would receive during a five-year space mission.6

Cooling in space is the work in progress; Import AI expects it to be a challenge because chips generate a lot of heat and radiating it away in a vacuum is very difficult.7

The Import AI author argues the idea makes sense given AI's growing compute and energy needs, and thinks it very likely that humanity moves a very large amount of computation into orbit quickly.89
