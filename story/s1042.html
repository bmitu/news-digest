Nadella AI trust remarks

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Nadella)

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said in a lengthy post on X that AI models should be assumed to be compromised and contained from the start, likening the safeguard to an emergency brake.12 TechCrunch reports that in a Saturday morning post on X, Nadella wrote that it was time "to step back and assess the trust architecture" of AI.3 Nadella said, "We can't treat Super Intelligence as a set of nested black boxes and simply accept or reject its recommendations, answers, and actions."45

He called for separating the model from the harness that orchestrates its work and for externalizing controls and safeguards.6 He also called for every meaningful model action to be documented with "tamper-proof human readable evidence" and for an authorized person always to be able to pause or shut down a model mid-task.789 Nadella also said more advanced models will require more advanced containment technologies that need to be standardized.10

The Verge says many of his recommendations, such as timely incident disclosure, independent audits, verifiable data and containment, align with what others in the industry have said, but that he appears to go slightly further on containment.1112 TechCrunch says he used the term "Super Intelligence", which it describes as the Trump administration's preferred term for AI, while The Verge complains that he refers to AI as "super intelligence" throughout the post. inference1314

TechCrunch frames the comments as coming as leading AI companies acknowledge more incidents in which they seemed to lose control of their models, and after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published a plan for more cautious AI development.15
