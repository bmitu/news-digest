Fable 5.2 capability caps

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · single origin, unclear which

A document reported in a Reddit-sourced thread says Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 were deliberately capability-capped during training in domains that could accelerate AI research and development, in addition to downstream safeguards.1 The limited areas include accelerator kernel optimization and LLM architecture improvement, which points to safety constraints built into capability development rather than only inference-time guardrails.23

The caps surfaced alongside strong benchmark results: Agent Arena reportedly ranks Fable 5.2 (Max) first with a +30.4% net improvement across 4.7K agentic sessions, about 1.6x the score of second-placed Opus 5.5 at +19.5%.45 The newsletter also reports API prices falling from $10/$50 to $8/$40 per 1M input/output tokens, with a median cost per task of $7.3 versus $5.5 for Opus 5.5.67

Many commenters doubted that safety was the real reason, pointing out that tuning kernels is also how everyday users get local models to run well, and suggesting the limit could really be a way of gating capabilities that shuts out competitors.8 Some other people leaving comments speculated that the firm might not impose identical limits on versions it uses only internally.9 Commenters further emphasized the real-world consequences, pointing out that the ban hits tasks such as tuning inference kernels for compact local models on legacy hardware and creating bespoke inference engines, and is not confined to speeding up cutting-edge AI.10
