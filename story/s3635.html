Huang admits affair

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 14 · 2 new articles this week · single origin, unclear which

On November 8, Jensen Huang admitted in a letter to Nvidia employees, also released as a press release, that he had been unfaithful to his wife.1 He did not name Alice Huang in the message but tied his infidelity to national security concerns raised in news reports, and Nvidia's board began an independent investigation that he said he would support.23

On November 11, The New York Times reported that Huang had also had an affair with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's vice president of healthcare, who reports directly to him.4 Nvidia then announced that Huang had resigned as CEO, with chief financial officer Colette Kress taking over as interim CEO while keeping her CFO duties.56

The Verge says the Times reported that Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to spy on the Nvidia CEO, though US intelligence officials reportedly disagree over how strong the evidence tying her to Beijing is.78 Nvidia spokesperson John Rizzo had earlier called Huang's interactions with Alice Huang not unusual, but Reuters reportedly found that Huang misled several Nvidia leaders about the nature of the relationship. inference910

Nvidia's stock fell from an all-time closing high of $239.24 on October 6 to $162.85 on November 13.11 On November 17, Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% year over year and ahead of Wall Street expectations, and said it does not plan to reinstate Huang.1213

Democrats Richard Neal and Ron Wyden announced plans in Congress to look into the Trump administration's handling of Nvidia's sales of chips to China. Separately, Republican Senator Tim Scott requested that both Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick appear before the Senate Banking Committee to give testimony.1415

Some in the industry defended Huang: Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney called his exit a "sad day" and Elon Musk said it would be an honor to hire him.1617
