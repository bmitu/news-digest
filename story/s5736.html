Claimed VP≠VNP proofs by Anthropic and OpenAI

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 09:22 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 25 · 1 new article today · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic and OpenAI each posted claimed proofs that VP ≠ VNP, the algebraic counterpart of P vs NP, with Anthropic reported to have posted first.12 Anthropic's claim reportedly appears as a paper (vp-vnp/paper.pdf) in the anthropics/formal-math GitHub repository, reportedly produced by an internal Claude research model.34 Per Latent Space, neither lab named a model or published an announcement.5 The result would not settle P vs NP, which would still need general Boolean circuit lower bounds, according to a cited commenter.6

A Reddit summary says the Anthropic post's comments contained no independent verification of the proof or its Lean formalization, and were mostly incredulous and hype-driven.78

Latent Space reports that nine new papers with Lean proofs, listed as an OpenAI batch, came out a day after a claimed VP ≠ VNP proof, and separately that an OpenAI paper claimed the square matrix multiplication exponent ω is exactly 9/4.910 Omri Weinstein called the ω result "truly surreal" if correct, since most experts expected ω = 2.11 Latent Space warns that a tally of papers can conceal a great deal, pointing out that OpenAI's revision history shows three retractions in October, which came about because a sign mistake in a single paper also affected two others.1213

Chau Ngo argues that AI-generated proofs do not by themselves offer broader or deeper understanding or new insight unless studied hard, and that the usual competition in proving hard conjectures is now quite futile because the machine does it much better than humans.1415
