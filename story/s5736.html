Claimed VP≠VNP proofs by Anthropic and OpenAI

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 25 · 1 new article today · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic and OpenAI have each posted a claimed proof that VP ≠ VNP, and Zvi Mowshowitz reports that the two reached it independently, with Anthropic publishing first. inference12 According to Latent Space, an Anthropic repository on GitHub reportedly holds a proof from an internal Claude research model, while OpenAI's later posting says its own was "obtained on November 6, 2026."34 Neither company identified the model involved or issued an announcement, Latent Space says.5

The VP ≠ VNP question, which Latent Space attributes to Valiant in 1979, is the algebraic counterpart of P vs NP, and the claimed result would mean that polynomials like the permanent have no small circuits over any field.6 The result would not settle P vs NP itself; Latent Space relays the point that doing so still requires lower bounds for general Boolean circuits. inference78

On the matter of verification, a Latent Space digest of a Reddit thread on Anthropic's claim noted that the post showed just the paper's opening page, and that the comments it reviewed offered no independent check of either the argument or a Lean formalization.91011

The Guardian calls the work a major advance on a question dating to the 1970s and says that, although the problem differs somewhat from code-breaking, it suggests AI models can produce groundbreaking results in this area.1213 Computer scientist Scott Aaronson, as cited by the Guardian, said the finding had led him to drink unusually large amounts of wine in his kitchen.14
