Anthropic Microsoft compute deals

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · single origin (The Information)

A new Anthropic filing ahead of its planned IPO shows the company has added up to $4.3 billion in compute agreements with Microsoft, as first reported by The Information.1 The Microsoft agreement is now valued at between $47.2 billion and $49.2 billion, up from $44.9 billion in the earlier filing. inference23 Zvi Mowshowitz's roundup separately describes Anthropic as disclosing $10 billion in new deals with Microsoft and Google.4

The earlier November filing put Anthropic's aggregate committed spend at about $895.4 billion, substantially all for compute and infrastructure, with Google at $121.3 billion, SpaceX at $120.5 billion, Amazon at $110 billion and Microsoft at $44.9 billion.56

The filing also says Anthropic's roughly $34.9 billion August 2026 agreement with Lambda is now terminable by either party because Lambda did not secure a facility lease by the required date.7 Anthropic says neither party has given notice of termination and that it does not currently intend to terminate.8

Anthropic reported a $567 million operating loss in the third quarter after a $974 million operating profit in the prior quarter, which it attributed primarily to short-term compute deals.9

The filing says Anthropic plans to offer Class A shares at $70 to $78, targeting a $1.4 trillion valuation.10 Mowshowitz notes that OpenAI's $1.25 trillion valuation is near the low end of Anthropic's valuation range.11
