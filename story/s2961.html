Google disables Search/AI for schools

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
5 sources · 272 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 6 · 5 new articles this week · 5 independent origins: Chalkbeat, EdSurge, Google, Mother Jones, The New York Times

Google announced on Nov. 3 that schools that turn off its AI features will lose access to Google Search, with its AI tools and Search to be off by default for K-12 student accounts from early 2027 unless administrators enable them.123 Google's education vice president Chris Phillips says Search and AI are deeply integrated, so schools that disable the AI features will not have access to Search.45 Google says it is piloting a "Guided Learning" feature in Search that walks students through problems step by step, and spokesperson Laura Breen says it is building these tools in partnership with educators.678

New York City, Los Angeles and Chicago are weighing alternatives to Google Search, with New York schools chancellor Kamar H. Samuels saying the city will explore other search options if Google does not reconsider.91011 A Chicago Public Schools spokesperson said the district was exploring ways to suppress the AI overview on its own, and Los Angeles Unified told EdSurge it has disabled Google's AI Overviews.1213 Some districts, including Fairfax County Public Schools and Niles Community Schools, and web-filtering firm Securly have built tools that hide Google's AI summaries.1415

Critics reacted sharply: New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani and Senator Bernie Sanders reportedly called the move "extortion," and Senator Ed Markey wrote that it puts schools in an impossible position.1617 Mother Jones reports that hundreds of Google workers, through a petition from the Alphabet Workers Union, are urging leadership to retract the policy, arguing it will push schools to shut out Google products altogether.1819

Not all advocates oppose the change: Erin Mote of InnovateEDU calls it a positive step toward district control, though she says Google should explain it clearly instead of leaving that to districts.2021
