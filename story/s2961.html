Google disables Search/AI for schools

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
6 sources · 188 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 6 · 8 new articles this week · 5 independent origins: Ars Technica, Chalkbeat, EdSource, Google, NPR

Google announced on Nov. 3 that it would turn off Search for schools that block its AI products, which sparked an uproar.123 Google says schools can switch off Gemini and Gemini Notebook and still use Search, but AI Mode and AI Overview stay built into Search, so there is no AI-free Search option.45 Google's education vice president Chris Phillips said schools that want AI off can choose not to have Search on at all.6 Google describes the changes as responding to educator feedback and giving schools more control, and says it is making its "Guided Learning" feature the default so students do not get quick answers to schoolwork.78

On Wednesday, Nov. 11, protests took place outside Google's offices in Cambridge, Massachusetts and Chicago, and Ars Technica reports families rallied in seven cities.91011 Chalkbeat counted about 30 people at the Chicago protest, which was led by Illinois Families for Public Schools, while Ars Technica said about 60 stood outside at its peak. inference121314 Chicago Public Schools said it is exploring ways to suppress the AI overview itself and would look to other search options if it cannot.151617

Zohran Mamdani, the mayor of New York City, accused Google of leveraging its dominant market position to push artificial intelligence into public schools, while Senator Ed Markey sent a letter to the company's chief executive urging that the move be undone.18 Lila Byock of Schools Beyond Screens called the move "bullying", while Fairplay's David Monahan said Google underestimated the backlash.1920 Illinois superintendent Hank Thiele, a longtime Google partner, said the episode may have damaged trust and that Google should have simply said "Our bad."2122

Former Google employee Tracy Pizzo theorizes the policy may be tied to antitrust cases Google recently won, since an AI off switch for schools could suggest Search and AI are separable; Google did not answer NPR's questions about this, and antitrust experts were not fully convinced.232425
