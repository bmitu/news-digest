Clef decision models and RL fine-tuning

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 14:51 UTC
2 sources · 638 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 2 · single origin (Cloudflare)

Cloudflare announced two Cloudflare-trained decision models, Clef and Clef-flash, hosted on Workers AI, and says it is open-sourcing them on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license.12 Cloudflare says Clef is currently the leader on the Jev Decision Index and that the models are fully Jev-API compatible; Jev is a decision model from Typesafe AI.345 Cloudflare says Clef differs from Jev by having a vision encoder for images (Jev only does text classification today) and a 64k context window versus Jev's 32k.67

Cloudflare says Clef is built on Qwen3.8-27B and Clef-flash on Qwen3.5-9B, with the backbones frozen and a routing head jointly optimized alongside rank-256 low-rank adapters, and that the decision step is non-autoregressive, making Clef significantly faster than autoregressive LLMs.89 Cloudflare says that across 43 eval benchmarks Clef beat other decision models on latency, except Laya, which it says is very fast but trades off quality.10 In a Threat Intelligence domain-classification test, Cloudflare says Clef took 2.2s, while its fastest general LLM, gpt-oss-120b, took 4.7s and returned only two classifications.111213

Cloudflare also debuted a reinforcement learning (RL) product for fine-tuning Clef, offered first through its forward-deployed engineer team and later as a self-serve platform.141516

On Reddit, as summarized by Latent Space, commenters were mostly positive about Clef filling a gap in local models and were eager to benchmark it, while one raised concern about quality after quantization below Q8.1718

How did Cloudflare train Clef, and what is RLCD (Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions)?

Cloudflare says Clef uses a frozen Qwen backbone (Qwen3.8-27B for Clef, Qwen3.5-9B for Clef-flash), with a routing head optimized jointly with rank-256 low-rank adapters.1 Cloudflare says its post-training combined label-smoothed cross-entropy on valid schema outputs with a Brier loss to refine probability calibration, using its own internal synthetic datasets that permute field orders, prompts and schema structures.234 Cloudflare describes RLCD, which it says stands for Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions, as a secondary optimization target. It says RLCD gives partial credit to adjacent ordinal choices, rewards fully precise record outputs, and applies a reference penalty to prevent distribution shift, for better accuracy and generalization.567 The RLCD name is not new to Cloudflare: TypeSafe AI's Jev, the model Clef is built to be compatible with, was described in a September 2026 Wikipedia entry as trained with a method of the same name, in which probabilities are optimized against outcomes rather than human rater preference. inference891011

Which Cloudflare use cases will the fine-tuned Clef models be built for, and how does the RL service work from data capture to redeployment?

Cloudflare says internal teams want fine-tuned Clef models to evaluate Trust & Safety submissions, triage Cloudflare Support requests, and be built into its Bot products to decide whether a crawler is a good or bad bot.1 The RL service starts with Cloudflare's forward-deployed engineer team working hands-on with customers, and Cloudflare says it will later become a self-serve platform to capture data, fine-tune and redeploy the model on Cloudflare.234 Cloudflare's RL service leverages existing platform primitives: AI Gateway passes AI traffic through and automatically creates a dataset of requests; Workers AI generates rollouts against the base Clef model; Cloudflare Containers serve as an RL sandbox for scoring and replaying agent actions; a new Trainer updates the weights of the fine-tuned Clef model; and the fine-tuned model is redeployed on Workers AI with BYO Model.567
