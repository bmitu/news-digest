Clef decision models and RL fine-tuning

Last scan Sat Oct 3, 17:01 UTC
2 sources · 622 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 2 · 1 new article on Oct 3 · single origin (Cloudflare)

Cloudflare announced two Cloudflare-trained decision models, Clef and Clef-flash, hosted on Workers AI, and says it is open-sourcing them on Hugging Face under an Apache 2.0 license.12 Cloudflare says Clef is currently the leader on the Jev Decision Index and that the models are fully Jev-API compatible; Jev is a decision model from Typesafe AI.345 Cloudflare says Clef differs from Jev by having a vision encoder for images (Jev only does text classification today) and a 64k context window versus Jev's 32k.67

Cloudflare says Clef is built on Qwen3.8-27B and Clef-flash on Qwen3.5-9B, with the backbones frozen and a routing head jointly optimized alongside rank-256 low-rank adapters, and that the decision step is non-autoregressive, making Clef significantly faster than autoregressive LLMs.89 Cloudflare says that across 43 eval benchmarks Clef beat other decision models on latency, except Laya, which it says is very fast but trades off quality.10 In a Threat Intelligence domain-classification test, Cloudflare says Clef took 2.2s, while its fastest general LLM, gpt-oss-120b, took 4.7s and returned only two classifications.111213

Cloudflare also debuted a reinforcement learning (RL) product for fine-tuning Clef, offered first through its forward-deployed engineer team and later as a self-serve platform.141516

On Reddit, as summarized by Latent Space, commenters were mostly positive about Clef filling a gap in local models and were eager to benchmark it, while one raised concern about quality after quantization below Q8.1718

How does Clef's decision step work, and why is it faster than a normal LLM?

Cloudflare says the decision step is non-autoregressive, so no intermediate text is generated token by token, which it credits for making Clef significantly faster than autoregressive LLMs.1 Cloudflare says Clef uses Qwen for a prefill-only pass and then scores the valid schema choices in parallel.2 According to Cloudflare, Clef and Clef-flash derive schema choices directly from internal backbone representations, using a specialized two-stage attention routing process in which every valid choice extracts context relevant to the prompt, allowing individual field parameters to cross-attend with other fields and back to the original payload prior to scoring.345 Cloudflare also says Clef models are hosted on Workers AI, which lets it use its GPUs at the edge for low network latency and faster decisions, on top of the latency benefits from the model itself.67 Cloudflare reports that in its own threat-intelligence test, Clef fetched, rendered and classified a website in 2.2 seconds, while its fastest general LLM, gpt-oss-120b, took 4.7 seconds and returned only two classifications.8

What will Cloudflare's RL fine-tuning service use, and how will customers capture data, train and redeploy a model?

Customers capture data by passing their AI traffic through AI Gateway, which automatically builds a dataset of requests for their use case.12 Training would generate rollouts against the base Clef model on Workers AI and score and replay agent actions in a Containers sandbox. A new Trainer component would then update the fine-tuned model's weights.345 The fine-tuned model would be redeployed on Workers AI through its Bring Your Own Model feature.6 Cloudflare says the self-serve platform is still to come. For now, fine-tuning is offered as a hands-on service with its forward-deployed engineer team.789

What did critics say about the benchmark choices, and do some see decision models as just rebranded zero-shot classifiers?

One commenter on the Clef release criticized the evaluation set as too weak, arguing Clef should be compared against the leading models on jevbench rather than weaker open Jev baselines.1 The comparisons that drew that criticism were against models such as Laya, Kev 9B and DiffusionGemma Jev.2 Cloudflare, for its part, says Clef currently leads the Jev Decision Index.34 As for whether decision models are just rebranded zero-shot classifiers, @mervenoyann calls decision models a rebrand of zero-shot classifiers.5 A Reddit post argued that Jev exposes standard constrained-choice classification semantics rather than a new model class, and that the right baselines are zero-shot or NLI classifiers, embeddings and rerankers.6 That post cited a Banking77 baseline in which BGE-small plus logistic regression reportedly scored 93.3% against Jev's 83.2%.7 Commenters were split: several likened Jev to long-standing tools such as spaCy and scikit-learn, while one said scaling such classifiers could still be commercially valuable.8910

What is RLCD, the RL method Cloudflare used on Clef, and how does it reward the model?

Cloudflare says RLCD, short for Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions, is a secondary optimization target it developed for training Clef.1 Cloudflare says it rewards the model in three ways. It gives partial credit to adjacent ordinal choices. It rewards fully precise record outputs. It applies a reference penalty to prevent distribution shift.2 Cloudflare says this gives Clef better accuracy and generalization.3
