Gemini 3.8 TTS release

4 sources · 331 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 27 · 3 new today · 3 independent origins: Google, Latent Space, Simon Willison

Google announced two new text-to-speech models, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS, saying they turn voice generation "from static presets into a dynamic creative studio."12

Google says Flash TTS is built for deep creative direction and character design, while Flash-Lite TTS is built for high-volume, cost-efficient scale such as dubbing and voice agents.345

Both models are rolling out from the announcement day in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, with Flash TTS also in Gemini Notebook and Flash-Lite TTS in Google Vids, while enterprise access via Gemini Enterprise is described as coming soon.678

Google says Flash TTS ranked #1 on Hume AI's Voice Design Benchmark (71.4), and that Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS took #1 and #2 on Hume AI's Overall Quality Index.91011

A Latent Space recap reports that the two models took #1 on all seven Voice Arena boards, with Flash-Lite leading US English at 1087 Elo, 19 points ahead of Cartesia Sonic-3.6.12

Simon Willison, who built a bring-your-own-key playground for the models, reported that Flash TTS took about 20 seconds to generate 1m 18s of audio at a cost of 2.74 cents.13

Latent Space relays that Willison estimated cost at under 1¢ per minute of generated audio, which is consistent with his 2.74 cents for 1m 18s. inference1415
