Gemini 3.8 TTS release

4 sources · 331 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 27 · 3 independent origins: Google, Latent Space, Simon Willison

Google introduced two new text-to-speech models, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS, and began rolling them out on the day of its announcement.1234 Google says Flash TTS is built for deep creative direction and character design, and Flash-Lite TTS for high-volume, cost-efficient scale.567 Google says developers get both models in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, enterprise API access via Gemini Enterprise is coming soon, and consumers get them in Gemini Notebook (Flash) and Google Vids (Flash-Lite).8910

Launch features include a library of 2,000+ voices, voice replication from a 30-second audio sample, and support for over 100 languages.1112131415 Google says voice replication requires a verbal consent recording, and that generated audio is watermarked with SynthID.161718

Google says Flash TTS took #1 on Hume AI’s Voice Design Benchmark (71.4), and that Flash and Flash-Lite took #1 and #2 on Hume AI’s Overall Quality Index.192021 Latent Space's newsletter says the two models took #1 on all seven Voice Arena boards, with Flash-Lite leading US English at 1087 Elo, 19 points ahead of Cartesia Sonic-3.6.2223

Simon Willison generated 1m 18s of audio with Flash TTS in about 20 seconds at a cost of 2.74 cents, while the newsletter reports that Willison estimated cost at under 1¢ per minute. inference2425 Willison also built a bring-your-own-key playground for the models, and praised the API's ability to define a full conversation between multiple characters with different voices.2627

How does Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS improve on the earlier Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS?

Google says Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS shows major improvements over Gemini 3.1 Flash TTS in long-form content and dual-speaker screenplay control.12 Google says the new model can maintain voice quality, pacing and character timbre across hours of continuous audio with minimal speaker drift, and can stage two-speaker scenes from a single script with distinct voices and natural turn-taking.345 Google also presents 3.8 Flash TTS as a move from 30 original voices to a library of 2,000+ voices, with voice design from natural-language prompts and voice replication from a 30-second sample, but which of these features 3.1 lacked was not reported. inference6789 Head-to-head benchmark numbers against 3.1 Flash TTS on speed or cost were not reported.

Which companies are already building on the new TTS models, and what are they using them for?

Google says it is partnering with companies like Figma, HeyGen, Linguana, Wondercraft, 99.co and Ollang, who are integrating its latest TTS models.12 According to Google, they are using the models to speed up global dubbing, localize media with regional accents, and power conversational voice agents at scale.34 Google also says developer platforms Agora, LiveKit, Pipecat and Vercel use the Gemini API to let developers build and deploy speech generation experiences.56 Within Google's own products, the models are meant to improve Gemini Notebook and Google Vids.78 All of these usage claims come from Google's own announcement, and no partner has described its use independently. inference9

What is voice remixing, and when will it be available?

Voice remixing is a coming-soon feature described in Google's Gemini 3.8 text-to-speech announcement. It lets users pick a voice from Google's voice library and fine-tune timbre, pitch, pace and accent.12 Users can dial in characteristics with prompts, and Google's examples are "add subtle Southern US accent" and "soften the delivery".34 A specific release date for voice remixing was not reported.
