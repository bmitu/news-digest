Epic vibe-coded launcher

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · single origin, unclear which

Epic Games released a preview of a revamped Epic Games Launcher for its store that it says it "vibe coded," according to Techdirt's account of Epic's official announcement post.12 Epic described the first release as "light on features" and compared it to a speedboat rather than a yacht, though it does include a shopping cart.3 Epic's X account claimed the launcher was faster than "the other guy," with a graphic showing it beating Valve's competitor on certain responsiveness benchmarks.45

Techdirt's writer is harshly critical, saying the launcher has graphical glitches and that using it to change game settings opened an unrelated application.67 A Twitch streamer replied under Epic's announcement that they would not leave Steam for it.8

Techdirt notes that Epic also announced it is bringing Fortnite to Linux via Proton, a compatibility layer made by Valve; one VR modder wrote on X that it was bold of Epic to criticize Valve/Steam while using Proton internally for Windows compatibility on Linux.910

The Verge reports that Epic recently launched a vibe-coded version of its PC games store and has reportedly shifted to "AI-accelerated development" for an upcoming Disney collaboration, while CEO Tim Sweeney wrote on X that he believes AI tools will lead to "a new golden age of gaming."1112 Techdirt also claims Epic's leadership told internal developers that AI use is compulsory, and that Tim Sweeney called Steam's AI disclosures "irresponsible."1314
