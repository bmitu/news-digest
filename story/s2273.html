Gemini in-chat document editing

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Google)

Google has added the ability to generate, preview and edit PDFs, Google Docs, Google Sheets, slide decks, Markdown files, TXT files and code directly inside Gemini chats, according to The Verge.1 The feature is available starting today for all Gemini users; PDF previews and editing work on web and mobile, while the other formats are web-only for now.2 Latent Space's AINews roundup also listed the update, describing users as able to generate and edit documents, PDFs, slides and spreadsheets inside the chat, citing the @GeminiApp account.3

Google says users can expand documents into full-screen mode and highlight text to ask Gemini questions or request changes, such as highlighting a table and asking Gemini to turn it into a graph.4

Google had added support for generating and downloading files in Gemini earlier this year, so this update lets users tweak those files without leaving the Gemini app.5 Gemini could already edit files through its Workspace integrations, such as the Gemini bar in Google Docs.6

The Verge notes that Anthropic launched a comparable feature earlier this year for generating and editing documents and presentations in Claude chats.7
