MiniMax H3-Image

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, MiniMax

MiniMax released MiniMax H3-Image, an open-weight image generation and editing model built on its H3 model, and the weights were quickly posted on Hugging Face, including a ComfyUI-ready package.123 Per Reddit posts summarizing MiniMax's announcement, the model claims 16-million-pixel generation, 4K × 4K output in 10–20 seconds, photorealism, strong instruction following, and up to 16 reference images.4

Artificial Analysis is cited as ranking it the #1 open-weights model for image editing and text-to-image.5 According to Arena, the model ranks first among open models on the Multi-Image Edit Arena with a score of 1365, level with both Nano Banana 2 and Pro.6 vLLM-Omni reportedly generates 2048² images with it in 0.63 seconds on 4×B300 GPUs.7

Commenters mainly praised the speed of the open-weight drop, which came roughly a day after brief invite-only access, in contrast to other labs' longer hype cycles.8910 Reaction was mostly positive and practical, centered on access and ComfyUI tooling rather than a debate over quality or licensing.11

However, some commenters raised concerns: the model is reportedly around 66 GB, which may limit local use, and one user had poor results with earlier MiniMax H3 first-frame workflows.121314 Others wanted comparisons against Qwen Image 2.1 for editing and MOSS Image / Krie 2 for text-to-image, as well as tests of its censorship level.151617
