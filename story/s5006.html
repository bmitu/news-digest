Thinking Machines Lab releases Inkling 2 Mini

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 3 new articles today · 2 independent origins: Hugging Face, Thinking Machines Lab

Thinking Machines Lab has released Inkling 2 Mini, an open-weights looped mixture-of-experts transformer licensed under Apache 2.0 and pitched as small enough for a laptop.12 The guide gives the release date as November 17, 2026, lists 26.4B parameters for the model, and says Inkling 2 Mini is the newest model in its size class.3 Hugging Face describes it as multimodal with about 26B total and 3B active parameters per token, where 12 middle layers run three times so that 24 physical layers behave like 48 logical ones.4567 Active-parameter figures also differ in framing: Hugging Face cites 3B active, while the Latent Space summary gives 3.4B before looping and roughly 5.4B effective per token. inference89 Per Hugging Face, the model ships with NVFP4 and MXFP8 expert weights in mixed precision, along with a DSpark drafter for extra speed at a small memory cost.1011

Latent Space reports a score of 20.1 on the AA Index, and claimed results of 48.2% on DeepSWE v1.1 and 46.5% on Vals Index v2.2 among open models under 100B.1213 On coding tasks, Hugging Face reports that the model is ahead on FrontierCode v2.0, matches Qwen3.8-27B on Terminal-Bench 4.0, and falls behind Qwen on AutomationBench-AA v1.0.6.14

The 1M-token context is advertised by Hugging Face, but commenters in the Latent Space roundup clarified that it is reached via YaRN and that native context is reportedly 256K. inference1516 A Thinking Machines employee said the model was trained end-to-end in nvfp4, and Unsloth observations reportedly show it keeping 90% top-1 even at 1-bit quantization.1718 Latent Space's roundup relays further claims: a 79.2% score on SWE-bench Verified, a training-free decoding method called LoopCD that raises AA-Omniscience from -9.0 to -3.4, and Unsloth fine-tuning that is up to 3x faster with 50% less VRAM.192021 According to Latent Space, anyone on Tinker whose account carries a positive balance can train and sample Inkling 2 Mini at no charge.22 According to the gpupartner.com guide, the file Unsloth recommends for Inkling 2 Mini, UD-Q4_K_XL, takes up 17.6 GB. That lets it sit on a 32GB RTX 5090 with roughly 14GB remaining for context. The guide adds that a 16 GB card can also handle the 4-bit file if the overflow goes into system RAM, though performance dips slightly.232425 Hugging Face calls it a refreshing local model that is at least comparable to Qwen 3.8 27B in its informal tests, but says more validation is needed.26 In that test, vLLM with the DSpark drafter failed twice with memory errors: the default 1M-token KV cache needed more memory than was free, and vLLM v0.34.0's higher default GPU memory utilization starved the drafter. Setting --max-model-len 262144 and --gpu-memory-utilization 0.85 fixed these.2728 Hugging Face says vLLM and SGLang both support the model only on nightly builds, with published serving recipes targeting NVIDIA B200 and GB300 GPUs rather than sm120 cards like the RTX PRO 6000. In its repeated runs, SGLang's results swung by about ±4.5% while vLLM's stayed within 0.4%.2930
