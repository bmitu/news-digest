Thinking Machines Lab releases Inkling 2 Mini

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 1 new article today · 2 independent origins: Hugging Face, Thinking Machines Lab

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini on November 17, 2026, according to gpupartner.com, which describes a mixture-of-experts model under an Apache 2.0 license that supports vision and a context of 1 million tokens.12 Latent Space, citing @tinkerapi, reported that Tinker users with a positive balance can now train and sample the model at no charge.3

On the model's size, sources broadly agree on the total, with gpupartner.com giving 26.4 billion parameters and a Hugging Face post about 26 billion, but they differ on the active count: 5.4 billion in the former and 3 billion per token in the latter. inference425 Hugging Face describes a looped Mixture-of-Experts design with 24 physical layers in which the middle 12 run three times, so every token passes through 48 layers.6

On performance, Hugging Face says the model tops its size class on version 5 of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and leads on 32 of 48 benchmarks against rivals such as Gemma 4 31B and Qwen3.8-27B.7 Scores relayed by Latent Space from @victormustar include 48.2% on DeepSWE, ranked first among models under 32B, and 79.2% on SWE-bench Verified.8 @NielsRogge also reports that Inkling 2 Mini outperforms GLM-5.3, a model 29 times its size, when answering open-ended questions involving Hugging Face's CLI and MCP tools.9

For local use, gpupartner.com says Unsloth's recommended UD-Q4_K_XL file takes 17.6 GB, and that the 4-bit version can still run, somewhat more slowly, on a 16 GB graphics card by spilling into system memory.1011 In a serving experiment run largely by a coding agent on a single NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000, Hugging Face measured 194.9 tokens per second for a single user on the live endpoint.121314

Separately, nokeepai released modified versions, including an uncensored one, aimed at curbing overlong reasoning loops, and reported that its add-on raised the original model's debugging score from 80% to 100%.151617
