Flow Engineering Series B

2 sources · 2 independent origins: Flow Engineering, Google

Flow Engineering, a startup offering AI tools for hardware design, announced on Wednesday that it raised a $50 million Series B round at a $750 million valuation.1 The round was co-led by Antonio Gracias of Valar Equity Partners and Gavin Baker of Atreides Management.23 Sequoia Capital, which led Flow's Series A last October, also participated, as did former Sequoia partner Roelof Botha as an individual investor, and Botha has joined Flow's board.45

TechCrunch describes Gracias's Valar as best known for investments in Elon Musk's companies, and Baker's Atreides as a hedge fund that has also backed Musk's companies and others like chipmaker Cerebras.67

Three-year-old, San Francisco-based Flow offers AI agents that automatically align CAD drawings with product requirements, simulation results and other testing.8 The company names Anduril, Rivian, Joby Aviation, General Motors PPU, RV Tech, Stoke Space and others as customers.910

Latent Space's AINews newsletter also noted the raise in a roundup, describing Flow as building AI tooling for hardware engineering and citing the same $50M Series B at a $750M valuation.11
