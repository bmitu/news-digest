Bluesky Attie expansion

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 18:59 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · 2 independent origins: TechCrunch, The Verge

Bluesky has expanded its Attie AI tool, which initially launched earlier this year to build custom feeds, so users can make small social apps such as games, polls, quizzes, dashboards and websites.12 The announcement came at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 on Thursday, in an interview with Bluesky CEO Toni Schneider.345 Schneider described Attie as "social vibe coding or vibe coding for your community," and said that vibe coding today is a solo experience whereas Attie lets people build for or with a group.67

Users create an app by typing a prompt, and the finished apps get their own link on the attie.site domain with attribution to the maker.89 According to TechCrunch, the creations live on the AT Protocol, creators are tied to their ATProto identity, and Attie will suggest ideas based on their public Bluesky profiles.1011 Users can bring their own AI model or use open-weight options, and Bluesky COO Rose Wang says Attie comes with Bluesky's distribution, so what people build does not stand alone.1213

Schneider said private data support for the protocol is in alpha testing, with a rollout planned by the end of the year.14 TechCrunch reports Bluesky has 47 million registered users but has seen declining engagement, as some who tried it returned to X.15

The Verge's Jay Peters found a Pokémon poll took a few minutes to generate and called the apps he tried "pretty lightweight novelties," though he liked the drum machine.1617
