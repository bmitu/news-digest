Qwen3.8-27B harness adapters

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Hugging Face)

Hugging Face says that freezing Qwen3.8-27B and adding a custom LoRA adapter trained with reinforcement learning inside the pi harness lifts its Terminal-Bench 2.1 pass@1 from 39% to 55%.1 Hugging Face says the adapter also cuts average token use per task from 2.9K to 1.5K, mostly because the model reasons less, calls fewer tools and does better on its first attempt.2 Latent Space's AINews newsletter also covered the pi adapter, citing an improvement from 15 to 32 solved problems among the 51 most difficult SWE-bench Pro tasks, along with 55% fewer tool invocations for each task.3

For each agent harness (pi, Hermes Agent and OpenCode), the team built a separate LoRA adapter on top of Qwen3.8-27B, applying reinforcement learning within the harness itself through TRL's AsyncGRPOTrainer, OpenEnv and harbor.45 According to Hugging Face, each of the three specialist adapters outperformed the base model on both benchmarks when run in its own harness. In the hard 51-task SWE-bench Pro subset, pi climbed from 39.2% to 64.7%, hermes went from 29.4% to 43.1%, and opencode moved from 35.3% to 51.0%.67 Across the entire Terminal-Bench 2.1, Hugging Face reports improvements of 16.3 points for pi and 14.1 for hermes. For opencode the overall improvement is 6.0 points, rising to 13.6 on the 66 tasks that had no role in selecting its checkpoint.89

Hugging Face adds that one merged "super-adapter" likewise outperformed the base model in all three harnesses, though with thinking turned off it retains a smaller share of each specialist's improvement, and the loss is greatest under pi.1011

Hugging Face reports that the adapters stayed close to the base model's 23.0% pass@1 on AutomationBench: pi at 27.0%, OpenCode at 25.3%, the merged adapter at 23.7% and Hermes lower at 17.7%.12 Hugging Face reads this as showing that harness-specific training neither transfers clearly to a very different setting nor breaks the model there, noting the differences are within noise except perhaps Hermes.13
