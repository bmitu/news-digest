DeepSeek V4.1 Flash release and evaluations

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 1 new article this week · 2 independent origins: DeepSeek, Teortaxes

Two Minute Papers promoted a release it calls DeepSeek v4.1 Flash 1017, pointing viewers to DeepSeek's API documentation news page.1

On the Epoch Capabilities Index, DeepSeek V4.1F-1017 scores 159, equal to GPT 5.5, according to a post by @teortaxesTex relayed by Latent Space; that is 3 points above V4.1F-0910 and 10 above V4-Pro, while Astra sits roughly 10 points higher. inference234 @scaling01 notes that no Chinese model has matched Mythos Preview (~161 ECI) after about 7 months.5 However, @wenhaocha1 argues the unweighted ECI exaggerates the gap, and that weighting coding more heavily shrinks it to about 2 months.6

Teortaxes calls 1017 extremely spiky, saying it scores near zero on some SWE evals and approaches the frontier only on cybersecurity and quant trading.7 He also flags "reward-hacking-shaped" behavior.8
