Anthropic S-1 IPO filing

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
14 sources · 1706 points on Hacker News · 10 independent origins: Anthropic, Ars Technica, Azeem Azhar, Bensign, Jamin Ball, Nate B. Jones, TechCrunch, The Verge, Transformer, Understanding AI

Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 registration statement on Monday and plans to list its Class A common stock on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT".123 The number of shares and the price range for the offering have not yet been set.4

The filing reports first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier, and an $80.7 billion net loss that TechCrunch says was largely driven by noncash accounting adjustments tied to convertible debt.567 The second quarter produced a $974 million operating profit, while Timothy B. Lee notes that Anthropic has not officially reported third-quarter revenue and that media reports put it at $16.8 billion, a slower pace of growth than the previous quarter.8910 Sources differ on the missing third-quarter results: The Verge calls their absence a gap in assessing profitability, whereas Zvi Mowshowitz says Anthropic is not hiding them and expects them in the next amendment. inference111213

Anthropic held $112.4 billion in cash and equivalents plus $19.3 billion in short-term investments at June 30, but The Verge's tally of its commitments comes to at least roughly $850 billion over 20 years, so continued fast growth is needed to meet them. inference1415 The Financial Times reports that Anthropic is expected to receive a high "junk" credit rating after the IPO; TechCrunch reached out to Anthropic and Blackstone, and both declined to comment.1617

On customers, the S-1 reports more than 11,900 customers spending at least $100,000 annually as of September 30 and net revenue retention of about 900%.1819

Under the governance structure, seven co-founders will form a "Founder LLC" controlling 50.1% of shareholder voting power, while the Long-Term Benefit Trust can appoint most of the board.2021 Law professor Ann Lipton says Anthropic is legally insulated from profit-seeking pressure, but cautions that the market reality remains because the company "desperately needs capital".2223

Senator Elizabeth Warren criticized the plan, saying the IPO would accelerate development of the technology while leaving Americans holding the bag if the AI bubble bursts.24
