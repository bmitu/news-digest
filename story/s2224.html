Anthropic filed an S-1 to go public last week and then filed an amended S-1 on Monday that added third-quarter results through September 30. inference123 CNBC reports Anthropic had about $17 billion in Q3 revenue and $33.24 billion for the nine months ended Sept. 30, and a Q3 net loss of $23.29 billion.456 CNBC says Q3 revenue growth of around 47% was a steep drop from 144% in Q2 and 132% in Q1.7

Regenerator, citing a reported Q3 figure of $16.8 billion, argues that Anthropic's growth flattened, saying it barely grew from July to September and that Q3 added less revenue than Q2.8910

TechCrunch reports compute took 58% of revenue in Q3, up from 48% the prior quarter, and cites the filing's note of $5.0 billion of compute under short-term commitments, which currently costs significantly more than comparable compute under multi-year commitments.1112 The filing cites aggregate committed spend of about $895.4 billion, substantially all related to compute and infrastructure.13 Writing in Tech Policy Press, an author argues the SEC should block the IPO until Anthropic provides two consecutive quarters of audited financial statements, citing at least $582 billion in non-cancelable commitments to five companies.1415

Transformer reports investors were reportedly pushing Anthropic toward a more modest IPO valuation of $1.5-1.6 trillion instead of the $2 trillion it had been eyeing, and that after the post-midterms AI sell-off investors told the WSJ even the revised figure could be too high, with one saying it might need to drop as low as $1 trillion.1617
