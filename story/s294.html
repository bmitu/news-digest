Tech companies create AI standards body

2 sources · single origin (The Washington Sun)

Google, OpenAI and Anthropic are reportedly jointly working on a plan to create their own AI safety-focused "standards body," according to The Information as relayed by the Washington Sun.1

Zvi says the three companies are planning to form the body by early in 2027, tentatively titled the Standards Authority for Frontier AI, or SAFA.2

Zvi calls the plan welcome news, and separately notes signs of greater cooperation across labs.34

Zvi expects no impactful consequences if the top labs coordinate on safety, even though lawyers may warn about antitrust problems.5

Zvi notes a suggestion from Lawfare to work around antitrust concerns by mutualizing risk through insurance, with the mutual enforcing safety and auditing standards.6

However, some AI safety experts reject the standards-body model, saying it effectively lets the firms police themselves.7

The Washington Sun reports that one alternative is a government tax on AI companies to fund safety operations, which would ensure independence and keep the cost from falling mainly on taxpayers.8
