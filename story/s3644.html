Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 release

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
9 sources · 1000 points on Hacker News · 5 independent origins: Anthropic, Artificial Analysis, Ethan Mollick, Simon Willison, TechCrunch

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on Monday, saying the models improve on its 5.1 versions in coding, reasoning and vision and generate output faster.12 Fable 5.2 launched immediately, while Mythos 5.2 is initially limited to vetted organizations. Anthropic describes Mythos as the same underlying model as Fable, without certain cybersecurity or biology safeguards.34 Anthropic says that, based on early testing, Fable 5.2 produced about 55% fewer classifier blocks than Fable 5.1, while a commenter objected that blocking most cybersecurity-related tasks could significantly limit legitimate security workflows.56

Fable pricing fell to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, from $10 and $50 for Fable 5.1, and Anthropic says responses come back 30% faster across effort levels.78 Artificial Analysis gave Fable 5.2 (Max) a score of 65 on its Intelligence Index, well above the median of 40 for similarly priced models.9 Artificial Analysis's own measurements, however, put Fable 5.2 (Max) among the slowest in its price tier at 44.6 minutes per index task, against a median of 12.7 minutes.10

Anthropic says it deliberately limited training advances in research skills that could most directly speed up AI development, so in areas like kernel optimization both models perform at about the level of Opus 5.5.1112 According to TechCrunch, METR's report concluded that Anthropic's in-house models were accelerating the company's AI development at about twice the normal pace, though significant uncertainty remains around that estimate.13 Anthropic assesses the risk of unsafe behavior in high-stakes situations as low, but its release notes concede that both models voice suspicion of being evaluated more often than recent models, which complicates testing.1415

Ethan Mollick says Fable 5.2 is very impressive, following on the already excellent Opus 5.5, and that he thinks it is "almost" AGI, or what he has called Jagged AGI.1617 Zvi Mowshowitz is more cautious: he says Fable 5.2 is likely the best choice for the hardest tasks but is not obviously a better daily driver than Opus 5.5.1819
