Gemini 4 Argon for robotics

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 08:37 UTC
2 sources · 233 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Google DeepMind)

Google DeepMind says Gemini 4 Argon for robotics marks a step change in physical understanding, code generation and visual-semantic reasoning.12 Google says Argon scores 87% on the Embodied Reasoning (ERQA) benchmark using pure reasoning, outperforming every other frontier model.34 Developers can use it today: Google says it is available in Google AI Studio and Antigravity to Google AI Ultra subscribers, and in the Gemini API on the paid tier.567

In one demonstration, Google says Argon built a calibrated MuJoCo simulation twin from a single photo of a real tabletop and collected 4,500+ domain-randomized episodes, which it says raised real-world robot success rate from 56% to 75% and cut completion time from 35s to 24s.8910 Google also says that, given a 136-second ALOHA 2 origami-folding video, Argon produced a four-level hindsight annotation hierarchy, including 6 semantic subtasks and 17 atomic primitives.111213 Google also describes Argon steering robots in the Gemini Omni World Model in a closed visual loop to rearrange Scrabble tiles, and, from a photo of a hand-drawn sketch, constructing a mujoco_warp scene and synthesizing a whole-body throw trajectory.141516

In an internal test, Google says Argon learned cornhole by watching a human video, and when the code was transferred to Apptronik's Apollo humanoid the robot sank seven consecutive shots in a real-world trial.1718

A quote attributed to Brad Dwyer says Argon ranked first in Roboflow's object detection evaluations and led its new text benchmark, and that Argon has been fully integrated into Roboflow.1920
