AMD acquires World Labs for $8.2B

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC

AMD reportedly bought World Labs for $8.2 billion, according to a newsletter item citing DL Weekly.1 MIT Technology Review reports that AMD acquired World Labs, which was cofounded by the Stanford AI researcher Fei-Fei Li, at the end of September for $8.2 billion.2

World Labs had raised $1 billion in funding in February, before the acquisition.3

MIT Technology Review says that by their own admission it is still early days for the field, and that Li described it late last year as "nascent".4 The article adds that world models are for now a promising area of research rather than an immediate route to general-purpose robotics.5 The newsletter frames the deal as part of wider industry consolidation, listing it alongside SemiAnalysis questioning NVIDIA's hardware neutrality after its acquisitions. inference67
