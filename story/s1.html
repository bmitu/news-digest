Claude Opus 5.5 release

4 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 2 new today · 5 independent origins: Anthropic, Artificial Analysis, Benjamin Breen, Dan Greenheck, Simon Willison

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in a new Claude 5.5 family, which it says performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most tasks and costs 40% less to run than Opus 5.123

Simon Willison reports that list pricing fell 20%, from $5/$25 to $4/$20 per million input/output tokens, with cache-read prices down 60%. Latent Space adds that the "40% cheaper" claim applies to default (medium) settings, and the per-task saving over Opus 5 disappears at max effort. inference45

Benchmark results are strong. Artificial Analysis puts Opus 5.5 first on its Coding Agent Index with 66, up from 60 for Opus 5. Zvi reports that Artificial Analysis's new Terminal-Bench-Science has GPT-6 Astra at 63% and Opus 5.5 on xhigh at 62%, and that no other model breaks 50%.67

Results are not uniformly in Opus 5.5's favor. Zvi says Astra or Fable 5.1 is still ahead in places, and that Fable 5.1 takes all seven Vals professionals rows. Astra leads WeirdML v3 at 42.2% against 31.2% for Opus 5.5.8910

Zvi says feedback is almost universally positive, and that the model is pleasant to talk to and write with. He reads the system card as showing a smaller version of Fable 5.1 rather than a new Opus iteration.1112

Some users report weaknesses. One says it processes a complex, nested-meaning task less deeply than Fable 5.1, and Zvi notes reports that it struggles to infer non-obvious intent.1314

Willison found that at "max" thinking, Opus 5.5 twice failed to return a pelican SVG because it hit the 128,000 output-token limit while reasoning. He suspects "max" is effectively useless, and Latent Space relays @theo's advice to avoid it.15161718
