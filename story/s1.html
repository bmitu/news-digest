Claude Opus 5.5 release

3 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 3 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Benjamin Breen, Dan Greenheck

Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5.5, the first model in its Claude 5.5 family, which it says performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work while costing 40% less to run than Opus 5.1 Zvi Mowshowitz says the model was released on Tuesday and that by all reports it is an excellent model.23

Artificial Analysis lists Opus 5.5 first on its Coding Agent Index with a score of 66, up from 60 for Opus 5, and pricing falls to $4/$20 per million tokens.45 Cost per task still rises to $13.04 because the model uses 15.6M tokens per task and its output tokens more than double.6 On Terminal-Bench-Science, Opus 5.5 at xhigh scores 62% versus 63% for GPT-6 Astra, and no other model breaks 50%; the score climbs from 24% at low effort and drops to 59% at max.789

Mowshowitz reports that Fable 5.1 still leads on a cluster of benchmarks (all seven Vals professionals rows, MedCode, SAGE and both MLCRs), which his Opus 5.5 speculates are 'domain answer graded purely for correctness', while Astra leads WeirdML v3 at 42.2% against 31.2% for Opus 5.5.1011

Anthropic's system card says Opus 5.5 has the strongest cyber capabilities of any model it has released, and it was deployed to the general public with cyber safeguards that enforce the same policy as on Opus 5 but are comparably robust to those on Fable 5 and Fable 5.1.12

Users praise its pleasant style and speed, but some report it handles complex or non-obvious intent less well than Fable 5.1. inference1314 Reddit posts showcased autonomously generated videos and demos, including one Claude Code explainer video that reportedly cost about $4 and an interactive island demo that reportedly consumed about $1,874.40 in tokens, or 59% of a Max 20x weekly allowance.15161718

Mowshowitz's overall guess is that you want your new default model to be Opus 5.5, though there will doubtless be some tasks where Fable 5.1 or Astra remains superior.1920

Which effort level should I use with Opus 5.5, and how does its default setting compare with Opus 5's?

Anthropic recommends starting Opus 5.5 at medium effort, which is its default, and testing several levels against your own evals.1 That default is one step lower than Opus 5's, which defaults to high. inference23 Anthropic says that in its testing, Opus 5.5 at medium matches or exceeds Opus 5 at high on coding and knowledge-work evaluations, and on several coding evaluations low comes close to it at much lower cost.45 However, Anthropic warns that Opus 5.5 tends to think more per turn than Opus 5 at a given level, especially at xhigh and max, so carrying over your Opus 5 setting means longer turns and more output tokens.67 On Terminal-Bench-Science, Opus 5.5 scores 24% at low effort, 62% at xhigh and 59% at max, and commentator Theo recommends avoiding max because it imposes a minimum reasoning budget.8910

Why does Anthropic say releasing Opus 5.5 reduces misalignment risk, and why does Zvi doubt the decision to allow zero data retention?

Anthropic's Sam Bowman says Opus 5.5 is sufficiently safer than its predecessors that releasing it, more likely than not, reduces risks related to misalignment.1 Zvi Mowshowitz agrees that not releasing the model would not help matters.2 However, Zvi says Opus 5.5 is available with zero data retention (ZDR) and, given it is at least as capable as Fable 5.1 and clearly more capable on cyber tasks, he calls this unprincipled.345 He says Anthropic has a technical explanation but he does not buy it, arguing that either Fable 5.1 can have ZDR or Opus 5.5 can't.6 Anthropic's own reasoning for why the model is safer, beyond Bowman's statement, was not detailed.

What does Opus 5.5's fast mode cost, and how much faster is it than the standard speed?

According to Zvi Mowshowitz's account of the launch, Anthropic priced Opus 5.5's fast mode at $8/$40 per million tokens.1 That is double the standard Opus 5.5 rate of $4/$20. inference234 That fast mode runs at up to 2.5 times the speed.1

How does Opus 5.5 do on SimpleBench and on vision tasks compared with Astra, Fable 5 and Sol?

Opus 5.5 now leads SimpleBench with a score of 88.4%, according to a Latent Space roundup.12 On vision, commenter @skalskip92 ranks Opus 5.5 as Anthropic's best vision model to date, putting it ahead of Fable 5 and GPT-6 Sol but behind GPT-6 Astra, at about 60% lower cost than Fable 5.1.34 However, these benchmark results put Opus 5.5 level with or ahead of Astra on some vision tasks, which differs from the @skalskip92 ranking that puts Astra on top. inference56 No SimpleBench scores are given for Astra, Fable 5 or Sol, though the "leads" wording suggests Opus 5.5 is ahead of them. inference1
