OpenAI perplexity-based AI development limits

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
6 sources · tracked since Nov 23 · 5 new articles today · 2 independent origins: METR, OpenAI

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published an essay, "Perplexity and Intelligence," arguing that perplexity (cross-entropy loss) should be a metric for comparing model developers and informing shared safety standards.123 He proposes common limits that developers would commit not to cross until their ability to understand and monitor AI behavior reaches a concrete milestone, such as a successor to chain-of-thought monitors.45 TechCrunch reports that Sam Altman described the post as "the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while."6

Pachocki says log-perplexity was about 0.99 for GPT-3.5 and about 0.91 for GPT-4, and that capabilities in cybersecurity and mathematics emerged once losses fell below 0.8.78910 He expects further transformative capabilities in the ~0.68-0.7 window, though he says he does not know what they will be.11 METR says that, on OpenAI's data, loss has fallen about seven times faster since early 2026 than in 2022 through 2025.1213

Microsoft, with OpenAI's permission, reported a log-perplexity of 0.819 for its MAI-2 Base on OpenAI's evaluation set, which Latent Space called a first win while saying at least a couple more top labs, notably Anthropic, would need to join.1415

Pachocki himself lists challenges: labs use different datasets and data mixtures, perplexity does not map directly onto tasks, chain-of-thought reasoning does not reduce perplexity, and the floor is unknown.1617 Critics cited by Latent Space say the measurement uses a narrow held-out code corpus and may reflect data-mix shifts, and others argue that post-training likely accounts for more than 75% of FLOPs, making pretraining perplexity the wrong pacing metric.1819 OpenAI staff respond that the eval is fixed over time and excludes model-generated code, while some researchers ask OpenAI to release the held-out set.2021

METR says its staff have no consensus on what the acceleration means, noting that it is not matched by benchmark scores, but it praises the disclosure and urges all developers to publish similar data.2223
