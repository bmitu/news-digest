Binance hack

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 6 · single origin, unclear which

Binance, described as the world's largest crypto exchange, was hacked, with OECD.AI's incident monitor putting the loss at $6.3 billion and describing it as one of the largest digital thefts ever.12 The OECD.AI summary, which is labelled AI generated, says North Korean hackers ran a months-long attack using fake job interviews and malicious AI tools, compromising engineers' computers.345 Latent Space's newsletter reports that about $6.3B was lost after a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, and notes that one post attributes the attack to North Korea.67

However, the figures differ across sources: Zvi Mowshowitz quotes a Watcher.Guru post claiming over $5.6 billion was stolen, including $1.45 billion in BTC, while OECD.AI and Latent Space cite $6.3 billion. inference8910 The role of AI is also disputed: OECD.AI says hackers used Binance's internal AI tool to control employees' computers and escalate access, whereas Mowshowitz says it was not a pure 'AI hacks Binance' story but a standard fake interview plus malware story dating back to June. inference1112

Mowshowitz argues the hack should not be attributed to particular recent AI releases and that AI's impact on hacking rates will have substantial practical lags.13 Mowshowitz says the story is big given the sums, but not headline-news big because Binance will cover losses, and he advises keeping self-custody holdings cold for a while.1415
