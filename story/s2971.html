Meta Muse Spaces

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Meta)

Meta has launched "spaces" in its Muse AI app, a feature that lets the assistant build customized, app-like experiences such as a shopping hub, school dashboard, music tracker or trivia game.1 Meta says users create a space by describing it in chat, whether it is a tracker, a planner or a project board.23 Users can also check things off, add notes and make edits inside spaces, and can keep them in the sidebar, move them to the main tab, or schedule when they appear.45

TechCrunch's writer describes spaces as basically breakout conversations on a particular topic, which Meta calls "custom experiences" and which live in the sidebar.6 Mark Zuckerberg posted on X that he had made a BBQ game, an event tracker and a home control dashboard with spaces.7

The Verge reports that Meta is rolling out spaces to all Muse users and is looking into ways to share and collaborate on spaces.8 Meta also announced that "Active Memory" and "Active Focus" capabilities from its Muse Wonder model are coming to the Muse app and Meta AI chatbot, which it says will help Muse recognize when past information becomes relevant and work better toward goals.910

TechCrunch says Muse agents have been greeted with a relatively warm reception, citing Zuckerberg's October earnings-call statement that its agents had 10 million users in the U.S. and Canada, and notes that Meta competes with other AI agents including OpenAI's dots and SpaceXAI's Grokbot.111213
