Anthropic shares fall below IPO price

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
5 sources · 564 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 25 · 4 independent origins: Bloomberg, Financial Times, Semafor, TechCrunch

Anthropic's stock fell below its $83 IPO price on Tuesday, trading as low as $76 by midday, according to TechCrunch, which put the drop at nearly 12% from Monday's close.1 Semafor's separate report put the Tuesday decline at more than 6% and called it the first dip below the IPO price.2 Bloomberg reports that the shares ended Tuesday 6.5% lower at $81.91, more than 1% under the offering price, which left Anthropic with a market value of roughly $1.5 trillion.34 According to Bloomberg, the stock first slipped under that price before the market opened, reaching as low as $81.28.5 TechCrunch says a fall below the IPO price is often seen as a negative signal, and that continued weak trading could pressure private startup valuations and weaken IPO prospects for other AI companies.67

Nvidia also fell more than 7% by midday, giving back part of a large Monday gain after Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO.8 Semafor attributes the drop to renewed AI anxiety as the optimism from Huang's return faded, and quotes an analyst telling the Financial Times that any recovery is likely to be limited.910 Bloomberg also places the slide within a wider retreat in equities, which had surged at the start of the week.11

A second Semafor piece argues the fall reflects IPO allocation gamesmanship, noting the listing was reportedly six times oversubscribed.1213 IPO researcher Jay Ritter says investors inflate their orders when a deal sounds oversubscribed, which does not reflect real desire to own the stock.1415

Zvi Mowshowitz writes that the stock's weakness was largely driven by the lost IPO bounce, Huang's absence from Nvidia and a broad tech decline rather than by a claimed Riemann Hypothesis proof, and he argues the market is badly underpricing AI.1617
