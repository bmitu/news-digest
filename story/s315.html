GPT 6.1 Sol: Near-Astra intelligence for a fifth of the price

4 sources · 1056 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · 2 new articles today · 4 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Latent Space, OpenAI, Simon Willison

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Sol, which it pitches as "near-Astra intelligence for a fifth of the price", and made it available starting the day of announcement to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex (not yet in Chat), and to developers through the API as gpt-6.1-sol.123 Standard API prices are $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.10 per million, which OpenAI says is 95% less than standard input pricing.456

OpenAI says that on DeepSWE v1.1 GPT-6.1 Sol matches GPT-6 Astra at roughly one-fifth of the cost and beats GPT-6 Sol's best score by 6.4 percentage points, at a lower reasoning effort and cost.7 On Terminal-Bench Science 0.1, OpenAI says it costs $5.47 per task at maximum effort versus $23.21 for Opus 5.5 and $23.80 for Astra, but concedes Astra still has the highest score (68.1%) and should be used for the hardest scientific research tasks.89 OpenAI says its factual-error rate on deliberately difficult prompts fell at low reasoning effort from 11.4% to 7.7%, while cautioning that these prompts are not representative of typical usage.1011

Artificial Analysis places GPT-6.1 Sol one point below Astra on its Intelligence Index at $0.72 versus $3.26 per task, and reports gains of 12 points on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 5 on HLE over GPT-6 Sol.121314 Artificial Analysis also reports that it uses 10–30% more output tokens than 6 Sol, and attributes the savings to fewer turns and a lower cache-read price.1516 Results are sensitive to the test harness: Theo's Codex-harness runs scored much higher than Artificial Analysis's mini-swe-agent runs, and Artificial Analysis disputes that there is a significant harness bump and asks about repeat counts.1718

Arena ranks it #3 on Code Arena WebDev at 1759, 70 points above GPT-6 Sol, and it is reported as the new #1 on MathArena.1920 Google's Gemini 4 Argon scores 53 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, edging GPT-6.1 Sol's 52.21 Simon Willison reported that his pelican-on-a-bicycle drawings for GPT-6.1 Sol were not notably different from those of the GPT-6 family.22

How does GPT-6.1 Sol's cost and score on OSWorld 2.0 computer-use tasks compare with Astra's?

OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol comes within 2.1 percentage points of Astra's score on OSWorld 2.0's offline set at maximum reasoning effort, at roughly one-seventh the cost per task.1 OpenAI puts the cost at roughly one-seventh of Astra's per task, which is cheaper than the one-fifth price gap it cites for tokens in general. inference23 Ari Weinstein, who leads the product and engineering team for Computer Use agents at OpenAI, says GPT-6.1 Sol is a fifth of the cost of Astra and a seventh of the cost if you're looking at Computer Use specifically.4 OpenAI also says GPT-6.1 Sol outperforms GPT-6 Sol by seven percentage points on OSWorld 2.0's offline set at maximum reasoning effort, at less than half the cost.5 The OSWorld 2.0 figures come from OpenAI's own launch materials, and there is no independent measurement of Sol's or Astra's computer-use cost or score.

What is GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast, and when will it be available?

GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is a faster version of OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol model, offering up to 8x faster token generation than standard speed in Codex.12 OpenAI describes Ultrafast as its premium speed tier for workloads where speed matters most, with about 300 tokens per second in Codex and up to 6x faster generation in the API.3 A hands-on report says generation is about 8x faster, but end-to-end agent tasks speed up only 2–4x because tool latency dominates.4 On timing, OpenAI said it would arrive "in the coming days" after the September 2026 launch of GPT-6.1 Sol, and its DevDay recap says only that it is "coming soon."56 The Ultrafast tier already exists for GPT-6 Astra, which OpenAI says is available today in the API and in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Pro 500 and Enterprise plans.7 No exact release date or plan availability for GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast was reported.

Why is GPT-6.1 Sol so much cheaper per task than GPT-6 Sol, even though it uses more output tokens?

Artificial Analysis measured GPT-6.1 Sol at $0.72 per task at max effort, against $1.04 for GPT-6 Sol.1 Artificial Analysis attributes the savings to Sol using fewer turns and having a lower cache-read price.2 OpenAI says cached input now costs $0.10 per million tokens, 50% less than GPT-6 Sol's cached input price.3 Because agents reuse context across requests, cheaper cached reads and fewer turns plausibly outweigh the modest rise in output tokens, whose price is unchanged at $2/$10 per million tokens. inference456
