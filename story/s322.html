Sign in with ChatGPT

2 sources · tracked since Sep 30 · single origin (OpenAI)

At OpenAI's Dev Day on Tuesday, OpenAI announced "Sign in with ChatGPT," which lets users bring their ChatGPT identity and spend their existing AI allowance in third-party apps.12 TechCrunch reports that OpenAI has 16 launch partners for the feature, including Cognition's Devin, Notion, Vercel, T3, OpenClaw and Dactyl, and says it plans to add more soon.3 Latent Space lists Devin, Nous Portal/Hermes and T3 Code as example partner apps.4

TechCrunch frames the feature as part of a broader OpenAI push to disrupt the traditional app store model, turning ChatGPT into a place where software is discovered, launched and used by people and agents.56 TechCrunch notes a gap: OpenAI did not announce a billing or revenue-sharing system comparable to the economic layer of traditional app stores, and has not said the features would remove the need for separate third-party subscriptions.78

The company says ChatGPT now has 1.2 billion weekly users, and the same event introduced Dots, agents that each run on their own cloud computer and connect to 4,000+ apps (per Latent Space; TechCrunch also says Dots have their own cloud computer and can connect to OpenAI's ecosystem of over 4,000 apps).910 Alongside this, OpenAI launched an enterprise marketplace with some 30-plus partners, and eligible customers can apply part of their OpenAI commitment toward approved partner software, OpenAI said.1112 Latent Space, in its recap, cites the commenter @apoorv03 as framing the moves as OpenAI competing to own the enterprise AI budget.13

Which ChatGPT plans can spend their allowance in partner apps, and can I limit how much each app uses?

Plan usage in partner apps is available to Plus and Pro users, according to OpenAI, though only in participating tools.1 Yes, you can limit each app: OpenAI says users can spend their allowance across 16 partners, including Cognition's Devin, Notion, Vercel, T3, OpenClaw and Dactyl, and control how much each can use.2 OpenAI also says eligible OpenAI usage in those apps counts toward your plan limits.3
