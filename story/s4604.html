Anthropic shares fall after IPO, market selloff

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
3 sources · 714 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · 2 independent origins: Fortune, NBC News

Anthropic's stock fell sharply on Friday, its second day of trading, and came close to dropping below its $83 IPO price, wiping out the gains from its blockbuster debut a day earlier.1 Fortune reports shares hit a low of $83.05, down more than 14% on the day, before recovering to about $87 at midday, while NBC News says the stock lost 9% on Friday and ended a few dollars above the IPO price. inference23 On Thursday the shares opened at $111.50, briefly pushed the company's market value above $2 trillion, and closed at $97.17, 17% above the offer price.45 The IPO price of $83 valued Anthropic at approximately $1.5 trillion, and Fortune reports the company raised more than $115 billion in the offering.67

The drop came during a broad market rout: NBC News reports the Nasdaq Composite finished down 5.3% and erased its 2026 gains, while the S&P 500 and the Dow entered correction territory.89 Nvidia also fell about 14% after a Reuters report that regulators are examining accounting irregularities at the chip company.10

According to NBC News, the decline developed over several weeks starting in early October, when stock prices came under strain from high oil prices, climbing interest rates, growing corporate debt, persistent inflation, supply chain problems and a weakening consumer.11

Richard Saperstein of Treasury Partners characterizes the market's current turmoil as an "AI repricing" that reflects data center construction that is slowing, fewer funding sources for AI-related projects, and tech earnings that are growing more slowly.12 Kevin Gordon of Charles Schwab says the market is being hit by "dual forces," and warns that if the downturn drags on, it could start to do greater damage to economic growth.13
