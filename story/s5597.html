Walmart enables Meta Muse shopping via Agent Gateway

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 23 · 1 new article today · single origin (Walmart)

Walmart is letting Meta's Muse AI agent shop its website on a customer's behalf, so the agent can find and buy Black Friday deals.1 Walmart says Muse support is "just the beginning" and that experiences for other AI agents and apps are on the way.2 Both outlets contrast Walmart's approach with Amazon, which has blocked or acted to stop third-party AI agents from shopping for customers (The Verge also names eBay). inference34

According to Walmart, the Agent Gateway helps AI agents find relevant products without sharing the user's purchase history.5 Muse buys through "Agent Profiles" in the customer's Walmart account, where users can set spending limits, approve purchases and review what Muse bought.6 TechCrunch adds that users can delete their agent's profile at any time.7 Per Walmart's instructions, shoppers sign in to Walmart via Muse, set a spending limit when first adding an item to the cart, and pay with a card saved in their Walmart account at normal Walmart.com prices.89

The sources differ on timing: TechCrunch describes the partnership as announced Tuesday and says the Agent Gateway launched in October, whereas The Verge says the gateway was announced in October and its live rollout came on November 24th. inference1011 The Verge also reports that Walmart told it after publication that the Agent Gateway launch had been delayed by 24 hours, and that shopping on Walmart via Muse will now be available on November 24th.1213
