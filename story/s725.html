Microsoft Surface and Copilot announcements

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
3 sources · 3 independent origins: Ars Technica, TechCrunch, The Verge

At its first live event in two years, Microsoft announced the Surface Laptop Ultra, the first device built on Nvidia's RTX Spark chip, with a starting price of $2,599 and shipping from October 16.1234 TechCrunch reports the Surface Laptop Ultra comes in two base models, one starting at $2,600 and one with a more powerful chip starting at $3,700, with prices rising to $5,900, and says Microsoft says the highest-end device is already out of stock.56

Microsoft also unveiled the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box at $5,999, which The Verge says is slated to ship in November.789 Ars Technica says the Dev Box claims one petaflop of AI compute, while The Verge says it is optimized for running local AI including models exceeding 120B parameters.101112

Microsoft is overhauling Copilot under a concept it calls "Hybrid Intelligence," which mixes local and cloud AI models and would give Copilot access to local files and the ability to act across the OS.1314 Jacob Andreou, Microsoft's EVP of Copilot, demonstrated the Autopilot tool gathering tax documents and drafting an email, and said Hybrid Intelligence features will arrive in Copilot over the "next couple months."1516

Microsoft also showed a new Windows search experience for quick actions, available this fall on Windows 11 according to Pavan Davuluri.17 Ars Technica concludes that questions about security and user acceptance remain, while TechCrunch notes Microsoft is offering up to $1,000 off for MacBook Pro trade-ins to court developers.1819
