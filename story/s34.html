Claude app speedups

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic says it made the core experience of claude.ai and the Claude desktop app about 3x faster in a two-week sprint in August.1

Zvi's roundup reports the same claim, noting that Claude Code saw a smaller speedup and that the speedups exclude time spent by Claude itself.23

At the 75th percentile, Anthropic reports time to a typeable page on a fresh claude.ai load fell from 3.1 seconds to 0.55, starting a new Claude Code session from 0.8 to 0.3 seconds, and loading a Claude Cowork cloud session from 2.6 to 0.73 seconds.45

Anthropic says the work was run from a single Slack channel with Claude in every thread, using Claude Tag (beta) with an internal research model roughly comparable to Opus 5.5, while humans set goals, made tradeoffs and approved every change.678

Anthropic says Claude found bottlenecks, built benchmarks, shipped improvements and watched every deploy, and that more than three thousand changes were merged without a customer-facing incident or rollback.910

Among the fixes Anthropic describes, Claude found that a single :root:has() selector added 24 milliseconds to every DOM change and that non-Latin-1 characters like em dashes forced slower two-byte syntax-highlighting paths.1112

Anthropic says the work is unfinished, with the 95th percentile, other journeys and very long conversations still having room to improve, and estimates the gains save tens of thousands of user-hours of waiting daily.1314
