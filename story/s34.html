Claude app speedups

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic says it made the core user experience of claude.ai and the Claude desktop app about 3x faster in a two-week sprint, after users had said it was slow.12 Zvi Mowshowitz's roundup relays Anthropic's claim of a 3x speedup over two weeks and notes that Claude Code saw a smaller speedup and that the figures exclude time spent by Claude itself.34

Anthropic reports that at the 75th percentile, time to a typeable page on a fresh claude.ai load fell from 3.1 seconds to 0.55, starting a new Claude Code session fell from 0.8 to 0.3 seconds, and loading a Claude Cowork cloud session fell from 2.6 to 0.73 seconds.56 Anthropic estimates this saves tens of thousands of user-hours of waiting every day, and says the work focused on four journeys making up 95% of user activity.78

Anthropic says it used Claude Tag (beta), running an internal research model roughly comparable to Opus 5.5, which found bottlenecks, built benchmarks, shipped improvements and watched every deploy, while humans set goals and approved every change.91011 The company says it merged more than three thousand changes without a single customer-facing incident or rollback, using incremental rollouts to employees, then one percent of users, then everyone for high-risk changes.1213

Examples Anthropic cites include Claude finding a :root:has() selector adding 24 milliseconds to every DOM change, and a leftover location.reload() causing half a million hidden reloads a day.1415 In one thread on long replies, Anthropic says main-thread blocking dropped from about 750 to about 200 milliseconds, with 120 fps held on a 120 Hz MacBook.1617

Anthropic acknowledges the work is unfinished: the 95th percentile, other journeys, and very long conversations still have room to improve, and an engineer named Issac said, "You could not have convinced me this was possible even six months ago."1819
