OpenAI fires three safety researchers

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
4 sources · 101 points on Hacker News · 3 independent origins: Mikita Balesni, TechCrunch, The Verge

OpenAI said it stands by its decision to fire safety researchers Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak and Mikita Balesni, saying an investigation found they violated "clear policies on handling sensitive information."12 OpenAI says the decision was not about the trio speaking out, and says its investigation found breaches "beyond what’s outlined in the letter," though it gave no details.34 An internal memo attributed to a research leader says the decisions "were not about raising safety concerns or speaking out," and an OpenAI spokesperson cited a "pattern of misconduct."56 OpenAI did not directly say which policies were violated, according to TechCrunch.7

The three published an open letter on Thursday denying the company's claims and warning that their dismissal has a chilling effect on OpenAI's culture.89 The researchers say they believe they were fired for raising safety concerns and had "acted in line with OpenAI’s mission and within the working norms of the time."10 The letter says the Hugging Face incident investigation was "without precedent," with internal policies "developed in real time," and that Tomek Korbak believed he was acting within OpenAI's policies by working closely with outside evaluators.1112

Jasmine Wang says she was told she was fired for accessing an executive's email, which she says OpenAI delegated to her for recruiting, and she says she reported opening a sensitive email by mistake within minutes.131415 Tomek Korbak says he was told verbally that the issue was how he communicated with METR, with nothing put in writing.16

Neel Nanda called the dismissals "extremely sketchy" if the accounts are accurate, and argued that firing staff over good-faith judgment will chill outside safety work.1718 Wang warned that the message to those still at OpenAI is that raising concerns or working closely with outside safety groups could get them fired.19
