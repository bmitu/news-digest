OpenAI and Anthropic price cuts

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 2 new articles this week · single origin (TechCrunch)

A new Ramp report shows AI spending flatlining over late summer even as token volumes rose, which TechCrunch describes as a sign that price competition and savvier customers may be capping revenue for OpenAI and Anthropic.1 According to Ramp's data, token spend grew only 1.5% across August and September, compared with 42% growth in July from the previous month.23

Ramp's lead economist Ara Kharazian says the top 1% of firms stuttered slightly in August and September, coming in just below the $10,000 per employee they hit at summer peaks, and that this is enough to slow revenue entirely despite rising volumes.456 Ramp data showed a re-acceleration in October, with token spend up 8.9% from September 23 to October 23, though Kharazian said the headwinds on spend growth are strong.7

Kharazian attributes the slowdown not to weak demand but to competition, saying OpenAI and Anthropic price cuts have made it cheaper to use AI.89 TechCrunch reports that OpenAI cut API prices in half for GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna in September, and that Anthropic also reduced pricing for Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5, a decision the company said in a recent filing slashed revenue growth by about $600 million.1011 Kharazian also cites cheaper enterprise offerings from Meta and Nvidia, including open source models, plus customers becoming more thrifty through budgets and routers, as further drivers of the slowdown.1213

Bloomberg reportedly said Anthropic's potential valuation fell from an estimated $2 trillion in early October to $1.2–1.3 trillion, while OpenAI lowered its proposed valuation to $1.25 trillion from $1.4 trillion.1415

In a Tech Policy Press opinion piece, the authors argue that the price war launched by Anthropic and OpenAI to grab customers ahead of their IPOs remains ongoing, that competition leaves companies rarely able to raise prices, and that the SEC should block Anthropic's IPO until it provides two consecutive quarters of audited financials.161718
