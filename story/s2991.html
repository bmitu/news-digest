Oracle $20B stock and bond sale

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · 200 points on Hacker News · single origin (CNBC)

Oracle filed plans on Monday to sell $10 billion in common stock and $10 billion in senior notes, about $20 billion in total, to finance its artificial intelligence buildout, according to SEC filings cited by CNBC. inference12 Oracle says it plans to use the proceeds for general corporate purposes, which may include capital expenditures, repayments of debt and future investments.3 Oracle also says it will turn to vendor financing, customer-provided hardware and customer prepayments, and expects to reach positive free cash flow in the second half of 2028.4

The raise had already been outlined at an investor day the previous week, where Oracle said it was limiting additional capital market funding for the fiscal 2027 to 2030 investment cycle to that $20 billion, yet shares fell 8.8% on Monday. inference56 Oracle's credit and stock have been under pressure: shares are down 33% in 2026 and 50% over 12 months, its five-year credit default swap spread is 292 (up 265% in a year), and S&P downgraded it to BBB- in July, with debt of $125.34 billion as of Aug. 31.789

Baird analyst Rob Oliver said the filing looks like what Oracle previewed and that he is unsure why the market is reacting so negatively.10 UBS analyst Karl Keirstead called the $20 billion cap "a major relief" given concerns about debt markets, though he noted UBS has argued those concerns were overstated.11 Pat Nerney of Dynasty Financial Partners says execution is what matters, warning that if Oracle has to return to the market in the back half of 2027 after calling this the last capital needed, "look out."1213

The Verge's Elizabeth Lopatto cited Oracle's need for another $20 billion as part of a wider AI debt wave that includes Meta, Alphabet and Microsoft, while PIMCO CEO Emmanuel Roman warned of a downturn and estimated $2 trillion in AI financing needs over five years. inference141516
