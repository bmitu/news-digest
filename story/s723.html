Typesafe AI raises $870M

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
3 sources · 382 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Latent Space, TypeSafe AI

TypeSafe AI, the developer of Jev, has raised $870 million at a $7.5 billion valuation, according to TechCrunch and the company's own announcement.12 The round was led by Andreessen Horowitz, with participation from Sequoia and existing investor DCVC.34 TypeSafe describes the round as a series A and says Martin Casado is joining its board.56

TechCrunch says the fundraise comes as little surprise given Jev went viral almost instantly after its September 15 release.7 Latent Space's AINews says Sequoia "leaked" that TypeSafe crossed 100M ARR in their first week.8 TypeSafe claims a third of Fortune 500 companies are already using the model, and says it has saved customers millions of dollars in production already.910

Jev is transformer-based but is not an LLM: it outputs probabilities, which the company calls "calibrated decisions," rather than text.11 TypeSafe claims Jev works significantly faster and uses far fewer tokens than LLMs, which TechCrunch says is what excites users and large corporations.12 Latent Space says several vendors shipped "decision" models on the same day, and that Jev is the reference point everyone benchmarks against.1314
