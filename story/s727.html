Nikon disqualifies Small World in Motion winner

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · single origin (BBC)

Nikon says its Small World in Motion first-place video, by Dr. Ning Xu, did not comply with the competition rules on generative AI, and the video has been disqualified.123 Nguyen Nam Nhat of Vietnam, with a video of a roundworm and a single-celled organism, is now the winner, according to the updated rankings.45 According to Ars Technica, the other winners also moved up a notch with Xu's disqualification.6

Xu's video was said to show cilia beating in the airway of a child with a respiratory condition, and Nikon had said it was reviewing the video after online skepticism about its authenticity.78 According to a BBC report cited by Ars Technica, some researchers said the video had structural abnormalities and cell features that appeared and disappeared, and others spotted what looked like AI watermarks in the source files.910

Nikon says its decision rests solely on the video's eligibility under the rules and should not be read as a judgment of Xu's professional reputation, scientific contributions, or intent.111213 Nikon also says the episode showed a need to revisit rules and evaluation procedures for future entries.1415
