Gemini 4 Neon release

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
5 sources · 376 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · 4 new articles today · 5 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Google, Nate B. Jones, Perplexity, Teortaxes

Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon, which it says delivers frontier-level intelligence with fast task completion for software engineering, knowledge work and science, and began rolling it out the same day.1234 Per Latent Space, Neon is the fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks, and the earlier Argon model is now retired.56 Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.15 and a 1 million-token context window.78 Gemini subscriptions will reportedly work in any harness starting December 2, according to kimmonismus.9 Latent Space also reports that Google reportedly raised effective subscription usage by about 75% through caching and harness work, and that Neon placed third on FrontierSWE at 62.4%.1011

According to Google, its Neon model takes third place overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, scoring 61 when set to xhigh thinking, and comes second on the Vals Index.12131415 According to Latent Space, Neon achieved the highest first-day usage ever seen on Google's paid APIs, while Perplexity has processed more than 120 billion Neon tokens. Perplexity's Aravind Srinivas ranks Neon's intelligence as sitting between Opus and Fable, though he considers it the strongest of the three when it comes to the style of its responses.161718 Google also claims a 32% lower cost per task than Carbon at its default high setting, and Latent Space reports that Neon matched Carbon's 60 on the Intelligence Index at high effort for $3.74 per task.192021 The Center for AI Safety newsletter reports that each task costs roughly 25% as much with Gemini 4 Neon as with Fable 5.2, and that Artificial Analysis places Neon in third position on its general intelligence benchmark, after Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra.2223 One estimate, attributed to the user teortaxesTex, puts Neon at xhigh roughly equal to Opus Max, at $6.44 versus $10.04 per Artificial Analysis task.24 Independent results are mixed: Latent Space says Neon debuted second on Agent Arena with 1,336 points while Carbon still leads the Text Arena, and on Vals Code Migration Neon scored below both Carbon and Hydrogen.252627 Latent Space's roundup lists Neon at 87.4% on Company Agent Bench, ahead of Fable 5.2 at 82.2% and GPT-6.2 Astra at 79.7%.28 Practitioner reports are positive: Lucas Beyer found that in a four-model comparison, including Opus 5.5, all built a working dataset viewer and Neon's was the most data-aware, while Thoughtful Lab says Neon found subtle vulnerabilities other models missed using a fraction of the tokens. inference2930

Simon Willison says the Gemini 4 pricing was already confusing and Neon made it worse, because Carbon is currently half price, making Neon the most expensive of the three until Carbon's price doubles on December 1st.313233 Willison also says his pelican test images for Neon look better than Hydrogen's and worse than Carbon's.34

A micjm.com analysis projects that Neon, assuming Carbon-level performance at the 127 tokens per second it saw via OpenRouter, would reach an Elo of 1,066 on its Agent Duel leaderboard, just below Opus 5.5 at 1,078, though the 90% interval for the difference spans -94 to +68.3536
