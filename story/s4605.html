Gemini 4 Neon release

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
7 sources · 1674 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Google, Simon Willison

Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon, which it says delivers frontier-level intelligence with fast task completion for software engineering, knowledge work and science, and began rolling it out the same day.1234 Per Latent Space, Neon is the fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks, and the earlier Argon model is now retired.56 Neon is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.15 and a 1 million-token context window.78

According to Google, its Neon model takes third place overall on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, scoring 61 when set to xhigh thinking, and comes second on the Vals Index.9101112 Google also claims a 32% lower cost per task than Carbon at its default high setting, and Latent Space reports that Neon matched Carbon's 60 on the Intelligence Index at high effort for $3.74 per task.131415 Independent results are mixed: Latent Space says Neon debuted second on Agent Arena with 1,336 points while Carbon still leads the Text Arena, and on Vals Code Migration Neon scored below both Carbon and Hydrogen.161718

Simon Willison says the Gemini 4 pricing was already confusing and Neon made it worse, because Carbon is currently half price, making Neon the most expensive of the three until Carbon's price doubles on December 1st.192021 Willison also says his pelican test images for Neon look better than Hydrogen's and worse than Carbon's.22

A micjm.com analysis projects that Neon, assuming Carbon-level performance at the 127 tokens per second it saw via OpenRouter, would reach an Elo of 1,066 on its Agent Duel leaderboard, just below Opus 5.5 at 1,078, though the 90% interval for the difference spans -94 to +68.2324
