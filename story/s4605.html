Gemini 4 Neon release

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 1 new article today · 7 independent origins: Artificial Analysis, Datalab, Google, Kelsey Piper, Nate B. Jones, Perplexity, Teortaxes

Google released Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, a day after OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra arrived, based on the timing Nate B Jones gives. inference1 Latent Space, citing posts on X, reports that the model broke Google's paid-API record for first-day usage and has reached Workspace customers in the Gemini app.23 Usage figures credited to Arav Srinivas also show Perplexity passing 120 billion Neon tokens, with Tuesday's 44.5 billion, counted with hours still left, already 23.5% above Monday's total. inference45

On benchmarks, the Center for AI Safety reports that Neon places third on an Artificial Analysis benchmark of overall intelligence, trailing Claude Fable 5.2 from Anthropic and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, while costing about a quarter as much per task as Fable 5.2.67 A separate estimate attributed to @teortaxesTex rates Neon xhigh as roughly equal to Opus Max, at $6.44 versus $10.04 per Artificial Analysis task.8

On coding, Latent Space cites a third place on FrontierSWE at 62.4%, and @VikParuchuri reports a small lead over Opus 5.5, 0.934 to 0.910, on a private benchmark drawn from the team's own agent runs. inference91011

Reception has been largely favorable: Latent Space calls Neon "the talk of the town," while Zvi Mowshowitz says feedback has been "remarkably positive" but sees relatively little discussion. inference121314 Jones finds Neon quick, clear and easy to work with, yet still likes using GPT-6.2 Astra for messy, difficult problems full of detail.1516 Srinivas ranks Neon's intelligence "between Opus and Fable" but its response style as best, and Jeremy Howard praises it as very good at teaching. inference1718 In a Reddit thread summarized by Latent Space, most commenters agreed the model is strong, but one argued that Antigravity tooling trails Claude's, and claims of superiority over rivals came without benchmark scores or evaluation methods.1920
