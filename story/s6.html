Amazon blocks Meta Muse from shopping

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new today · single origin (Nate B. Jones)

Amazon has blocked Muse, Meta's new personal AI agent, from its store, according to two commentators who mention it only briefly. inference12

Zvi describes Grok Bot and Muse as personal AI agents that can do various things for you, with Muse meant to handle tasks for individuals.3

Nate B Jones says Muse is a profoundly important launch because it gives people outside Silicon Valley a reason to care about AI, and that it reached the top of the US free iPhone chart ten days after launching on September 8.45

Jones says he asked Muse to review his subscriptions; it identified $5,350 a year in spending and so far has canceled $1,285 a year, and he stresses that the $5,350 is future spending avoided rather than a refund or a claim that it was all waste.67

Jones links the block to Amazon's advertising business, saying Amazon runs a $68.6 billion advertising business on being the place you decide, and he treats this as the reason it locked Muse out. inference8

Jones argues an assistant that helps you cancel will eventually help you buy, putting it between you and everyone who wants to sell to you.910

Zvi is more skeptical: he has not covered Muse, saying it was unclear what differentiated it from Codex and Claude Code and asking who would trust Meta with that kind of access.11

The passages report that Muse users are automatically opted in to having their interactions with the agent used for AI model training.12
