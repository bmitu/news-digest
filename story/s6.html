Amazon blocks Meta Muse from shopping

3 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · 1 new article today · single origin (Nate B. Jones)

Amazon has blocked Meta's Muse AI agent from shopping its store on behalf of users, according to GeekWire as relayed by The Verge.1 A popup began appearing on Sunday for Muse users saying that continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use.2 According to GeekWire, Meta did not notify Amazon that Muse would access its store, and Amazon also raised privacy and security concerns about Muse failing to identify itself when browsing and seemingly capturing customer credentials.34

Muse has been popular: it topped the App Store charts and has 600,000 US daily active users by an Apptopia estimate.5 Jones, who says he used Muse to find $5,350 a year in subscriptions and cancel $1,285 of them, calls it a profoundly important launch.67

Other coverage is more critical: Wired's Reece Rogers, as relayed by Zvi Mowshowitz, reports that Muse is better at surveilling than helping, and Zvi Mowshowitz says he hasn't covered Muse and Grok Bot partly because, frankly, who would want to trust SpaceX or Meta with that kind of access.89

Security concerns have also surfaced: two developers say they coaxed Muse into sharing its root filesystem, which Meta denies is a security breach, and Meta patched a zero-day in its macOS app found by researcher Patrick Wardle.1011

How did Meta respond to Jason Aten's claim that Muse read his Messages without permission, and what did Meta say about it?

Meta pushed back, with VP of Communications Andy Stone making clear the company does not believe Muse read Aten's Messages without his consent.1 Stone said on X that the Mac app's Messages integration is entirely opt-in. He said users must enable both Full Disk Access and the Messages connector, and that Muse can't read Messages otherwise.23 TechCrunch summarizes Meta's position as being that what Aten described did not and could not have happened.4 Singleton's description of the setup is that Full Disk Access options are grayed out unless enabled. Granting access requires manual confirmation in macOS Settings and triggers a restart of the Muse app, which Singleton said makes an accidental choice even less likely.567 Aten's claim, as reported, is that Full Disk Access was off when Muse read his messages. AppleInsider reports he said Muse synced 187,000 lines from his Messages database anyway.8910
