Amazon blocks Meta Muse from shopping

2 sources · tracked since Sep 27 · single origin (Nate B. Jones)

What did Meta's Muse actually do that made Amazon block it, and what specifically was Amazon worried about?

Muse is Meta's AI agent, designed to help with tasks such as researching topics, preparing briefings, and shopping.1 Amazon blocked Meta's agents from its shopping platform as part of efforts to prevent third-party bots from using its systems.2 The Verge reports that Amazon has shunned Muse from accessing its retail platform entirely over concerns about it capturing customer credentials.3 Nate B Jones offers a commercial reading: he argues that the thing that makes an assistant useful to you also puts it between you and everyone who wants to sell you something, that Amazon worked that out early and locked Muse out of its store, and he notes that Amazon runs a $68.6 billion advertising business on being the place you decide.45 The reports do not say what Muse did on Amazon's site that triggered the block. They describe Amazon's concerns, not a specific incident.

What is Meta planning next for Muse, such as the Muse Charm device, glasses and a business version?

Meta will ship Muse Charm, a keychain-sized device for talking to Muse, in December.12 The Charm is activated by a fingerprint sensor, has a screen showing the Muse avatar and offers real-time voice interaction. Zuckerberg pitched it as the fastest way to talk to Muse for people not wearing glasses.34 On glasses, Muse is coming to all Meta glasses, activated by saying its name. Meta's Alexandr Wang labelled this "coming soon".5 For business, Meta is expanding Muse to small businesses with integrations such as Shopify, Dropbox and Slack. Muse for Small Business is free with usage limits, and businesses that want more usage can buy a subscription.67 Meta has also introduced a Meta Enterprise Platform, which it hired Chirantan "CJ" Desai, the CEO of MongoDB, to lead. Meta says it will focus on bringing its full technology stack, including Muse, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code, to businesses and developers.89

How much does Muse cost, and how does Meta plan to make money from it?
