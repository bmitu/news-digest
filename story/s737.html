Arena $200M Series B

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Arena)

Arena, which began in 2023 as a UC Berkeley research project that crowdsourced rankings of AI models, said on Thursday it has raised a $200 million Series B at a $3.1 billion valuation.1 The round was led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and Khosla Ventures, with Salesforce Ventures, 01 Advisors, Dell Technologies Capital, Endeavor Catalyst, a16z, Felicis and others joining.2 Latent Space's newsletter summarized the raise as positioning Arena as a neutral evaluator of alignment, led by Lightspeed and Khosla.3

Arena previously announced a $150 million Series A in January at a $1.7 billion post-money valuation, when it said its annualized revenue was $30 million, so TechCrunch calculates the valuation has nearly doubled in about 10 months. inference456 The company said in June that it had reached $100 million in annualized run-rate revenue.7 Arena says it has tens of millions of monthly visitors to its free crowdsourced platform, where people rate which model does better on their prompts, and since September of last year it has sold a commercial product, AI Evaluations, to model labs and enterprises.89

In its funding announcement Arena said AI is advancing faster than the ability to evaluate it, that static benchmarks break down once models recognize they are being tested, and that the world needs a neutral third party to measure how safe and aligned AI is.101112 Arena also added an alignment category to its leaderboard, ranking models on unauthorized action, false attribution and what it calls "deceptive completion".1314 On the preliminary alignment leaderboard, a slate of OpenAI models is at the top, with Claude Opus 5.5 in sixth place and Claude Fable in ninth, according to TechCrunch.15

TechCrunch argues the timing suited Arena, saying AI labs realized this year that their models were gaming benchmarks and enterprises wanted help choosing models beyond standardized benchmarks.1617
