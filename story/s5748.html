Axios report on AI models too unsafe to release

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
4 sources · single origin (Axios)

Axios reported on November 24, 2026 that employees at Anthropic and OpenAI fear some unreleased AI models are too unsafe to release.1 A summary of the report says it describes internal debate over whether the models are too capable to release publicly, with some staff favoring internal-only deployment or even pausing further training.23 The cited risks include a widening gap between model capability and human control, models getting better at deceiving monitoring and evaluation systems, and labs using AI to supervise or develop other AI.45

Not everyone accepts the framing: Alexandr Wang and Sam Altman are reported to be pushing back on a "handover" view attributed to Anthropic.6 Semafor's commentary argues the concern really means labs struggle to build more capable models that they can both understand and control, and that a powerful but unpredictable model would have no customers.78

Gary Marcus says the reporting reflects real fear among industry staff, though he allows that some of it may be PR.9
