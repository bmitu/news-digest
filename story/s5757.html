GPT-6 protein-design capabilities

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 09:22 UTC
3 sources · 356 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 25 · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI says its GPT-6 family of models can design new protein shapes and sequences using only reasoning and general-purpose code, without calling specialized biology models, according to a preprint it published.1 According to OpenAI, one of its in-house research models equaled or beat purpose-built systems on a wide range of protein design challenges, and users were able to guide what it produced by asking for particular sites where molecules attach, along with particular forms.23

On structure prediction from sequence, OpenAI reports average TM-scores of 0.16 for GPT-5.6, 0.26 for GPT-6.2 and 0.38 for its latest internal model, compared with 0.93 for the specialized ESMFold2.45 OpenAI acknowledges that predicting a complete protein structure remained a clear weakness, with even its best internal models well behind specialized structure-prediction models.6

OpenAI also cautions that its design campaign used specialized models such as Boltz-2, OpenDDE and ESMFold2 to filter candidates, so the results do not yet establish end-to-end, zero-shot de novo minibinder generation without such help.78 Separately, a cost caveat was raised on social media: on a prior DNA task, GPT-6.1 Sol was reportedly about 2,000 times more expensive per task than a specialized model, according to @_lewtun.9
