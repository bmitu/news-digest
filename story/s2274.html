Chrome Gmail autofill

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Google)

Google is expanding Chrome's enhanced autofill so that it uses Gemini with Personal Intelligence to suggest information drawn from users' Gmail when they fill out forms.1 According to The Verge, it can retrieve details such as a shipment's tracking number, a flight booking number or hotel reservation, or the order number of a recent purchase.2 TechCrunch lists a somewhat broader set of possible suggestions, including a membership ID, address, phone number, or credit card number, and says the company announced the update on Wednesday.34

The rollout begins in the US, through Gemini in Chrome on desktop, Android and iOS, for users who have Personal Intelligence turned on.567 The Verge reports that sensitive data shown as a suggestion remains encrypted, and that users can manage what enhanced autofill can access in Chrome's settings or turn it off entirely.89

TechCrunch's Sarah Perez describes the update as seemingly minor but says it could make Chrome easier to use and give users another reason to stay in Google's AI ecosystem.10 Perez also likens it to Apple's feature that detects text-message verification codes and shows them above the keyboard.11
