EmbeddingGemma 2 release

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
5 sources · 435 points on Hacker News · single origin (Google)

Google DeepMind launched EmbeddingGemma 2, an open multimodal embedding model with 740 million parameters that puts text, code, images, video and audio in one shared embedding space, released under an Apache 2.0 license and built on the Gemma 4 architecture.123 Google says the original EmbeddingGemma, introduced last year, has passed 20 million downloads, and that the new model is best-in-class for its size, with leading scores among sub-1B multimodal embedders across benchmarks like MTEB Code and MAEB.4567 Google says MTEB Code improved by 9.92 points, from 68.76 to 78.68.89

Google says the model needs as little as 270M parameters for text-only use, with optional 170M vision and 300M audio encoders, and that its Matryoshka Representation Learning lets developers cut vectors from 768 to 512, 256 or 128 dimensions for up to 6x storage reduction.101112 Google reports an 8K-token context window, 4x larger than EmbeddingGemma 1, that handles up to 5.5 minutes of audio, 29 images or 58 video frames, and says that with quantization on a Google Pixel 11 Pro it needs about 191MB of active RAM for text-only weights and about 567MB for the full multimodal model.131415

Weights are on Hugging Face and Kaggle, and Google lists support for tools including transformers, MLX, vLLM, llama.cpp, SGLang, Ollama and LMStudio.161718 A Hugging Face post summarized by Latent Space says llama.cpp support was already merged and GGUF weights were available from ggml-org and Unsloth, and that the model's audio embeddings were new to some commenters.1920 Latent Space's summary also reports a different breakdown of the variants than Google's, citing 740M omni, 440M text+vision and 570M text+audio variants, and attributes a reported +14% on MTEB Code to Phil Schmid, a figure that differs from the 9.92-point gain Google gives. inference212223

Simon Willison welcomed the Apache 2.0 license, arguing that a closed, proprietary, hosted-only embedding model makes little sense because the vendor is likely someday to stop offering it, leaving users to pay to re-calculate millions of stored vectors.2425 One Reddit commenter objected that community posts linked mainly to Unsloth conversions rather than Google's original page, arguing Google deserves attribution.26
