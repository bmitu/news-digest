XBOW Chrome exploit bounty

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 18:59 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (XBOW)

XBOW says Google awarded it a $250,000 bounty for a full-chain exploit of Chrome, the second such bounty in Chrome's history, according to XBOW's Oege de Moor.12 De Moor says the bounty was the first awarded under Google's 2026 rules, and that he had set the team the challenge of a Chrome full-chain exploit by the Black Hat conference in August.34

The winning exploit took six days and $40,000 of compute, and was built with GPT-5.6 Sol.56

The sources differ on how autonomous the work was: de Moor credits "Brendan and the agents", and the Latent Space newsletter says it was built by humans and agents and was not described as fully autonomous. inference78

De Moor concludes that the cost of finding truly critical vulnerabilities is collapsing for attackers and defenders alike, and argues that restricting powerful models is not the answer.910 Zvi Mowshowitz says it was lucky the group was "the good guys" and argues that the bounty should have been ten times larger.1112 Mowshowitz notes that GPT-5.6 Sol is now two generations behind.13
