OpenAI science access for researchers

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI said it is broadening its program for academic researchers so that as many as 200,000 scientists around the globe can use ChatGPT at no cost; the next round of applications starts Monday, November 23.123 Grants will be handed out on a first-come, first-served basis, 50,000 in all for the new round, according to the company. A single grant extends to the recipient plus as many as two collaborators, so the round could reach 150,000 researchers.45 Semafor reports the expansion doubles the number of researchers the company initially offered when the pilot launched in July.6

OpenAI says 20,000 researchers in 33 countries have joined since the pilot began in July.7 OpenAI also says that, based on researcher feedback, it is moving away from lottery-based selection, and that it found most pilot participants would have stayed within ChatGPT Plus usage limits.89

OpenAI's Economic Research team examined over 580,000 science-related messages sent by 1,819 scientists and also polled 251 participants. By the surveyed scientists' own estimates, finishing the same work at equal quality without AI would have taken 1.9 times longer.1011 According to OpenAI, 98% of those who answered the discovery-timeline question expected AI to accelerate discovery in their field.12 The report itself cautions that productivity figures are self-reported, discovery results are expectations, and the findings do not establish causal effects.13 A Latent Space newsletter summarized the study more strongly, saying OpenAI reports scientists roughly doubled their productivity, which goes beyond the report's self-reported time estimates. inference1415

OpenAI also points to ChatGPT Edu, through which universities can give their campus communities access to ChatGPT, and says it contributed $5 million to a new Simons Foundation initiative.1617
