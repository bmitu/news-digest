Gemini Robotics 2.5

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: Ars Technica, Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind announced Gemini Robotics 2.5 on November 10, 2026, calling it its most capable vision-language-action (VLA) model to date, and it is rolling out to robotics partners rather than being generally available.123 Google reports a 30% to 100% higher success rate than previous baselines, depending on the task type, with tasks completed 20% to 30% faster.45

In a proof-of-concept with Apptronik and Mercedes-Benz, Apollo 2 humanoid robots performing automotive kitting at factories in Hungary and Germany reached a 96% success rate.678

Google credits Gemini 4 Argon with accelerating development by acting as a simulation engine that generates varied practice runs and digital twins from real camera scenes.910

Google says inference-time reasoning guidance improved safe task completion by 33% around obstacles and by 43% around nearby people.11 DeepMind also released ASIMOV-Jailbreak, an open-source benchmark of prompts disguising unsafe robot requests, and reports give different figures for it. inference1213 Google's own post says Argon reaches 99.5% jailbreak resistance versus 82% for Gemini Robotics ER 2, while Ars Technica reports 98.8% versus 73.5%. inference1415

Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam cautions that 96% is probably not enough for a company like Mercedes-Benz, and DeepMind director Kanishka Rao calls closing the remaining 4% gap a "steep road".1617 Whitwam also notes the model still runs on cloud servers, so the gains come with latency, and DeepMind Robotics head Carolina Parada acknowledges a trade-off between generalization and perfection in any one application.1819
