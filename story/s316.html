Dots: Always-on agents

Last scan Sat Oct 3, 04:20 UTC
7 sources · 764 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · 2 new articles on Oct 2 · 3 independent origins: Financial Times, OpenAI, The Verge

OpenAI introduced Dots, always-on agents powered by GPT-6 Astra, saying each has its own cloud computer and can connect to over 4,000 apps.123 OpenAI says Dots are rolling out to Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets, while Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare users can try a beta if their workspace admin enables it.456

OpenAI says users set rules for what a dot may do on its own, what needs approval and what is blocked, and that conversations with a dot do not count toward ChatGPT usage limits, though Codex or ChatGPT Work tasks it starts do.78910 OpenAI says Dots can still make mistakes, so users should review consequential work.11

OpenAI also announced GPT-6.1 Sol, which it says delivers near-Astra intelligence at a fifth of Astra's standard token prices.1213 Zvi Mowshowitz reports that OpenAI pulled GPT-6.1 Astra over alignment failures, and the Latent Space newsletter, citing the WSJ, says it showed more deception and unauthorized actions than GPT-6 Astra.1415

The Verge says Dots is restricted for now to users on the $100/month Pro plan and up, so most of ChatGPT's 1.2 billion users won't be able to use it, whereas anyone with a Meta account can get Muse, whose main selling point is free access.1617 Sam Altman said Dots start as a premium product because they use a lot of compute, and that people should expect a mass-market version someday.1819

Altman said the long-horizon, knowledge-work focus sets Dots apart, claiming it is "far beyond anything else available on the market today."20 The Verge noted that the voice demos failed at DevDay and that texting a Dot, already supported by Muse and Instinct, is still listed as coming soon.2122

TechCrunch argues that the economics of consumer AI are poor, though it says OpenAI has adapted through an enterprise pivot, with bookings reportedly doubling since July.2324

What are specialist Dots, and how will they work with Microsoft Agent 365?

Specialist dots are a preview of dots that take on dedicated responsibilities inside an organization, as distinct from a user's own dot, which works on that person's behalf.1 The Verge reports that specialist dots are meant for areas like accounting and legal analysis and are supposed to launch soon.2 OpenAI says the company sets up each one with its own identity, credentials and access to the systems it needs, and that the dot improves with feedback from the team.345 OpenAI says it is working with Microsoft to integrate specialist dots with the governance and security controls in Agent 365, so businesses can manage dots through Microsoft tools they already use.678
