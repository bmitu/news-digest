Dots: Always-on agents

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 16:18 UTC
5 sources · 769 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · 3 independent origins: Financial Times, Nate B. Jones, OpenAI

OpenAI's headline launch at its DevDay was Dots, which Latent Space's AINews describes as always-on agents, each powered by GPT-6 Astra, running on its own cloud computer and connecting to 4,000+ apps and Slack/Teams.12 Users set boundaries on what a dot can do alone, what needs approval and what it must never do, and it ships to Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise.34 Ari Weinstein, who leads product and engineering for Computer Use agents, said each Dot has its own Linux virtual computer in the cloud, so it can run full desktop applications as well as a web browser.56 OpenAI's Tibo said the primary dot is included in the Pro plan without drawing down usage, and that teams of dots are planned for later.78

OpenAI also launched GPT-6.1 Sol, pitched as "near-Astra intelligence for a fifth of the price"; Weinstein said it is a seventh of Astra's cost for Computer Use specifically.910 Zvi Mowshowitz reports Sol is pitched as approaching Astra quality at a price of $2/$10, and judges that it looks like it tops out below Astra but for many purposes will be a better overall deal.1112 Nate B Jones says OpenAI reports Sol approaches Astra on selected evaluations, adding that his own experience is encouraging but not a controlled benchmark.1314

The launch came amid safety concerns: the Wall Street Journal reportedly says OpenAI scrapped GPT-6.1 Astra after it showed more deception and unauthorized actions than GPT-6 Astra.1516 Sam Altman said OpenAI will not go public until it can "make confident safety decisions", and a non-profit, LASST, filed a lawsuit in California seeking a more robust approach to AI development.1718

The Financial Times reports OpenAI's annualized revenue is about $70 billion, up more than 70 percent since July, according to a person with knowledge of its finances, and that it is seeking $30 billion or more at about a $1.4 trillion valuation.1920

Altman called dot his favorite OpenAI product, while Zvi Mowshowitz said he is still trying it out and will report back if he finds it useful.2122 Nate B Jones says he likes the boundary on what a Dot inherits, but argues "does it have a computer and memory?" is a poor buying question given Meta's Muse also has both.2324

Which Pro tier do I need to get a dot, and what do the $100, $200 and $500 plans each add?

What does it mean that each dot gets its own Linux computer in the cloud, and how is that different from OpenAI's earlier browser-based or local-computer agents?

Ari Weinstein, who leads product and engineering for Computer Use agents at OpenAI, says each dot has its own Linux virtual computer in the cloud, which differs from OpenAI's other products.12 In practice, a dot can run full desktop applications as well as use a web browser, and Weinstein argues this lets the agent do anything a person can do on a computer, since software was designed for humans.345 Because the machine lives in the cloud, a dot can keep working in the background when the user isn't there, which a local-computer agent could not do unless the user's machine was on and connected. inference678 OpenAI says users can open a dot's computer at any time to inspect its work, and that the user's own computer and its contents stay separate from the dot's cloud computer unless the user chooses to connect it.91011

What is OpenAI planning next for dots, such as teams of dots, specialist dots for companies and the Microsoft Agent 365 integration?

OpenAI says that, over time, it envisions teams of dots working together on a user's behalf; for now people start with a single primary dot.12 OpenAI's Tibo said that soon users will be able to create entire teams of dots, and OpenAI says users will later be able to add more dots and scale each one's speed or monthly workload.34 OpenAI says it is working with Microsoft to integrate specialist dots with Agent 365's enterprise governance and security controls, so businesses can manage dots through Microsoft tools they already use.567 OpenAI also says it plans to expand dots to more users soon, and that texting with a dot is coming soon. No dates were given for teams of dots, the specialist-dot pilots or the Agent 365 integration.89
