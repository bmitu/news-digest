Dots: Always-on agents

6 sources · 756 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 29 · 4 new articles today · 4 independent origins: Financial Times, OpenAI, TechCrunch, The Verge

OpenAI launched Dots, which it describes as always-on agents, on Tuesday, and began rolling them out to Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise plans in eligible markets.12 OpenAI says each dot is powered by GPT-6 Astra, has its own cloud computer, and can connect to over 4,000 apps through plugins, with access through ChatGPT, Slack or Teams.345 OpenAI says users can set Custom Rules to allow, require approval for, or block specific actions, and that proactive background research uses read-only tools.678 OpenAI says the first dot is included in the plan at no extra cost, and that conversations with it don't count toward usage limits, while Codex or ChatGPT Work tasks it starts do.9101112

At the same event OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Sol, which it says delivers near-Astra intelligence at a fifth of its standard input and output token prices, along with a program letting users use a ChatGPT allowance across 16 partners including Devin, Notion and Vercel.131415 TechCrunch reads the announcements together as a push to disrupt the app store model, with ChatGPT, which OpenAI says has 1.2 billion weekly users, becoming a place to discover and launch software. inference1617 However, TechCrunch notes OpenAI did not announce a billing or revenue-sharing system comparable to traditional app stores.18

The Verge reports that a Dots voice demo went awry at DevDay, and that Sam Altman said Dots feel like "an always-on helper, collaborator … that you can give very large pieces of work to and it gets to know you and helps proactively over time."1920 TechCrunch argues the underlying economics of consumer AI are not getting any better, but says OpenAI seems to have adapted well through a widely reported pivot to enterprise, with enterprise bookings reportedly doubling since July.21

Can a dot use my own laptop, and how does it handle saved passwords and sign-ins to websites?

Yes, a dot can use your laptop if you give it permission, though OpenAI says connecting your own machine is optional.12 By default, OpenAI says each dot works on its own cloud computer, and your computer and its contents stay separate unless you choose to connect it.3 For saved passwords, OpenAI says that when signing into supported websites, dots can use saved passwords without exposing them to the model.4 Separately, OpenAI says people can sign in to a range of tools with their ChatGPT account to connect plugins faster and manage fewer passwords.5 That password handling applies only to supported websites, and OpenAI's description doesn't say which sites are supported or how the passwords are stored. inference4
