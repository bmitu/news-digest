RTX 3000-series BF16 unlock

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · 351 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 24 · 4 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: John Whitaker, Zagitta

A Linux kernel module called GPU unlock, published on GitHub, unlocks FP/BF16 instruction issue rate on Nvidia RTX 3000-series cards to their professional equivalents, doubling the FP/BF16 FMA issue speed into FP32 operations on GA102, GA103, GA104 and GA106 cards.12 The module runs before the unmodified NVIDIA driver binds the card, so it is neither a UEFI application nor a patched NVIDIA driver and does not need re-patching for each new driver release.34

On a 3090 Ti at 450 W, the project's README reports BF16 GEMM rising from 87.50 to 157.41 TFLOPS, FP16 from 87.46 to 118.39 TFLOPS and TF32 from 42.58 to 82.61 TFLOPS.5 The README also says matrix-heavy work such as LLM prefill (+14–36%), diffusion and FlashAttention speeds up, while memory-bound LLM decode and INT8 work stay about the same, and that reports comparing outputs found them bit-identical.678

User-reported BF16 GEMM gains vary by card, from +25–67% on the RTX 3090 (the lowest figure from cards capped at 225 W) to roughly +80% on the RTX 3060 12 GB, while one RTX 3080 Ti 20 GB stayed at half rate.9101112

Latent Space's roundup of Reddit threads says a post framed it as NVIDIA locking BF16 via a kernel rather than hardware, citing BF16 tensor pipe gains of 82→155 TFLOPS, though it says the claim relies on unofficial patching and may not scale to end-to-end workloads.1314 Another Reddit post, as summarized, claims Turing, Ampere and Ada tensor ops with FP32 accumulation are half-rate via fuses and that unlocks exploit a signed Booter/SEC2 vulnerability, while consumer Blackwell cannot currently be unlocked; one user measured RTX 3090 BF16 going from 60.201 to 115.658.151617

Commenters were cautious: one said real-world gains may be only 10–12%, others worried about stability, and one argued die identity does not prove unlock safety because of wafer binning.181920 A Latent Space digest rated the consumer GPU unlocks "mostly verified," citing a 2080 Ti unlock and about +50% BF16/TF32 matmul with identical outputs from John O. Whitaker, who also found decode for memory-bound local MoE barely changes.2122
