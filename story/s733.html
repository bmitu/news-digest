Anthropic Cyber Verification Program expansion

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
3 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic has launched an expanded Cyber Verification Program (CVP) that gives qualifying security professionals advanced cyber capabilities and reduced blocking classifiers, now organized into three access tiers.12 Anthropic says each tier includes its most capable models, including Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Mythos 5.1, and that the program merges the earlier Project Glasswing and CVP offerings.34

Defense Access covers defensive work such as incident response and malware reverse-engineering. Red Team Access adds authorized penetration testing and red-teaming, but still blocks actions such as deploying ransomware.567 In Red Team Access nothing was blocked and the model completed 34 of 50 tasks, which Anthropic says is effectively equivalent to its 67.6% success rate with no safeguards.89 Specialized Access, with the fewest cyber blocks, is reserved for a limited set of verified organizations authorized to test safety systems that could impact people's lives or disrupt markets, such as flight operating systems, power grids, telecom networks, interbank transfer infrastructure, and government administrative networks.1011

Anthropic says Project Glasswing partners found at least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities between April and July 2026, and that its own open-source scanning found an additional 5,500 between April and October 2026. It says more than 33,000 of the verified vulnerabilities have so far been rated critical or high severity.121314151617 Anthropic itself calls these figures an undercount based on partial survey data, and it expects the true impact to be at least five times higher. It also notes that fewer than 50% of partners disclosed patched numbers.18192021

Anthropic says its generally available models keep conservative cyber safeguards that block most cyber work, because cybersecurity is dual use.22 Commentators noted the expansion, with Latent Space's newsletter listing it among the day's top stories and Zvi Mowshowitz also flagging it. The passages contain no critical reaction to the program itself. inference2324
