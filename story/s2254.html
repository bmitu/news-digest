Meta AI training business terms

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 30 · 2 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: Meta, Reuters

Meta has paused a planned update to its commercial terms that would have required business account holders to let it use content from linked external websites, including for AI training.12 The changes were first set to take effect on October 30, but Meta pushed the date to November 20 amid pushback from users, courts and regulators.3 Meta's notice says the updates "will not take effect on November 20, 2026," that it is continuing to consult stakeholders, and that existing terms remain in effect.456

Nieman Lab reported that courts in Brazil and Norway issued preliminary injunctions blocking the terms, and the UK's Competition and Markets Authority opened a formal investigation into whether Meta was exploiting a dominant position to seize valuable AI training data.78 In the US, Senators Adam Schiff and Marsha Blackburn reportedly sent letters asking Mark Zuckerberg to withdraw the terms.9

Meta says its crawling practices are unchanged and that content owners can keep signaling preferences through robots.txt, but Nieman Lab noted the revised terms contained no specific language about respecting robots.txt.1011

Meta had removed the word "train" from the list of allowed uses and narrowed them to "advertising and business products," but Theo Bamber, chief executive of the UK's News Media Association, argued that "develop" posed the same threat. inference1213 Bamber called Meta's conduct "staggering" and wrote, "Does Meta think we can’t read?"1415
