DeepSeek raises $15B

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Bloomberg)

The Chinese AI lab DeepSeek has reportedly raised about $15 billion, roughly 100 billion yuan, according to a Bloomberg report relayed by TechCrunch.12 The report came two days after the U.S. ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek's AI technology from their systems over security concerns.3 Potential investors reportedly include the battery maker CATL and Tencent, among others.4 DeepSeek did not immediately respond to TechCrunch's request for comment.5

The new round follows one in June, when DeepSeek raised more than $7 billion at a $50 billion valuation.6 DeepSeek was also reportedly seeking an additional $1.5 billion at a valuation of around $71 billion in July, with a potential IPO in China as early as next year.7 Liang Wenfeng, who founded DeepSeek in 2023, retains an estimated 81.6% stake in its Chinese operating entity following the earlier round, according to WireScreen's analysis of corporate ownership data.89

The report says DeepSeek is ramping up spending on AI infrastructure and open models for domestic and international markets, and TechCrunch says the expansion could further intensify competition among domestic rivals.1011 TechCrunch frames the round as the latest sign of investors pouring billions into AI companies worldwide, and notes Moonshot AI's $2 billion raise in May.1213 Zvi Mowshowitz said his sentiment about AI stocks did not go up on hearing that DeepSeek raises $15 billion.14
