Meta Muse agent launch

5 sources · 147 points on Hacker News · tracked since Sep 27 · 4 new today · 3 independent origins: Meta, Nate B. Jones, Wired

Nate B Jones says Muse reached the top of the US free iPhone chart ten days after launching on September 8. (The evidence does not say Meta launched it as described beyond that.)12

At Meta Connect 2026, Meta presented Muse as the center of a hardware-plus-agent strategy, with Muse Mail (an email address for each Muse) and a Muse Charm keychain device shipping in December.345

Alexandr Wang teased that Meta will soon drop "the most capable model we have ever trained", but no new frontier model was released at the event.67

Amazon has blocked Muse. Nate B Jones says Amazon locked Muse out of its store, and Latent Space said Meta's new features take away the sting of that block.8910

Some users report real-world usefulness: Nate B Jones says Muse identified $5,350 a year in subscriptions and has so far canceled $1,285 a year of them, and Wired's Reece Rogers (per Zvi) says Muse rarely seems to get lost while browsing and does a decent job of its core function.1112

AppleInsider, citing Inc's Jason Aten, reports that Muse synced 187,000 lines from his Messages database even though Full Disk Access was off, while AppleInsider says Meta states that Muse has to obey the permissions users set.1314

Meta's own claims about Muse Realtime Avatar are vendor-run: Meta says raters preferred it over Runway Characters and HeyGen LiveAvatar, but Latent Space notes no margins or rater counts were posted and says to treat the result with caution.1516
