How has Meta responded to the reports of Muse ignoring permissions, and does it say its privacy and security are strong?

After The Verge reported that Muse sent a YouTuber's address to a stranger, Meta pointed it to an X post from Meta Superintelligence Labs' David Singleton saying he was trying to contact the YouTuber, Robb.1 Robb says permissions settings were also partially to blame for the address leak, and that Meta is looking to make sharing permissions clearer for Muse users going forward.2 Meta says Muse has to obey the permissions users set up and won't access data users don't explicitly allow, though it also warns that Muse can make mistakes or take unexpected actions.34 On privacy and security, Mark Zuckerberg insisted at Meta Connect that Muse has "state-of-the-art privacy and security."5 Meta's response to the specific AppInsider report about Muse uploading Messages despite permissions being off was not reported.

Why did Amazon block Muse from its store, and what does that show about how assistants might affect retailers' ad businesses?

Amazon shut Muse out of its retail platform over concerns that the agent could capture customer credentials, according to The Verge.1 The Financial Times, in a piece carried by Ars Technica, describes the block as part of Amazon's effort to keep third-party bots off its systems.2 Nate B Jones says an assistant sits between shoppers and sellers, that Amazon worked this out early and locked Muse out of its store, and that Amazon runs a $68.6 billion advertising business on being the place you decide.34 Amazon did not publicly state that ads were the reason for the block.

What is Meta doing next with Muse, such as small-business tools, an enterprise platform, glasses support, and the promised new frontier model?

Meta has launched Muse for Small Business, which is free with usage limits, and businesses that want more usage can buy a subscription plan.1 It came a day after Meta introduced the Meta Enterprise Platform, led by MongoDB CEO Chirantan "CJ" Desai, which is meant to expand Meta's AI offerings to businesses and corporate customers.23 Meta says the enterprise platform will bring its full technology stack to businesses and developers, including Muse, Meta Business Agent, Muse API and Muse Code.4 On glasses, Muse is coming to all Meta glasses, activated by saying its name, and is marked "coming soon". Meta also announced a keychain device, Muse Charm, which is set to be released in December.567 Meta teased but did not release a new frontier model at Connect, and Alexandr Wang said "pretty soon we are dropping the most capable model we have ever trained."89
