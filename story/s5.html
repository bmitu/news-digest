What does Muse Home Link actually do, and can it control things like TVs and speakers in my home?

Muse Home Link is a gadget Meta built with its new open-source Muse Gadgets tools, and it lets the Muse AI agent connect to a home network and talk to the smart devices on it.1 Meta says you use community-built skills to have the agent turn on a light, control your TV, or send a document to a printer, depending on your setup.2 Because Meta says what it can do depends on your setup, how much of your home it controls will vary. inference3 TechCrunch guesses the free units have already been claimed, given that Friedman's post drew nearly 30,000 views in a few hours.4
