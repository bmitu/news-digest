Agent Skills donated to AAIF

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
3 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic announced that it is donating Agent Skills, the open standard that gives AI agents specialized knowledge and workflows, to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation.12 Anthropic says it is committed to keeping the standard open, community-driven and vendor-neutral, and that the AAIF will provide a neutral home so the community has a say in where it goes next.345 The donation covers the Agent Skills name and logo, the GitHub organization and the agentskills.io domain, and Anthropic says the standard can still be used or contributed to without joining the AAIF.67

Anthropic says engineers from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI and other companies will maintain the standard as individuals, with David Soria Parra and Mahesh Murag as Lead Maintainers.8 However, a Latent Space newsletter roundup describes governance by a technical steering committee (TSC) with members from Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and others, a detail that differs from Anthropic's own description of individual maintainers. inference910 Anthropic has proposed a starting roadmap covering versioned releases, a formal proposal process and security guidance for people who write and install skills.11

Anthropic says more than 70 agent platforms, including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and VS Code, have integrated Agent Skills since the standard was published in December 2025.1213 Anthropic also says 1 million skills have been published and nearly 280 million installed.14

Developer Simon Willison says he predicted this outcome last December, when he wrote that he wouldn't be surprised to see skills end up in the AAIF.1516
