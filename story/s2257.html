Reddit login shift and stock fall

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
5 sources · 2 independent origins: BBC, Reddit

On its third-quarter earnings call on Thursday, Reddit announced it is transitioning to a primarily "logged-in experience", a move TechCrunch says is designed to protect its data from AI scraping and enable data-licensing deals with model providers.12 CEO Steve Huffman said in a shareholder letter that logging in is better for the business because of more signals for ad targeting and higher engagement, and that it helps protect Reddit's data corpus and adapt to emerging regulations.34 Huffman, who said he personally resisted the change because of his belief in the open internet, told Axios it is not a response to Google but is "about making the best version of Reddit."5 The change will not be immediate: Reddit will first test it in select markets outside the U.S. in the fourth quarter, and has no timeline for a worldwide rollout.67

Global daily active users were flat quarter-over-quarter at 130.3 million, while U.S. daily users fell from 53.2 million in Q2 to 50.7 million in Q3.8 Reddit said search referrals fell by more than 1 million per week on average in October and warned that daily users could decline by a low-double-digit percentage by next quarter if the trend continues.91011

Financially the quarter was strong: revenue rose 53% to $895 million, ahead of Wall Street's expectations of about $875 million, and advertising revenue grew 54% to $847 million.121314

Reddit's stock fell sharply after the report, though sources give different figures: Platformer says 15 percent, while the BBC says it plunged 29% by Friday. inference1516 The BBC places the drop in a wider tech selloff over AI spending, in which Alphabet lost more than $500bn in value on Thursday and the Nasdaq closed 2.8% lower.1718 Platformer argues that Google's AI answers and Reddit's traffic loss show the claim that AI is good for the web is "falling apart", and describes a loop in which sites lose traffic, gate their content and sell access to it.1920
