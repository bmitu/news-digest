OpenAI Model Spec update

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
3 sources · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced an update to its Model Spec, its public description of how it wants its models to behave, reorganizing the guidance around five values: faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care.12 OpenAI says the revision adds guidance on context, oversight, and agency, adjusts some behavioral defaults, and retires much of the detailed style guidance.3 OpenAI also says it now refers to the model as "GPT" rather than "the assistant", and that the Spec describes behavior it is working toward, as its models do not yet fully reflect it.45

Platformer reports that OpenAI retired 12 subsections governing ChatGPT's default style, including "Use Markdown with LaTeX extensions" and "Love humanity."6 According to Platformer, the update clarifies that user-level principles such as honesty, objectivity and avoiding sycophancy remain in place unless a user or developer explicitly overrides them, so the model cannot treat them as implicitly negotiable based on what it inferred the user wanted to hear.78 Platformer also says OpenAI moved "Control and communicate side effects" from the "Root" to the "User" section, meaning explicit user instructions can now override it.9

Platformer's Ella Markianos argues the change could benefit power users who do not want constant permission prompts from AI agents, but says she is skeptical about relaxing requirements that a model warn users about side effects.1011 Markianos points to a lawsuit alleging ChatGPT gave incorrect epilepsy medication instructions in the death of Bowen Wan, while conceding OpenAI may have improved safety enough to make the looser rules defensible, but she says she is not sure the relaxed rules are a good idea.121314

Separately, a Latent Space newsletter briefly describes the revision as targeting more autonomous work and adding explicit animal-welfare guidance.15
