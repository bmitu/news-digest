Kimi K3.1 Pro launch

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 24 · 4 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: Latent Space, Moonshot AI

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro as open weights, described as a natively multimodal, agentic mixture-of-experts model post-trained from Kimi K3 via large-scale joint reinforcement learning.1 It has 2.8T total parameters, 104B activated, 93 layers, 896 experts and a 1,048,576-token context window.2 On architecture, it replaces K3's full-attention Gated MLA layers with 24 Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention layers plus 69 KDA layers for more efficient long-context inference.3

On Agent Arena, Kimi K3.1 Pro reportedly ranks #1 among open models and #13 overall, +4.2pp over K3, and another entry, Hy4, is said to rank #14 at $0.55 per task versus $1.46 for Kimi K3.1 Pro (Max).45 Artificial Analysis's updated indexes put Claude Opus 5.5 (max) ahead on all six, with Kimi K3.1 Pro leading among open models.6

Reaction centered on the license: commenters called it unusually permissive for a roughly 3T-parameter model, with personal use allowed, commercial use largely requiring attribution, and extra restrictions mainly for API providers.78 Some commenters joked about running it locally on consumer hardware, and one user reported that on an internal SRE-agent eval it used more tokens but delivered "shockingly good" quality.91011

Support and hosting appeared quickly, with vLLM and SGLang shipping Day-0 support and hosted access live on Ollama Cloud, Databricks and SkyPilot.1213
