Hybrid Reasoning Agents paper

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
2 sources · 517 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (HybridAgents authors)

Researchers including Jiaru Zou submitted a paper to arXiv on 13 October 2026 introducing Hybrid Reasoning Agents (HybridAgents), which dynamically navigate between latent reasoning and text-based interaction throughout a unified generation process.12 The authors say modern LLM agents reason and interact through text, while latent reasoning is efficient but lacks native tool access and reasoning transparency, and that their design aims to bridge the two.3

Architecturally, a lightweight HybridLink enables seamless transitions between text and latent modes, and the agent's continuous working memory preserves lossless hybrid context across multiple turns; the authors say these designs preserve tool-integrated agentic capabilities while making latent thoughts directly inspectable.45 Training uses two post-training algorithms: HybridSFT stabilizes tool-integrated hybrid generation, and HybridRL learns when to think in latent space and when to act through text.6

The authors evaluated HybridAgent at 3 model scales across 11 benchmarks covering mathematics, science, medicine, search, and agentic reasoning.7 The authors report that, compared with frontier LLM Agents and agentic frameworks spanning both text-based and latent methods, HybridAgent consistently improves performance by 10.4%, while delivering 35.5%-54.7% token reduction, 1.8x-2.6x faster inference, and 2.7x-3.7x cost savings.8 The figures are the authors' own reported results; the passages include no independent evaluation or criticism of this paper. inference910
