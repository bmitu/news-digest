Netflix AI Discover tab

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 6 · single origin (Netflix)

Netflix is rolling out a new "AI Discover" tab in its TV app, where it will show experimental, AI-powered features meant to help users find shows and movies to watch.1 TechCrunch reports that Netflix announced the tab on Tuesday.2 The rollout is gradually starting in the US for members who use Netflix in English on supported TVs, with TechCrunch saying more users are planned in the coming weeks.34

The first experiment surfaces content around themes such as "Something Light," "Family Movie Night," or "Quick Watches," and once a user picks a theme, Netflix shows matching recommendations.567 According to TechCrunch, the curated themes feature clips from shows and movies and draw on mood, viewing preferences, available time, or what is new and trending.8 Users can pick more specific themes or choose "something different" to start over, and each title has a "Tune Your Recommendations" button to save titles or exclude ones they do not want suggested again.910

Netflix says AI Discover is powered by "a new intelligence layer we’ve been building that responds to what members tell us in real time and keeps learning."1112 Netflix describes the tab as its "test kitchen" and says user feedback will help decide which features to keep or improve before bringing them to the main Netflix experience.1314

TechCrunch suggests the tab shows Netflix wants to make its growing library easier to navigate, moving discovery away from endless scrolling toward a more personalized, AI-powered experience.15 The Verge notes the announcement follows Netflix laying off around 1,000 employees as growth has slowed and users are reportedly watching the service less.16
