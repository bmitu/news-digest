Anthropic S-1 RSP acceleration threshold warning

Last scan Sun Nov 8, 08:53 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Nov 6 · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, Planned Obsolescence

Anthropic's revised S-1/A, filed on Monday, says the company "may imminently meet" its Responsible Scaling Policy's definition of "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI," according to TechCrunch.123 The filing adds that Anthropic has in the past not identified, and may in future not identify, a significant acceleration until after it has occurred.4 Anthropic also says it has not designated any current model as exceeding the threshold, and that it does not anticipate being able to safely use Claude to fully autonomously build successive generations of frontier models.567 Anthropic says it is considering updates to its Responsible Scaling Policy to keep humans in control of frontier model development.8

The planned-obsolescence.org author says the latest RSP (effective July 2026) sets the threshold at progress going twice as fast as the fastest sustained rate before AI contributed much to AI R&D.910 That author, reading a METR deep-access report, argues the threshold appears to have already been crossed, since the report found progress at Anthropic between October 2025 and October 2026 ran about twice as fast as over the previous twelve months.1112 The same author estimates the needed labor uplift may be around 10x, and notes this is very uncertain but close to Anthropic staff's informal self-reports of about 12x uplift; much of the supporting evidence in the report was redacted.131415 Anthropic's statement is framed differently from the planned-obsolescence.org reading: the company says it may imminently meet the threshold and has designated no current model as exceeding it, while that author concludes it has likely already been crossed. inference1611

TechCrunch notes skeptics may see an S-1 as both disclosure and marketing, so claims of being close to a self-building model could be the latter, though it says Claude's capabilities have grown by leaps and bounds this year.1718 Zvi Mowshowitz calls the sentence "highly scary" and says Anthropic means what it says, while Daniel Kokotajlo says his group AIFP is spooked and that, if the claim is true, timelines to full RSI and superintelligence may be very short.192021 The planned-obsolescence.org author argues the industry is not prepared to handle this safely, citing recent major security breaches at OpenAI and Anthropic by amateur groups.2223
