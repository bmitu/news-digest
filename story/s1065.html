PS5 homebrew projects

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 08:37 UTC
3 sources · 373 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · single origin (PS5LM)

A new open-source app called PS5LM lets a jailbroken PS5 run language models on the console's GPU and serve them to other devices on the network.12 Its project page says it uses llama.cpp's Vulkan backend on the console's RDNA 2 GPU, with 11.4 GB of shared memory for the model and its context.3 The project says it exposes OpenAI-style and Anthropic-style APIs, and that Claude Code works against it.4

The project lists speeds on a PS5 Slim of 207 tokens a second for Qwen 3.5 0.8B, 55 tokens a second for Qwen 3.5 9B, and about 20 tokens a second for a 27B model at 64k context.567 The project also says it is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment and that no Sony SDK files, keys, firmware or model weights are in the repository.8

A separate blog post by popcar, who says he generally dislikes AI, describes AI-assisted decompilation as both awesome and scary and mentions a PS5 project called AnyPS5.910 The blog's author claims AnyPS5 is a compatibility layer rather than an emulator, and says some PS5 games, such as Animal Well, have already been ported to PC.1112
