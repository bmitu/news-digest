Meta Style Sketch app

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Apple)

Meta has quietly launched Style Sketch, an AI fashion app that helps users put together outfit ideas using clothes they already own, according to TechCrunch.1 The app connects to Instagram to build a virtual closet from photos users have posted, or users can add items manually, and it then suggests outfit combinations and lets users try them on a "realistic model matched to your body shape and skin tone," per the app description.23 It can also build outfits around a chosen item and make day-by-day trip outfit plans that account for weather and itinerary.45

Social media consultant Matt Navarra spotted the app and says it is available only in Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand, and TechCrunch says it is on iOS and Android there.67 The Verge says it could not verify the location restrictions itself, and both outlets say it is unclear whether Meta will release the app more widely. inference8910 Meta did not respond to TechCrunch's request for comment, and The Verge says it has reached out to Meta. inference1112

Meta also recently launched Waddle World, a "tamagotchi-style" pet game in which virtual creatures chat with users and doodle to illustrate conversations.13 The Verge reports that its store listings call it "a daily journal disguised as a pet," and that it is listed as free and available only to adults in the Philippines.1415

TechCrunch says Style Sketch is one of many recent Meta AI apps, alongside Waddle World, Pocket and Vibes, and that the Wall Street Journal reported Mark Zuckerberg told employees Meta plans to release far more apps than before because AI has made shipping faster and easier.1617 TechCrunch offers its own view that, against existing wardrobe apps such as Alta and Google Photos' tools, Meta will likely rely less on novelty and more on Instagram integration.1819
