Gemini chat history and training toggles

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 2 independent origins: Google, TechCrunch

Google is rolling out separate Gemini settings for chat history and AI training, so users can keep their chat history without letting Google use their conversations to train its models.12 Previously, the only way to opt out of contributing chats to training was to turn history off; both were tied to one "Keep Activity" setting.34 For people who had earlier switched off Keep Activity, Google says the two new settings will start out disabled, whereas people whose Keep Activity was on will see their chat history remain active.56 Google says the change is rolling out globally, though it may take a few weeks to reach everyone.7

At the same time, Google is lengthening how long it keeps chats made with history off, from 72 hours to about two months.8910 TechCrunch asked Google why it made the change, but the company gave no answer and referred the outlet to a blog post. That post states that conversations are retained for a longer period so Google can reply to users, handle feedback, and keep its services and their users safe.1112 TechCrunch notes that OpenAI says it may keep temporary ChatGPT chats for up to 30 days and Anthropic keeps Claude incognito chats for 30 days by default, though companies may hold some data longer for legal or safety reasons.1314

Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam says opting out applies only to future use, since data already fed into Google's AI is already part of the training data, and that opting out of training doesn't mean Google won't have the data, which it may keep for up to three years.1516 He calls the training control a welcome privacy improvement even with the longer retention, and notes that users wanting no retention now have fewer options.1718
