Anthropic IPO pricing at $83

Last scan Mon Nov 23, 13:58 UTC
6 sources · 422 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · 4 independent origins: Anthropic, Bloomberg, Semafor, TechCrunch

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, above its initially set range, with Nasdaq trading under the ticker "ANT" expected to begin on November 19.12345 The offering covers 980 million shares, up from 825 million initially planned, which TechCrunch says raised $81.3 billion and surpassed SpaceX's $75 billion record to become the largest IPO ever by proceeds.678 Anthropic's own release says 956,351,354 of the shares are being sold by the company and 23,648,646 by selling stockholders, and that underwriters hold a 30-day option on up to 147 million more shares.910 Several leading technology firms, among them AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners, further committed to purchasing $26 billion worth of shares without voting rights through a private placement. TechCrunch says that each of the seven co-founders sold close to 3.4 million shares.1112

TechCrunch reports that the IPO price puts Anthropic's fully diluted valuation near $1.5 trillion. That is higher than the $965 billion figure from May, yet lower than the $2 trillion it had reportedly aimed for a few months before.131415

Semafor says the price shows investor enthusiasm survived turmoil in tech stocks, including Jensen Huang's ouster as Nvidia CEO, and that investors see Anthropic as a pure-play AI company.16 Zvi Mowshowitz writes that the IPO was six times oversubscribed at its original price and argues the $83 price still seems too low, while a pre-pricing Semafor piece put orders at nearly seven times the available shares. inference171819

Bloomberg Intelligence cautioned that Anthropic's roughly $900 billion in infrastructure financing pressures it to sustain premium token pricing, and Derek Thompson warned the whole industry could correct if financing and demand tighten together.2021
