Anthropic IPO pricing at $83

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Anthropic)

Per the Latent Space AI News roundup for November 17–18, 2026, Anthropic set its IPO price at $83 a share, which landed higher than the range it had first targeted.12 Latent Space says the deal involves 980M shares worth roughly $81.3B at the $83 price, and around $79.4B of that total comes from Anthropic's own share sales, calculated prior to fees.3 Trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT is expected to begin November 19.4

Zvi Mowshowitz says the $83 price implies a valuation of about $1.5 trillion, slightly above the target range.5 Mowshowitz says the IPO was six times oversubscribed at its original price, and he cites Matt Levine as saying many people want Anthropic stock.6 Mowshowitz argues the price still seems obviously too low to him, and says bankers remain remarkably close to undefeated at convincing companies to underprice, though he does not think an IPO bump is worth billions of dollars.78
