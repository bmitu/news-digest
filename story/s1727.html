Nvidia launches Nemotron 3.5 open models for agents and AI PCs

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 1 new article this week · single origin (Nvidia)

Nvidia launched new open-weight Nemotron 3.5 models on Thursday, designed for always-on agents and edge computing, including the 120-billion-parameter Nemotron 3.5 Super VL for enterprise agents and two lightweight Bolt models for local use.123 Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B are available now, with Bolt E4B due later this month, according to Nvidia.4

Nvidia says Super VL uses fewer parameters than Nemotron 3 Ultra but can deliver similar accuracy in instruction-following and tool-use tasks.5 Nvidia also claims that, when post-trained on specific agentic tasks, Super VL completes tasks faster than competing models from Anthropic and OpenAI.6 TechCrunch notes that such performance claims are standard for model launches, but says Nvidia backed them with a list of companies it says use Super VL, including Skydance, EY, Ramp, Crowdstrike, Perplexity and Salesforce.78

Nvidia vice president Kari Briski wrote that, unlike closed frontier models, organizations can post-train Super VL on their own data, deploy it on their own infrastructure and own what they build.9 TechCrunch frames the launch as Nvidia leaning into enterprises running their own open models, as proprietary AI labs, which are both Nvidia customers and investments, look to other chips such as AWS's Trainium and Cerebras. inference1011

The Bolt models are intended to run locally on new AI PCs using the Nvidia RTX Spark chip and on workstations running the DGX Spark, with partners such as Microsoft and Dell releasing the PCs.1213 Of the Bolt models, the Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B is a 74-billion-parameter model for local agentic tasks, and the smaller Bolt E4B is aimed at conversational agents.1415 TechCrunch also suggests privacy-wary consumers may be reluctant to let AI agents into their lives, while Briski says PC users can delegate projects without sending workloads to the cloud.1617

Latent Space reports that Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B was demonstrated handling conversation and tools alongside Cosmos vision locally on a Jetson Orin Nano 8GB, and a Reddit-derived chart put the 74B Bolt at an Artificial Analysis score of 21, below the 34 of the much smaller Qwen3.8-27B. inference1819
