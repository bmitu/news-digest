Liquid AI LFM3 release

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Liquid AI)

Liquid AI released LFM3, a family of six roughly 2B-parameter open-weight models (text, vision, audio, omni and two decision models) on a new tri-hybrid architecture, with weights available on Hugging Face.12 Reddit summaries say llama.cpp support has already been merged, and Ollama availability is also reported.345

The centerpiece, LFM3-1.7B, is a 1.73B text model with 13 gated short convolutions, 5 sliding-window attention layers and 6 global attention layers, and a footprint of about 1 GB at 4-bit.678 Liquid says LFM3-1.7B outperforms its earlier LFM2.5-2.6B while using 35% fewer parameters.9 Per Liquid's own evaluation as relayed by omarsar0, LFM3-1.7B scores 38.4 against 28.9 for Qwen3.5-9B on WebArena-Verified.10 However, Liquid cautions that the small text model is not intended for agentic coding or complex multi-step reasoning.11

Liquid says LFM3-VL-2B (1.9B parameters) raises the average across 62 vision benchmarks from 57.6 to 71.4 versus LFM2.5-VL-3B, on par with Gemma 4 12B.12 Liquid also says LFM3-Omni-2B beats models of up to 10.7B parameters, such as Qwen2.5-Omni-7B, on Daily-Omni, WorldSense and AV-SpeakerBench, and that a LFM3-VL-2B-based robotics policy reached 96.2% average success across the four LIBERO suites.1314

On Reddit, top comments were mostly reactions, with the main sentiment being surprise at the breadth of the release and the apparent benchmark strength for such small on-device models.15
