Cammarata argues that researchers should look more closely at the original neurons, rather than relying only on tools such as sparse autoencoders, and says some apparent confusion came from the investigative tools.12

Cammarata says that what they see is about 90% of neurons having basically one job, and posted an attribution graph from Kimi K3 using only neurons, which he says is, as far as they know, the first published attribution graph of a multiple trillion parameter model.34 Nate B Jones writes that the headline 99–100% result concerns the final layer of an improved vision model, while the roughly 90% figure is a preliminary estimate for neuron poles in Qwen3-32B, and he stresses that neither is a percentage of AI safety.567 Jones also calls the broader argument a working hypothesis.8

Zvi Mowshowitz says the claim is bold and will be directly challenged, that most people updated toward more neurons having central concepts, and that some of the hype got out of hand.91011
