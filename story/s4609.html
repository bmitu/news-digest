Nvidia Q3 earnings

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Nvidia)

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on Tuesday, November 17, up 103% from a year earlier and above its own $108 billion guidance, in its first earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO.123 Data center revenue was $107.6 billion, up 110%, and profit reached a record $65.3 billion.45 The Verge reports that Vera Rubin generated nearly $20 billion in its first shipping quarter, which Nvidia called its fastest product ramp ever.678 Nvidia guided to fourth-quarter revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, and raised its growth forecast for the fiscal year ending January 2028 to about 75% from 70%.91011

Hours before the report, Huang had said on X he would be honored to return as CEO if the board decided it was in the company's best interest.1213 Board chair Harvey Jones said at the start of the call that the board does not intend to reinstate Huang, and that the company would not take questions about the circumstances of his resignation or the ongoing investigation.1415 On TechCrunch's Equity podcast, Kirsten Korosec said Elon Musk and others are pushing for Huang's return and circulating a petition, while Huang says he is prepared to come back.16 Korosec said she thinks the board wants to look in control and not be forced into a decision by Musk, and Tim De Chant said how fast it dismissed the petitions may mean more is going on behind the scenes than outsiders know.1718 Interim CEO Colette Kress told investors it is "business as usual" and that customers, suppliers and partners are behind the company.192021

Kress also said that Nvidia will stop selling H200 chips in China, according to TechCrunch.222324 Anthony Ha said critics of Nvidia's China stance estimate that about 20% of its business ultimately comes from China through chips sold to firms in places like Malaysia, which would not show up in its financials, so ending those sales could significantly hurt its bottom line.2526 Commentators focus on Nvidia's financing of the AI ecosystem: Fortune notes Kress said Nvidia would stay "disciplined" and is "rigorously evaluating" future guarantees and investments, and The Verge's Elizabeth Lopatto reads a new earnings-release paragraph limiting neocloud commitments as a sign Nvidia may be less liberal with cash. inference2728

Investors initially cheered, with shares up about 4% after hours, but The Verge says the stock was around $150 by Thursday, nearly 40 percent below its October 6 high, erasing the post-earnings pop.2930
