Microsoft Maia 300 chip

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Microsoft, Semi Doped

Microsoft announced its third-generation Maia in-house AI chip, the Maia 300, which it designed to be the "primary AI inference system" for its products and services.1 Maia 300 is to be rolled out to production in 2027, and The Verge reports it will let Microsoft rely mainly on its own chips rather than those from Nvidia and AMD.23 Maia 300 succeeds Maia 200, which was only announced in January and is already running production AI workloads across Microsoft, including Microsoft Copilot, GitHub Copilot and Microsoft Foundry.45 Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman says Microsoft needs a platform it owns and co-designs with its models, making running Microsoft AI models on Maia a critical focus.6

The chip uses a chiplet architecture on TSMC's N2P process that separates compute, memory controller and I/O functions, which Microsoft's Azure hardware and infrastructure team says increases the silicon area dedicated to compute.78

Scott Guthrie, executive vice president of Microsoft's cloud group, says Microsoft expects Maia 300 to deliver 40 percent more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU-based systems.910 Guthrie also says a full 96-accelerator configuration delivers up to 7x the rack-scale token throughput of Maia 200.1110 Latent Space's newsletter also relays Microsoft's claims of 40% more tokens per megawatt and 7× the rack-scale throughput of Maia 200, and separately notes a networking design with a two-tier scale-up domain of up to 12,288 accelerators.101213
