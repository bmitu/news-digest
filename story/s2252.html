ChatGPT sending users to 404 Media

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · 263 points on Hacker News · single origin (404 Media)

404 Media reports that ChatGPT has been telling people with ordinary tech support problems that 404 Media, specifically, can help them, and in some cases it has written the emails they send to the outlet.12 The article's author, Jason, says he got an email from a man in Russia whose Instagram account had been banned, and that the email seemed AI-generated, like many others the outlet has received recently.34 According to the conversation the man shared, he asked ChatGPT how to get his account back, and after discussion of Instagram's automated review processes, the bot said emailing Meta was probably not possible but eventually told him to email 404 Media's Jason instead.56 ChatGPT reportedly recommended contacting both Russian and international journalists, listed several outlets including 404 Media with the address jason@404media.co, and then wrote the exact email in English that the man sent.78

404 Media says that because big tech platforms essentially do not have customer support and make it impossible for users to contact a human, many people experiencing run-of-the-mill tech problems have turned to ChatGPT.9 About two weeks before the article, 404 Media began emailing these people to ask how they found the outlet, and says the answer was universally that an AI chatbot recommended it.10
