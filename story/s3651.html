Claude Meetings, Whiteboards and Sheets

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
5 sources · 400 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, The Verge

Anthropic unveiled Claude Meetings, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets at a San Francisco event on Tuesday, aimed at non-technical knowledge workers.1 Anthropic product lead Nicholas Lin told The Verge that adopting AI still involves a lot of friction for non-developers, which he presented as the gap the company wants to close.23

With Meetings, Claude joins a video call as a visible participant and starts work during the conversation. The feature is in beta on Team and Enterprise plans and currently works with Google Meet.4567 By default Claude stays out of calls that invite outsiders and leaves if an outsider joins midway, though an admin can permit outside guests.89 Anthropic says Claude saves a transcript, summary and screen descriptions rather than audio, and that during the beta some organizations, such as those with zero data retention or HIPAA-ready setups, cannot use it.1011 Notes and transcripts are free during the beta, and artifacts made in calls are free through February 10, 2027.1213

Anthropic is launching Whiteboards and Sheets in beta on all paid plans, and Claude Dashboards becomes generally available under the new name Claude Data.141516 Anthropic concedes that Sheets has only simple spreadsheet functionality for now, with more planned.17 Interactive connectors will let apps such as Adobe, Atlassian and HubSpot open inside Claude, and The Verge reports Anthropic's connector marketplace has grown to more than 9,000 options.1819

The Verge places the launch in a competitive field, noting that Google's Gemini and OpenAI's ChatGPT can already join meetings or take notes, and says Anthropic faces pressure to grow revenue ahead of its IPO. inference2021 Reddit commenters in one summary saw the release as part of a broader IPO-readiness push through rapid beta releases.22
