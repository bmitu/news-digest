California AG subpoenas Nvidia over China chip sales

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 1 new article today · single origin, unclear which

California Attorney General Rob Bonta issued subpoenas to Nvidia over reports that the company sold advanced AI chips to China, his office announced on Thursday.1 Bonta says his office is examining whether Nvidia or Jensen Huang's actions led to China obtaining advanced chips or confidential information, and whether Nvidia misled investors and the public about that risk in violation of the law.2 Bonta said the conduct of Nvidia and its former CEO raises "bright red flags," including about how the company may have misled investors.3

Separately, The Wall Street Journal reportedly said the SEC is leading a similar probe, though the Journal could not confirm its exact focus, only that it includes whether Nvidia misled investors.45 These probes follow Huang's departure from his post, which came amid allegations that he was romantically involved with a woman said to be a Chinese spy and employed as an executive at Megaspeed.67

On Thursday, the House Foreign Affairs Committee made public records indicating that Washington approved selling close to 900,000 AI chips made by Nvidia and AMD to firms in China that the Defense Department has labeled military companies.8 An Nvidia spokesperson says the licensing review lasted over half a year with strict conditions and limited volumes, and that the company has since stopped shipping H200s to China.910

Zvi Mowshowitz's post says Huang was later reinstated as Nvidia CEO, with Donald Trump publicly praising the board's decision and saying the government has no evidence of unlawful conduct by Huang.1112 The reaction from critics was harsh. Newt Gingrich called it deeply troubling that Huang had ties to a spy for Communist China and aided Beijing in obtaining cutting-edge chips, only to be restored to the CEO role. Mitt Romney hinted that the chip sales reeked of "profit, corruption" and possibly something worse.1314
