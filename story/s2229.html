OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra release

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
6 sources · 637 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Latent Space, OpenAI

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Terra, which it describes as a fast, efficient model combining detailed visual reasoning and precise computer use with clearer, more natural writing.1 It is available starting the day of announcement to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and to developers through the API as gpt-6.1-terra.23 Standard API pricing is $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens, which OpenAI says is 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol.45

OpenAI says that on Agents' Last Exam at medium effort Terra scores 15 percentage points above GPT-5.6 Terra at about one-third the cost, and performs on par with GPT-6 Astra at one-tenth the price per token.67 OpenAI also says the error rate on its internal error-inducing prompts at low effort fell from 53.6% to 16.7%, and that Terminal-Bench 4.0 at maximum effort rose to 50.3% from 21.5%, while Sol and Astra remain better for the most complex coding tasks.8910

Artificial Analysis, as relayed by Latent Space, reported a 6-point Intelligence Index gain at max effort for under a third of the cost per task ($0.40 vs $1.40), a hallucination rate falling from 88% to 38%, and a Coding Agent Index of 48 versus Sol's 54.1112 Other independent evals reported by Latent Space put Terra at #14 in Code Arena WebDev and 5th overall on ParseBench, strong on tables but weak on layout and charts.1314

OpenAI's Ted Sanders said Terra is probably better than Astra and Sol at technical communication, similar on vision and computer use but much faster, and a bit worse on most other things, calling it not a bombshell release for subscribers.1516 Reddit commenters questioned Terra's place in the lineup, noting it is only 2x cheaper than Sol but 10x more expensive than Luna, which one argued could push users needing more than Luna straight to Sol.1718
