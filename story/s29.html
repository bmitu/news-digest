Nvidia acquires HuggingFace

How does Nvidia's OpenShell safety platform compare with what OpenAI and other labs are doing, and how many firms have joined it?

Nvidia's approach is an engineering one: its OpenShell sandbox sets runtime limits on what agents can access or do, rather than relying only on prompt rules or model-level safeguards.123 OpenAI is pursuing its own path: TechCrunch says it is developing its own safeguards for its research and products, disclosing the worst incident it discovers, and has its own AI cybersecurity consortium for sharing information, called the Defense Factory, which Anthropic, Amazon Web Services, and Google signed on to support.45 OpenAI is not a public member of the consortium, but an OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch the company supports Nvidia's work, and TechCrunch reports OpenAI is working with Nvidia on agent security, including on OpenShell.67 Other labs are split: Anthropic is a supporter and is working with Nvidia to integrate its managed agents with OpenShell, while Amazon, Google and Apple have not joined. inference89 Nvidia's rivals Arm and Intel have also signed on as supporters, since OpenShell can be modified to work with other chips.1011
