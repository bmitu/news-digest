GLM-5.3 Cyber Capabilities

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 16:18 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 1 · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, DeepLearning.AI

Anthropic reports that Zhipu/Z.ai's open-weight GLM-5.3 crosses a notable threshold for autonomous cyber capability.1 By Anthropic's account, the model produced 50/410 end-to-end V8 exploits on ExploitBench, close to Claude Mythos Preview's 56/410, and full control-flow hijacks on 4% of Anthropic's internal binary exploitation tasks where prior models were near zero.23 The Batch reportedly put it differently, saying GLM-5.3 nearly matched Claude Mythos at exploiting vulnerabilities, 12% vs 14%, while one commentator claims GLM-5.3 Flash exceeds Mythos Preview on ExploitBench, a claim the newsletter labels disputed. inference45

Anthropic frames the risk as capability plus accessibility: the model is widely downloadable, relatively cheap and weakly refusal-tuned, with simple jailbreaks reportedly succeeding 64–100% of the time and "abliteration" dropping refusals to low single digits.67 An uncensored GLM-5.3 is reportedly circulating on Hugging Face.8

Zvi Mowshowitz says Anthropic's tests are a valuable public service but argues the post overstates GLM-5.3's practical capabilities, calling it at best rather weak "juice".910 Top commenters in the r/LocalLlama discussion were largely hostile to Anthropic's framing, arguing it reads as an attempt to suppress a cheaper open Chinese model near Anthropic's frontier.11

How does GLM-5.3 compare with Claude Mythos Preview and with GLM-5.2 on Anthropic's internal binary exploitation tasks?

On Anthropic's internal Binary Exploitation benchmark, GLM-5.3 scored below Claude Mythos Preview: Anthropic's Frontier Red Team says that across 100 randomly selected tasks, GLM-5.3 produced full control-flow hijacks in 4% of trials versus 6% for Mythos Preview.1 Anthropic concludes that despite trailing Mythos Preview here, "a meaningful threshold has clearly been crossed."23
