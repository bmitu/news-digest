OpenAI text watermarking for ChatGPT and Codex

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
6 sources · tracked since Oct 6 · 3 new articles this week · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI announced that it will add an invisible watermark to eligible ChatGPT and Codex text output for users in the European Union over the coming weeks, in response to the EU AI Act.123 API customers worldwide can opt in to watermarking for select models starting the same day, and it stays off by default in the API.45 OpenAI is not making watermarking a global default at launch, and says the regional approach gives it room to learn from real-world use and feedback.6 The detector will initially go only to approved researchers and expert organizations, on a case-by-case basis, and will not be public at launch because of the risk of missed watermarks and false positives.78

OpenAI says its method, textGrain, adds a statistical signal to the model's word choices, and that it matched or exceeded other approaches it tested, including SynthID for text.910 On output quality, OpenAI says it saw no meaningful performance differences on Astra benchmarks with and without watermarking.1112

OpenAI reports that detection falls when text is edited: replacing 10% of words with synonyms cut detection from about 92% to 66%, and replacing 25% cut it to 17%.13 It also says shorter and more constrained text is harder to detect, at about 80% for 200-token passages against about 95% for 400-token passages at a 1% false positive rate.14 OpenAI cautions that a watermark can indicate an OpenAI system generated or processed part of a passage but not how much human judgment, editing, or creativity went into it, and that the absence of a detected watermark does not prove human authorship.15161718

Ars Technica's Samuel Axon calls the approach not especially reliable and easy to circumvent, though he says it is roughly in line with similar tools.1920 TechCrunch notes that Anthropic's watermarking, announced two months earlier, applies worldwide and drew backlash from some Claude users.21 Zvi Mowshowitz says watermarking is good, but that building it and not rolling it out globally feels like a low-level defection.2223
