Alpöge Riemann Hypothesis proof claim

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 09:22 UTC
15 sources · 558 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · 1 new article today · 5 independent origins: Lean Prover Community, Levent Alpöge, TechCrunch, The Verge, ladyxtel

Levent Alpöge said he believes he has, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, and says the method easily extends much further, resolving the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis.123 He released the proof as Lean code rather than a written paper; Timothy B. Lee reports it runs to 17 million lines, announced on Friday, November 20.456 Alpöge later said the "250,000+ pages" figure was a joke, and admitted the Lean code is "full of garbage" though it compiles.789

The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative said they have full confidence the proof is correct, and Semafor reports two independent groups said they verified it.1011 MIT mathematician Andrew Sutherland called it "an historic moment" and "a human triumph".1213

A rival, AI-free proof by Stanford postdoc Alessandro Masullo, about 900 pages and announced by Ravi Vakil hours after Alpöge's, is still being examined and is not expressed in Lean.1415

Gary Marcus argues the result is amazing but widely misread: he says it needed extensive human guidance, does not mean AGI has arrived, and relied on math-specific tools like Lean and mathlib that may not generalize.161718
