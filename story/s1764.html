EXCLUSIVE: ByteDance, Alibaba, Tencent place over $35 billion worth of Nvidia chip orders

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 1 new article this week · single origin (Reuters)

Reuters reports that ByteDance, Alibaba and Tencent have placed more than $35 billion worth of Nvidia chip orders.1 A Reddit post frames the orders as roughly 3 million RTX PRO 5500 cards at about $11,400 each, likely aimed at LLM inference and AI video generation.2 A pricing correction noted the implied unit cost is about $11,400 per card, not $11.40, aligning the order size with high-end professional/datacenter GPU pricing.3

A quoted source says Nvidia may fulfill only 5–10% of the requested quantities and can produce at most 3 million RTX PRO 5500 cards globally in 2027, against more than 6 million requested by Chinese customers.4 Commenters therefore infer that the $35B+ order book is heavily supply-constrained and may not be fully deliverable. inference56

Some commenters speculate that Nvidia may deprioritize lower-margin workstation-class cards as datacenter demand dominates, and one noted that even the $16,000 RTX 6000 Pro could be less attractive to prioritize.78 Commenters are also skeptical that future GPU generations will make current RTX PRO cards affordable for local LLM users, though some hope datacenter refresh cycles could lower used-market prices.9
