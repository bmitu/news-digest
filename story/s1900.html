OpenAI Decisions API

Last scan Sat Oct 24, 14:03 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: OpenAI, TechCrunch

OpenAI has made its Decisions API model widely available, with TechCrunch reporting it became available to all customers on Thursday after being announced at the company's developer day on September 29.12 Latent Space's newsletter describes the API as now generally available with up to 400 questions per call, citing OpenAI Devs.3

TechCrunch presents it as a competitor to TypeSafe's Jev model, which it says created a category of deep learning models that act as super classifiers and recently made TypeSafe, a startup founded by a former researcher at the frontier lab, a $7.6 billion company.45 OpenAI product lead Nikunj Handa says the team took ChatGPT Luna and reconfigured it to speed up inference and lower cost, rather than building a specially calibrated decision model.6

Handa says the main gain is speed, cutting responses from one to three seconds to 100 to 180 milliseconds.7 Andrew Hsu, CTO of Speak, says the API cut the time between actions at his company from 1.4 seconds to .13 seconds.8

On public benchmarks OpenAI's model does not lead: the AIM System One Index and the Artificial Analysis Decision Index tend to favor Liquid's D1 Ultra and Base Lab's Elephant-1, and TechCrunch says OpenAI's model ranks eighth and fourteenth while the latest Jev is seventh and third.910 TechCrunch adds that TypeSafe's offerings remain the most widely queried on OpenRouter, and that it is unclear what makes a good decision model beyond "it works for you".1112
