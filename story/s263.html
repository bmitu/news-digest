Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 launch

Last scan Tue Oct 6, 16:18 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Sep 29 · single origin (Latent Space)

Zvi Mowshowitz reports that Claude Sonnet 5.5 exists, advertised as a strong upgrade over Sonnet 5 that on average uses 30% fewer tokens.1

Mowshowitz gives its base price as half of Opus, $2/$10, with $2.50 for cache writes and $0.20 for cache reads on both models.2 Latent Space's roundup says Sonnet 5.5 [Max] debuted at #3 on an arena leaderboard and ranked #1 in the Chat category.3 It costs $2.74 per task versus $1.58 for the #2 Opus 5.5, which keeps it off the Pareto frontier.4

Mowshowitz says he presumes there is a narrow window where the discount is worthwhile, but mostly he would just stick with Opus 5.5.5

Separately, Reddit posters allege that Opus 5.5 regressed after launch, though Latent Space's summary says no commenter provided reproducible benchmarks or direct evidence.67
