Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 launch

2 sources · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, Simon Willison

Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in the Claude 5.5 family, one week after Opus 5.5 and the day before OpenAI DevDay.1

Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 is "a clear upgrade over Sonnet 5" that runs more than 30% faster and costs up to 30% less for most work.2

Simon Willison notes it is priced the same as Sonnet 5 but appears to beat it on every benchmark, and says it appears almost as good as Opus 5.5 on some coding tasks.34

Latent Space's AINews summary says early independent evals place it at or near Opus 5.5 on several leaderboards.5

Sonnet 5.5 now powers the free tier on claude.ai, which Willison calls the most interesting aspect, arguing Anthropic has a much more capable free offering than ChatGPT's free tier, which he says uses Luna 5.6.67

Anthropic positions the model for well-scoped everyday tasks, and it shipped on day one on the Claude Platform and Claude Code, and in third-party tools such as GitHub Copilot in VS Code and Cursor.8910

Anthropic also extended "preserved thinking" to counter distillation via account-switching, keeping reasoning traces in the org that generated them.11

Willison reported a bug shared with Opus 5.5: at "max" thinking effort his test prompt consumed 128,000 tokens at a cost of $1.28 and failed to produce an SVG.12

Anthropic's @mikeyk said Haiku 5.5 will "round out the family in the coming weeks," and Willison hopes it will be price-competitive with GPT-6 Luna.131415
