Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 launch

2 sources · tracked since Sep 29 · 2 independent origins: Anthropic, Simon Willison

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in the Claude 5.5 family, one week after Opus 5.5 and the day before OpenAI DevDay, according to Latent Space's AINews roundup.1 Haiku 5.5 has not yet launched: Anthropic's Mike Krieger (@mikeyk) said, per the Latent Space AINews roundup, that it will "round out the family in the coming weeks", and Simon Willison notes that Anthropic's announcement reiterates that Haiku 5.5 will be available "in the coming weeks".23

Anthropic and @claudeai called Sonnet 5.5 "a clear upgrade over Sonnet 5" and claim it runs more than 30% faster and costs up to 30% less for most work.4 Simon Willison notes it is priced the same as Sonnet 5 but appears to beat it on every benchmark.5 The AINews roundup says early independent evals place it at or near Opus 5.5 on several leaderboards, and Willison says it appears almost as good as Opus 5.5 on some coding tasks.67

@ClaudeDevs positions the model for "well-scoped everyday tasks like fixing bugs and quickly iterating on features."8 It shipped day-one on the Claude Platform and Claude Code with a usage reset valid until Oct 22, and was also offered in third-party tools including GitHub Copilot in VS Code.910

Willison calls the most interesting point that Sonnet 5.5 now powers the free tier on claude.ai, which he says gives Anthropic a much more capable free offering than ChatGPT's Luna 5.6 tier.1112 Willison also reported that, as with Opus 5.5, the "max" thinking effort run used 128,000 tokens at a cost of $1.28 and failed to produce an SVG.13

Why did Anthropic extend "preserved thinking" alongside this launch, and what does it change when a session moves to another account?
