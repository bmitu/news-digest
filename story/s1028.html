Anthropic anti-distillation defenses

Last scan Sat Oct 17, 12:46 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic detailed its continued efforts against distillation and says early evidence suggests its methods have reduced the amount of data harvested by distillers by around 90% to 99%.12 A separate summary likewise attributes to Anthropic a 90–99% reduction in extractable distillation, including action against large fake-account networks, while saying implications for rival model quality remain speculation.3 Zvi Mowshowitz describes Anthropic's response as controlling what can be done with reasoning, especially on new accounts, blocking and patching extraction attempts, and charging for blocked distillation attempts, and notes Anthropic expects a continued cat-and-mouse game.45 Mowshowitz also says that if Anthropic is tracking results properly this is a big improvement, though problems remain.6

Anthropic's stated reason for concern is that distillation lets companies build near-frontier models that have been released without proportionally robust safeguards.7 Anthropic's research post reportedly alleges that Zhipu's GLM-5.3 distilled cyber capabilities from leading U.S. models and has bypassable safeguards.8

Mowshowitz says he is strongly in the anti-distillation camp but calls it disingenuous for Anthropic not to admit that its commercial interests are a factor, and he says distillation has become active, with prompts designed to extract reasoning traces across hundreds of thousands of fraudulent accounts.910 Mowshowitz further speculates that Chinese open models rely heavily on distillation of GPT and Claude and would fall back to other US labs' models if Anthropic's and OpenAI's defenses succeed.11
