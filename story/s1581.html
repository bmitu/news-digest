PyTorch 2.15 Apple silicon

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
2 sources · single origin (PyTorch)

PyTorch 2.15, whose release candidate the Hugging Face PyTorch team describes, adds FP8 support on Apple-silicon MPS and replaces several MPSGraph paths with dedicated Metal kernels.12 The Hugging Face team says PyTorch 2.15 adds torch.float8_e4m3fn tensors on MPS, using one byte per value, half the storage of FP16 or BF16, and that they can be multiplied with F.scaled_mm.34

Hugging Face reports a 1.23× geometric-mean speedup for linear layers across 96 BF16 layer shapes from real models, with the biggest wins on small projections such as MoE gates.56 It also says an FP32 softmax over a [1, 128256] row of logits drops from 57 µs to 7 µs, about 8× faster than the MPSGraph version.7

A Latent Space newsletter summarizing the release cites 5–6.5× faster MoE decoding, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B rising from 9.4 to 61.3 tokens/s on an M3 Ultra, and Wan2.2 VAE peak driver memory falling from about 270 GiB to 8 GiB.89 The newsletter also notes that these gains land in PyTorch itself rather than in a separate inference runtime.10

The Hugging Face post says MPS now requires macOS 15 or later and PyTorch 2.15 requires Python 3.11 or later.11 It also says bundled shaders will build for Metal 3.1 by default, shrinking libtorch_cpu.dylib by about 25%, from 384 MiB to 287 MiB.12
