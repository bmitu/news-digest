Mercury 3 diffusion LLM

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
2 sources · 256 points on Hacker News · single origin (Inception)

Inception announced Mercury 3, which it calls the most capable diffusion-based LLM released to date, and says it outperforms GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on a range of benchmarks while running 4–8x faster at 1,000+ tokens per second.123 Inception says it has scaled Mercury more than 40x in total parameters, that intelligence across a range of standard benchmarks has risen 3x from Mercury 1 (mini) to Mercury 3, and that Mercury 3 demonstrates that text diffusion scales.45 Inception lists a speed of 1,068 tokens per second on NVIDIA GPUs and a 1M-token context, up from 260K for Mercury 2.5.67

Pricing is $0.20 per million input and $0.70 per million output tokens, with a 50% launch discount to $0.10 and $0.35.8 The model is available on the Inception API Platform and through OpenRouter, Baseten and Vercel, and is OpenAI API compatible.910

Other early-access partners quoted by Inception were positive: Octen AI's Kuan Zou reports a median generation time of 0.9 seconds and a median end-to-end response time of 2.2 seconds, and Lorikeet's Michael Gribben says that in early testing Mercury 3 beat other models on latency while also performing higher on evals and in production.1112

A Latent Space newsletter relays Inception's claims and notes that Baseten offers the model with 1M-token context.313 The newsletter adds that @scaling01 estimates Mercury 3 at over 280B parameters if Mercury 1 was about 7B, an estimate marked unconfirmed.14
