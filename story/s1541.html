Opencode breached, rotate keys to be safe.

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 19 · 2 new articles this week · 2 independent origins: OpenCode, TechCrunch

OpenCode, an open source AI coding agent, said on Saturday that attackers broke into an older system overnight, affecting 1.4% of users of its online platform, OpenCode Console, where some users store API keys.1 OpenCode wrote on X that the breach was related to a large-scale distillation attack, and that affected users were notified by email, but some skeptics called the distillation framing an overstretch and say it remains unproven.23

Co-founder Dax Raad said the open source app and command-line tool were not affected, and the company has told affected users to revoke and replace their keys directly with each provider.45 Anthropic and Google blocked the API keys they believed were exposed, OpenAI said it worked with OpenCode to restore access and refund its API customers, and OpenCode said its own refunds are underway.67

Raad said attackers had been trying to break in nonstop for 48 hours, and AI models the team used for analysis began refusing to work when the malicious code triggered safety filters.8910 OpenCode later got access to OpenAI's Daybreak Blue program for vetted users, which Raad said improved the team's ability to investigate the attack.111213

TechCrunch reports OpenCode has not detailed how attackers gained access to the older system, how keys were stored, or the full extent of misuse, and says the incident remains ongoing.1415
