Alphabet Q3 earnings and stock drop

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
4 sources · 4 independent origins: Alphabet, Ars Technica, BBC, TechCrunch

Alphabet's stock fell about 12% after its third-quarter earnings report, which showed 25% revenue growth to $127.6 billion but also slowing search growth and rising AI spending. inference12 Platformer reported that the 12.6% drop erased around $500 billion in market value and was Alphabet's worst day as a public company, while the BBC said it continued falling another 1.4% the next day. inference34 Platformer said most of the selloff came from investor worries about the scale of the AI infrastructure buildout.5

Search revenue grew 12% year over year, down from 17% in the second quarter and 19% in the first, and Ars Technica reported that it was flat in the quarter. inference67 Ars Technica also reported earnings per share of $2.12 against a consensus projection of $3.02, while Cloud beat expectations. inference89 TechCrunch reported Google Cloud revenue rose 103% to $30.7 billion.10 Ars Technica reported that negative cash flow widened from -$5.8 billion to -$9.06 billion, and that Google still expects total 2026 spending to be between $195 billion and $205 billion.1112

According to Philipp Schindler, Google's chief business officer, each AI Mode query earns less revenue than a similar web search because it has fewer ads that cost less to run, according to Ars Technica.13 Platformer noted that Schindler did not say how much less AI Mode earns or what he was comparing it to.14 Google announced it will expand ads in AI Mode into international markets alongside multi-turn ads in the US, and Schindler said click-through rates for sponsored product listings in AI Mode are comparable to organic listings.151617
