Signal blocks AI agent screen capture

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Signal)

Signal is blocking screenshots in its app on iOS 27 and, on Android, enabling protection when it detects an AI assistant trying to read the screen, in order to stop built-in OS AI agents from capturing users' messages.12 Signal president Meredith Whittaker says the agentic features in Google's and Apple's systems, such as Gemini and Siri AI, undermine Signal's ability to independently guarantee privacy.34 Users can turn the protection off in settings, but Signal warns that this would potentially expose their and their contacts' content to the AI agents and break its privacy guarantees.56

Signal itself calls the fix incomplete, since it does not cover other routes such as AI agents retrieving content from the device filesystem.78 The protection is also not available on macOS, which Signal says is because it cannot reliably block screenshots there; for macOS 27 it only recommends adding Signal to the Excluded Apps list in Siri settings.910 Signal says it took a similar step last year, using screenshot blocking to stop Microsoft's Recall from storing screenshots of Signal messages on Windows 11.1112

Whittaker argues that Apple, Google, Microsoft and others give developers no easy way to restrict AI data access without drastic compromises to the core user experience.1314 Signal says it has spent considerable developer time on forensic investigations because of a lack of documentation on how these agents work, and it calls on the three companies to provide simple technical tools and accurate documentation.1516 Whittaker describes the integration of AI agents into operating systems as an existential threat to privacy at the application layer, and says Signal is the canary in the coal mine for the wider ecosystem.1718
