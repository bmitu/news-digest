GMO Internet breach

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
3 sources · single origin (GMO Internet)

Japan's GMO Internet is investigating a data leak after suspected unauthorized access to its Onamae cloud desktop service, which provides virtualized desktops to financial traders.1 According to TechCrunch's timeline, GMO shut down the Onamae Desktop Cloud service on October 21 after discovering unauthorized access, and received a hacker email demanding about 8 billion yen (~$50 million).23 GMO advised customers to reset passwords amid reports of "unauthorized operations" on customer trading accounts, and said 27,000 servers may be affected.45

On October 24 GMO said it would offer customers compensation and alternative service, and on October 27 it said a suspected attacker had posted information that appeared linked to the company and its customers.67 GMO said on its blog that it believes the attacker has published information allegedly linked to customers and its business, but the contents were still being verified and the full extent of the incident remained unclear.89

Zvi Mowshowitz praises GMO for refusing to pay the $50 million ransom, and says it urged users to unplug network cables, put a bounty on the attackers and promised compensation; he also says the first record the attackers released has been disputed as inaccurate.101112

A Reddit post summarized in Latent Space cites GMO Internet Group CEO Masatoshi Kumagai warning that AI-enabled attacks are scaling so fast that "the internet itself" may be nearing collapse without government intervention, and alleges unauthorized trades across 70+ brokerages.1314 Commenters disagreed on the framing: some saw the unauthorized trades as potentially catastrophic, while others called the collapse claim possibly sensationalist and asked for clearer technical explanation of real-world impact.1516

TechCrunch also notes GMO's cybersecurity unit had reported a Chinese AI-enabled tool called Artex used in hacks on Japanese companies, but GMO has not publicly established a connection between Artex and the Onamae breach.1718
