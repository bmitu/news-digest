OpenAI dots assistant

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: OpenAI, TechCrunch

OpenAI released dots, an always-on personal assistant that keeps working in the background, at its developer day in late September, according to TechCrunch.12 It is available in the ChatGPT desktop and mobile apps to subscribers on the Pro tier or higher, with plans starting at $100 a month, and once connected to calendar, email, computer and other tools it tries to help get work done.34 A later update says ChatGPT dot now supports texting as a preview for US Pro users.5

Alexander Embiricos, who leads OpenAI's enterprise product team, says the product grew from framing the assistant as a single entity in one conversation, and notes much of its functionality could be achieved with the Codex coding app, but with more work and hand-holding.67 The assistant's proactivity is central: TechCrunch's testing found its ability to think ahead apparent and often useful but not quite there, flagging calendar conflicts and deadlines without necessarily doing so in a timely fashion.89 Embiricos says that the first time his dot flagged a conflict, early in development, it told him three minutes before the event, and he expects the timing to improve over time.1011

On privacy, OpenAI says dots has the same privacy controls as ChatGPT, including turning off model training and controlling which resources it accesses, but TechCrunch says the controls aren't granular and the app can be pushy, for instance refusing read-only access to cloud drives.1213 Embiricos says OpenAI needs to build "super, super, super granular controls" and describes a feature called proactive research, in which dots periodically reviews what it knows about the user and proposes ways to help.1415

Embiricos also says OpenAI is in deep conversations with payment processors; TechCrunch says letting an AI assistant spend money through partnerships with payment companies would be a potentially lucrative first for OpenAI but also a big ask for consumers.1617
