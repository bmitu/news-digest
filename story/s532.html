Anthropic Vatican consciousness lobbying

Last scan Mon Oct 5, 14:51 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Oct 3 · single origin (Christopher Hale)

According to a New York Times report, as relayed by Latent Space, Anthropic's Chris Olah raised pulling out of the launch of the Pope's AI encyclical, whose text rejects machine consciousness.1 Olah's team reportedly lobbied the Pope's advisers to take model consciousness seriously, and he ultimately attended.2 The article reportedly opens with Olah saying "we don’t know if A.I. models are conscious".3 The NYT report on Olah and the encyclical was the most-engaged item in Latent Space's roundup, at 28.7K.4

TechCrunch, citing the New York Times, says Anthropic has actively lobbied the Vatican to reconsider its position on non-human consciousness, apparently with little success.5

TechCrunch says Pope Leo XIV gave a more detailed statement on AI in an encyclical letter in May, emphasizing human dignity amid increasing automation.6 Separately, Pope Leo XIV posted on X on a Friday that there is an "ontological difference" between human art and what machines generate, saying algorithms lack the spark of humanity.78
