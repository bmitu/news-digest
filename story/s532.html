Last scan Sun Oct 4, 15:06 UTC

TechCrunch says Pope Leo XIV gave a more detailed statement on AI in an encyclical letter in May, emphasizing human dignity amid increasing automation.6 Separately, Pope Leo XIV posted on X on a Friday that there is an "ontological difference" between human art and what machines generate, saying algorithms lack the spark of humanity.78

How does DeepMind's new Bayesian framework for assessing AI consciousness compare with the way Anthropic frames the question?

DeepMind's new paper, 'From cacophony to hierarchy: a principled framework for assessing AI consciousness,' offers a Bayesian framework for deciding how much weight to put on various theories of consciousness and working out what that implies, according to Zvi Mowshowitz.1 Both approaches treat AI consciousness as unresolved rather than settled. DeepMind's is a structured, probability-weighted method, while Anthropic's is voiced as an admission of uncertainty and a push for others to take the question seriously. inference234 Latent Space reports that Olah's team reportedly lobbied the Pope's advisers to take model consciousness seriously, and TechCrunch, citing a New York Times report, says Anthropic has actively lobbied the Vatican to reconsider its position on non-human consciousness, apparently finding little success.567 Not every voice backs Anthropic's approach: Aidan Gomez criticized the campaign as moral arrogance.89 It was not reported whether Anthropic has endorsed or responded to DeepMind's framework.
