Simons Foundation AI math initiative

Last scan Mon Oct 19, 23:45 UTC
2 sources · 249 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Simons Foundation)

The Simons Foundation announced on October 15, 2026 a set of initiatives to help the mathematics community navigate the rise of artificial intelligence, with the foundation, Anthropic and OpenAI committing $15 million in combined funding and resources.12 Latent Space's newsletter summary reports that OpenAI and Anthropic each committed $5M to Simons Foundation programs that help mathematicians examine AI-produced results.3 The foundation will manage and award the money, including through an open call for proposals, to support workshops and initiatives for evaluating recent AI-assisted results, learning new tools, sharing perspectives and shaping collective standards for AI use.4 Anthropic and OpenAI will also provide AI tools and computing resources to researchers the foundation supports, extending beyond mathematics to basic science.5

Simons Foundation President David Spergel says the aim is to help the mathematical community come together and forge the best path forward as AI reshapes research.6 Spergel argues that the community, not any single company or institution, should drive discussions of how these tools are evaluated, adopted and used.7 The foundation says it was an early backer of machine learning for science, including funding development of the Lean proof assistant.8

Latent Space also reports that Jacob Tsimerman will lead a new Mathematical AI Safety Institute, with $10M in API credits from OpenAI; the Simons Foundation's own release does not mention this. inference92
