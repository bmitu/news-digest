Claude physics results

Last scan Sun Nov 22, 14:05 UTC
2 sources · 572 points on Hacker News · tracked since Nov 20 · single origin (Anthropic)

Anthropic says Claude solved two open problems in black-hole physics by running small groups of agents that were copies of itself.12 In the first problem, Anthropic says Claude found that the phase transition in the weakly coupled quantum theory is an abrupt jump as temperature rises, analogous to Hawking and Page's result. In 2003, Ofer Aharony and colleagues had shown the question hinged on a single number appearing three loops in.34 In the second problem, on the modular bootstrap for 3D quantum gravity, Anthropic says Claude proved that the lightest black hole can be no heavier than c/Λ, with Λ approximately 9.1266, and that this ceiling is optimal for this version of the method.56

Earlier numerical work had suggested a ceiling of about c/9.1, while Tom Hartman, Dalimil Mazáč and Leonardo Rastelli had analytically proved only c/8.5, so Claude's exact result closes that gap. inference78 Anthropic adds that the result shows this version of the method cannot on its own push the ceiling down to c/12, so other ingredients will be needed.9

Anthropic says almost every piece of the Hawking-Page-type calculation was done twice by independent agents, with parts also checked against earlier literature.1011 Anthropic says the bootstrap results were formally verified in Lean in September and that a physicist checked every main result.1213 Anthropic also says a physicist who independently used Claude on the Hawking-Page problem later shared a result that matched an earlier agent's answer, reached by a different method.1415

In a separate piece, Timothy B. Lee reports that prominent physicists such as Juan Maldacena are already getting help from AI models, and that Andreas Karch runs AI agents around the clock.1617 Lee speculates that if AI keeps improving, theoretical physics might come to resemble a humanities field, with physicists interpreting knowledge rather than discovering it.1819
