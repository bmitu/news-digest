Instinct AI agent launch

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: Instinct, The Verge

Instinct, an AI agent now valued at $10 billion as of its latest funding round, announced on Monday that users will be able to add it to chats with friends, according to TechCrunch.12 The company says the group chats work even if a user's friends haven't joined Instinct.3 The feature is rolling out first to early access users and is to expand to all users "soon".4

Founder Noah Shinn says a user's personal agent will ask permission before connecting with the group's agent, that users choose which groups they trust and can withdraw that trust, and that the group's Instinct is siloed from users' personal accounts.56 Shinn also says that pending replies from a personal agent are held when new members join a group chat.7

Instinct launched in August with an invite-only, no-marketing approach and, according to The Verge, quickly became the buzziest thing in AI, praised for its text-message interface.89 TechCrunch reports Muse doesn't yet offer group chat, while Meta's older Meta AI app can be added to group chats on several platforms.10

On pricing, Shinn says Instinct is free for invite-only users and the intent is to keep it affordable, while analyst Avi Greengart of Techsponential argues that if users aren't paying directly, they are paying indirectly.1112
