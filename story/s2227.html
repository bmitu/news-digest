Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
5 sources · 336 points on Hacker News · single origin (Artificial Analysis)

Artificial Analysis announced Intelligence Index v5, which it calls its biggest upgrade since v4 launched in January, with 2 new benchmarks and 3 upgrades, and 60% of the index now based on private test sets or held-out answers.1234 According to the Latent Space summary, v5 adds FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench-Science, swaps HLE for HLE-Diamond, drops GDPval-AA, SciCode and AA-LCR, and gives four categories equal 25% weights.5

Artificial Analysis reports that Claude Opus 5.5 (max) led at 58, ahead of GPT-6 Astra (57) and GPT-6.1 Sol (55), with Anthropic and OpenAI holding the top five models.67 However, Google (via Sundar Pichai) said its Gemini 4 Carbon ranks #1 on v5 at 59, and the Latent Space summary reports Artificial Analysis as saying it leads 4 of the 9 index evals.89

Artificial Analysis says the open-weights gap widened from 3 points to 12, with Kimi K3.1 Pro (weights not yet released) top at 46, while DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 and GLM-5.3 lead released weights at 32, below Claude Haiku 5.5 at 36.101112 It also says cost per task varies by more than 13x among leading models, from $0.96 for GPT-6.1 Terra to $13.04 for Claude Fable 5.1.1314

Zvi Mowshowitz says he agrees with Dean Ball that the new ratings closely match his intuitions, and Ball says it is the first time an Artificial Analysis ranking even roughly approximates his own subjective rankings of models.1516

Nathan Lambert's Interconnects calls the ranking reasonable at first glance but says it shows strain, such as GPT 6.1 Terra beating Claude Fable 5.1, and that the previous index flattered open models too strongly.171819 Interconnects calls the shift to private benchmarks directionally defensible but not unanimous, since it requires complete trust in the benchmark builder and removes community bug-finding, and it would prefer transparency.202122

On Reddit, commenters were skeptical of the timing and methodology, arguing definitions shift when open models approach the frontier and speculating, without evidence, about a link to Anthropic's IPO narrative; the thread offered no concrete data.232425
