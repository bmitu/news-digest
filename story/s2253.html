Google AI evasive on 2020 election

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
3 sources · 169 points on Hacker News · 2 independent origins: Ars Technica, Mediaite

Ars Technica's Kyle Orland reported that Google's AI Mode gave evasive answers when asked who won the 2020 US presidential election, telling him to consult the Federal Election Commission (FEC) rather than naming a winner.12

Orland says he got similarly indirect answers for the 2024 election and, via a link instead of an answer, for 2012, while AI Mode gave detailed and correct winners for every other presidential election back to 2000.345

Orland reports that Google's Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude and the America.gov AI model could all tell him who won recent presidential elections, and that VPN checks from Taiwan, Russia, France and Canada returned straightforward, accurate answers.67 Repeating the questions led AI Mode to name the winner for all three elections where it had previously only given links.8

Asked why, AI Mode blamed very strict safety filters for recent and sensitive election topics, but Orland cautions that AI models are unreliable at describing their own internal processes, so this is not the final word.910 A Google spokesperson first said the Gemini app answers the question more fully but that the Search response was not the most helpful, and later said the company is fixing the issue with 2020 election queries.1112

Techdirt's Ctrl-Alt-Speech podcast listed a Mediaite story, "Google's AI Chatbot Refuses to Answer Question of Who Won 2020 Presidential Election," among the topics covered in that week's episode.13
