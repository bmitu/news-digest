llama.cpp and ggml 1.0

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 14 · single origin (llama.cpp (ggml-org))

Version 1.0 has now been reached by both llama.cpp and ggml. According to the AINews newsletter from Latent Space, the release signals that the projects are stable, and it also drops 35 deprecated functions from the public header of llama.cpp.1 Hugging Face and ggml-org announced the v1.0.0 release, saying they intend to keep supporting a wide range of platforms and model types while ensuring the runtime stays "robust and lean" for downstream integrations.2 Among the new features mentioned: people who run mixture-of-experts models that exceed their GPU's memory can now reserve a cache for the experts they use most often.3

Hugging Face also announced Llama desktop apps built on llama.cpp, intended to cut local model setup to a few clicks.4 The newsletter says SGLang also announced a 1.0 release, though its main story that day was a separate "No AI Slop" manifesto.5

The main concern raised in comments is maintainership: commenters say llama.cpp has many open issues and PRs and ask for more maintainers.6 Other commenters emphasize its practical value as a hands-on way to start experimenting with local models.7
