Google reports autonomous AI cyberattacks

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 20 · 1 new article today · single origin (Google)

A report from Google's Threat Intelligence Group states that, for the first time, it has spotted malicious actors relying on artificial intelligence to carry out cyberattacks with complete automation.1 The report warned that the adversarial AI landscape has reached a critical inflection point.2 According to Google, as relayed in a post quoted by Zvi Mowshowitz, hackers are using AI to scan software updates, work out which flaw each patch fixes, and build attacks before companies can install the fix, with no human involved.3 Google is quoted as saying that the era of autonomous attacks has arrived and that defending against machine-speed threats at human speed is not feasible.45

Google says its answer is Google AI Threat Defense, a system pairing Gemini with other leading-edge models to locate and repair vulnerabilities ahead of any attacker's attempt to exploit them.6 Zvi Mowshowitz suggests the defenders' window may soon be over and expects attackers to hold the advantage, since they can target weaker defenses such as unpatched systems.78

The Center for AI Safety newsletter separately reports that some organizations hit by recent outages have attributed them to a rush to fix AI-discovered vulnerabilities before malicious actors could exploit them, while saying cyberattacks were not the cause.9
