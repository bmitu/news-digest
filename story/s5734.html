OpenAI matrix multiplication exponent 9/4 claim

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 09:22 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 25 · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI has claimed that the matrix multiplication exponent ω is exactly 9/4, in a 111-page paper, according to the posts quoted in the coverage.12 The new result is said to build on an October release in which OpenAI's model found an upper bound of 9/4.34

OpenAI's Sébastien Bubeck says the model then worked on a lower bound, for which nothing had been known before, and that the announcement shows 9/4 is tight.56 Bubeck adds that an older result by Yin Tat Lee and collaborators implies linear programs could then be solved in time n^{9/4}.7 Latent Space's AINews calls the lower bound the biggest news, because a matching lower and upper bound leaves only one possible value.89 One account says the result reportedly comes through an explicit superlinear circuit lower bound.10

Deedy framed the result as moving the best known value from 2.3712 in 2026 to 2.25, after 36 years of shaving off 0.005.11 However, Zvi Mowshowitz's roundup says the claim is actively disappointing, since many had hoped the exponent was 2.12 Bubeck says he never expected to see such a result in his lifetime.13
