OpenAI matrix multiplication exponent 9/4 claim

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
2 sources · tracked since Nov 25 · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI has claimed in a new 111-page paper that ω, the exponent of matrix multiplication, equals exactly 9/4. inference12 According to OpenAI's Sébastien Bubeck, the company's October 6 release had set 9/4 as a new upper bound, and its model then worked on the lower bound, leading to the announcement that the figure is tight.3456

Latent Space calls the lower bound the bigger news and says that, if it holds, it fixes the exponent for square matrices at 9/4.78 The outlet reports that OpenAI formalized the equality in Lean for arithmetic circuits without division, over any field, and that the result reportedly comes through an explicit superlinear lower bound on circuits.910 Bubeck says no lower bound had been known before, and that the result, combined with earlier work by Yin Tat Lee and collaborators, also means linear programs can be solved in n^{9/4} time.111213

Deedy, quoted by Zvi Mowshowitz, describes the problem as 57 years old and says that work from 1990 to August 2026 had trimmed only 0.005 from the bound, citing the figure 2.3712, before OpenAI's result put the value at 2.25.1415 Bubeck called it the sort of result he had never expected to witness during his life, while Deedy ranks the problem as arguably second in algorithms only to P = NP. inference1617 Mowshowitz's post, however, calls the outcome disappointing because many had hoped the exponent would turn out to be 2.18
