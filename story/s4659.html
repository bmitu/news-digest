Hugging Face Inference Providers via OpenRouter

Last scan Sat Nov 21, 06:54 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Hugging Face)

Hugging Face announced that it is switching its Inference Providers service for language models from its own custom router to OpenRouter's, so requests go through OpenRouter, which picks a provider per call on live price, speed and reliability.12 A Latent Space newsletter item also noted that Inference Providers now runs on OpenRouter with the same endpoint and token.3 Hugging Face states that for every chat model, its router works with the Chat Completions and Responses APIs from OpenAI as well as Anthropic's Messages API. It adds that prices stay as they were: users pay the provider's rate, with no extra Hugging Face charge.45 Image, video and audio generation are not affected and continue to run directly through Hugging Face Inference Providers rather than OpenRouter.6

Drawing on OpenRouter figures, Hugging Face reports that the share of tokens served by open-weight models climbed from 26.8% in October 2025 to 71.7% by the end of September 2026, and that on average these models are roughly four times cheaper than closed ones.78 It also acknowledges that the best open-weight models still trail proprietary ones on the hardest benchmarks, citing the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index where Kimi K3.1 Pro scores 46 against 65 for Claude Fable 5.2, at roughly a seventh of the cost per task.910

OpenRouter's own measurements show that after it rebuilt its routing, capacity errors fell 46%, time to first token got 16.9% better, and throughput rose 16.3%.11

Users can still steer routing with model-id suffixes such as :fastest, :cheapest or a named provider, but a breaking change means they can no longer use their own provider API keys for language models, since requests are now billed to their Hugging Face account or organization.1213 Hugging Face calls this the first step of the partnership, with plans to let users connect their OpenRouter account and route more tasks through OpenRouter.1415
