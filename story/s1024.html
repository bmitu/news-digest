Epic Disney game AI-first shift

Last scan Tue Oct 20, 08:37 UTC
3 sources · tracked since Oct 17 · single origin (Bloomberg)

Bloomberg reported that Epic Games delayed the release of an upcoming video-game collaboration with Walt Disney Co. and told its developers to use more artificial intelligence tools to finish the game, according to people familiar with the project. Developers were instructed to implement an "AI first" production model using tools such as Anthropic's Claude.123 One of the game's directors reportedly told staff in an internal message, "No more hand-written code," and that all art would be "generated with full AI workflows."456 The game is reportedly a Marvel and Star Wars shooter that was slated for March 2027 as part of a $1.5 billion Epic–Disney collaboration announced in early 2024; Ars Technica, citing the report, says it is now delayed indefinitely amid the AI shakeup and slower-than-expected, understaffed development.78

Bloomberg's account, as relayed by Zvi Mowshowitz, says the announcement left some Epic developers stunned on a video call, with some joking about asking Claude to fix its own mistakes.910 An Epic spokesperson said the company is "embracing AI-accelerated development, led by Epic developers who are the visionaries and creative drivers for everything we do."1112

Epic CEO Tim Sweeney wrote that a "huge pivot" is underway to retool for maximum AI acceleration, and that the aim is not to do the same work with fewer people but to let everyone build more and better.1314 Ars Technica notes that Epic laid off over 1,000 employees in March, citing a downturn in Fortnite engagement, and that Sweeney has argued AI tools will be involved in nearly all future production.151617

Zvi Mowshowitz argues the gamers have a point, saying that saying "everything is AI" is probably a one-way ticket to slop and that the tech and engineers are not ready for a broad prohibition on human code.1819 Mowshowitz also notes that the gaming audience is highly averse to AI, especially AI art, and says AI can still do bespoke work well with proper care.2021
