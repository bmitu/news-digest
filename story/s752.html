Iranian ChatGPT influence operation

Last scan Sat Oct 10, 12:51 UTC
2 sources · 187 points on Hacker News · single origin (OpenAI)

OpenAI says it recently banned two influence operations, one from Russia and one from Iran, that used its models alongside more traditional techniques to support "false front" entities that laundered geopolitical, conflict-related messaging to target audiences.12 OpenAI rated the Iranian operation Category 4 on the IO Breakout Scale, while it assessed the Russian one as Category 5, the first Category 5 operation it has disrupted since it began reporting.34

The Iranian operation, which OpenAI dubbed "Bogus Bylines," used a cluster of ChatGPT accounts that prompted in Persian, generated content in Persian and English, and used VPNs to hide their location.567 According to OpenAI, the operation used the models to refine long-form articles and editorial pitches, generate social media comments, and refine internal reports; the long-form articles focused on the US-Iran conflict.89

It pitched articles under seven fake bylines, including Ervin B. Hoskins, Noah Lamington, Sophia Gonzalez, Michael Harrison, Ericka Feusier, Jenny Williams and Alice Johnson, each purporting to be a Western journalist.1011 OpenAI says some personas were backed by social media accounts; Ervin B. Hoskins's bio claimed he was an American freelance writer, but transparency settings located his accounts in Iran.1213

OpenAI identified almost 100 articles published or syndicated under the bylines across roughly a dozen small to medium online outlets, whereas none of the social media comments appears to have drawn substantial engagement.1415 OpenAI also says the operators' internal reports used a deceptive calculation, possibly to inflate their apparent social media impact.16

OpenAI says the activity looked consistent with a commercial actor running a for-hire campaign but it could not identify the actor, and it has shared information with the relevant authorities.1718 OpenAI argues the operations closely resembled complex pre-AI influence operations, comparing the personas to the "Alice Donovan" front, and notes Meta disrupted an Iranian operation in March that also featured a "Michael Harrison" persona.1920
