Businesses in ChatGPT

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
4 sources · 3 independent origins: OpenAI, TechCrunch, The Verge

OpenAI rolled out a Business Hub for ChatGPT on Tuesday, and the feature is in beta.1234 It also introduces Business Agents, which OpenAI is offering first to eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses.56 According to The Verge, users can chat with these verified agents about a brand's offerings, such as for comparison shopping, and OpenAI says those chats will be labeled and kept apart from the main conversation.78

Business Hub offers aggregated insights, including the number of mentions and website clicks, plus "discovery topics" where a business's website is cited.910 OpenAI has not disclosed how verification works; VP of monetization Benji Shomair said only that different methods are used for different business types and markets.1112 A Latent Space roundup reports OpenAI's statement that user conversations are not shared with businesses.13

TechCrunch says the profiles closely resemble those offered by Google and other search engines, and that ChatGPT appears to be moving toward being more like Google.1415 TechCrunch notes that OpenAI's earlier effort to make ChatGPT a direct shopping hub, which included a checkout cart, failed and was retired in March, and says it is unclear whether this approach will do better.1617 OpenAI itself calls the announcement an early step in bringing businesses more deeply into ChatGPT.1819
