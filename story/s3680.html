SoftBank turbine loss

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
4 sources · 3 independent origins: Latitude Media, Reuters, TechCrunch

TechCrunch reports that SoftBank announced a $7.1 billion quarterly loss on Tuesday, more than half of it tied to power-generating equipment.1 Semafor and Latitude Media instead describe a $3.7 billion hit tied to turbine and power equipment orders, which Latitude Media says was a write-down of advance payments.23 According to reports, SoftBank paid deposits to secure over 13 GW of turbine capacity, which exceeds its actual requirement by nearly half.4

SoftBank says in its presentation materials that the loss is unrelated to the Ohio PORTS-Pike project and that it is entitled to rebates of up to $2.1 billion on future equipment purchases.5

Peter Gardett, CEO of Noreva, told TechCrunch the charge amounts to the price discovery finally happening after SoftBank over-ordered.6 Heatmap argues that SoftBank's failure to sell the turbines to a higher bidder weakly suggests demand for energy-producing assets may be softer than AI boosters believed.7

Latitude Media says walking away from the reservations feeds widespread fear that AI buildouts are outrunning real demand, and a fund manager quoted by Semafor says investors fear the music might stop. inference89 TechCrunch, however, judges that the losses are unlikely to trigger mass cancellations of gas power plants, though SoftBank may have found a ceiling for its gas ambitions.1011
