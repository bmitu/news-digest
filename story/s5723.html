Tech stocks rebound after Huang reinstatement

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
4 sources · tracked since Nov 24 · 3 new articles today · 2 independent origins: Fortune, Semafor

The Atlantic reports that Nvidia's board brought Jensen Huang back as chief executive. He had stepped down in the middle of November following claims that he was involved with at least one staff member, as well as with a client alleged to have been an intelligence asset for China's government.12 The Atlantic reports that Donald Trump reportedly intervened with the board to broker the return, and Trump said on CNBC that he had been speaking to the board directly and indirectly.34

Nvidia shares jumped 31 percent, the S&P 500 rose 4.7 percent and the Nasdaq 6.4 percent, and the Dow gained 1,463 points.5 Fortune puts the gain at about $950 billion in Nvidia market value, while Semafor calls it the largest single-day value creation in market history at $1 trillion.67 Zvi Mowshowitz's estimate, which assumes the market would otherwise have stayed flat, is that Huang coming back lifted Nvidia's value by about $1 trillion and the market as a whole by about $3 trillion.8 Danielle Fong, quoted by Mowshowitz, argues that much of the rise came before Trump's post, since an earlier premarket announcement came at 8:55 and Trump posted at 9:07, so he might account for only about 10 of the 30 points. inference91011

However, the rally did not last: Semafor says the surge faded quickly and Nvidia closed Tuesday at its July 2025 levels, and Mowshowitz notes it gave back a chunk the next day without a clear reason.1213 Fortune reports that stocks plunged Wednesday morning, with the Dow down more than 1,000 points and Nvidia down as much as 8%, before recovering much of the loss.14

The Atlantic's author characterizes the episode as a hostage situation in which AI leaders act with impunity, and notes that Elon Musk wrote that "The mistake has been corrected" while lawmakers in both parties are reportedly livid.151617 Semafor reports that when Huang spoke to staff on Tuesday, he said he does not intend to alter any of the underlying structural flaws that brought about the crisis.18
