Google Personal Intelligence in cars

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Google)

The Verge reports that Gemini can tap into Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, YouTube, and Search.1

Google's examples include checking flight status using details from a Gmail confirmation, and asking Gemini to "take me to my doctor's appointment" or to navigate to a car wash seen in a recent photo.234 Ars Technica's Ryan Whitwam says Google sees Personal Intelligence as a differentiator versus other AI systems, since it already holds so much user data.5

Google says the feature works only if users enable the Personal Intelligence setting, and personalized responses appear only when a phone is connected to the car.67 Google says that without Personal Intelligence, navigation, music, calls and questions still work, but answers won't be tailored to the user.8 Whitwam notes that in Google built-in cars, users can turn the feature off in in-car settings even if their phone has it enabled.9

Whitwam notes that Google has a habit of nudging users to opt in to data sharing in Gemini, so people may not realize they are sharing account data with Gemini, and that those settings will transfer over to Android Auto.10 He adds that Google says it doesn't use personal data to train Gemini but does use inputs and outputs, which may contain personal information that the company tries to filter without making promises, and he recommends checking data-sharing settings.1112
