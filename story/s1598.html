Attorneys general in Character.AI bankruptcy

Last scan Sat Nov 28, 17:01 UTC
12 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · single origin (Virginia Attorney General)

The states say a Dec. 1 auction is set to sell chats from over 21 million U.S. accounts, including more than 2.1 million registered to minors, along with users' personal data such as IP addresses. inference12

According to the states, whoever buys the data would be free of Character.AI's privacy policies and of any obligation to comply with requests to delete information, and could pass the data along to others without informing users, the states or the court.345 They also say Character.AI gave bidders information about its safety failures and legal exposure that it did not share with the court, the states or users.67

The only disclosed bidder, Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, was formed in Delaware on Nov. 13, one day before Character.AI picked it as the base bid, and the states ask the court to reject it and deny its $350,000 break-up fee.8910 At a minimum, the states say any sale must exclude kids' chats and other sensitive data and include protective conditions.111213 In their Nov. 20 filing, the states also ask Judge Brendan Shannon to keep specific kinds of sensitive data, as well as models trained on that data, out of the sale. inference1415

Connecticut Attorney General William Tong said Character.AI promised users they would never sell their personal information, and that the company now wants to abuse the bankruptcy process to do exactly what it said it wouldn't do: auction off private chat records and personal details.161718 The states argue that seeking bankruptcy protection does not free a company from laws protecting such data, and note that Character.AI's privacy policy told users, "we do not sell personal information for monetary consideration." inference1920

The coalition also opposed a separate $20 million Disney licensing deal, which has not received final court approval. Reports differ on the coalition's size, citing 40, 42 and at least 42 states. inference21222324 The Verge reports that Disney ended up buying some Character.AI assets and is taking on its chief executive, Karandeep Anand, in what it described as a sort of "acquihire."25

Minnesota's release says the United States Trustee selected data privacy expert Lucy Thompson, who will file reports identifying privacy issues and proposing protections before any assets change hands. Pennsylvania's office did not name her.262728 The privacy ombudsman's report is due a week before a Dec. 3 sale hearing, and a Tech Policy Press commentary argues that this allows only days to review years of training data and that the sale of data and models should wait. inference2930
