Attorneys general in Character.AI bankruptcy

Last scan Thu Nov 26, 05:11 UTC
12 sources · tracked since Oct 21 · 11 new articles today · single origin (Virginia Attorney General)

According to the states, whoever buys the data would be free of Character.AI's privacy policies and of any obligation to comply with requests to delete information, and could pass the data along to others without informing users, the states or the court.123 They also say Character.AI gave bidders information about its safety failures and legal exposure that it did not share with the court, the states or users.45

The only disclosed bidder, Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, was formed in Delaware on Nov. 13, one day before Character.AI picked it as the base bid, and the states ask the court to reject it and deny its $350,000 break-up fee.678 At a minimum, the states say any sale must exclude kids' chats and other sensitive data and include protective conditions.910

Connecticut Attorney General William Tong said Character.AI promised users they would never sell their personal information, and that the company now wants to abuse the bankruptcy process to do exactly what it said it wouldn't do: auction off private chat records and personal details.111213

The coalition also opposed a separate $20 million Disney licensing deal, which has not received final court approval. Reports differ on the coalition's size, citing 40, 42 and at least 42 states. inference14151617

Minnesota's release says the United States Trustee selected data privacy expert Lucy Thompson, who will file reports identifying privacy issues and proposing protections before any assets change hands. Pennsylvania's office did not name her.181920
