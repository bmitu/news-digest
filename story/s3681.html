CoreWeave Q3 earnings

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 2 independent origins: CoreWeave, TechCrunch

TechCrunch reports that CoreWeave's earnings, published on a Tuesday, showed $3.66 billion in revenue for the third quarter, a 168% increase over the same period the year before. The company also recorded a net loss of $709 million, along with interest costs of $846 million for the quarter.12 Semafor says the company beat Wall Street expectations and raised its annual forecast, which surprised investors.345

CoreWeave said it anticipates roughly $9 billion coming in as customer deposits and prepayments across the following three months. Over $3 billion of that is linked to fresh contracts covering capacity the company has already put in place.6 Intrator said CoreWeave is shifting some capacity to short-term deals at more than $40 million per megawatt annually, versus $20 million to $25 million for new four- and five-year contracts.7 TechCrunch notes the trade-off: prepayments could lock in prices that look less attractive if demand keeps surging, and lenders favor longer-term contracts for their predictable cash flow.89

Reports differ on how the market views the company: Semafor says US tech stocks rallied on the results, yet CoreWeave's roughly $41 billion of net debt is not covered by its cash generation. inference1011 Semafor's two accounts of the year-to-date share decline also disagree, one saying shares are down 20% and the other nearly 40%. inference1213 TechCrunch adds that bond investors are less upbeat, with yields on some CoreWeave bonds above 15% last month, reflecting worry about debt funding data center buildouts.14

A Bloomberg columnist, quoted by Semafor, said a healthier CoreWeave offers some reassurance to the broader AI market and especially to Anthropic.15
