Tesla-SpaceX merger

Last scan Sat Oct 31, 06:24 UTC
2 sources · single origin (Financial Times)

Elon Musk revealed that Tesla and SpaceX were exploring a merger that could accelerate his push into autonomous driving, disclosing it five days after a viral "flying" Roadster stunt.1 Musk argues the combination would pair the best car and rocket companies, saying people will see the results of that collaboration.2

The context is Tesla's pressure to compete in autonomy: cars generate 75 percent of its revenue, it delivered 1.6 million vehicles last year against a target cut from 20 million to 5 million by 2030, and it ceded the top EV spot to BYD.345

A separate newsletter also reports that Musk is looking to merge Tesla and SpaceX, which it says owns xAI.6 An Ars Technica piece says a merged company could speed research into materials, AI and manufacturing while cutting costs and improving access to critical parts such as chips.7

Lei Xing of AutoXing says autonomy will likely benefit from SpaceX technology, and one top-15 Tesla shareholder said he supported the merger.89 The deal could also boost Musk's control: William Blair analyst Jed Dorsheimer says that, if shareholders and regulators approve, Musk could control 50 percent or more of the merged entity.10

Max Willson, an industry observer and Tesla enthusiast, says Elon Musk is playing a dangerous game, and warns that trouble in AI could spread across the group.1112
