AO3 outage

Last scan Sat Nov 7, 03:59 UTC
2 sources · 2 independent origins: 404 Media, Organization for Transformative Works

AO3, the fanfiction repository owned by the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW), went down on Thursday after a security incident, according to the OTW, and is expected to stay down for as long as four weeks.12 The OTW says no data has been lost, and AO3 hosts nearly 18.5 million works.34 Zvi Mowshowitz's roundup likewise noted that AO3 is down for two to four weeks and that the operators assure users all data is intact.5 404 Media reports that readers and authors are generally distraught at being unable to access the site, potentially for a month.6

During the outage, a site called ao3.life scraped AO3 works, reuploaded them and charged for AI-generated audiobook versions, according to 404 Media.7 Its creator, who goes by Ganesh, says the site got over 2,000 takedown requests in the single day it was up and had 126,592 unique users within 24 hours, and went down Monday evening after widespread backlash.8910 Ganesh says only about 11 audiobooks were sold, that the charging was meant to cover server costs, and that the site promised authors 91% of profits.1112

One AO3 author posted on Bluesky that works they had archive locked were still on the site, and the site required authors to prove ownership by leaving a note on their AO3 profile, which 404 Media says is a mystery to do while AO3 is down.131415 Separately, some people are sharing Google Drive links of previously downloaded works on Reddit and Discord, which 404 Media says has alarmed and angered a lot of writers.16
