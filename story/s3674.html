Apple Harbor local AI app

Last scan Sat Nov 14, 15:53 UTC
3 sources · 567 points on Hacker News · single origin (9to5Mac)

Leaker Aaron Perris says he found evidence of a new Apple Mac app called Harbor, which is meant to make it easy to run local AI models on one or more Macs.1 According to the app's description that Perris posted, Harbor turns a Mac into a private AI inference server that runs open-weight models locally on Apple silicon, with data staying on the user's hardware and nothing reaching the cloud.2

The listing says that connecting two or more Thunderbolt 5-capable Macs lets Harbor pool their unified memory into a single inference resource, allowing models that exceed any single computer's capacity or more concurrent requests.34 The listing also says Harbor automatically detects devices over Thunderbolt 5 to form a cluster, and lets users browse, download and load open-weight models from within the app.56

9to5Mac reports that Harbor is currently in a private preview under Apple's Claris brand and says it seems likely to launch publicly in the coming weeks and months, which is the outlet's expectation rather than a confirmed date.7
