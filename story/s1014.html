Anthropic builds Scion source control

Last scan Wed Oct 21, 20:11 UTC
3 sources · 683 points on Hacker News · tracked since Oct 17 · 1 new article this week · 2 independent origins: DeepLearning.AI, Rain (sunshowers)

Zvi Mowshowitz writes that Anthropic's needs are very different from the way Git works, as they are full of agents and the codebase is way too big to be copying, and that Scion is a lot more efficient for them.1 Latent Space's summary, citing a post by dl_weekly, says the replacement was built with seven people after merges grew from 2,500 to over 30,000 per day.2 According to Anthropic's post as quoted by Mowshowitz, a handwritten prototype of the git-to-Scion ingestion pipeline was rewritten by an early internal snapshot of Claude Mythos Preview, producing a version that used 20x less memory and was 4x faster.3 Mowshowitz also says Anthropic reports that Claude Mythos Preview and Claude Tag, both available internally, accelerated how quickly it could build Scion.4

The author says crediting Sapling only for "hard-won lessons" while not mentioning Mononoke borders on plagiarism, though they say they have no problem with the design being copied.56 In a follow-up, the author claims to have received private confirmation that Scion intentionally copied Mononoke and that personnel overlapped, and says the changeset model matches Mononoke's "Bonsai changeset" exactly.78 The author adds that this makes Anthropic's achievement significantly less impressive, though still quite stunning.910 The follow-up says Nick G. from Anthropic responded on Hacker News and that sufficient attribution has since been added to Anthropic's blog post, but the author stands by their earlier posts.1112
